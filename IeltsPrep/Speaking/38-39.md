# IELTS Speaking Part 2 — Cue Cards 38 & 39

---

# 38. Describe a person who often helps others

## You should say:
- Who this person is
- How often he/she helps others
- How/why he/she helps others
- And how you feel about this person

## Band 6.5–7 Answer

I would like to talk about my uncle Yanis, who is one of the kindest and most generous people I have ever met. He is also a talented web developer and a top-rated freelancer.

I have known him for quite a long time, and I know that he often helps other people, especially when they face problems related to web development. For example, if someone is trying to fix a bug or add a new feature to a website or software, he is always willing to help them. He usually explains the problem clearly and teaches them how to solve it.

What I particularly admire about him is that he does not simply give people the solution. Instead, he patiently listens to their problems and explains how they can deal with similar issues in the future. I think this is a very useful way of helping others because they can learn something instead of simply depending on him.

I really admire him because he is not only a skilled software engineer but also a kind and helpful person. His willingness to support others has inspired me a lot, and I would like to become more like him in the future.

# 39. Describe a piece of technology (not a phone) that you would like to own

## You should say:
- What it is
- How much it costs
- How you knew about it
- And explain why you would like to own it

## Band 6.5–7 Answer

I would like to talk about a Mac, which is a piece of technology I have wanted to own for quite some time. As I work as a software engineer, I believe that having a powerful computer would make my work much easier and more efficient.

The particular Mac I have in mind would cost around one lakh taka, depending on its specifications. I first became interested in it because I frequently research different technologies and tools, especially devices that are related to my work. I have watched several YouTube videos and read technology blogs to learn more about Macs and their performance.

At the moment, I am using a regular PC, but it is not very fast because it has limited RAM and storage. When I work with development tools such as Android Studio, I sometimes need more RAM and faster storage, particularly an SSD, to run everything smoothly.

The main reason I would like to own a Mac is that I believe it would allow me to work more efficiently and improve my productivity. It could help me run demanding software more smoothly and reduce the amount of time I spend waiting for applications to load or compile.

Overall, I think a Mac would be a worthwhile investment for me because it could improve my current workflow and help me deliver better-quality projects. As a software engineer, having a reliable and powerful computer would be extremely beneficial for my work.

# ⭐ Useful Structures to Memorize

## 1. Introducing a person

> I would like to talk about ___, who is one of the ___ people I have ever met.

## 2. Introducing technology

> I would like to talk about ___, which is a piece of technology I have wanted to own for quite some time.

## 3. Giving an example

> For example, if someone is trying to ___, he/she is always willing to help.

## 4. Explaining what you admire

> What I particularly admire about him/her is that ___.

## 5. Not only... but also...

> He/She is not only ___ but also ___.

## 6. Explaining the reason

> The main reason I would like to ___ is that ___.

## 7. Explaining the benefit

> I believe that ___ would allow me to work more efficiently and improve my productivity.

## 8. Giving your final opinion

> Overall, I think ___ would be a worthwhile investment because ___.
