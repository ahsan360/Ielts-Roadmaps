# IELTS Speaking Test — Part 2 & Part 3 Responses

## Part 2 — Special Cake 🎂

### Cue Card Prompt
**Describe a special cake you received from others**

### Candidate Response
I would like to talk about a special cake that one of my friends brought for me last year on my birthday. It happened at my home, and it was actually quite unexpected because I hadn't arranged any party or celebration for my birthday.

My friend remembered my birthday and suddenly came to my home with a cake. When I saw him with the cake, I was really surprised because I wasn't expecting him to visit me. First, we cut the cake together, and then we had lunch. After that, we spent the rest of the day together, talking, relaxing, and enjoying each other's company.

What made the cake particularly special was not its price or appearance, but the emotion behind it. Although the cake itself wasn't very expensive, my friend remembered my birthday and made an effort to celebrate it with me. That really touched me because I hadn't even invited him or told him that I was planning anything.

Another thing I liked was the flavor. He knew that I had loved chocolate cake since my childhood, so he made sure to get a chocolate cake for me. Overall, it was a simple cake, but because it came from a close friend and carried so much emotion, it became a very memorable experience for me.

> **⏱️ Delivery Time:** Approximately 1:45–2:00 minutes

## Part 3 — Follow-up Discussion Questions

### Question 1
**What do you think of people using their mobile phones during a meal?**

> **Response:**  
> I think using mobile phones during a meal is generally a bad habit, especially when people are eating with their family or friends. The main reason is that they can become so focused on their phones that they don't communicate with the people around them. Even though they are physically sitting together, they may not actually interact with each other. As a result, it can reduce face-to-face communication and gradually affect their relationships.
>
> **⏱️ Delivery Time:** 30–35 seconds

---

### Question 2
**What are the differences between special food in India and other countries?**

> **Response:**  
> I think Indian food is quite different from food in many other countries, mainly because of the use of spices and condiments. Indian dishes often contain a wide variety of spices, which gives them a strong and distinctive flavor. In contrast, some other countries tend to use fewer spices in their traditional dishes. So, I would say that the rich use of spices is one of the main things that makes Indian food special and different from food in other countries.
>
> **⏱️ Delivery Time:** 30–35 seconds

---

### Question 3
**Is there any food in your country that is eaten at special times or on special occasions?**

> **Response:**  
> Yes, definitely. One food that is commonly eaten on special occasions in Bangladesh is biryani. It is a very popular traditional dish, and people often have it at weddings, birthdays, family gatherings, and other celebrations. In fact, many people feel that a celebration is incomplete without a good plate of biryani. That's why I would say that biryani has become almost symbolic of special occasions in Bangladesh.
>
> **⏱️ Delivery Time:** 30–35 seconds

---

### Question 4
**Why are some people willing to spend a lot of money on meals on special days?**

> **Response:**  
> I think people are willing to spend a lot of money on special days because they want to make the occasion memorable. For example, on birthdays, anniversaries, or other important celebrations, they may go to an expensive restaurant or invite a large number of people. Although it can be quite costly, they usually don't consider it a waste of money because the occasion is meaningful to them. They see it as a way of creating memorable moments with their loved ones.
>
> **⏱️ Delivery Time:** 30–35 seconds

---

### Question 5
**Do you think it's good to communicate when eating with your family?**

> **Response:**  
> Yes, absolutely. I think communicating with family members while eating is a very good habit because people are often busy with work or other responsibilities during the day. Mealtimes, especially dinner, give family members an opportunity to sit together and talk about their daily experiences. They can ask each other how their day went and discuss different things. I believe this kind of face-to-face communication can strengthen family relationships, which is why I think it is much better than using mobile phones during meals.
>
> **⏱️ Delivery Time:** 30–35 seconds

Overall, it was a simple cake, but the thought and emotion behind it made the experience extremely memorable for me.
