Part 3
1. Do you think parents should teach their children how to protect the environment?

Yes, I think parents should teach their children how to protect nature because it is a good way for them to understand the importance of environmental protection. At school or university, there are usually few practical activities related to protecting nature, as most subjects are theoretical. Therefore, parents can teach their children through real-life situations. As a result, children can learn how to deal with environmental problems and natural disasters.

2. What laws about the environment are effective in your country?

Currently, several environmental laws are in effect in my country. For example, there is a ban on certain plastic products, and people are encouraged to dispose of waste in appropriate places. If an individual doesn't obey these rules, they can be punished with a fine of around five to ten dollars. As a result, these regulations can help reduce pollution and encourage people to keep their surroundings clean.

3. Which do you think people prefer, rewards or punishment, when it comes to government intervention in environmental protection?

I think it depends on the situation and personal preferences. People who usually follow government rules may feel that rewards are more motivating because they can feel that their efforts are recognized. For example, if individuals help keep their communities clean, they could receive some kind of reward. On the other hand, people who regularly disobey rules might consider punishment a burden, but it can still discourage them from breaking environmental regulations.

4. Is it easy for children in cities to get close to the natural world?

I don't think it is very easy for children in cities because many urban areas have limited green spaces and fewer trees. Therefore, children may need to leave the city to experience nature. For example, they can visit rural areas, which usually offer plenty of greenery and natural surroundings. In these places, the natural environment is often better preserved, so children can spend more time outdoors and develop a closer connection with nature.

5. What can people do to protect the natural world?

People can take several measures to protect nature. For example, they can reduce their use of fossil fuels and use renewable energy instead. They can also avoid activities that damage the natural environment, such as throwing garbage in public places or cutting down trees unnecessarily. I think these simple actions can help protect the environment and preserve nature for future generations.

6. Is it important to teach students environmental protection at school?

Of course, I think it is very important because if students are taught about environmental protection at school, they can learn about it at an early age. As a result, they can become involved in activities that help protect nature. They can also understand how human activities damage the environment and learn what they can do to prevent environmental destruction. Therefore, schools can play an important role in developing environmentally responsible citizens.
