### 3. Home / Accommodation
* Do you live in a house or flat? Or What kind of housing/accommodation do you live in?[cite: 1, 2]
* Please describe it a little.[cite: 1, 2]
* What's your favorite room in your home?[cite: 1, 2]
* What have you done to your room to make it look nice?[cite: 1, 2]
* Who do you live with?[cite: 1, 2]
* Which room does your family spend most of the time in?[cite: 1, 2]
* How long have you lived there?[cite: 1, 2]
* Do you plan to live there a long time?[cite: 2]
* (If you answer you haven't lived there long) What's the difference between where you are living now and where you have lived in the past?[cite: 2]
* What do you usually do in your house/flat/room?[cite: 2]
* Are the transport facilities to your hometown very good?[cite: 2]
* Do you plan to move?[cite: 2]
* Would you like to move to another place to live? (Why/why not?)[cite: 2]
* Are you prepared to move?[cite: 2]

## Part 2: Cue Cards

### Topic 1: Special Cake
**Describe a special cake you received from others**[cite: 5]
You should say:[cite: 5]
* When it happened[cite: 5]
* Where it happened[cite: 5]
* Who you got the cake from[cite: 5]
* And explain why it's a special cake[cite: 5]

### Topic 2: Natural World Caregiver
**Describe a person who likes to look after the natural world**[cite: 6]
You should say:[cite: 6]
* Who this person is[cite: 6]
* What he or she does[cite: 6]
* How he or she does it[cite: 6]
* How often he or she does it[cite: 6]
* And explain how you feel about this person[cite: 6]

### Topic 3: Story/Book with Animals
**Describe a story/book with animals in it**[cite: 7]
You should say:[cite: 7]
* What animals are in it[cite: 7]
* What the story/book is about[cite: 7]
* Why you read the story/book[cite: 7]
* And explain what you think of this story/book[cite: 7]

### Topic 4: Problem Using an Electronic Device
**Describe a time when you had a problem using an electronic device**[cite: 8]
You should say:[cite: 8]
* When it happened[cite: 8]
* Where it happened[cite: 8]
* What the problem was[cite: 8]
* And explain how you solved the problem at last[cite: 8]

## Part 3: Discussion / Follow-up Questions

### Topic 1 Follow-ups (Special Cake / Food)
* What do you think of people using their mobile phones during a meal?[cite: 5]
* What are the differences between special food in India and other countries?[cite: 5]
* Is there any food in your country that is eaten at special times or on special occasions?[cite: 5]
* Why are some people willing to spend a lot of money on meals on special days?[cite: 5]
* Do you think it's good to communicate when eating with your family?[cite: 5]

### Topic 2 Follow-ups (Natural World / Environment)
* Do you think parents should teach their children how to protect the environment?[cite: 6]
* What laws about the environment are effective in your country?[cite: 6]
* Which do you think people prefer, rewards or punishment, when it comes to government intervention in environmental protection?[cite: 6]
* Is it easy for children in cities to get close to the natural world?[cite: 6]
* What can people do to protect the natural world?[cite: 6]
* Is it important to teach students environmental protection at school?[cite: 6]

* Do many people keep pets in your country?[cite: 7]
* What are the advantages of keeping a pet?[cite: 7]
* Why do people always tell children stories with animals?[cite: 7]

### Topic 4 Follow-ups (Electronic Devices / Technology)
