# IELTS Speaking — Band 6.5 Sample Answers

## Part 1: General Questions

---

# 1. Work

### Q1. What work do you do?

**Answer:**

I work as a software engineer. I mainly develop web applications and backend systems. Most of the time, I solve technical problems and sometimes I learn about algorithms and data structures.

### Q2. Why did you choose to do that type of work?

**Answer:**

I chose software engineering because I have always been interested in computers and technology. I also enjoy solving problems and building useful applications. I thought this career would give me a good opportunity to use my skills in a practical way.

### Q3. Do you like your job?

**Answer:**

Yes, I do. I like it because every day brings new challenges, so I don't usually do exactly the same thing. Sometimes the work can be stressful, but I find it rewarding when I solve a difficult problem.

### Q4. Is it very interesting?

**Answer:**

Yes, I find it quite interesting. Technology changes very quickly, so I have to learn new things regularly. I also enjoy working on different projects because they give me different kinds of challenges.

### Q5. Do you miss being a student?

**Answer:**

Yes, I sometimes miss those days. When I was a student, I had more time to spend with my friends and family. However, I prefer working now because I can apply my knowledge to real-world projects.

---

# 2. Studies

### Q1. What subject did you study?

**Answer:**

I studied Computer Science and Engineering at university. I learned about programming, algorithms, data structures and several mathematical subjects. I found some of them quite challenging, but they were useful for my career.

### Q2. Why did you choose to study that subject?

**Answer:**

I chose it because I was very interested in computers and technology. I also enjoyed solving logical problems. I thought studying computer science would allow me to build useful software and develop a good career.

### Q3. Do you like your subject? Why?

**Answer:**

Yes, I do. I particularly enjoyed programming and algorithms because I had to solve different kinds of problems. It was sometimes difficult, but I felt satisfied whenever I found a solution.

### Q4. Is it very interesting?

**Answer:**

Yes, definitely. I think computer science is interesting because technology is constantly changing. There are always new tools and technologies to learn, so it doesn't feel repetitive.

### Q5. Are you looking forward to working?

**Answer:**

Yes, definitely. I am interested in developing my career and gaining more practical experience. I think working on real-world projects can teach me things that I cannot learn from textbooks alone.

### Q6. Do you prefer to study in the mornings or afternoons?

**Answer:**

I prefer studying in the morning because I can concentrate better at that time. My mind feels fresh, so I usually do difficult tasks in the morning. In the afternoon, I sometimes feel less energetic.

---

# 3. Home / Accommodation

### Q1. Do you live in a house or a flat?

**Answer:**

I live in a house with my family. It's in a fairly quiet area, and there are several shops and other facilities nearby. Overall, I find it quite comfortable and convenient.

### Q2. Please describe it a little.

**Answer:**

It's a reasonably spacious house with several bedrooms, a living room and a kitchen. We also have a small outdoor area where we can sit and relax. The neighborhood is relatively peaceful.

### Q3. What's your favorite room in your home?

**Answer:**

My favorite room is my bedroom because it is the quietest part of the house. I usually work, read or watch videos there. I feel comfortable there, especially when I need some time alone.

### Q4. What have you done to your room to make it look nice?

**Answer:**

I've kept my room fairly simple because I don't like having too many things around me. I have a comfortable desk, some books and a few small decorations. I also try to keep everything organized.

### Q5. Who do you live with?

**Answer:**

I live with my family. We usually spend some time together in the evening, particularly during dinner. I enjoy living with them because we can support each other.

### Q6. Which room does your family spend most of the time in?

**Answer:**

We probably spend the most time in the living room. We usually sit there in the evening, watch television and talk about our day. It's basically the main place where we spend time together.

### Q7. How long have you lived there?

**Answer:**

I've lived there for most of my life. My family has been living there for many years, so I have a lot of childhood memories associated with the house.

### Q8. Do you plan to live there for a long time?

**Answer:**

I'm not completely sure. I feel comfortable there, but I may move somewhere else in the future because of my career. It will probably depend on where I find a suitable opportunity.

### Q9. What do you usually do in your house?

**Answer:**

I usually work, study and spend time with my family. In my free time, I watch YouTube videos, read something or simply relax. I also sometimes work on personal projects.

### Q10. Are the transport facilities to your hometown very good?

**Answer:**

Yes, I would say they are reasonably good. There are buses and other forms of transport connecting my hometown with major cities. However, travelling can sometimes be difficult because of traffic.

### Q11. Do you plan to move?

**Answer:**

Yes, possibly. If I find a good career opportunity in another city or country, I would consider moving. However, I would need to plan everything carefully before making such a decision.

### Q12. Would you like to move to another place to live?

**Answer:**

Yes, I would be open to it. Living somewhere new would allow me to experience a different environment and meet different people. However, I would probably miss my family and hometown.

### Q13. Are you prepared to move?

**Answer:**

Yes, I think I am. Moving to a new place can be challenging, but I'm willing to adapt if it is necessary for my career. I would just need to prepare everything in advance.

# 4. Hometown

### Q1. What's the name of your hometown?

**Answer:**

My hometown is Sylhet, which is in the northeastern part of Bangladesh.

### Q2. Is it a big city or a small place?

**Answer:**

It's a moderately sized city. It isn't as crowded as Dhaka, but it has most of the facilities that people need. It's also surrounded by a lot of greenery.

### Q3. Please describe your hometown a little.

**Answer:**

Sylhet is well known for its tea gardens, rivers and natural scenery. It is relatively peaceful compared with some larger cities in Bangladesh. There are also several popular tourist destinations nearby.

### Q4. How long have you been living there?

**Answer:**

I've lived there for most of my life. I grew up there, so I know the city quite well and have many childhood memories there.

### Q5. Do you like your hometown?

**Answer:**

Yes, I really like it. What I appreciate most is its peaceful atmosphere and natural beauty. I also enjoy the local food and spending time with my friends there.

### Q6. Do you like living there?

**Answer:**

Yes, I do. Life is relatively relaxed, and there are many natural places where I can go when I need a break. However, there are fewer career opportunities than in some larger cities.

### Q7. Would you say it's an interesting place?

**Answer:**

Yes, definitely. There are many interesting places to visit, especially if you enjoy nature. For example, there are tea gardens, rivers and several scenic areas around the city.

### Q8. What do you like most about your hometown?

**Answer:**

I would say the natural environment. There is a lot of greenery, and the atmosphere is generally peaceful. I think that's one of the main reasons people enjoy visiting Sylhet.

### Q9. What's your favorite part of your hometown?

**Answer:**

My favorite part is probably the areas around the tea gardens and rivers. I enjoy going there with my friends because we can relax and enjoy the scenery.

### Q10. Is there anything you dislike about it?

**Answer:**

Yes. One thing I don't like is the traffic in some busy areas. Also, there aren't as many professional opportunities as there are in larger cities.

### Q11. Do you think you will continue living there for a long time?

**Answer:**

I'm not sure. I would like to stay there because my family lives there, but my career might require me to move somewhere else. So, it will probably depend on my future opportunities.

### Q12. Are there any tourist attractions in your hometown?

**Answer:**

Yes, there are several. Jaflong is probably one of the most famous places, and there are also many tea gardens and natural attractions. A lot of tourists visit Sylhet to enjoy the scenery.

### Q13. Did your friends also grow up in the same place as you?

**Answer:**

Yes, some of my closest friends grew up in Sylhet as well. We visited many of the same places when we were younger, so we have quite a few shared memories.

### Q14. Would you like to live there in the future?

**Answer:**

Yes, I would consider it. If I could find a good job that allowed me to work remotely, I would probably prefer to stay in Sylhet because I really like the peaceful environment.

### Sample Answer

# Topic 1: Special Cake / Food

### Q1. What do you think of people using their mobile phones during a meal?

**Answer:**

I don't think it's a very good habit, especially when people are eating with their families or friends. Mealtimes can be a good opportunity to communicate and spend quality time together. However, checking a phone briefly for an urgent message is understandable.

### Q2. What are the differences between special food in India and other countries?

**Answer:**

I think every country has its own special food based on its culture and traditions. In India, for example, many special dishes contain a variety of spices and are often prepared during festivals. Other countries may have completely different ingredients and cooking methods.

### Q3. Is there any food in your country that is eaten at special times or on special occasions?

**Answer:**

Yes. In Bangladesh, certain traditional dishes are commonly prepared during festivals, weddings and other family occasions. For example, biryani is very popular at celebrations. Sweet dishes are also often served when guests visit or during important events.

### Q4. Why are some people willing to spend a lot of money on meals on special days?

**Answer:**

I think special days are important to people, so they want to make them memorable. Some people also see expensive meals as a way of showing appreciation to their family or friends. In addition, they may want to try food that they normally cannot afford.

### Q5. Do you think it's good to communicate when eating with your family?

**Answer:**

Yes, definitely. I think family meals are a good opportunity to talk about everyday life and maintain close relationships. If family members regularly eat together and communicate, they may develop a stronger connection.


# Topic 2: Natural World / Environment

### Q1. Do you think parents should teach their children how to protect the environment?

**Answer:**

Yes, definitely. Parents play an important role in developing children's habits. For example, they can teach children not to waste water, reduce plastic use and keep their surroundings clean. If children learn these habits early, they may continue them as adults.

### Q2. What laws about the environment are effective in your country?

**Answer:**

I think laws related to reducing plastic pollution and protecting forests can be effective. However, simply having laws is not enough. The authorities also need to enforce them properly and make sure that people follow the rules.

### Q3. Which do you think people prefer, rewards or punishment, when it comes to government intervention in environmental protection?

**Answer:**

I think people generally respond well to rewards because they give them a positive reason to change their behavior. For example, the government could provide incentives to businesses that reduce plastic waste. However, punishment may also be necessary when people repeatedly break environmental laws.

### Q4. Is it easy for children in cities to get close to the natural world?

**Answer:**

Not always. Cities often have many buildings and busy roads, so children may have fewer opportunities to experience nature. However, parents can take them to parks, gardens or nearby natural areas during weekends.

### Q5. What can people do to protect the natural world?

**Answer:**

There are several things people can do. They can reduce plastic use, save water and electricity, and use public transport when possible. They can also plant trees and avoid throwing rubbish in public places.

### Q6. Is it important to teach students environmental protection at school?

**Answer:**

Yes, I think it is important. Schools can teach children about environmental problems and encourage them to develop responsible habits. For example, students could take part in tree-planting activities or recycling programs.


# Topic 3: Animals / Pets / Stories

### Q1. Should schools teach children about animals?

**Answer:**

Yes, I think they should. Learning about animals can help children understand nature and develop a sense of responsibility. It can also make lessons more interesting, especially when students learn through pictures, videos or outdoor activities.

### Q2. Some people think pets should not be kept in cities. What do you think?

**Answer:**

I don't completely agree with that idea. I think people can keep pets in cities as long as they have enough space and can take proper care of them. However, keeping large animals in a very small apartment may not be suitable.

### Q3. Many people regard pets as members of their family. What do you think?

**Answer:**

I can understand why people feel that way. Pets often live with their owners for many years and provide companionship. People may become emotionally attached to them, so it is natural for them to consider their pets part of the family.

### Q4. Do many people keep pets in your country?

**Answer:**

Yes, some people do, although I don't think pets are as common as they are in some Western countries. Cats and birds are particularly popular. However, some families avoid keeping pets because they require time and regular care.

### Q5. What are the advantages of keeping a pet?

**Answer:**

One major advantage is companionship. Pets can help people feel less lonely, particularly if they live alone. They can also teach children responsibility because children can help with feeding and taking care of them.

### Q6. Why do people always tell children stories with animals?

**Answer:**

I think children naturally find animals interesting. Animal characters can make stories more entertaining and easier to remember. In addition, these stories often teach moral lessons about friendship, honesty or cooperation.


# Topic 4: Electronic Devices / Technology

### Q1. Why are people keen on buying new electronic devices?

**Answer:**

I think people buy new devices because they want better features and improved performance. Technology develops quickly, so newer products often offer better cameras, faster processors or longer battery life. Some people also buy them because they enjoy using the latest technology.

### Q2. What impact do electronic devices have on people?

**Answer:**

Electronic devices have both positive and negative effects. On the positive side, they make communication, education and work much easier. However, excessive use can reduce physical activity and face-to-face communication. So, I think the impact depends largely on how people use these devices.
