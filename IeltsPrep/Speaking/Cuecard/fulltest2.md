# IELTS Speaking — Band 6.5 Sample Answers

## Part 1 Questions

---

# 11. Tidiness

### 1. Do you like to keep things tidy?

Yes, I do. I prefer to keep my room and workplace tidy because it helps me concentrate better. If things are messy, I sometimes find it difficult to focus on my work.

### 2. Did you use to keep your room tidy as a child?

Not really. When I was a child, I was quite careless about keeping my room tidy. My parents often reminded me to put my books and clothes in the right place.

---

# 12. Music

### 1. Do you prefer sad or happy music?

I generally prefer happy music because it improves my mood. When I am tired or stressed, I usually listen to something energetic because it helps me feel more positive.

### 2. Does happy music make you feel more excited?

Yes, definitely. Happy music can make me feel more energetic, especially when I am travelling or doing some routine work. Sometimes I even sing along with it.

---

# 13. Teacher

### 1. Do you have a favourite teacher?

Yes, I do. One of my favourite teachers was my school mathematics teacher. He was very patient and always explained difficult topics with simple examples.

### 2. In what way has your favourite teacher helped you?

He helped me become more confident in solving difficult problems. Whenever I made a mistake, he did not criticise me. Instead, he explained where I had gone wrong and encouraged me to try again.

### 3. Are you still in touch with your primary school teachers?

Not regularly. I have lost contact with most of my primary school teachers because many years have passed. However, I sometimes see their updates on social media.

### 4. Do you have a teacher from your past that you still remember?

Yes. I still remember one of my English teachers because he was extremely supportive. He encouraged me to speak English in front of the class, which helped me overcome my fear of making mistakes.

### 5. Do you want to be a teacher in the future?

I don't think so, at least not as a full-time career. I would rather work in the technology sector. However, I would enjoy teaching programming to beginners because I like explaining technical problems.

---

# 14. Social Media

### 1. Have you ever posted anything on social media?

Yes, I have. I sometimes post photos from trips, special occasions, or interesting moments from my daily life. However, I don't post very frequently.

### 2. When did you start using social media?

I started using social media when I was in secondary school. At that time, I mainly used it to communicate with my classmates and keep in touch with friends.

### 3. Do you think you spend too much time on social media?

Sometimes, yes. If I am not careful, I can spend quite a lot of time scrolling through short videos. I try to limit my usage because it can easily become a distraction.

### 4. Do your friends use social media?

Yes, almost all of my friends use social media. They mainly use it to communicate, share photos and follow news or entertainment content.

### 5. What do people often do on social media?

People do many different things. They communicate with friends, watch videos, share photographs, follow celebrities and businesses, and sometimes use social media to learn new things or promote their work.

---

# 15. Clothing

### 1. What kind of clothes do you like to wear?

I usually prefer simple and comfortable clothes. For everyday activities, I often wear T-shirts, jeans or casual trousers because they are practical and easy to wear.

### 2. Do you prefer to wear comfortable and casual clothes or smart clothes?

I definitely prefer comfortable and casual clothes for everyday life. However, I wear smart clothes when I attend an important meeting, a formal event, or a job interview.

### 3. Do you like wearing T-shirts?

Yes, I do. T-shirts are comfortable, affordable and easy to match with different types of trousers. I wear them quite often, especially when I am at home or going somewhere casually.

### 4. Do you spend a lot of time choosing clothes?

No, not really. I usually choose clothes quite quickly because I don't pay much attention to fashion. For me, comfort and a simple appearance are more important.

# 16. Dream and Ambition

### 1. What was your childhood dream?

When I was a child, I wanted to become a successful professional and have a comfortable life. As I grew older, I became particularly interested in computers, so my career goal gradually changed towards software engineering.

### 2. Are you the kind of person who sticks to dreams?

Yes, I would say so. Sometimes my plans change, but I usually keep working towards my main goals. I believe that consistency is more important than making quick progress.

### 3. What is your dream job?

My dream job is to work as a software engineer at a well-known international technology company. I would like to work on challenging products, learn from experienced engineers, and develop my technical skills.

### 4. Do you think you are an ambitious person?

Yes, I think I am reasonably ambitious. I usually set goals for myself and try to improve gradually. However, I don't want to sacrifice my personal life just to achieve professional success.

# 4. Describe a time when you had a problem using an electronic device

### You should say:

* When it happened
* Where it happened
* What the problem was
* And explain how you solved the problem at last

### Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about a time when I had a problem with my laptop.

It happened about a year ago while I was working from home. I was in the middle of an important project, and suddenly my laptop became extremely slow. After a few minutes, it stopped responding completely. I tried clicking different applications, but nothing happened.

At first, I was quite worried because I had some important work that I needed to finish that day. I restarted the laptop several times, but the problem kept coming back. Then I checked the storage and discovered that my computer was almost completely full.

Since I work in the technology field, I had some idea about how to solve the problem. I removed some unnecessary files and applications and transferred several large files to an external drive. After that, I restarted the laptop again, and it started working normally.

It was quite stressful at the time, but I learned that I should regularly clean up my computer and keep enough free storage. Since then, I have tried to manage my files more carefully.

# 5. Describe an advertisement with a famous person in it

### You should say:

* Who the person is
* Where you can see it
* What the advertisement is about
* And explain how you feel about the advertisement

### Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about an advertisement featuring Cristiano Ronaldo.

I have seen advertisements featuring him on social media and on television. He has appeared in advertisements for several international brands, particularly sports and lifestyle products.

The advertisement I remember most clearly was related to sportswear. In the advertisement, Ronaldo was wearing sports clothes and promoting the product by showing how it could be used during exercise and training. The advertisement was short, energetic and visually attractive.

I think the main reason the company chose him was his worldwide popularity and his connection with sports. Millions of people recognise him, so his appearance can immediately attract attention to a product.

Personally, I found the advertisement quite interesting because it was energetic and professionally produced. However, I don't think a celebrity's presence automatically means that a product is good. I would still check the quality and price before buying it.

Overall, I think it was an effective advertisement because it was memorable and connected the celebrity's image with the product.

# 6. Describe a challenging technological problem you faced

### You should say:

* What the problem was
* When and where you faced it
* How challenging it was
* And explain how you solved it

### Sample Answer

I'd like to describe a challenging technological problem that I faced while working on a software project.

It happened when I was working as a software engineer. I was developing a feature for a web application, and suddenly the system became very slow when a large number of users accessed it at the same time.

The problem was quite challenging because the application worked perfectly when there were only a few users. However, when the number of users increased, some requests took several seconds to complete. At first, I couldn't identify the exact reason.

I started investigating the problem step by step. I checked the server logs, database queries and application performance. Eventually, I discovered that some database queries were not properly optimised. I improved the queries and added suitable database indexes. I also reduced some unnecessary requests to the server.

After making these changes, I tested the system again with a larger amount of data. The response time improved significantly.

Although the problem was stressful, it was a valuable experience for me because it taught me how important performance testing and systematic debugging are in software development.

# Part 3 — Electronic Devices

## 1. Why are people keen on buying new electronic devices?

There are several reasons for this. First of all, technology develops very quickly, so new devices often have better features and improved performance. Secondly, people sometimes replace their old devices because they become slow or stop receiving software updates. Finally, advertising also encourages people to buy the latest products.

---

## 2. What impact do electronic devices have on people?

Electronic devices have both positive and negative effects. On the positive side, they make communication easier and allow people to access information quickly. They are also useful for education and work. However, excessive use can reduce physical activity and may cause people to spend too much time looking at screens.

---

# Part 3 — Advertisements

## 3. What are the advantages and disadvantages of advertisements?

Advertisements are useful because they inform consumers about new products and services. They also help companies attract customers and compete in the market. However, some advertisements can be misleading or encourage people to buy things they do not really need. Therefore, consumers should think carefully before making a purchase.

---

## 4. Why are many advertisements endorsed by celebrities? How useful are they?

Companies often use celebrities because they already have large numbers of followers and are easily recognised. When a famous person promotes a product, people may become more interested in it. However, I don't think celebrity endorsement is always effective because some consumers may focus more on the product's quality and price than on the celebrity.

---

## 5. What is the most important factor in an advertisement?

I think the most important factor is the ability to attract people's attention while clearly communicating the main message. An advertisement can be visually attractive, but if people do not understand what product is being promoted, it is not very useful. Therefore, clarity and memorability are both important.

---

## 6. Why are some advertisements boring?

Some advertisements are boring because they use the same ideas repeatedly. For example, if an advertisement simply describes a product without an interesting story or visual element, people may lose interest quickly. Another reason is that some advertisements are too long and contain unnecessary information.

---

## 7. Is advertising important for a company? Why?

Yes, I think it is important, particularly for new companies. Advertising helps businesses introduce their products to potential customers and build awareness of their brand. Without effective promotion, even a good product may remain unknown to many people. However, advertising should be combined with good product quality.

---

## 8. Which is more effective, online advertising or offline advertising?

I think the effectiveness depends on the target audience and the type of product. Online advertising can reach a very specific group of people and is usually easier to measure. Offline advertising, such as billboards and television commercials, can reach a broader audience. For many modern businesses, however, online advertising is particularly useful because people spend a lot of time on the internet.

---

# Part 3 — Technology & AI

## 9. What are the advantages and disadvantages of AI?

AI has many advantages. It can automate repetitive tasks, analyse large amounts of information and help people work more efficiently. It can also support education, healthcare and many other areas.

However, there are some disadvantages as well. People may become overly dependent on AI, and some jobs could change or disappear because of automation. There are also concerns about privacy, misinformation and the misuse of AI.

---

## 10. Do you think people today should learn about AI technology?

Yes, definitely. AI is becoming an important part of modern life, so people should at least understand its basic functions and limitations. They do not necessarily need to become AI experts, but they should know how to use these tools responsibly and identify inaccurate information.

---

## 11. Should children learn to use AI?

Yes, but they should learn how to use it responsibly rather than simply depending on it. For example, children could use AI to understand difficult concepts, generate ideas or practise certain skills. At the same time, teachers and parents should teach them to think independently and verify the information provided by AI.

---

## 12. How can AI help in our lives?

AI can help us in many practical ways. It can translate languages, summarise information, help people learn new skills and automate repetitive tasks. At work, it can also assist with writing, programming, data analysis and customer service. As a result, people can save time and focus on more important tasks.

---

## 13. Do you think students are overly reliant on AI?

Some students probably are. Instead of trying to understand a topic themselves, they may ask AI to complete their assignments. This can save time in the short term, but it may prevent them from developing important skills such as critical thinking and problem-solving.

---

## 14. What can teachers do to stop students from relying too much on AI?

Teachers should encourage students to use AI as a learning tool rather than as a replacement for their own thinking. They can ask students to explain their answers, show their working process, or discuss their ideas in class. Teachers can also design assignments that require personal opinions, practical work and original thinking.

---

# Useful Band 6.5 Speaking Structure

For most **Part 1** questions, try to use:

> **Answer → Reason → Small example**

Example:

> **Yes, I do.** I prefer comfortable clothes **because** I feel more relaxed in them. **For example,** I usually wear T-shirts and jeans when I go outside casually.

For **Part 3**, use:

> **Direct answer → Explanation → Example → Result/contrast**

Example:

> **Yes, I think AI is useful.** It can save people a lot of time by doing repetitive tasks. **For example,** students can use it to understand difficult concepts. **However,** they should not depend on it completely because they still need to develop their own skills.

For **Part 2**, use this simple sequence:

> **Introduction → Background → Main events/details → Problem/action → Result → Feeling**

### Important Band 6.5 rule

You do **not** need extremely difficult vocabulary to achieve 6.5. Focus on:

* accurate **subject–verb agreement**
* correct **past/present tense**
* correct **articles** (`a`, `an`, `the`)
* correct **prepositions**
* clear **singular/plural nouns**
* linking ideas naturally with `because`, `although`, `while`, `so`, `however`, `for example`
* avoiding excessive fillers such as **“uh”, “mmm”, “I mean”**
* speaking for **1–2 minutes without stopping** in Part 2
* giving a **reason or example** instead of giving one-sentence answers
