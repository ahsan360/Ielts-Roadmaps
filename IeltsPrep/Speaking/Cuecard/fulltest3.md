# 24. Sports Team

### 1. Have you ever been part of a sports team?

Yes, I have. When I was at school, I was part of a football team. We used to play against other teams from nearby schools. I was not the best player, but I really enjoyed playing with my friends.

### 2. Are team sports popular in your culture?

Yes, definitely. Team sports, especially football and cricket, are extremely popular in Bangladesh. Many people watch national and international matches with their friends and family. Children also often play these sports in schools and local playgrounds.

### 3. Do you like watching team games? Why?

Yes, I do. I find team games more exciting because there is usually a lot of teamwork and competition. I particularly enjoy watching football because the result can change within a few minutes.

### 4. What are the differences between team sports and individual sports?

The main difference is that team sports require people to cooperate with each other, whereas individual sports mainly depend on one person. For example, football requires good communication among players. In contrast, a runner is mostly responsible for their own performance.

# 25. Typing

### 1. Do you prefer typing or handwriting?

I definitely prefer typing because it is much faster and more convenient for me. Since I work with computers, I type almost everything, including emails, documents and messages. However, I still use handwriting when I need to make quick notes.

### 2. Do you type on a desktop or laptop keyboard every day?

Yes, I do. I use a keyboard almost every day because I work as a software engineer. I spend several hours writing code, sending emails and working on different projects.

### 3. When did you learn how to type on a keyboard?

I started learning how to type when I was a teenager. At first, I used to look at the keyboard while typing, but gradually I became more familiar with the keys. Now I can type without looking at the keyboard most of the time.

### 4. How did you improve your typing?

I improved mainly through regular practice. Since I use a computer every day, I naturally became faster over time. I also used some online typing websites to practise my speed and accuracy.

# 26. Food

### 1. What is your favourite food?

My favourite food is probably biryani. I particularly like chicken or beef biryani because it is rich in flavour and has a wonderful smell. I usually eat it when I have a special occasion or when I go to a good restaurant.

### 2. What kind of food did you like when you were young?

When I was young, I really enjoyed simple homemade food. I particularly liked rice with chicken, fish and different kinds of vegetables. I was also very fond of sweets and snacks.

### 3. Has your favourite food changed since you were a child?

Yes, it has changed to some extent. When I was a child, I preferred fast food and sweets, but now I prefer traditional food and healthier meals. I have become more conscious of what I eat.

### 4. Do you eat different foods at different times of the year?

Yes, sometimes I do. During summer, I prefer lighter meals and seasonal fruits because they are refreshing. In winter, I enjoy warm and spicy food. During festivals, we also prepare some traditional dishes.

# 27. Hobbies

### 1. Do you have any hobbies?

Yes, I have several hobbies. I enjoy travelling, exploring new places and learning about technology. I also like watching educational videos online when I have free time.

### 2. Did you have any hobbies when you were a child?

Yes. When I was a child, I enjoyed playing football and cricket with my friends. I also liked watching cartoons and spending time outdoors. At that time, I had much more free time than I do now.

### 3. Do you have a hobby that you've had since childhood?

Yes, travelling is probably one of them. I have always enjoyed visiting new places and seeing different environments. Even now, whenever I get enough free time, I try to travel with my friends or family.

### 4. Do you have the same hobbies as your family members?

Not exactly. We have a few hobbies in common, such as travelling and watching movies, but our interests are not completely the same. For example, I am more interested in technology, whereas some of my family members prefer gardening or watching television.

### 1. What gift have you received recently?

### 2. Have you ever sent handmade gifts to others?

Yes, I have, although not very often. When I was younger, I sometimes made simple handmade cards for my friends and family members. I think handmade gifts are special because they require time and personal effort.

### 3. Have you ever received a great gift?

Yes, I have. One memorable gift was a birthday cake that a close friend gave me. I was expecting a normal cake, but my friend arranged a flavour that I particularly liked, so it was a pleasant surprise.

### 4. What do you consider when choosing a gift?

# 29. Day Off

### 1. When was the last time you had a few days off?

I had a few days off recently when I finished some important work. I used the time to relax, spend time with my family and catch up with some friends. I also went out for a short trip.

### 2. What do you usually do when you have days off?

I usually try to balance relaxation and entertainment. I spend some time with my family, watch movies or videos, and sometimes go somewhere nearby. If I have enough time, I also like travelling to a new place.

### 4. What would you like to do if you had a day off tomorrow?

# 30. Keys

### 1. Do you always bring a lot of keys with you?

No, I don't. I normally carry only a few keys, such as my house key and sometimes a key for my office or workplace. I prefer not to carry unnecessary things in my pocket.

### 2. Have you ever lost your keys?

Yes, I have. Once, I couldn't find my house key after coming back from outside. I was quite worried, but eventually I found it in one of my bags.

### 3. Do you often forget your keys and lock yourself out?

No, fortunately, I don't. I usually check my pockets before leaving home. However, I have occasionally forgotten my keys inside the house.

### 4. Do you think it's a good idea to leave your keys with a neighbour?

Yes, I think it can be useful, especially if the neighbour is trustworthy. For example, if someone loses their keys or has an emergency, the neighbour can help them get into their house.

# 31. Morning Time

### 1. Do you like getting up early in the morning?

Yes, I do. I feel that mornings are usually quiet and peaceful, so I can concentrate better. I also feel more productive when I start my day early.

### 2. What do you usually do in the morning?

I usually get up, wash my face and have breakfast. Then I check my schedule and start working or studying. I also like having a cup of coffee because it helps me feel more energetic.

### 3. What did you do in the morning when you were little? Why?

When I was little, I usually woke up early and got ready for school. After having breakfast, I went to school with my friends. I enjoyed those mornings because I could meet my friends and spend time with them.

### 4. Are there any differences between your mornings now and in the past?

Yes, there are quite a few differences. When I was a child, my mornings were mainly focused on school and playing with friends. Nowadays, I have more responsibilities, so I usually spend my mornings working, studying or planning my day.

### 5. Do you spend your mornings doing the same things on weekends and weekdays? Why?

No, not really. On weekdays, I usually follow a fixed routine because I have work or other responsibilities. At weekends, I have more free time, so I can wake up later and spend the morning more casually.

* What it is
* What it is about
* How often you watch it

The program explained several practical uses of AI, such as writing, programming, education and business. What I found particularly interesting was the way the presenter explained complicated ideas using simple examples. I also learned about some possible advantages and disadvantages of using AI.

I don't watch this particular program every day, but I usually watch similar videos a few times a week. Since I work in the technology field, I like keeping myself updated with new developments.

I really enjoyed watching it because it was both informative and entertaining. It also gave me some ideas about how I could use new technologies in my own work. Overall, I think online programs like this are a useful way to learn something new without spending a lot of time.

**Describe a place you have travelled to that you would like to recommend to others.**

You should say:

* What it is
* Where it is
* What you saw and did there

I would like to talk about Gangtok, a beautiful city in the Indian state of Sikkim, which I visited with some of my close friends.

I travelled there about a year ago, and it was my first international trip. We spent quite a lot of time planning the journey because we needed to arrange our transport and other things in advance.

During the trip, I saw beautiful mountains, clean roads and amazing natural scenery. We also visited some interesting places around Gangtok and travelled to Lachung. One of the things I enjoyed most was simply looking at the mountains and enjoying the peaceful environment. We also tried some local food, especially momos, which I really liked.

I would definitely recommend this place to other people because it is very different from a busy city. It is a good destination for people who enjoy nature, mountains and peaceful surroundings. I also think it is a good place for taking photographs and spending quality time with friends.

Overall, the trip was a memorable experience for me, and I would love to visit Sikkim again in the future.

# Topic 9: Describe a person who is good at learning and speaking new languages

### Cue Card

**Describe a person who is good at learning and speaking new languages.**

You should say:

* How you got to know him/her
* How he/she learns a new language
* What languages he/she can speak
* And explain how you feel about him/her

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about my uncle, whom I have known since I was a child. He is a very supportive person, and one thing I admire about him is his ability to learn languages.

He learns new languages mainly through regular practice. He watches videos, reads simple books and tries to speak with people who already know the language. He also writes down new words and uses them in everyday conversations. I think his consistency is the main reason he can learn languages quite quickly.

He can speak Bengali and English fluently, and he has also been learning German. He is particularly interested in improving his German because he wants to communicate more comfortably with German-speaking people.

I really admire him because he is not afraid of making mistakes when learning a new language. Instead, he keeps practising until he becomes more confident. His attitude has also encouraged me to take language learning more seriously.

Overall, I think learning a new language requires patience, regular practice and confidence, and he is a good example of these qualities.

# Topic 7: TV Shows / Online Programs

### 1. What are the differences between the TV programs young people like to watch and those old people like to watch?

There are some clear differences. Young people often prefer entertainment, comedy, gaming and online content because these programs are usually fast-paced and interactive. Older people may prefer news, documentaries, dramas or traditional television programs. However, these are general trends, and people's preferences can vary considerably.

### 2. What makes a popular TV or online program?

I think several factors are important. First of all, the content needs to be interesting and relevant to the audience. Secondly, good presenters and a clear storyline can make a program more enjoyable. Finally, social media can help a program become popular because people can easily share clips and discuss them online.

### 3. What kinds of TV or online programs are popular in your country?

In Bangladesh, dramas, sports, news and entertainment programs are quite popular. Cricket matches attract a particularly large audience. Nowadays, YouTube and other online platforms are also becoming increasingly popular, especially among younger people.

# Topic 8: Travel & Destinations

### 1. Where do people in your country often go for holidays?

People in Bangladesh often visit places such as Cox's Bazar, Sylhet, Sajek and Bandarban. Many people prefer destinations with beaches, mountains, rivers or natural scenery. During public holidays, popular tourist destinations can become quite crowded.

### 2. What is the ideal length for a holiday?

I think around five to seven days is a good length for a holiday. It is long enough to visit several places without feeling rushed. However, the ideal length depends on the destination, budget and the person's schedule.

### 3. How do people usually plan holidays?

Most people start by deciding where they want to go and how much money they can spend. Then they arrange transportation and accommodation and make a rough itinerary. Nowadays, people also use online reviews, maps and travel videos to make better decisions.

### 4. Is it important to plan a holiday?

Yes, I think some planning is important. It can help people manage their budget and avoid unnecessary problems with transport or accommodation. However, I don't think every minute needs to be planned because having some free time can make the trip more enjoyable.

# Topic 9: Language Learning

### 1. Are there many people who can speak foreign languages in your country?

Yes, quite a lot of people can speak at least some English because it is widely taught in schools and universities. However, the level of fluency varies considerably. People who work in international companies or travel frequently may have more opportunities to practise foreign languages.

### 2. Does speaking other languages help at work?

Yes, definitely. Knowing another language can make communication easier with international clients and colleagues. It can also create more career opportunities, particularly in areas such as technology, tourism, education and international business.

### 3. Do people learn any languages other than English?

Yes, some people learn languages such as German, French, Arabic, Chinese and Japanese. Their reasons are different. Some learn them for education or employment, while others learn them because they are interested in another culture.

### 4. Why is it easier for children to learn new things than for adults?

Children often have more time to learn and are generally less afraid of making mistakes. They can also learn naturally through games, conversations and daily activities. Adults, on the other hand, often have jobs and family responsibilities, so they may have less time to practise.

### 5. How do people learn new things?

People learn in many different ways. They can attend classes, read books, watch educational videos or use online courses. However, I think regular practice is one of the most important factors because simply studying something without using it may not be enough to remember it.
