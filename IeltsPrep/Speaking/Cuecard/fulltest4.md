## Part 1: Short Answer Questions

# 36. Rules

### 1. Are there any rules for students at your school?

Yes, there are several rules for students. For example, they have to attend classes regularly, submit their assignments on time, and behave respectfully towards teachers and other students. They are also not allowed to use their phones during lessons.

### 2. Do you think students would benefit more from more rules?

I don't think students necessarily need more rules. There should be enough rules to maintain discipline, but too many rules might make students feel restricted. I think a few clear and reasonable rules are more effective.

### 3. Have you ever had a really dedicated teacher?

Yes, I have. When I was at school, I had a teacher who was very dedicated to his students. He always explained difficult topics patiently and stayed after class to help students who were struggling.

### 4. Do you prefer to have more or fewer rules at school?

I prefer to have fewer but clearer rules. If there are too many rules, students may find them difficult to remember. In my opinion, schools should focus on rules that are genuinely necessary for discipline and safety.

# 37. Doing Something Well

### 1. Do you have an experience when you did something well?

Yes. I remember completing a difficult programming project when I was at university. I spent several days working on it and eventually managed to solve the main problems. I felt really satisfied because I had put a lot of effort into it.

### 2. Do you have an experience when your teacher thought you did a good job?

Yes. During university, one of my teachers praised me for a programming assignment. He said that my solution was well organised and that I had explained my ideas clearly. His comments gave me more confidence in my abilities.

### 3. Do you often tell your friends when they do something well?

Yes, I usually do. If one of my friends achieves something or does a good job, I try to appreciate their effort. I think a few positive words can motivate people and make them feel more confident.

# 38. Public Places

### 1. Have you ever talked with someone you don't know in public places?

Yes, a few times. For example, I have occasionally talked to strangers while waiting for a bus or sitting in a waiting area. Usually, the conversation starts with something simple, such as asking for directions or discussing the weather.

### 2. Do you wear headphones in public places?

Yes, quite often. I usually wear headphones when I am travelling or waiting somewhere because I like listening to music or podcasts. However, I take them off when I need to communicate with someone.

### 3. Would you like to see more public places near where you live?

Definitely. I would like to have more parks, walking areas and public libraries near my home. These places would give people somewhere comfortable to relax, exercise or spend time with their families.

### 4. Do you often go to public places with your friends?

Yes, I do. I sometimes go to restaurants, parks or shopping areas with my friends. We usually meet at weekends because we are busy with work during the week.

# 39. Staying with Old People

### 1. Have you ever worked with old people?

Yes, I have had some experience working with older people. In professional and family situations, I have communicated with people who were much older than me. I think these experiences taught me to be more patient and respectful.

### 2. Are you happy to work with people who are older than you?

Yes, absolutely. I don't think age is a major issue in the workplace. In fact, older colleagues often have more experience, so I can learn a lot from them.

### 3. Do you enjoy spending time with old people?

Yes, I do. I enjoy listening to their stories because they have experienced many different things in life. Sometimes their advice is also useful because it is based on their personal experience.

### 4. What are the benefits of being friends with or working with old people?

One major benefit is that we can learn from their experience. They may have faced situations that younger people have never experienced. They can also offer practical advice and help younger people avoid certain mistakes.

# 40. Growing Vegetables/Fruits

### 1. Are you interested in growing vegetables and fruits?

Yes, to some extent. I am interested in growing vegetables, although I haven't had much practical experience. I think it would be enjoyable to grow fresh vegetables at home and eat something that I had produced myself.

### 2. Is growing vegetables popular in your country?

Yes, especially in rural areas. Many families have small gardens where they grow vegetables for their own consumption. It is less common in large cities because people often have limited space.

### 3. Do many people grow vegetables in your city?

Not really. Some people grow vegetables in their gardens or on rooftops, but most people don't have enough space. Many city residents simply buy vegetables from local markets.

### 4. Do you think it's easy to grow vegetables?

I think it depends on the type of vegetable. Some vegetables are relatively easy to grow, but others require proper soil, regular watering and suitable weather conditions. So, beginners may need some basic knowledge before starting.

### 5. Should schools teach students how to grow vegetables?

Yes, I think it would be a useful practical skill. Students could learn where food comes from and understand the importance of healthy eating. It could also teach them responsibility because plants need regular care.

# 41. Going Out

### 1. Do you bring food or snacks with you when going out?

Sometimes I do, especially when I know that I will be outside for several hours. I might take some biscuits, fruit or a bottle of water. However, if I am going somewhere for a short time, I usually don't take any food.

### 2. Do you always take your mobile phone with you when going out?

Yes, almost always. My phone is important because I use it for communication, navigation and online payments. I also sometimes use it to check emails or messages when I am outside.

### 3. Do you often bring cash with you?

Not very often. Nowadays, I usually use mobile banking or cards for payments. However, I still carry a small amount of cash because some small shops or local businesses may not accept digital payments.

### 4. How often do you use cash?

I use cash occasionally. I mainly use digital payment methods because they are convenient and quick. I usually use cash when I visit a small shop or a place where digital payment is unavailable.

# 42. Quiet/Noisy Places

### 1. Do you like quiet or noisy places?

I definitely prefer quiet places. I can concentrate better when there is less noise, especially when I am studying or working. I don't mind noise when I am socialising, but I prefer a peaceful environment for important tasks.

### 2. Do you like to go to quiet or noisy places on weekends when you are free?

It depends on my mood. If I have had a busy week, I prefer going somewhere quiet so that I can relax. However, if I want to socialise with my friends, I don't mind visiting a busy or noisy place.

# 43. Advertisement

### 1. What kind of advertising do you like?

I prefer advertisements that are short, informative and creative. I particularly like advertisements that clearly explain the benefits of a product instead of simply trying to attract attention. Useful advertisements can actually help me make purchasing decisions.

### 2. Do you like advertisements?

It depends on the advertisement. I don't really like advertisements that interrupt videos repeatedly, but I don't mind useful or entertaining ones. Sometimes a well-designed advertisement can actually be interesting.

### 3. Do you see a lot of advertising on trains or other transport?

Yes, there are quite a lot of advertisements on public transport in many places. They can be displayed on walls, screens and sometimes inside vehicles. Companies use these spaces because many people see them every day.

### 4. Is there an advertisement that made an impression on you when you were a child?

Yes. I remember seeing advertisements for popular snacks and soft drinks when I was a child. They were colourful and often had catchy music, so they were easy to remember. At that age, I found them very entertaining.

# 44. Crowded Place

### 1. Is the city where you live crowded?

Yes, some areas are quite crowded, especially during working hours. Roads, markets and shopping areas can become very busy. However, there are also quieter residential areas where people can enjoy a more peaceful environment.

### 2. Is there a crowded place near where you live?

Yes, there is a busy local market near my home. It becomes particularly crowded in the evening because many people go there after work to buy food and other daily necessities.

### 3. Do you like crowded places?

Not usually. I prefer places where I have enough space to move around comfortably. However, I don't mind crowded places when I am attending an interesting event with my friends.

### 4. Do most people like crowded places?

I think it depends on the person and the situation. Some people enjoy crowded places because they are lively and social. Others prefer quiet places because they feel more comfortable and relaxed there.

### 5. When was the last time you were in a crowded place?

The last time was probably when I visited a busy shopping area. It was in the evening, and many people were there because they had finished work. It was quite difficult to move around because the place was extremely busy.

# 45. Chatting

### 1. Do you like chatting with friends?

Yes, definitely. I enjoy chatting with my friends because it helps me relax after a busy day. We usually talk about our work, future plans, technology and sometimes funny things that have happened recently.

### 2. What do you usually chat about with friends?

It depends on the friend. With some friends, I discuss work and technology, while with others I talk about travelling, family or everyday life. We also sometimes discuss our future plans and career goals.

### 3. Do you prefer to chat with a group of people or with only one friend?

I generally prefer chatting with one or two close friends. It is easier to have a meaningful conversation in a small group. In a large group, several people may speak at the same time, so it can be difficult to follow the conversation.

### 4. Do you prefer to communicate face-to-face or via social media?

I prefer face-to-face communication when possible. It feels more natural and allows me to understand people's reactions better. However, social media is more convenient when my friends live far away.

### 5. Do you argue with friends?

Occasionally, yes. It is normal for close friends to have different opinions. However, I usually try to discuss the issue calmly rather than allowing a small disagreement to damage the friendship.

# 11. Describe a time when you were stuck in a traffic jam for a very long time

### Sample Answer

I remember being stuck in a traffic jam for several hours when I was travelling with some friends. It happened when we were returning home after visiting a popular tourist destination.

We were travelling by car, and at first everything was normal. However, after reaching a busy area, the traffic suddenly became extremely slow. There were hundreds of cars and buses on the road, and eventually our car barely moved.

While we were waiting, I tried to make the situation less boring. I listened to music, checked some messages on my phone and talked with my friends. We also discussed our trip and made jokes to keep ourselves entertained.

At first, I was quite relaxed because I thought the traffic would clear within a short time. However, after more than an hour, I became frustrated and tired. The weather was also quite warm, which made the situation more uncomfortable.

Eventually, the traffic started moving again, and we continued our journey. Although it was an unpleasant experience, I still remember it because we spent a lot of time talking and laughing together while we were stuck.

# 12. Describe a special day out that cost you little money/didn't cost you much

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about a day when I went out with a few close friends. It was a simple trip, but I remember it because we had a great time without spending much money.

It happened last year when we decided to visit a natural area near my hometown. We travelled there by local transport and spent most of the day walking around, taking photographs and talking to each other.

We did not spend much money. Most of our expenses were for transportation and some snacks and drinks. I think each person spent only a small amount, so it was quite affordable.

What made the day special was the company rather than the place itself. We were all busy with our studies and work, so we rarely had enough time to spend together. During that trip, we could relax, enjoy the natural surroundings and talk about our future plans.

I still feel happy when I remember that day. It taught me that having a memorable experience does not necessarily require a lot of money. Sometimes spending time with good friends is enough to make a day special.

# Part 3: Discussion — Long-Term Goals

## 1. What goals should a society have?

I think a society should have several important goals. For example, it should aim to provide good education, healthcare and employment opportunities for its citizens. It should also focus on protecting the environment and maintaining public safety. These goals can help improve people's quality of life.

## 2. Do people need to have goals?

Yes, I believe goals are important because they give people a sense of direction. Without a clear goal, people may find it difficult to decide what they should focus on. However, goals do not always have to be ambitious; even small personal goals can motivate people.

## 3. What goals do people at your age have?

People around my age usually have career-related goals. Many want to find a stable job, increase their income or develop professional skills. Some people also want to travel, buy a house or start a family. Of course, their priorities depend on their personal circumstances.

## 4. Is it necessary to give advice to children?

Yes, I think children need guidance because they do not have enough life experience to make every decision independently. Parents and teachers can help them understand the consequences of their choices. However, children should also be given some freedom to learn from their own experiences.

## 5. What goals do young people usually have?

Young people often have goals related to education and careers. For example, they may want to attend a good university, develop professional skills or get a well-paid job. Some also have personal goals, such as travelling, becoming financially independent or maintaining a healthy lifestyle.

## 6. What should people do to achieve their goals?

First, they should set a realistic and specific goal. Then, they need to create a practical plan and work on it consistently. It is also important to review their progress and make changes when necessary. Most importantly, they should not give up when they face difficulties.

# Part 3: Traffic Jam / Transportation

## 1. Do you like to use public transport?

Yes, I do, especially when I am travelling in a busy city. Public transport can be cheaper than using a private car, and people don't have to worry about parking. However, it can sometimes be uncomfortable when buses or trains are overcrowded.

## 2. Would you rather be in a car or a bus in a traffic jam?

I would probably prefer to be in a car. A private car gives me more privacy and I can listen to music or adjust the air conditioning. However, if the traffic jam is extremely long, public transport may be more practical because I don't have to concentrate on driving.

## 3. How can we solve the traffic jam problem?

There are several possible solutions. Governments can improve public transport and encourage people to use buses and trains instead of private cars. They can also improve traffic management and construct roads in areas where they are genuinely necessary. In addition, better urban planning could reduce unnecessary travel.

## 4. Do you think developing public transport can solve traffic jam problems?

It can certainly help reduce the problem, although it may not solve it completely. If public transport is affordable, reliable and comfortable, more people may choose it instead of private cars. As a result, the number of vehicles on the roads could decrease.

## 5. Do you think the highways will help reduce traffic jams?

Highways can help in some situations because they allow vehicles to travel more efficiently between different areas. However, simply constructing more roads may not be enough. If the number of vehicles continues to increase, the new roads may eventually become crowded as well.

## 6. What are good ways to manage traffic?

One effective method is to use modern traffic-management systems, such as traffic signals and cameras. Authorities can also create dedicated lanes for buses and improve public transport. In busy areas, better parking management and stricter enforcement of traffic rules could also help.

# Part 3: Leisure & Free Time

## 1. Do people like to spend their leisure time out in your country?

Yes, many people enjoy going out during their free time. They may visit restaurants, parks, shopping centres or tourist attractions. However, some people prefer staying at home because it is more comfortable and less expensive.

## 2. How do people spend their leisure time in your country?

People spend their free time in different ways. Young people often meet friends, watch films, play sports or use social media. Families may visit relatives or travel to nearby places. Many people also enjoy watching television or using their smartphones at home.

## 3. How does technology affect the way people spend their leisure time?

Technology has changed leisure activities considerably. People can now watch films, play games, listen to music and communicate with friends without leaving home. Although this is convenient, spending too much time on devices can reduce physical activity and face-to-face communication.

## 4. Do you think only old people have time for leisure?

No, I don't think so. People of all ages need some leisure time, although the amount of free time varies. Young people may be busy with education or work, while older people may have fewer professional responsibilities. However, everyone needs time to relax.

## 5. Why do people like to have days off?

People need days off because regular work or study can be mentally and physically tiring. A day off allows them to relax, spend time with their families and do activities they enjoy. It can also help them return to work with more energy and concentration.

## 6. Going out to have holidays is tiring. Why do people still want to do it?

Although travelling can be tiring, people enjoy the experiences they get from it. Holidays allow them to see new places, try different foods and learn about other cultures. They can also escape their normal routine and create memorable experiences with family or friends. So, for many people, the benefits are worth the effort.
