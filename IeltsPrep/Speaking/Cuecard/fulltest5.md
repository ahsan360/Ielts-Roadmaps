# IELTS Speaking Practice — Band 6.5

## How to Use These Answers

These answers deliberately use **similar structures** so they are easier to remember and adapt during the IELTS Speaking test.

### Useful structures

* **Yes, definitely.**
* **I usually...**
* **Whenever I have free time, I...**
* **The main reason is that...**
* **I think... because...**
* **It depends on...**
* **I have been... for several years.**
* **I particularly like... because...**
* **However, I would say that...**
* **Overall, I think...**

# PART 1 — INTRODUCTION & INTERVIEW

# 46. Friends

### 1. Do you invite friends to your home?

Yes, I do. I sometimes invite my close friends to my home, especially when we have free time. We usually have some snacks, talk about different things and spend a few hours together.

### 2. Do you prefer to spend time with one friend or with a group of friends?

I usually prefer spending time with a small group of friends because it is more enjoyable. We can talk about different topics and share our experiences with each other.

### 3. Do you often go out with your friends?

Yes, I do. Whenever we have enough free time, we usually go out together. We might visit a restaurant, have coffee or simply walk around the city.

### 4. Where do you often meet each other?

We usually meet at a restaurant or a coffee shop near our homes. I prefer these places because they are comfortable and we can talk without too much disturbance.

### 5. Do you have a friend you have known for a long time?

Yes, I do. I have a close friend whom I have known since childhood. We have been friends for many years, so we understand each other quite well.

### 6. What do you usually do with your friends?

We usually talk about our personal lives, watch movies or go somewhere to eat. Sometimes, we also discuss our future plans and give advice to each other.

### 7. How important are friends to you?

Friends are quite important to me because they provide emotional support and make my life more enjoyable. Whenever I face a problem, I can usually discuss it with my close friends.

### 8. Why are some places suitable for a meeting while others are not?

I think a suitable meeting place should be comfortable and relatively quiet. If a place is too crowded or noisy, it becomes difficult to have a proper conversation.

### 9. Is there a difference between where you meet friends now and where you used to meet them in the past?

Yes, definitely. When I was younger, I usually met my friends at school or in our neighbourhood. Nowadays, we normally meet at restaurants, coffee shops or other public places.

## Cue Card 13 — A Home You Like to Visit

### Question

**Describe a home that you like to visit but do not want to live in.**

You should say:

* Where it is
* What it is like
* Why you like to visit it
* And explain why you would not like to live there

### Sample Answer

Well, I would like to talk about a house that belongs to one of my close relatives. It is located in a quiet area not very far from my home.

I have known this house for several years, and it is quite beautiful and comfortable. It has several bedrooms, a large living room and a nice outdoor area. There are also many trees around the house, so the environment is peaceful and relaxing.

I like visiting this house because I can spend quality time with my relatives there. Whenever I visit them, we usually have some food, talk about different things and sometimes watch television together. I particularly like the outdoor area because it is quiet and has a pleasant atmosphere.

However, I would not like to live there permanently. The main reason is that it is quite far from the places where I usually work and study. It would take me a considerable amount of time to travel every day. In addition, I prefer living in a place where shops, restaurants and other facilities are nearby.

Overall, I really enjoy visiting this house because it is peaceful and comfortable, but I do not think it would be suitable for my everyday life.

## Cue Card 14 — Learning Another Language

### Question

**Describe a thing you did to learn another language.**

You should say:

* What language you learned
* What you did
* How it helped you learn the language
* And how you felt about it

### Sample Answer

Well, I would like to talk about something I did to learn German. I started learning German because I was interested in studying and working in a German-speaking environment.

At first, I found the language quite difficult, especially the grammar and pronunciation. So, I decided to create a simple daily routine. I spent about one hour every day learning new words, practising grammar and listening to German conversations.

I also used online videos and language-learning applications. Whenever I learned a new word, I tried to make a simple sentence with it. I also listened to German conversations because I wanted to become familiar with the pronunciation.

This method helped me because regular practice made the language more familiar to me. Instead of studying for many hours once a week, I tried to practise a little every day. As a result, I gradually became more confident.

I felt quite motivated because I could see my progress. Of course, I still make mistakes, but I believe that consistent practice is the best way to improve a language.

Overall, learning German has been challenging but useful, and I hope to become much more fluent in the future.

## Cue Card 15 — A Happy Event You Organized

### Question

**Describe a time when you organized a happy event successfully.**

You should say:

* What the event was
* How you prepared for it
* Who helped you to organize it
* And explain why you think it was a successful event

### Sample Answer

Well, I would like to talk about a small celebration that I organized with some of my close friends. It was a birthday celebration for one of my friends, and we organized it at a restaurant.

At first, I discussed the plan with my friends and decided on a suitable date and place. Then, we contacted the restaurant, ordered some food and arranged a small birthday cake. We also prepared a simple surprise for our friend.

A few of my close friends helped me with the preparation. Some of them arranged the cake, while others contacted the guests and made sure that everything was ready before the event.

The event was successful because everything went according to our plan. Our friend was genuinely surprised, and everyone enjoyed the food and conversation. We also took many photographs together, so we had some memorable moments.

I felt really happy and satisfied because I had spent time with my friends and had successfully organized something special for someone close to me.

Overall, it was a simple event, but it was successful because everyone worked together and enjoyed themselves.

# Topic 13 — Homes

### 1. Do people in your country like to visit others' homes?

Yes, definitely. People in my country generally enjoy visiting relatives and friends because family and social relationships are quite important. Whenever people have free time, they often visit each other's homes.

### 2. What do people do when they visit others?

They usually have food, drink tea and talk about different things. Sometimes, they watch television together or discuss family and personal matters.

### 3. What kind of place do people in your country like to live in?

Most people prefer a comfortable and peaceful place to live. They usually want their home to be close to schools, shops, hospitals and public transport.

### 4. What's the difference between homes in cities and those in the countryside?

The main difference is space and location. Houses in the countryside are usually larger and have more outdoor space, while homes in cities are often smaller because land is more expensive.

# Topic 14 — Language Learning

### 1. What are the advantages and disadvantages of learning a language?

There are several advantages to learning another language. It can help people communicate with foreigners and create better educational or professional opportunities. However, learning a language can be time-consuming and sometimes frustrating.

### 2. Some people think that technology has made it unnecessary to learn languages. What do you think?

I do not completely agree with this idea. Technology can help people translate languages quickly, but it cannot always replace real communication. Learning a language also helps people understand another culture.

### 3. What difficulties do people face when learning a language?

The main difficulties are usually vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation. In addition, some people feel nervous when they have to speak with others because they are afraid of making mistakes.

### 4. Do you think language learning is important? Why?

Yes, definitely. Learning another language can improve communication skills and provide access to different cultures. It can also be useful when people travel, study or work abroad.

### 5. Which is better, to study a language alone or to study it in a group? Why?

I think both methods have advantages. Studying alone allows people to learn at their own pace, while studying in a group gives them more opportunities to practise speaking with others.

### 6. What is the best way to learn a language?

In my opinion, regular practice is the most effective way. People should learn some vocabulary, practise grammar and listen to real conversations every day. They should also try to speak without being afraid of making mistakes.

# Topic 15 — Organizing and Preparation

### 1. How would you feel when you were not well prepared for something?

I would probably feel nervous and uncomfortable. When I am not properly prepared, I usually lose confidence because I do not know what to expect.

### 2. Do you prefer to prepare and organize an activity or just take part in an activity?

I prefer to prepare and organize an activity because I like knowing what I need to do. However, sometimes it is more enjoyable to simply participate and enjoy the experience.

### 3. How can parents help children to be organized?

Parents can teach children to follow a simple routine. For example, they can encourage them to organize their books, complete their homework on time and prepare their things before going to school.

### 4. On what occasions do people need to be organized?

People need to be organized when they have important responsibilities. For example, they need to be organized before an examination, a job interview, a trip or an important event.

### 5. Does everything need to be well prepared?

No, I do not think everything needs to be perfectly prepared. Some situations are quite simple and can be handled spontaneously. However, important events usually require proper preparation.

### 6. Do people need others' help when organizing things?

Yes, sometimes they do. It depends on the size and difficulty of the task. For a large event, getting help from other people can save time and make the whole process easier.

# UNIVERSAL SPEAKING STRUCTURES

## Structure 1 — Personal Questions

**Yes, I do.**
**I usually...**
**The main reason is that...**
**Whenever I have free time, I...**

Example:

> Yes, I do. I usually go out with my friends whenever I have free time. The main reason is that it helps me relax after a busy week.

---

## Structure 2 — Preference Questions

**I usually prefer... because...**
**Of course, ... is also good, but...**
**For me, ... is more important.**

Example:

> I usually prefer comfortable shoes because I wear them for several hours a day. Of course, fashionable shoes are also attractive, but for me, comfort is more important.

---

## Structure 3 — Why Questions

**The main reason is that...**
**This is mainly because...**
**As a result,...**

Example:

> The main reason is that I can concentrate better in a quiet environment. This is mainly because there are fewer distractions. As a result, I can finish my work more quickly.

---

## Structure 4 — Past Questions

**When I was younger,...**
**I used to...**
**However, nowadays,...**

Example:

> When I was younger, I used to visit the library with my classmates. However, nowadays, I usually read books online.

---

## Structure 5 — "It Depends"

**It depends on the situation.**
**If..., I usually...**
**However, if..., I prefer...**

Example:

> It depends on the situation. If I am tired, I usually stay at home. However, if I have enough energy, I prefer going out with my friends.

---

## Structure 6 — Part 3 Opinion

**In my opinion,...**
**The main reason is that...**
**For example,...**
**As a result,...**

Example:

> In my opinion, learning another language is important. The main reason is that it improves communication skills. For example, people can communicate with foreigners more easily. As a result, they may have better opportunities to study or work abroad.

---

# PART 3 UNIVERSAL TEMPLATE

For most Part 3 questions, use:

> **Yes, definitely. / No, I don't think so.**
>
> **In my opinion,...**
>
> **The main reason is that...**
>
> **For example,...**
>
> **As a result,...**
>
> **However, it depends on the situation.**

This structure is sufficient for a **Band 6.5-style response** when your grammar is accurate, your ideas are relevant, and your pronunciation and fluency are reasonably consistent.
