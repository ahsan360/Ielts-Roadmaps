# Part 1 — Sample Answers

## 56. Chocolates

### 1. Do you like eating chocolates? Why or why not?

Yes, I do. I particularly enjoy dark chocolate because it has a rich flavour and is not excessively sweet. I usually have a small amount when I want to relax or treat myself.

### 2. How often do you eat chocolates?

I eat chocolate once or twice a week. I don't eat it every day because I try to maintain a balanced diet. However, I sometimes have it with a cup of coffee when I am studying or working.

### 3. Did you often eat chocolate when you were a kid?

Yes, I did. When I was a child, I used to eat chocolate quite often, especially when my parents bought it for me. I was particularly excited whenever I received chocolate as a gift.

### 4. Why do you think chocolate is popular around the world?

I think chocolate is popular because it tastes delicious and comes in many different varieties. Besides, it is relatively affordable and can be enjoyed on many occasions, so people of different ages tend to like it.

### 5. What's your favourite flavour of chocolate?

My favourite is dark chocolate. I like it because it has a slightly bitter taste, which I find more enjoyable than extremely sweet chocolate. I also feel that it goes well with coffee.

### 6. Do you think it is good to use chocolate as gifts to others?

Yes, definitely. Chocolate is a simple and thoughtful gift because most people enjoy it. It is particularly suitable for birthdays or special occasions, although I would consider the person's preferences before buying it.

# 57. Singing

### 1. Do you like singing? Why?

Yes, I do, although I am not a particularly talented singer. I sometimes sing when I am alone because it helps me relax and improves my mood. I usually sing along with songs that I already know.

### 2. Have you ever learnt how to sing?

No, I have never taken formal singing lessons. I have mainly learnt by listening to songs and trying to imitate the singers. Although I am not very good at it, I still enjoy singing casually.

### 3. Who do you want to sing for?

I would like to sing for my close friends and family members. I would feel more comfortable singing in front of people I know well because I would not be too worried about making mistakes.

### 4. Do you think singing can bring happiness to people?

Yes, I believe it can. Singing can help people express their emotions and reduce stress, especially when they are feeling tired or worried. In addition, singing together can create a positive atmosphere and bring people closer.

# 58. Outer Space and Stars

### 1. Have you ever learnt about outer space and stars?

Yes, I learnt a little about them at school. We studied basic topics such as planets, stars and the solar system. Although I did not study astronomy in depth, I have always found the subject fascinating.

### 2. Do you like science fiction movies? Why?

Yes, I do. I enjoy them because they allow me to imagine what the future might look like. I particularly like movies that involve space travel, advanced technology and unknown planets.

### 3. Do you want to know more about outer space?

Yes, absolutely. There are still so many unanswered questions about the universe, which makes it extremely interesting. If I had more free time, I would like to learn about planets, black holes and the possibility of life beyond Earth.

### 4. Do you want to go into outer space in the future?

Yes, I would love to, although it seems unrealistic at the moment. If space travel becomes safer and more affordable in the future, I would definitely consider it because it would be an unforgettable experience.

# 59. Lists

### 1. Do you make a list when you shop?

Yes, I usually do. Before I go shopping, I make a short list of the things I actually need. This helps me save time and prevents me from buying unnecessary items.

### 2. Do you make a list for your work? Does it work?

Yes, I often make a to-do list for my work. I usually write down the most important tasks and complete them one by one. It works quite well because it helps me organise my workload and avoid forgetting important tasks.

### 3. Why don't some people like making lists?

I think some people find making lists unnecessary or time-consuming. They may prefer to remember things mentally and deal with them spontaneously. However, when people have many responsibilities, a list can make their daily life more manageable.

### 4. Do you prefer to make a list on paper or your mobile phone?

I prefer using my mobile phone because it is more convenient. I always carry my phone with me, so I can update my list whenever I need to. Also, I can easily remove completed tasks or add new ones.

## Topic 16: Describe a time when you sent a message or an email to someone but received no reply for a long time

### Question

**Describe a time when you sent a message or an email to someone but received no reply for a long time.**

You should say:

* Who you sent it to
* What the message/email was about
* Whether you finally received the reply
* And explain how you felt about the experience

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about a time when I sent an important message to one of my close friends but did not receive a reply for several days.

It happened a few months ago when I was planning a short trip with some of my friends. I sent him a message because I wanted to confirm whether he was available and whether he wanted to join us. I expected him to reply within a few hours because we usually communicate quite frequently.

However, he did not respond for almost three days. At first, I thought he might be busy with work, so I did not worry too much. After a couple of days, though, I became slightly concerned because I needed his answer before making the final arrangements.

Eventually, he replied and explained that he had been extremely busy and had not checked his phone properly. Fortunately, he was still interested in joining the trip, so everything worked out well.

At first, I felt a little frustrated because I was waiting for his response, but later I understood that he had a genuine reason. **Although waiting for a reply was inconvenient, the experience taught me that I should not immediately assume that someone is ignoring me when they do not respond.**

# Part 3 — Topic 16

### 1. In what situations do people spend a long time responding to others' messages?

People may take a long time to respond when they are busy at work or studying. They might also be travelling, attending a meeting, or dealing with a personal problem. **Although they may want to reply, they sometimes postpone it because they have more urgent responsibilities.**

### 2. In what situations do people not respond to messages at all?

People may ignore messages when they do not know the sender or when they are not interested in continuing the conversation. Sometimes, they may also forget to reply because they receive a large number of messages every day.

### 3. What would you do if you did not receive a reply after sending out a message?

If the message were important, I would probably send a polite follow-up message after a reasonable amount of time. If there were still no response, I might call the person directly. **However, if the matter were not urgent, I would simply wait because the person might have a valid reason for not replying.**

### 4. Why do some people prefer sending a message instead of making a call?

I think messages are more convenient because people can respond whenever they have time. They are also useful when someone is busy or unable to speak. **For example, if I am working in a quiet environment, I can send a short message without disturbing anyone.**

### 5. How do you show your respect in your messages?

I try to use polite language and avoid sending messages at inappropriate times. If I am speaking to someone older or in a professional position, I use more formal expressions. I also try to reply within a reasonable period because responding on time shows consideration for other people.

### 6. Why do some people feel angry when others don't reply to their message?

Some people may feel ignored when they do not receive a reply, particularly if the message is important. They may think that the other person is deliberately avoiding them. **However, I think people should be patient because there could be many reasons why someone cannot respond immediately.**

# Part 2 — Topic 17

## Describe a city you enjoyed visiting

### Question

**Describe a city you enjoyed visiting.**

You should say:

* Where it is
* When you visited it
* How long you stayed there
* What you did there
* And explain why you enjoyed visiting it

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about **Gangtok**, a beautiful city in the Indian state of Sikkim, which I visited last year with some of my close friends.

We stayed there for around four or five days, and during that time we explored several interesting places in and around the city. We walked around the main streets, visited local attractions, tried different types of food and spent quite a lot of time enjoying the natural scenery.

One of the most memorable parts of the trip was visiting the mountainous areas around Gangtok. The weather was cool and pleasant, and the views were completely different from what I normally see in my hometown. We also visited Lachung, where we saw beautiful mountains and enjoyed the peaceful atmosphere.

**What I particularly liked about Gangtok was that it offered both natural beauty and modern facilities.** The city was developed enough to provide comfortable accommodation and restaurants, while the surrounding areas were calm and scenic.

I really enjoyed the visit because I could escape from my usual routine and spend quality time with my friends. **Although the journey was sometimes tiring, the experience was memorable because I was able to explore a new place and enjoy a different culture.**

# Part 3 — Topic 17

### 1. Before you travel to a city, what factors would you consider?

Before travelling to a city, I would consider several factors, such as the weather, transportation, accommodation and tourist attractions. I would also check the cost of living because it helps me prepare a suitable budget. **If I were travelling abroad, I would also research the local culture and safety conditions beforehand.**

### 2. What are the disadvantages of living in a very famous city?

One major disadvantage is the high cost of living. Famous cities often have expensive housing, food and transportation. They can also be overcrowded, which may result in traffic congestion and noise. **Although these cities provide many opportunities, their fast-paced lifestyle can sometimes be stressful.**

### 3. Do you prefer to visit well-developed cities or cities with a long history?

I enjoy both, but I slightly prefer cities with a long history because I can learn about their culture and traditions. **When I visit a historical city, I can explore old buildings and museums while also experiencing the local way of life.** However, well-developed cities are also attractive because they usually have better transportation and modern facilities.

### 4. For those who live in cities, is it because they want to or have to?

I think it depends on the individual. Some people choose to live in cities because there are better employment opportunities, educational institutions and healthcare facilities. Others may move there because they have no practical alternative. **For example, young professionals may have to live in a city because most of the jobs in their field are concentrated there.**

# Part 3 — Topic 18

### 1. What short-term jobs do young people do in other countries?

Young people often take temporary jobs in areas such as hospitality, retail, tourism and customer service. Some students also work as interns because they want to gain practical experience. **These jobs can be useful because young people can earn money while developing communication and professional skills.**

### 2. What challenges do young people face when working abroad?

One major challenge is language. If people cannot communicate effectively, they may find it difficult to work with colleagues or customers. They may also experience cultural differences and homesickness. **Although working abroad can be exciting, adapting to a completely different environment may take some time.**

### 3. What are the benefits of working for an international company?

There are several benefits. Employees can work with people from different cultural backgrounds and learn different approaches to business. They may also receive better training and have more opportunities for career development. **In addition, international experience can strengthen a person's CV because it demonstrates adaptability and communication skills.**

### 4. What personal skills are required to work in an international company?

I think communication and teamwork are particularly important. Employees need to communicate clearly with people who may have different cultural backgrounds. They should also be adaptable because working methods can vary between countries. **If someone is open-minded and willing to learn from others, they are more likely to adapt successfully to an international working environment.**
