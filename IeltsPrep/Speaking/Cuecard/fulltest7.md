# PART 1 — QUESTIONS + ANSWERS

# Topic 66: Names

### 1. Does your name have any special meaning?

Yes, my name has a special meaning. My parents chose it because they wanted me to have a name with a positive meaning. Although I did not understand its meaning when I was a child, I appreciate it much more now.

### 2. How would you choose names for your next generation?

I would choose a name that has a meaningful and positive background. I would probably discuss it with my family because choosing a name is an important decision. I would also make sure that the name is easy to pronounce and remember.

### 3. Does anyone in your family have the same name as you?

No, nobody in my immediate family has exactly the same name as me. However, I have some relatives whose names are quite similar. It can sometimes be confusing when several people have similar names.

### 4. Are there any differences between how Indians name their children now and in the past?

Yes, I think there are some differences. In the past, parents often preferred traditional names that were connected to their culture or religion, whereas many parents nowadays prefer modern and unique names. Since people are more exposed to international cultures, they sometimes choose names that are easy to pronounce internationally.

### 5. Are there any names that are more popular than others in India?

Yes, definitely. Some traditional and religious names are quite common, although their popularity varies from one region to another. Nowadays, some parents also prefer short and modern names because they are easier to remember.

# Topic 67: Jewelry

### 1. Do you often wear jewelry?

No, I do not often wear jewelry. I prefer to keep my appearance simple, so I normally wear a watch or sometimes a simple bracelet. However, I might wear something more formal when I attend a special occasion.

### 2. What types of jewelry do you like?

I prefer simple and minimal jewelry rather than expensive or flashy pieces. For example, I like simple rings and bracelets because they look elegant without attracting too much attention.

### 3. Do you usually buy jewelry?

No, I rarely buy jewelry because it is not something I use regularly. If I need to buy a piece, I usually choose something simple and durable rather than something very expensive.

### 4. Why do you think some people like to wear a piece of jewelry for a long time?

I think people sometimes become emotionally attached to a particular piece of jewelry. For example, if someone receives a ring or necklace from a family member, they may wear it for many years because it reminds them of that person.

# Topic 68: Geography

### 1. Do you like geography?

Yes, I quite like geography because it helps me understand different countries and places. When I was at school, I particularly enjoyed learning about mountains, rivers, and different types of landscapes.

### 2. Do you think geography is useful?

Yes, absolutely. Geography is useful because it helps people understand the world around them. For example, when we learn about climate, natural resources, and population, we can understand why people live differently in different regions.

### 3. Have you ever learned geography?

Yes, I studied geography at school. We learned about different countries, continents, rivers, mountains, and climates. Although I do not remember all the details now, the basic knowledge I gained is still useful.

### 4. Do you want to be a geography teacher?

No, I do not think I would like to become a geography teacher. Although I find the subject interesting, I would prefer to work in the technology sector because I enjoy solving problems and working with computers.

### 5. Do you want to learn more about the geography of other countries?

Yes, definitely. I enjoy travelling, so learning about the geography of other countries would help me understand the places I visit. If I know something about a country's landscape and climate before travelling there, I can prepare myself better.

# Topic 69: Flowers

### 1. Do people in your country often give flowers to others?

Yes, people in my country often give flowers on special occasions. For example, they may give flowers during birthdays, weddings, or anniversaries. I think flowers are popular because they are a simple way of showing love and respect.

### 2. Do you know anyone who loves flowers?

Yes, I know several people who love flowers. One of my relatives, for example, enjoys gardening and grows different types of flowers around his house. He spends quite a lot of time taking care of them.

### 3. Are there a lot of flowers where you live?

Yes, there are quite a lot of flowers in my area, especially during certain seasons. Although my neighbourhood is becoming more developed, there are still gardens and small green spaces where people grow flowers.

# Topic 70: Birthday

### 1. What do you usually do on your birthday?

I usually spend my birthday with my family and close friends. We normally have a small meal together, and sometimes we cut a cake. I prefer a simple celebration because I feel more comfortable when I am surrounded by people I know well.

### 2. What did you do on your birthday when you were young?

When I was young, my birthday was usually more exciting because my family organised a small celebration. I would invite some friends, eat cake, and receive presents. Since I was a child, I was particularly excited about getting gifts.

### 3. Do you think it is important for you to celebrate your birthday?

Yes, I think it is important to some extent. Although I do not need a big party, I like spending some time with my family because it gives me an opportunity to appreciate the people who are close to me.

### 4. Whose birthday do you think is the most important to celebrate in India?

I think it depends on the family. However, parents' birthdays can be particularly meaningful because children often want to show their appreciation for everything their parents have done for them.

# Topic 71: Plan/Schedule

### 1. Do you like making plans?

Yes, I like making plans because they help me organise my time. When I have several things to do, I usually make a simple schedule so that I do not forget anything important.

### 2. Do you use paper or your cellphone to make plans?

I normally use my cellphone because it is more convenient. I can create reminders and update my schedule whenever necessary, whereas using paper is less flexible.

### 3. What are the benefits of making plans?

There are several benefits. Firstly, planning helps people manage their time effectively. Secondly, it reduces stress because we know what we need to do. If I have a clear plan, I can usually concentrate better on my work.

# Topic 73: Spending Time by Yourself

### 1. Do you usually spend time by yourself?

Yes, I do, especially when I need to concentrate on something. I sometimes spend a few hours alone because I can work more efficiently when there are fewer distractions.

### 2. What did you do last time when you were by yourself?

The last time I was alone, I stayed at home and worked on some personal tasks. After finishing them, I watched a few videos and had a cup of coffee, which helped me relax.

### 3. How do you usually spend time by yourself?

I usually read something, watch videos, or work on my personal projects. If I have had a stressful day, I prefer doing something relaxing rather than studying or working.

### 4. Do you like spending time by yourself?

Yes, I do, although I also enjoy spending time with other people. When I am alone, I can think clearly and focus on my own interests without being interrupted.

# Topic 74: Writing

### 1. Do you write a lot?

Yes, I write quite regularly, particularly because I work in the technology sector. I often write emails, documentation, messages, and technical notes as part of my work.

### 2. What do you like to write? Why?

I mainly like writing notes and ideas related to my work and studies. I find writing useful because it helps me organise my thoughts, especially when I am dealing with a complicated problem.

### 3. Do you think the things you write would change in the future?

Yes, I think they will change. As technology develops, I will probably do more digital writing and less handwriting. Also, my writing style may become more professional as I gain more experience.

### 4. Do you prefer typing or handwriting when you are writing?

I definitely prefer typing because it is faster and more convenient. I can easily edit or reorganise my ideas, whereas changing something that I have written by hand can be difficult.

# Topic 19: Describe a boring place

## Part 2 — Cue Card

### Describe a boring place.

You should say:

* Where it is
* Who you went there with
* What you did there
* And explain why you think it is a boring place

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about a government office that I visited a few months ago. It is located in my city, and I went there with one of my friends because I needed to complete some official paperwork.

When we arrived there, we had to wait for quite a long time because there were many people. We sat on some old chairs and waited for our turn. While we were waiting, there was not much to do because the place did not have any interesting facilities or activities.

I found the place extremely boring because we spent almost two hours there without doing anything useful. Although I was with my friend, we could not talk much because the environment was very quiet and crowded.

Another reason I found it boring was that the process was quite slow. If the office had a better system, people might be able to complete their work much faster.

Overall, it was a boring place because I had to spend a long time waiting, and there was almost nothing interesting to do.

## Part 3

### 1. Why do most children think education is boring?

I think some children find education boring because they have to study subjects that they are not interested in. In addition, if teachers mainly use lectures and textbooks, students may lose concentration. If classes included more practical activities and games, children might become more interested in learning.

### 2. Why aren't young people willing to listen to the experiences of older people?

I think many young people believe that their generation is different from previous generations. Since technology and society are changing rapidly, they sometimes think that older people's experiences are no longer relevant. However, older people can still provide valuable advice, especially when young people face difficult situations.

### 3. What can people do when they feel bored?

There are many things people can do when they feel bored. They can exercise, read a book, meet friends, or learn a new skill. If someone has access to the internet, they can also watch educational videos or take an online course.

### 4. Why are some teachers' classes boring? Are there any solutions?

Some teachers' classes may be boring because they use the same teaching method every day. If a teacher only speaks while students listen, it can be difficult for them to remain focused. One possible solution is to include discussions, group activities, videos, and practical examples so that students can participate more actively.

# Topic 20: Describe a new law you would like to introduce in your country

## Part 2 — Cue Card

### Describe a new law you would like to introduce in your country.

You should say:

* What law it is
* What changes this law brings
* Whether this new law will be popular
* How you came up with the new law
* And explain how you feel about this new law

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about a law that would require schools to provide better road-safety education for students. I think this would be particularly useful because many children travel to school by walking, cycling, or using public transport.

Under this law, schools would have to provide regular lessons about road safety. Students would learn how to cross roads safely, understand traffic signs, and behave responsibly when they are near busy roads.

I think this law could bring several positive changes. For example, children would become more aware of traffic rules, which could help them make safer decisions. Schools could also organise practical sessions so that students could learn how to deal with real-life traffic situations.

I came up with this idea because I have noticed that many young people do not always understand basic road-safety rules. Although parents can teach these things at home, I think schools can provide more systematic education.

I believe this law could receive support from many parents because they are concerned about their children's safety. However, implementing it would require proper planning and trained teachers.

Overall, I feel that this would be a useful law because it could help young people develop safer habits from an early age.

## Part 3

### 1. What rules should students follow at school?

Students should follow rules related to attendance, discipline, respect, and safety. For example, they should arrive on time, listen to their teachers, and avoid disturbing other students. They should also take care of school property because it is shared by everyone.

### 2. Do people in your country usually obey the law?

I think it depends on the situation and the type of law. Many people follow laws in their daily lives, but some people may ignore certain rules when they believe that enforcement is weak. For this reason, consistent enforcement and public awareness are both important.

### 3. What kinds of behavior are considered good behavior?

Good behaviour usually means treating other people with respect and behaving responsibly. For example, helping others, being honest, keeping promises, and following social rules are generally considered good behaviour. People who behave politely are usually respected by those around them.

### 4. Do you think children can learn about the law outside of school?

Yes, definitely. Children can learn about rules and laws from their parents, community, books, and online educational resources. For example, parents can explain why certain rules exist when children encounter them in everyday life.

### 5. What are the benefits for people to obey rules?

Following rules can make society safer and more organised. For example, when drivers follow traffic rules, the risk of accidents can be reduced. Similarly, when people respect public rules, it becomes easier for everyone to live together peacefully.

### 6. How can parents teach children to obey rules?

Parents should explain the reasons behind rules rather than simply telling children what to do. They should also set a good example because children often copy their parents' behaviour. If parents consistently follow rules themselves, children are more likely to develop the same habit.

# Topic 21: Describe a time when you got up early

## Part 2 — Cue Card

### Describe a time when you got up early.

You should say:

* When it was
* What you did
* Why you got up early
* And how you felt about it

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about a time when I got up very early to go on a trip with some of my close friends. It happened last year when we decided to visit a tourist destination outside my city.

Normally, I wake up at around seven in the morning, but on that day I got up at about five o'clock. I prepared myself, had a quick breakfast, and left home early so that we could avoid heavy traffic.

The main reason I got up early was that we wanted to reach the destination as soon as possible. Since the journey was quite long, leaving early helped us save some time and enjoy the place for longer.

At first, I felt a little sleepy because I was not used to waking up that early. However, after I had breakfast and started travelling with my friends, I became more energetic and excited.

I actually enjoyed the experience because the roads were relatively quiet, and we could see the sunrise during our journey. Although getting up early was difficult at first, I felt satisfied because it helped us make better use of the day.

Overall, it was a memorable experience, and it showed me that waking up early can be worthwhile when there is a good reason to do it.

## Part 3

### 1. Do you know anyone who likes to get up early?

Yes, I know a few people who enjoy getting up early. One of my relatives, for example, usually wakes up before sunrise because he likes to exercise and work in his garden. He says that mornings are peaceful, so he can concentrate better.

### 2. Why do people get up early?

People get up early for different reasons. Some people need to go to work or school, while others prefer to exercise or complete important tasks before the day becomes busy. If people wake up early, they may also have more time to organise their daily activities.

### 3. What kinds of occasions need people to arrive early?

People usually need to arrive early for important occasions such as job interviews, examinations, flights, weddings, and business meetings. Arriving early is particularly important when punctuality is expected because being late can create a negative impression.

### 4. Why do some people like to stay up late?

Some people stay up late because they feel more productive at night. For example, students may study late because the environment is quieter and there are fewer distractions. Others may stay awake because they enjoy watching films, using social media, or talking to friends.
