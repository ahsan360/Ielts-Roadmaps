# Part 1: Walking in Daily Life and Beyond

## 1. How much walking do you do in your daily life?

I walk quite a lot in my daily routine. I usually walk to nearby shops, take short walks after meals, and sometimes go for an evening walk to clear my mind. Although I don't walk everywhere, I try to stay active by walking whenever it's practical instead of using transport.

---

## 2. Did you walk more when you were at school than now?

Yes, definitely. When I was at school, I walked much more because I had to move between classrooms, participate in outdoor activities, and sometimes walk to school. Nowadays, I spend most of my time working on a computer, so I have fewer opportunities to walk during the day.

---

## 3. What places are there to go for a walk near where you live?

Fortunately, there are several pleasant places to walk near my home. There are a few parks, quiet residential streets, and beautiful tea gardens where people often go for a relaxing walk. I particularly enjoy walking in the evening because the weather is usually cooler and the surroundings are peaceful.

---

## 4. Would you ever like to go on a walking holiday?

Absolutely. I'd love to go on a walking holiday, especially in a place with breathtaking natural scenery, such as the mountains or the countryside. I think it would be a wonderful opportunity to enjoy nature, improve my fitness, and escape the stress of everyday life. It would also allow me to explore places at a slower pace and appreciate the local culture more deeply.
