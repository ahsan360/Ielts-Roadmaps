# Part 1: Fruit Preferences and Purchases

## 1. What's your favourite fruit?

My favourite fruit is **mango**, especially during the summer. I love its sweet taste, juicy texture, and refreshing flavour. Since Bangladesh is famous for producing high-quality mangoes, I always look forward to mango season every year.

---

## 2. Are there any kinds of fruit that you don't like eating?

Yes, there are a few. I don't particularly enjoy eating **grapefruit** because it has a slightly bitter taste. Although I know it's nutritious, I simply don't find it as enjoyable as sweeter fruits like mangoes, bananas, or grapes.

---

## 3. Do you like eating cooked food that has fruit in it?

Yes, I do, although I don't eat it very often. I enjoy dishes that combine fruit with meat or desserts, such as apple pie or chicken cooked with pineapple. I think fruit adds a unique flavour that makes the dish more delicious and refreshing.

---

## 4. Where's the best place to buy fruit where you live?

In my area, the best place to buy fruit is the **local fresh market** because the fruit is usually fresher and more affordable than in supermarkets. There are also many seasonal fruits available, and customers have plenty of options to choose from. Whenever possible, I prefer buying directly from local vendors.
