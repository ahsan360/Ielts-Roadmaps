# Part 1: Fruit Preferences and Purchases

## 1. What's your favourite fruit?

My favourite fruit is **mango**, especially during the summer. I love its sweet taste, juicy texture, and refreshing flavour. Since Bangladesh is famous for producing high-quality mangoes, I always look forward to mango season every year.

---

## 2. Are there any kinds of fruit that you don't like eating?

Yes, there are a few. I don't particularly enjoy eating **grapefruit** because it has a slightly bitter taste. Although I know it's nutritious, I simply don't find it as enjoyable as sweeter fruits like mangoes, bananas, or grapes.

---

## 3. Do you like eating cooked food that has fruit in it?

Yes, I do, although I don't eat it very often. I enjoy dishes that combine fruit with meat or desserts, such as apple pie or chicken cooked with pineapple. I think fruit adds a unique flavour that makes the dish more delicious and refreshing.

---

## 4. Where's the best place to buy fruit where you live?

In my area, the best place to buy fruit is the **local fresh market** because the fruit is usually fresher and more affordable than in supermarkets. There are also many seasonal fruits available, and customers have plenty of options to choose from. Whenever possible, I prefer buying directly from local vendors.

# Part 2: Describe a Time When You Changed a Plan You Had Made

## Introduction

I'd like to talk about a time when I had to change my travel plan. It happened last year when I was planning a trip to **Sajek Valley**, one of the most beautiful tourist destinations in Bangladesh. I had been looking forward to the trip for several weeks because I wanted to relax and spend some quality time with my friends.

---

## What your original plan was

My original plan was to travel to Sajek Valley with four of my close friends during a long weekend. We had already booked our hotel, arranged transportation, and prepared a detailed itinerary. We planned to enjoy the breathtaking scenery, watch the sunrise, and explore the local attractions.

---

## Why you changed it

Unfortunately, just two days before our departure, the weather forecast predicted heavy rainfall and there was also a risk of landslides in the hill areas. Since our safety was the top priority, we decided it wasn't worth taking the risk. Although we were disappointed, we agreed that changing the plan was the most sensible decision.

---

## What new plan you made

Instead of travelling to Sajek, we decided to visit **Sylhet**, which was much safer at that time. We explored Ratargul Swamp Forest, visited tea gardens, enjoyed local cuisine, and spent time chatting together. Even though it wasn't our first choice, we still had an amazing time and created many wonderful memories.

---

## How you felt about changing your plan

At first, I felt frustrated because I had been really excited about visiting Sajek. However, after arriving in Sylhet, I realised that changing the plan was the right decision. The trip turned out to be enjoyable, relaxing, and completely stress-free. Looking back, I'm glad we prioritised our safety over our excitement.

---

## Conclusion

Overall, this experience taught me that being flexible is an important part of travelling. Sometimes unexpected situations force us to change our plans, but those changes can lead to equally memorable experiences.
