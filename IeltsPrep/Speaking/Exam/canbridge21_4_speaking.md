# Part 1: Bread

## 1. When do you usually eat bread?

I usually eat bread for breakfast because it's quick, convenient, and easy to prepare. I often have it with eggs, butter, or jam, along with a cup of coffee. Occasionally, I also eat bread as a light evening snack when I don't have enough time to cook.

---

## 2. How important is bread in your culture?

Bread is fairly important in my country, although rice is still the staple food. Many people eat bread for breakfast or as a quick snack because it's affordable, widely available, and convenient. In recent years, different varieties of bread have become increasingly popular, especially among younger people.

---

## 3. Have you tried any kinds of bread from other countries?

Yes, I have. I've tried several types of bread from other countries, including baguettes, croissants, and garlic bread. I really enjoyed their unique flavours and textures. Although they're quite different from the bread we commonly eat in Bangladesh, I think they're delicious and worth trying.

---

## 4. Would you be interested in learning how to make bread?

Definitely. I think learning how to bake bread would be both enjoyable and practical. It would allow me to prepare fresh, homemade bread whenever I wanted, and I could experiment with different ingredients and flavours. Besides that, I believe baking can be a relaxing and rewarding hobby.

# Part 2: Describe a Person You Know Who Is Very Competitive

## Band 7.5+ Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about one of my close friends, **Rahim**, whom I've known since my university days. He's one of the most competitive people I've ever met, especially when it comes to academics, sports, and his professional career.

At university, he always aimed to achieve the highest grades in every semester. Even after graduation, his competitive nature didn't change. As a software engineer, he constantly tries to improve his technical skills, solve challenging programming problems, and learn new technologies before anyone else on his team. Besides work, he's also very competitive whenever we play badminton or online games. He never gives up easily and always tries to perform better than he did before.

I'd say he's been quite successful because his determination has helped him accomplish many of his goals. He graduated with excellent academic results, secured a job at a reputable software company, and has earned several promotions within a relatively short period. Whenever he sets a goal, he works consistently until he achieves it.

I think the main reason he's so competitive is that he genuinely enjoys challenging himself. Rather than comparing himself with others, he focuses on continuous self-improvement. His family has also encouraged him to work hard from an early age, which has shaped his mindset. Whenever he falls short of his expectations, he treats it as a learning opportunity instead of giving up.

Overall, I really admire his determination and strong work ethic. Spending time with him motivates me to push myself harder and constantly improve, so I believe his competitive attitude has had a very positive influence on my own life.

# Part 3: Competitions

## 1. What kinds of competitions do people like to enter in your country?

In Bangladesh, people participate in a wide range of competitions. Students often take part in academic contests such as debate competitions, science fairs, and programming competitions. Many people also enjoy sports tournaments like cricket and football. In addition, talent shows, photography contests, and business competitions have become increasingly popular, especially among young people.

---

## 2. What do you think is the best kind of prize to win in a competition?

Personally, I think scholarships, cash prizes, or career opportunities are the most valuable rewards because they can have a long-term impact on a person's future. Although trophies and medals are meaningful symbols of achievement, practical rewards often motivate people even more and help them achieve their goals.

---

## 3. Why do people like watching quiz shows and other competitions on TV?

I think people enjoy these programmes because they're both entertaining and educational. Quiz shows allow viewers to test their own knowledge while learning new facts. Competitive programmes also create excitement because people become emotionally involved and want to see who will win. In addition, many viewers admire contestants who demonstrate exceptional talent or intelligence.

---

# Being Competitive in Sports

## 4. How important is it to be very competitive at sport?

I think competitiveness is important because it encourages athletes to train harder and continuously improve their performance. A healthy competitive attitude can build discipline, confidence, and resilience. However, it's equally important to respect opponents and maintain good sportsmanship, as excessive competitiveness can sometimes lead to unnecessary pressure or conflict.

---

## 5. Do you think sportspeople perform better when they play against competitors who are stronger than they are?

Yes, I believe they often do. Competing against stronger opponents pushes athletes beyond their comfort zones and helps them identify their weaknesses. As a result, they become more motivated to improve their skills and develop better strategies. Even if they don't win, the experience can contribute significantly to their long-term development.

---

## 6. Why do some people think that taking part in sport is more important than winning?

Many people believe participation is more important because sport teaches valuable life skills such as teamwork, discipline, perseverance, and respect. Winning is temporary, but the lessons learned through regular participation can benefit people throughout their lives. Moreover, not everyone can be a champion, but everyone can enjoy the physical and mental benefits of being active.
