# IELTS Speaking Part 2 --- People & Relationships

## Complete Master Guide for the 24 Cue Cards

### Target

The aim is **Band 6.5+**, not memorising 24 separate speeches. Use a
small story bank, then change the **focus** according to the cue card.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. The Core Strategy

Use:

**One familiar person → one real incident → one specific angle**

Do not memorise complete answers word for word. Memorise: - who the
person is - how you know them - 5--8 character traits - 2--3 memorable
incidents - what they do - how they have influenced you - flexible
grammar structures - useful vocabulary

## Your main people bank

### Person A --- Your uncle

Use this as the main person for: - caring about nature - growing
plants - helping others - solving problems - planning - having an
interesting life - inspiring you - being creative, **only where
genuinely appropriate**

**Core facts** - You have known him since childhood. - He is an
experienced software engineer. - He is practical, patient and
organised. - He enjoys gardening and planting trees. - He cares about
reducing unnecessary plastic use. - He often gives practical advice. -
He usually thinks carefully before making decisions. - He has influenced
your attitude towards learning and personal development.

### Person B --- A childhood friend

Use this for: - childhood friend - important friend - learning something
independently - good habits - encouraging someone - helping each other

**Core facts** - You have known the friend for many years. - You share
several memorable experiences. - The friend is supportive and
dependable. - The friend learned a useful skill independently. - You
sometimes study, travel or spend free time together. - You value the
friendship because you can communicate honestly.

### Person C --- A successful professional

Use this for: - successful businessperson - person working in a
successful company - successful career - professional inspiration

Prepare one genuine person you know or know about. Keep the details
factual and simple.

### Person D --- A famous/special person

Use this for: - successful sportsperson - singer/musician - famous
person - creative person - famous person you would like to meet

Choose a real person you genuinely know enough about. Do not invent
detailed personal information.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. The Universal Person Structure

Use this 7-part structure for almost every person cue card:

1.  **Who** --- who the person is
2.  **Connection** --- how/when you know them
3.  **Appearance/personality** --- 2--4 characteristics
4.  **Relevant quality** --- the quality required by the cue card
5.  **Specific incident** --- one example
6.  **Impact** --- how the person affected you or others
7.  **Reflection** --- why you remember/admire them

### Opening

> I'd like to talk about someone I have known for a long time, and that
> is my uncle.

### Background

> I have known him since I was a child, so I have had plenty of
> opportunities to observe his personality and the way he treats other
> people.

### Description

> He is a fairly calm and practical person, and one thing I particularly
> admire about him is that he tends to think carefully before taking
> action.

### Specific example

> For example, whenever he faces a problem, he usually breaks it down
> into smaller parts and looks for a practical solution.

### Personal reaction

> What I appreciate most about him is that he does not simply give
> advice; he usually explains why he thinks a particular approach would
> work.

### Ending

> For these reasons, he has had a positive influence on me, and I still
> learn a lot from him.

# 5. Flexible Band-6.5 Grammar

Use these naturally.

### Although

> Although he is extremely busy, he always finds time to help other
> people.

### Relative clause

> He is someone who has had a significant influence on me.

### Because

> I admire him because he always approaches problems logically.

### One of the reasons why

> One of the reasons why I admire him is that he is extremely
> dependable.

### Whenever

> Whenever I face a difficult decision, I often ask him for advice.

### If

> If I ever have a serious problem, he is one of the first people I
> contact.

### Present perfect

> I have known him since I was a child.

### Used to

> When I was younger, we used to spend a lot of time together.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. What NOT to do

Do not say the same prepared paragraph for every person cue card.

Do not invent facts just to fit the question.

Do not use ten difficult words in every answer.

Do not memorise every sentence.

Instead: **remember the story → identify the required quality → tell one
relevant incident → explain your reaction.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Practice Method for Today

### Round 1 --- Story recall

Choose each core person and speak for 60 seconds using only keywords.

### Round 2 --- Adaptation

Take 5 cue cards and identify: **Person + required quality + incident +
feeling**

### Round 3 --- Full answers

For 8 random People cue cards: - 1 minute preparation - 2 minutes
speaking - no restarting - record yourself

### Round 4 --- Error correction

After recording, find only: - 3 grammar errors - 3 repeated words - 1
fluency problem

Correct those and repeat 2 answers.

### Round 5 --- Random test

Ask someone to select a cue card randomly. You should be able to connect
it to a suitable person within 10--15 seconds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Band 6.5 Checklist

Before finishing today's practice, you should be able to:

-   speak for almost 2 minutes
-   tell a coherent story
-   use past and present tenses accurately
-   use some complex sentences
-   avoid excessive hesitation
-   use vocabulary beyond basic adjectives
-   answer the exact cue-card focus
-   give at least one specific example
-   explain your feelings and reasons

**Core principle: Do not memorise 24 answers. Build 4--6 people and
adapt them intelligently.**
