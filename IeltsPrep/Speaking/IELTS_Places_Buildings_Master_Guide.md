# IELTS Speaking Part 2 --- Places & Buildings

## Complete Master Guide for the 22 Cue Cards

### Target

The goal is **Band 6.5+** through flexible storytelling, accurate
grammar, useful vocabulary and natural delivery.

Do not memorise 22 separate speeches. Build a small **place bank**, then
change the focus according to the cue card.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Universal Place Structure

Use:

1.  **Identify the place**
2.  **Location**
3.  **When/why you went**
4.  **Appearance and surroundings**
5.  **What you did/saw**
6.  **Atmosphere**
7.  **How you felt**
8.  **Why you remember/recommend it**

### Opening

> I'd like to talk about Jaflong, a scenic destination in the Sylhet
> region that I have visited several times.

### Location

> It is located in a relatively natural area and is known for its
> mountains, river and greenery.

### Description

> What I particularly like about the place is the combination of the
> river, surrounding hills and fresh air.

### Activity

> When I go there, I usually walk around, take photographs and spend
> time talking with my friends.

### Feeling

> Whenever I visit, I feel that I can temporarily escape the pressure of
> everyday life.

### Ending

> That is why it remains one of the places I would happily recommend to
> other people.

# 5. Sensory Details

To make a place answer more vivid, use one detail from each category.

### Sight

> I could see green hills stretching into the distance.

### Sound

> The sound of flowing water made the atmosphere remarkably peaceful.

### Air/weather

> The fresh air was a pleasant change from the busy city.

### Feeling

> I felt mentally refreshed after spending a few hours there.

Do not overload the answer. Two or three sensory details are enough.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Flexible Band-6.5 Grammar

### Relative clause

> It is a place that is particularly famous for its natural scenery.

### Although

> Although it can become crowded during holidays, I still enjoy visiting
> it.

### Because

> I like this place because it allows me to get away from my normal
> routine.

### Where

> It is an area where people can relax and enjoy the natural
> environment.

### Which

> There is a river nearby, which makes the scenery even more attractive.

### Whenever

> Whenever I visit, I usually spend time walking around with my friends.

### If

> If I had more free time, I would probably stay there for a few days.

### Compared with

> Compared with my hometown, the atmosphere there is much more peaceful.

# 8. What NOT to Do

Do not memorise one identical paragraph for: - natural place - quiet
place - tourist destination - countryside place

Do not describe a place with only adjectives.

Do not invent experiences.

Do not spend the whole answer describing appearance. Include: **what
happened + what you did + how you felt + why it matters.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Practice Method for Today

### Round 1 --- Place recall

Speak for 60 seconds about each core place using only keywords.

### Round 2 --- Adaptation

Take Jaflong and answer 6 different cue cards with different focuses.

### Round 3 --- Full answers

Choose 8 random Places & Buildings cue cards: - 1 minute preparation - 2
minutes speaking - record everything - no restarting

### Round 4 --- Error analysis

For each recording, identify: - 3 grammar errors - 3 repeated words - 1
weak sentence - 1 place where you hesitated

Then correct and repeat the weakest two answers.

### Round 5 --- Mixed test

Mix People and Places. You should be able to choose a suitable story
within 10--15 seconds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. Band 6.5 Checklist

You should aim to: - speak for nearly two minutes - maintain a clear
storyline - use past/present/future appropriately - produce some complex
sentences - avoid long silent pauses - use specific descriptive
vocabulary - answer the exact question - include reasons and feelings -
give at least one concrete detail - avoid excessive repetition

## Final principle

**Do not memorise 22 place answers. Build 5--6 places and learn how to
change the angle.**

Your formula is:

**Place → Context → Description → Activity → Feeling → Reflection**

Once this becomes automatic, many apparently different cue cards become
variations of the same underlying story.
