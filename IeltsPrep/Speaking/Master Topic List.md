# IELTS Speaking Part 2 - Master Topic List

A comprehensive collection of 25 high-frequency IELTS Speaking Part 2 cue card topics, categorized for targeted practice.

---

## People & Relationships
1. **An interesting person you met recently**
2. **A person who inspires you**
3. **An intelligent or creative person you know**
4. **A person you enjoy working or studying with**
5. **A famous person you would like to meet**
6. **A successful businessperson you admire**

---

## Places & Travel
7. **A place that exceeded your expectations**
8. **A quiet place you visit to relax**
9. **A city or town you would like to live in**
10. **A memorable journey or trip**
11. **A tourist spot you would recommend**
12. **A historical building or landmark**

## Objects, Technology & Products

## Events, Experiences & Activities
18. **A time you had to solve a difficult problem**

21. **An event you attended where you learned something new**

---

### Practice Tips for Part 2
- **Timing:** Aim to speak continuously for 1.5 to 2 minutes.
- **Preparation:** Use the 1-minute prep time to outline a chronological flow (Past -> Present -> Future/Reflection).
- **Elaboration:** Always explain *why* something was significant to build lexical resource and sentence complexity.
