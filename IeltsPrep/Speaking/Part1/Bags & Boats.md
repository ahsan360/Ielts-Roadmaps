# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. Have you ever taken a ride on a boat?

### 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- In Sylhet
- River sightseeing
- Relaxing experience

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I have. I’ve taken several boat rides in Sylhet, especially while visiting places like Ratargul Swamp Forest. It was a peaceful and memorable experience because I could enjoy the beautiful scenery and fresh air. I always find travelling on the water much more relaxing than travelling by road.

---

## 2. Do you like travelling by boat?

### 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Calm
- Beautiful views
- Not every day

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. I really enjoy travelling by boat because it offers a completely different experience from travelling by car or bus. The slow pace and beautiful surroundings make the journey much more enjoyable. Although I don't travel by boat very often, I always look forward to the opportunity.

---

## 3. What are the advantages of travelling by boat?

### 💡 Ideas
- Relaxing
- Beautiful scenery
- Less traffic
- Tourist attraction

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

One major advantage is that travelling by boat is usually much more peaceful than travelling on busy roads. It also allows people to enjoy stunning natural scenery along rivers or lakes. In some areas, boats can even be a faster way to reach destinations while avoiding traffic congestion.

---

## 4. Do people in your country like to travel by boat?

### 💡 Ideas
- Bangladesh has many rivers
- Common in rural areas
- Popular among tourists

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, many people do. Since Bangladesh is a river-based country, boats are still an important means of transport, particularly in rural regions. They're also popular among tourists who want to explore scenic places or enjoy river cruises.

---

## 5. Will it get more popular in the future?

### 💡 Ideas
- Tourism
- Better transportation
- Eco-friendly

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I believe so. As tourism continues to develop, more people may choose boat trips for leisure and sightseeing. In addition, environmentally friendly water transport could become more popular if governments invest in better infrastructure.

---

## 6. Do you like bags?

### 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Practical
- Use daily

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, definitely. I think bags are essential because they help me carry everything I need throughout the day. Besides being practical, a well-designed bag can also complement a person's overall appearance.

---

## 7. What types of bags do you like?

### 💡 Ideas
- Backpack
- Laptop
- Comfortable
- Durable

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I prefer backpacks, especially those designed for laptops. They’re comfortable to carry, offer plenty of storage space, and keep my belongings well organised. I also prefer bags made from durable and water-resistant materials.

---

## 8. Do you usually carry a bag when you go out?

### 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Daily
- Work/Study

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, almost every day. Whenever I leave home for work or study, I carry a backpack because it holds all my daily essentials, including my laptop, charger, notebook, and water bottle.

---

## 9. What types of bags do you use in your everyday life?

### 💡 Ideas
- Backpack
- Travel bag
- Small sling bag

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Most of the time, I use a laptop backpack. If I'm travelling, I switch to a larger travel backpack with more storage. Occasionally, I carry a small crossbody bag when I only need my phone, wallet, and keys.

---

## 10. What do you put in these bags?

### 💡 Ideas
- Laptop
- Wallet
- Phone
- Charger
- Notebook
- Earphones

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I usually carry my laptop, phone charger, wallet, notebook, earphones, a water bottle, and sometimes a book. If I'm travelling, I also pack a power bank and a few personal items.

---

## 11. What sorts of bags do women like to buy?

### 💡 Ideas
- Fashionable
- Practical
- Handbags
- Tote bags
- Backpacks

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

It really depends on personal preference, but many women seem to like handbags, tote bags, and crossbody bags because they're both stylish and practical. Recently, fashionable backpacks have also become increasingly popular since they combine comfort with functionality.
