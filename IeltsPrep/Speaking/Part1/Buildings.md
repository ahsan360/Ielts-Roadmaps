# Part 1: Buildings

## 1. Are there tall buildings near your home?

Yes, there are several tall buildings near my home, particularly
apartment blocks and commercial buildings. The area has been developing
quite rapidly, so new buildings seem to appear every few years.

## 2. Do you like photos of buildings?

Yes, but it depends on the building. I'm not particularly interested in
ordinary buildings, but I enjoy looking at photographs of buildings with
distinctive architecture, historical significance, or unusual designs.

## 3. Is there a building that you would like to visit?

Yes, I'd love to visit the Burj Khalifa in Dubai one day. I'm fascinated
by modern architecture, and I'd like to experience the view from one of
its observation areas. I think seeing a city from such a great height
would be unforgettable.

## 4. Do you want to live in a tall building?

Yes, I would consider it, provided that the building is safe,
well-maintained, and conveniently located. Living in a high-rise
apartment could offer excellent views and access to modern facilities,
although I'd also want enough privacy and a comfortable living
environment.
