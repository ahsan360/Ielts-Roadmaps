# Part 1: Cars and Travelling

## 1. Did you enjoy travelling by car when you were a kid?

Yes, I did. I particularly enjoyed long car journeys with my family because they gave us an opportunity to spend quality time together. I used to look out of the window, listen to music, and get excited whenever we travelled to a new place. Those journeys are still some of my fondest childhood memories.

---

## 2. What types of cars do you like?

I tend to prefer modern, reliable, and fuel-efficient cars. I'm particularly interested in cars that combine good performance with advanced technology and a comfortable interior. I don't necessarily need a luxury car; for me, practicality and reliability are more important than appearance.

---

## 3. Do you prefer to be a driver or a passenger?

I generally prefer being a driver because I enjoy having control over the journey. I can choose the route, control the speed, and stop whenever I need to. However, when I'm travelling a long distance, I sometimes prefer being a passenger because I can relax and enjoy the scenery instead of concentrating on the road.

---

## 4. What do you usually do when there is a traffic jam?

When I'm stuck in traffic, I usually try to remain patient rather than getting frustrated. I often listen to music, podcasts, or something educational while waiting. If the traffic is particularly heavy, I use a navigation app to check whether there is an alternative route.

---

## 5. What kind of car would you like to own in the future?

I'd like to own a comfortable and fuel-efficient electric car in the future. Ideally, it would have advanced safety features, a spacious interior, and a good driving range. I'm particularly attracted to electric vehicles because they are environmentally friendly and could reduce long-term fuel and maintenance costs.

---

## 6. Do you think the colour of a car matters?

Yes, but I think it depends on personal preference. Personally, I prefer neutral colours such as black, white, or silver because they look elegant and are relatively easy to maintain. However, some people deliberately choose bright colours because they want their cars to stand out and reflect their personality.

---
