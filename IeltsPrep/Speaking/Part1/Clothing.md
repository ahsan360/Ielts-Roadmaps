# Part 1: Clothing

## 1. What kind of clothes do you like to wear?

I generally prefer comfortable and simple clothes, such as T-shirts,
jeans, and casual shirts. Since I spend a lot of time working, I usually
choose clothes that allow me to move around comfortably. For formal
occasions, however, I prefer smart and well-fitted outfits.

## 2. Do you prefer to wear comfortable and casual clothes or smart clothes?

I definitely prefer comfortable and casual clothes for everyday life.
They are more practical and suitable for my daily routine. However, I
think smart clothes are essential for formal occasions because dressing
appropriately creates a good impression.

## 3. Do you like wearing T-shirts?

Yes, I do. T-shirts are probably one of the most practical items of
clothing because they are comfortable, versatile, and easy to maintain.
I can wear them at home, while travelling, or when meeting friends
casually.

## 4. Do you spend a lot of time choosing clothes?

Not really. I usually decide what to wear quite quickly because I prefer
keeping my wardrobe relatively simple. I tend to choose clothes based on
comfort, the weather, and the occasion rather than spending a long time
thinking about my outfit.
