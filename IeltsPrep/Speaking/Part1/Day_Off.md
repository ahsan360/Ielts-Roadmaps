# IELTS Speaking Band 7+ Sample Answers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part 1: Day Off

## 1. When was the last time you had a day off?

I had a day off quite recently, probably a few weeks ago. I had been
quite busy with work, so I decided to take a break and spend some
quality time with my family. It was a simple but refreshing day.

## 2. What do you usually do when you have days off?

It depends on how tired I am. If I need to recharge, I usually stay at
home, watch something, read, or catch up on sleep. If I feel energetic,
I might go out with friends, explore a new place, or have a meal at a
restaurant.

## 3. Do you usually spend your days off with your parents or friends?

I usually divide my time between them. If I've been particularly busy, I
prefer spending time with my family because I don't always get enough
opportunities to talk to them. However, I also enjoy meeting friends
when I want to socialise and have some fun.

## 4. What would you like to do if you had a day off tomorrow?

If I had a day off tomorrow, I'd probably take a short trip somewhere
peaceful. I'd like to get away from my usual routine, enjoy some natural
scenery, have good food, and spend some uninterrupted time relaxing. I
think that would help me return to work feeling refreshed.
