# Part 1: Doing Something Well

## 1. Do you have an experience when you did something well?

Yes, definitely. I remember completing an important project at work and
everything went smoothly. I had planned the work carefully, solved
several problems during the process, and managed to finish it on time. I
felt quite proud of the result.

## 2. Do you have an experience when your teacher thought you did a good job?

Yes, I do. I remember writing an essay in English class, and my teacher
praised me for the quality of my work. I was particularly happy because
English was not always easy for me, so the positive feedback gave me
more confidence.

## 3. Do you often tell your friends when they do something well?

Yes, I do. If a friend does something particularly well, I usually tell
them because I think genuine appreciation can make people feel more
confident. I also believe that encouraging others can strengthen
friendships and create a more positive relationship.
