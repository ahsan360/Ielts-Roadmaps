# Part 1: Dream and Ambition

## 1. What was your childhood dream?

When I was a child, I dreamed of becoming a pilot because I was
fascinated by aeroplanes and travelling around the world. I thought it
would be an exciting career that would allow me to explore different
countries and experience different cultures.

## 2. Are you the kind of person who sticks to your dreams?

I'd say I am, although I also understand that circumstances can change.
I usually set clear goals and work towards them consistently, but I'm
willing to adapt my plans if I discover a better opportunity or realise
that my original goal is no longer suitable.

## 3. What is your dream job?

My dream job would be to work as a highly skilled software engineer at a
world-leading technology company. I'd like to work on challenging
projects, solve complex problems, and collaborate with talented
engineers from different backgrounds.

## 4. Do you think you are ambitious?

Yes, I would consider myself quite ambitious. I enjoy setting
challenging goals and continuously improving my skills. However, I don't
think ambition should come at the expense of health, relationships, or
personal well-being, so I try to maintain a reasonable balance.
