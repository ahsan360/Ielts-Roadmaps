# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. Do you dream much at night?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Not every night
- Usually after a busy day
- Sometimes very realistic

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do, although not every night. I tend to have more vivid dreams when I've had a particularly busy or stressful day. Most of my dreams are quite random, but occasionally they feel surprisingly realistic and stay in my mind for a while.

---

## 2. Do you often remember your dreams?

### 💡 Ideas

- Sometimes
- Forget quickly
- Remember unusual ones

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Only sometimes. Most of my dreams disappear from my memory within a few minutes of waking up. However, if I have a particularly unusual or emotional dream, I can usually remember it for several days.

---

## 3. Do you think we can learn anything from dreams?

### 💡 Ideas

- Sometimes
- Reflect emotions
- Not always meaningful

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

To some extent, yes. I think dreams can reflect our emotions, worries, or aspirations because they're influenced by our subconscious mind. That said, I don't believe every dream has a hidden meaning. Sometimes they're simply a result of our imagination.

---

## 4. Do people in your country talk about their dreams?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Family and friends
- Funny or strange dreams

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, many people do. It's quite common for family members or close friends to talk about funny, strange, or frightening dreams during casual conversations. Some people even enjoy interpreting dreams, especially older generations.

---

## 5. Do you think that dreams can come true?

### 💡 Ideas

- Personal goals
- Hard work
- Not magical

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, but only in a practical sense. I believe our ambitions and life goals can come true if we work consistently towards them. However, I don't think dreams become reality through luck alone. Determination, persistence, and hard work are far more important.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I see dreams every night.

✅ I have dreams almost every night.

---

❌ My dream came true by luck.

✅ My dream came true because of consistent hard work.

---

❌ Dreams have many meaning.

✅ Dreams can have different meanings.

---

❌ I don't remember my dream.

✅ I don't usually remember my dreams.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

Differentiate between **sleep dreams** and **life dreams (goals)** if the opportunity arises.

### ❌ Basic

> Yes, dreams come true.

### ✅ Band 8+

> If we're talking about life ambitions, then yes, I believe dreams can come true through perseverance and dedication. However, if we're referring to the dreams we have while sleeping, I don't think they predict the future.
