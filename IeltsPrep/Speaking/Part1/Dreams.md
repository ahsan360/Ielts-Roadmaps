# Part 1: Dreams

## 1. Can you remember the dreams you have?

Yes, sometimes. I can usually remember a dream for a short time after
waking up, but I tend to forget most of the details quite quickly. I
remember the dreams that are particularly vivid or unusual.

## 2. Do you share your dreams with others?

Occasionally, especially if the dream was interesting or someone I know
appeared in it. However, I don't usually talk about my dreams because I
tend to forget them quite quickly.

## 3. Do you think dreams have special meanings?

I'm not completely sure. I think some dreams may reflect our fears,
thoughts, or experiences, but I don't necessarily believe that every
dream has a deeper meaning. Sometimes a dream may simply be the result
of what we have been thinking about during the day.

## 4. Do you want to make your dreams come true?

Yes, definitely. I have several personal and professional goals that I'd
like to achieve in the future. I think working consistently towards
those goals is more important than simply hoping they will happen.
