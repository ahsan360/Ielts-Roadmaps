# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. What kinds of emails do you receive about your work or studies?

### 💡 Ideas

- Work updates
- Interview invitations
- Course notifications
- GitHub/LinkedIn notifications

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I mostly receive professional emails related to work and learning. They usually include project updates, interview invitations, online course notifications, and messages from platforms like GitHub and LinkedIn. I also receive newsletters about software development and artificial intelligence to keep myself updated.

---

## 2. Do you prefer to email, phone, or text your friends?

### 💡 Ideas

- Text messages
- Quick
- Email for formal communication

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I usually prefer text messaging because it's quick and convenient. My friends and I mostly communicate through messaging apps since they allow instant replies. However, I use email whenever I need to send important information or documents.

---

## 3. Do you reply to emails as soon as you receive them?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Important emails first
- Busy sometimes

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, whenever possible. I try to reply to important emails within a few hours because I believe prompt communication reflects professionalism. If I'm busy, I at least acknowledge the email and respond in detail later.

---

## 4. Are you happy to receive emails that are advertising things?

### 💡 Ideas

- Usually no
- Spam
- Useful discounts sometimes

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Not usually. Most advertising emails are irrelevant to me, so I either ignore them or delete them immediately. However, I don't mind receiving promotional emails if they offer useful discounts on books, software, or online courses.

---

## 5. Do you email people often?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Professional communication
- Less for friends

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, quite often. I regularly use email for professional communication, especially when applying for jobs, contacting recruiters, or discussing projects. For casual conversations, though, I prefer messaging apps.

---

## 6. Do you chat with friends or family more with email?

### 💡 Ideas

- No
- Messaging apps
- Faster

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

No, not really. I rarely use email to communicate with my family or friends because messaging apps are much faster and more convenient. Email is mainly reserved for formal or work-related communication.

---

## 7. Do you think people prefer to receive an email or a letter?

### 💡 Ideas

- Email
- Faster
- Letters for special occasions

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Nowadays, I think most people prefer emails because they're fast, free, and convenient. That said, handwritten letters still have emotional value and are much more meaningful on special occasions such as birthdays or anniversaries.

---

## 8. Do you think it's easier to write an email or a letter?

### 💡 Ideas

- Email
- Easy editing
- Quick

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think writing an email is much easier because it's faster and more flexible. If I make a mistake, I can edit it instantly before sending it. In contrast, writing a letter takes more time and requires greater effort.

---

## 9. Do you think email will eventually replace letter writing?

### 💡 Ideas

- Mostly yes
- Already common
- Letters remain sentimental

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

To a large extent, yes. Emails have already replaced letters for most business and everyday communication because they're far more efficient. Nevertheless, I believe handwritten letters will continue to exist for sentimental occasions, as they feel more personal and heartfelt.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I send mail every day.

✅ I send **emails** every day.

---

❌ I check email.

✅ I check **my email** several times a day.

---

❌ I reply immediately always.

✅ I usually reply as soon as possible.

---

❌ Letter is better because emotional.

✅ Letters are more meaningful because they carry emotional value.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

Compare different communication methods instead of giving a one-word answer.

### ❌ Basic

> I like email.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I prefer email for formal communication because it's professional, organised, and allows me to attach important documents. However, when chatting with friends, I usually use messaging apps since they're much faster and more interactive.
