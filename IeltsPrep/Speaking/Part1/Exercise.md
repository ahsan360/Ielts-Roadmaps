# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. Do you do much exercise?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- 4–5 times a week
- Walking
- Home workouts
- Stay healthy

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. I try to exercise at least four or five times a week because I believe it's essential for maintaining both physical and mental health. Most of the time, I go for a brisk walk or do bodyweight exercises at home. Regular exercise helps me stay energetic and improves my concentration throughout the day.

---

## 2. Do you do more exercise now than when you were a child?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- More aware of health
- Child: outdoor games
- Adult: planned exercise

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, definitely. When I was a child, I stayed active by playing outdoor games with my friends, but I didn't think of it as exercise. Nowadays, I follow a more structured fitness routine because I'm much more aware of the importance of staying healthy.

---

## 3. Do people in your country do a lot of exercise?

### 💡 Ideas

- Mixed
- Young people go to gyms
- Others walk
- Busy lifestyles

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

It varies from person to person. Many young people go to gyms or play sports regularly, while older adults often prefer walking. However, due to increasingly busy lifestyles, a significant number of people don't get enough physical activity, which can lead to health problems.

---

## 4. What do you think is the best exercise to keep fit?

### 💡 Ideas

- Walking
- Easy
- Affordable
- Suitable for everyone

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In my opinion, brisk walking is one of the best forms of exercise. It's simple, doesn't require any special equipment, and is suitable for people of all ages. Besides improving cardiovascular health, it also helps reduce stress and maintain a healthy body weight.

> **Alternative:** If you go to the gym, you can say:
>
> *"I think a combination of strength training and cardio is ideal because it improves overall fitness, builds muscle, and enhances endurance."*

---

## 5. Have the types of exercise people like to do changed since you were a child?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- More gyms
- Yoga
- Fitness apps
- Home workouts

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, they've changed quite a lot. When I was younger, most people simply played outdoor sports like football or cricket. Nowadays, many people prefer going to the gym, practising yoga, or following workout videos through fitness apps and social media. Technology has made exercise much more accessible.

# Band 8+ Tip

Don't just name an exercise—explain **why** it's effective.

### ❌ Basic

> I like walking.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I particularly enjoy brisk walking because it's simple, relaxing, and doesn't require any expensive equipment. It helps me stay fit while also giving me time to clear my mind after a busy day.

# Extra Band 8+ Follow-up Ideas

### If the examiner asks:
**"Do you prefer exercising alone or with others?"**

**💡 Ideas**
- Alone
- Better concentration
- Flexible schedule

**🗣️ Sample Answer**

I usually prefer exercising alone because it allows me to focus better and follow my own pace. It's also easier to fit into my schedule, as I don't have to coordinate with anyone else. However, playing sports with friends can be more enjoyable and motivating.

---

### If the examiner asks:
**"Should schools encourage students to exercise more?"**

**🗣️ Sample Answer**

Absolutely. Regular physical activity improves not only students' physical health but also their concentration and academic performance. Schools should provide more opportunities for sports and educate students about the importance of maintaining a healthy lifestyle from an early age.
