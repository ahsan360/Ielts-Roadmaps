# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. How many people are there in your immediate family?

### 💡 Ideas

- Small family
- Parents and siblings

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

There are four people in my immediate family: my parents, my sibling, and me. Although we aren't a large family, we're very close and always support one another through both good and difficult times.

---

## 2. Who do you get on best with within your family?

### 💡 Ideas

- Mother/Father
- Supportive
- Gives advice

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I get along best with my mother because she's incredibly supportive and understanding. Whenever I face a challenge, she's the first person I turn to for advice. We have a strong bond and can talk openly about almost anything.

---

## 3. Do you have a large extended family?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Cousins
- Family gatherings

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. I have quite a large extended family, including grandparents, uncles, aunts, and many cousins. We usually get together during religious festivals and family celebrations, which gives us an opportunity to strengthen our relationships.

---

## 4. What do you do together with your family?

### 💡 Ideas

- Eat together
- Travel
- Watch movies

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

We enjoy doing many activities together. We often have dinner as a family, watch films, visit relatives, and occasionally go on short trips. These moments help us stay connected despite our busy schedules.

---

## 5. Why is family important to you?

### 💡 Ideas

- Love
- Support
- Motivation

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Family is extremely important to me because they're my biggest source of love, encouragement, and emotional support. No matter what happens in life, I know I can always rely on them, and that gives me confidence to pursue my goals.

---

## 6. Do you do housework at home?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Regularly
- Shared responsibility

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. I believe household chores should be shared among family members rather than being the responsibility of just one person. I try to help whenever I have some free time.

---

## 7. What kind of housework do you often do?

### 💡 Ideas

- Wash dishes
- Tidy room
- Laundry

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I usually wash the dishes, tidy my room, do the laundry, and occasionally help clean the kitchen. These tasks don't take much time, but they make the home cleaner and more organised.

---

## 8. Did you do housework when you were a child?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Simple chores
- Learned responsibility

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I did. My parents encouraged me to do simple household chores such as making my bed and organising my room. Looking back, these responsibilities taught me discipline and independence from an early age.

---

## 9. Do you think that children should do housework?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Responsibility
- Independence

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. I think children should be given age-appropriate household responsibilities because they help develop discipline, responsibility, and practical life skills. However, the workload shouldn't interfere with their education or free time.

---

## 10. Do you all live in the same house?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Convenient
- Support each other

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, we currently live together. Living in the same house allows us to spend more time together and support one another in our daily lives. It also makes family meals and conversations much more frequent.

---

## 11. Who is your favourite family member?

### 💡 Ideas

- Mother/Father
- Kind
- Inspiring

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

It's difficult to choose because I love all my family members equally. However, if I had to pick one, I'd say my mother. She's kind, patient, and always motivates me to become a better person.

---

## 12. Do you have a large family or a small family?

### 💡 Ideas

- Immediate family small
- Extended family large

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I have a relatively small immediate family, but my extended family is quite large. Personally, I enjoy having a large extended family because family gatherings are always lively and enjoyable.

---

## 13. Can you tell me something about your family members?

### 💡 Ideas

- Different personalities
- Supportive
- Hard-working

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Each member of my family has a unique personality. My parents are hardworking and supportive, while my sibling is friendly and humorous. Although we're different in many ways, we always respect and encourage one another.

---

## 14. How much time do you manage to spend with members of your family?

### 💡 Ideas

- Every evening
- Weekends
- Dinner together

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I try to spend at least a few hours with my family every day, especially in the evening. We usually have dinner together and talk about our day. At weekends, we spend even more time together by visiting relatives or watching films.

---

## 15. Do you get on well with your family? Why?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Respect
- Communication

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. I think the main reason is that we communicate openly and respect each other's opinions. Even when we disagree, we try to resolve problems calmly instead of arguing, which helps maintain a positive family atmosphere.

# Common Mistakes

❌ My family have four members.

✅ There are four people in my family.

---

❌ I do houseworks.

✅ I do housework.

---

❌ My mother always give me advice.

✅ My mother always gives me good advice.

---

❌ Children should do all housework.

✅ Children should help with age-appropriate household chores.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

Whenever possible, support your opinion with a brief personal example.

### ❌ Basic

> Yes, I do housework.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Yes, I regularly help with household chores such as washing the dishes and doing the laundry. I believe sharing responsibilities not only keeps the house tidy but also teaches valuable life skills and teamwork.
