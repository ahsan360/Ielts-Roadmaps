# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. Have you ever given anybody flowers?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Mother
- Teacher
- Birthday
- Special occasions

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I have. I usually give flowers on special occasions such as birthdays or Mother's Day. I think flowers are a simple yet meaningful way to express appreciation and make someone feel valued.

---

## 2. Is it popular to give flowers to people in your country?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Weddings
- Birthdays
- Teachers
- Religious events

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, it's quite popular in Bangladesh, especially during weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, and cultural celebrations. People also present flowers to teachers and guests as a sign of respect and hospitality.

---

## 3. On what special occasions do people give flowers in your country?

### 💡 Ideas

- Weddings
- Birthdays
- Anniversaries
- Graduation
- Victory Day
- Mother's Day

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People usually give flowers on occasions such as weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, graduation ceremonies, and Mother's Day. In Bangladesh, flowers are also commonly presented during cultural programmes and national celebrations to show respect and admiration.

---

## 4. What kinds of flowers would you like to receive as a present?

### 💡 Ideas

- Roses
- Lilies
- Sunflowers
- Pleasant fragrance
- Elegant

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I'd be happy to receive almost any kind of flower, but I particularly like white lilies and red roses because they're elegant and have a pleasant fragrance. More importantly, I value the thought behind the gift rather than the type of flower itself.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I like flower.

✅ I like flowers.

---

❌ I give flower to my mother.

✅ I gave flowers to my mother.

---

❌ Flower have good smell.

✅ Flowers have a pleasant fragrance.

---

❌ Flowers make people happy always.

✅ Flowers often make people feel happy and appreciated.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

When talking about gifts, focus on the **meaning** rather than just the object.

### ❌ Basic

> I like roses.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I particularly like roses because they're elegant and symbolize love and appreciation. However, I believe the thought behind the gift is far more meaningful than the flowers themselves.
