# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. Do you enjoy cooking?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Relaxing
- Try new recipes

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I really enjoy cooking. I find it both relaxing and rewarding because it gives me an opportunity to experiment with different recipes and flavours. Although I'm not a professional cook, I enjoy preparing simple home-cooked meals for myself and my family.

---

## 2. What type of things can you cook?

### 💡 Ideas

- Rice
- Chicken curry
- Fried rice
- Pasta
- Eggs

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I can cook several everyday dishes, such as rice, chicken curry, fried rice, pasta, and omelettes. Whenever I have some free time, I also enjoy trying new recipes that I find online or on YouTube.

---

## 3. What kinds of food are popular in your country?

### 💡 Ideas

- Rice
- Fish
- Curry
- Biryani
- Street food

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In Bangladesh, rice is the staple food and is usually served with fish, meat, or vegetable curries. Traditional dishes like biryani, khichuri, and beef curry are extremely popular. Street foods such as *fuchka*, *chotpoti*, and *jhal muri* are also favourites, especially among young people.

---

## 4. Is it an important part of your culture to have dinner parties?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Festivals
- Weddings
- Family gatherings

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, definitely. Hosting dinner parties is an important part of our culture, particularly during religious festivals, weddings, and family celebrations. It's a wonderful opportunity for relatives and friends to come together, enjoy delicious food, and strengthen their relationships.

---

## 5. Do you prefer to eat with other people or on your own?

### 💡 Ideas

- With family
- Better conversation
- More enjoyable

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I definitely prefer eating with other people, especially my family or close friends. Sharing a meal gives us a chance to have meaningful conversations and spend quality time together. I think food tastes even better when it's enjoyed in good company.

---

## 6. Do you have a healthy diet?

### 💡 Ideas

- Mostly yes
- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Occasionally fast food

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I'd say I have a fairly healthy diet. I try to include vegetables, fruits, eggs, and lean protein in my meals and drink plenty of water. Of course, I occasionally eat fast food, but I try to do so in moderation.

---

## 7. Do you prefer eating at home or eating out?

### 💡 Ideas

- Home
- Healthier
- Cheaper
- Restaurants occasionally

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Most of the time, I prefer eating at home because home-cooked meals are healthier, more affordable, and prepared according to my taste. However, I do enjoy eating out occasionally to try different cuisines and celebrate special occasions.

---

## 8. Do you like ordering food to be delivered?

### 💡 Ideas

- Sometimes
- Busy
- Convenient

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, occasionally. Food delivery is extremely convenient when I'm busy or don't have time to cook. However, I don't rely on it very often because I prefer fresh, home-cooked meals whenever possible.

---

## 9. Who do you get food delivered with?

### 💡 Ideas

- Family
- Friends
- Alone sometimes

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Most of the time, I order food with my family, especially during weekends or when we're celebrating something. Occasionally, I also order food with friends while watching a film or working on a project together.

---

## 10. Do you eat meals differently now compared to when you were little?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- More healthy
- Less junk food
- More balanced

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, my eating habits have changed quite a bit. As a child, I loved snacks and sugary foods, but now I'm much more conscious of my health. I try to maintain a balanced diet and pay closer attention to the nutritional value of the food I eat.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I like eat rice.

✅ I like eating rice.

---

❌ I can cook many foods.

✅ I can cook many dishes.

---

❌ I eat outside every day.

✅ I eat out occasionally.

---

❌ Fast food is not good for health.

✅ Fast food isn't very healthy, so I try to eat it only occasionally.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

Avoid giving only yes/no answers. Add a reason and a personal example.

### ❌ Basic

> I like cooking.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I really enjoy cooking because it allows me to experiment with different flavours and recipes. It's also a relaxing activity, especially during weekends when I have more free time.

# Extra Band 8+ Follow-up Ideas

### If the examiner asks:
**"Do young people eat more fast food nowadays?"**

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Busy lifestyles
- Delivery apps

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I think they do. Many young people lead busy lives, so they often choose fast food because it's quick and convenient. The popularity of food delivery apps has also made fast food much more accessible. However, I think it's important to maintain a balanced diet and avoid eating it too frequently.

---

### If the examiner asks:
**"Should children learn to cook?"**

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Learning to cook is an essential life skill. It helps children become more independent, encourages healthy eating habits, and teaches them responsibility. Even learning to prepare a few simple meals can be very beneficial in the long run.
