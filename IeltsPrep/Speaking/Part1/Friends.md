# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. Do you have a lot of friends?

### 💡 Ideas

- Small circle
- Quality over quantity
- Close friends

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I wouldn't say I have a large number of friends. Instead, I prefer having a small circle of close friends whom I genuinely trust. I believe that having a few loyal and dependable friends is far more valuable than knowing lots of people superficially.

---

## 2. Who is your best friend and why?

### 💡 Ideas

- School friend
- Honest
- Supportive
- Similar interests

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

My best friend is someone I've known since high school. What I admire most about him is his honesty and willingness to support me whenever I face difficulties. We also share similar interests, particularly in technology and travelling, so we always have plenty to talk about.

---

## 3. Who would you most like to be friends with and why?

### 💡 Ideas

- Successful entrepreneur
- Scientist
- Athlete
- Learn from them

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I'd love to be friends with someone who is highly successful in the technology industry, such as an experienced software engineer or entrepreneur. I think spending time with people like that would inspire me, broaden my perspective, and help me grow both personally and professionally.

> **Alternative:** You can mention a famous person if you're comfortable doing so.

---

## 4. What kind of person can you make friends with easily?

### 💡 Ideas

- Honest
- Kind
- Good listener
- Positive attitude

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I usually become friends with people who are kind, open-minded, and respectful. I also appreciate those who have a positive attitude and are good listeners because conversations become much more enjoyable and meaningful.

---

## 5. Which is more important to you, friends or family?

### 💡 Ideas

- Family first
- Friends important too
- Different roles

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

If I had to choose, I'd say family is more important because they've always supported me unconditionally. However, good friends also play an important role in life. They offer different perspectives, emotional support, and companionship, so I believe both are essential in their own ways.

# Band 8+ Tip

When describing a friend, explain **why** you value them instead of simply listing personality traits.

### ❌ Basic

> My best friend is kind.

### ✅ Band 8+

> My best friend is incredibly kind and dependable. Whenever I encounter a problem, he's always willing to listen and offer practical advice, which is one of the reasons I trust him so much.

# Extra Band 8+ Follow-up Ideas

### If the examiner asks:
**"Is it easy to make friends nowadays?"**

### 💡 Ideas

- Easier online
- Harder to build deep friendships

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think it's easier than ever to meet new people thanks to social media and online communities. However, building genuine, long-lasting friendships is still challenging because trust and mutual understanding take time to develop.

---

### If the examiner asks:
**"Do friendships change as people get older?"**

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, they often do. As people grow older, they usually become busier with work and family responsibilities, so they may have fewer friends. However, the friendships they maintain tend to be deeper and more meaningful because they're built on trust and shared experiences.
