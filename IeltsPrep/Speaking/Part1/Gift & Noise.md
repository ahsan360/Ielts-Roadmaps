# Questions & Answers

---

# 🟢 Noise

---

## 1. Do you mind noises?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- During study/work
- Quiet place

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do, especially when I'm studying or working. Loud noises make it difficult for me to concentrate, so I prefer a quiet and peaceful environment. That's one of the reasons I enjoy working in libraries or calm cafés.

---

## 2. What types of noise do you come across in your daily life?

### 💡 Ideas

- Traffic
- Horns
- Construction
- People talking

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

The most common noises I hear are traffic, vehicle horns, construction work, and people talking in crowded places. Fortunately, I live in a relatively peaceful area, so these noises aren't too overwhelming most of the time.

---

## 3. Are there any sounds that you like?

### 💡 Ideas

- Rain
- Birds
- Ocean
- Soft music

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I really enjoy natural sounds such as rainfall, birds singing, and waves crashing against the shore. I find them incredibly relaxing, and they help me reduce stress after a busy day.

---

## 4. Where can you hear loud noises?

### 💡 Ideas

- Cities
- Construction sites
- Markets
- Festivals

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Loud noises are quite common in busy city centres, construction sites, markets, and during festivals. These places are usually crowded, so the overall noise level tends to be much higher than in residential areas.

---

## 5. Do you think there's too much noise in modern society?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Urbanisation
- Vehicles
- Technology

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. Rapid urbanisation and the increasing number of vehicles have significantly contributed to noise pollution. In my opinion, excessive noise not only causes stress but can also affect people's physical and mental health.

---

## 6. Are cities becoming noisier?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- More vehicles
- Construction
- Population growth

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Definitely. As cities continue to expand, the number of vehicles, construction projects, and commercial activities also increases. As a result, urban areas are becoming much noisier than they were in the past.

---

# 🟢 Gifts

---

## 7. When do you send gifts?

### 💡 Ideas

- Birthdays
- Weddings
- Festivals
- Graduation

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I usually send gifts on special occasions such as birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, and religious festivals. I believe giving a thoughtful gift is a wonderful way to express love and appreciation.

---

## 8. When was the last time you received a gift?

### 💡 Ideas

- Birthday
- Friend
- Book
- Watch

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

The last gift I received was on my birthday. One of my close friends gave me a book related to software engineering because he knew I enjoy learning new technologies. It was both thoughtful and useful.

---

## 9. Have you received a gift you didn't like?

### 💡 Ideas

- Rarely
- Appreciate effort
- It's the thought

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Not really. Even if a gift isn't something I would personally choose, I still appreciate the effort and kindness behind it. For me, it's the thought that counts rather than the actual value of the gift.

---

## 10. How do you feel when you receive a gift?

### 💡 Ideas

- Happy
- Appreciated
- Grateful

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I always feel happy and grateful because it shows that someone has taken the time to think about me. Regardless of the gift's price, I value the kindness and thoughtfulness behind the gesture.

---

## 11. Do people in your country send gifts to show their generosity?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Festivals
- Weddings
- Hospitality

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, absolutely. In Bangladesh, exchanging gifts is a common tradition during festivals, weddings, birthdays, and family gatherings. It's considered a meaningful way to express generosity, strengthen relationships, and show respect for others.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I don't like loud voice.

✅ I don't like loud noises.

---

❌ Gift is important because expensive.

✅ A thoughtful gift is more meaningful than an expensive one.

---

❌ I receive gift yesterday.

✅ I received a gift yesterday.

---

❌ City become noisy.

✅ Cities are becoming noisier.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

Always explain **why** you feel a certain way.

### ❌ Basic

> I don't like noise.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I don't like excessive noise because it affects my concentration and makes me feel stressed. That's why I prefer spending time in peaceful places whenever possible.
