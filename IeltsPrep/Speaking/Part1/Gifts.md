# IELTS Speaking Band 7+ Sample Answers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part 1: Gifts

## 1. What gift have you received recently?

Recently, I received a useful electronic accessory from a friend on my
birthday. It wasn't particularly expensive, but it was something I
genuinely needed, so I really appreciated the thought behind it.

## 2. Have you ever sent handmade gifts to others?

Yes, I have, although not very often. On one occasion, I prepared a
personalised gift for a close friend because I wanted to give them
something meaningful rather than simply buying an expensive item from a
shop.

## 3. Have you ever received a gift?

Yes, I've received quite a few gifts over the years. Some of the most
memorable ones were from my family and close friends because they were
connected to special occasions or personal memories. I tend to value
sentimental gifts more than expensive ones.

## 4. What do you consider when choosing a gift?

I usually consider the person's interests, personality, and practical
needs. I also think about the occasion and my relationship with that
person. Personally, I believe a thoughtful and well-chosen gift is much
more meaningful than something expensive.
