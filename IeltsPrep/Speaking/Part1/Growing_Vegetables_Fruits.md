# Part 1: Growing Vegetables and Fruits

## 1. Are you interested in growing vegetables and fruits?

Yes, I am. I'm quite interested in growing vegetables and fruits because
I think it's healthy and rewarding. I don't currently have enough space
for a large garden, but I'd like to grow a few plants at home if I get
the opportunity.

## 2. Is growing vegetables popular in your country?

Yes, it is. A lot of people grow vegetables in their backyards,
particularly in rural areas. Some people do it mainly for personal
consumption, while others grow vegetables because they want fresh food
and would rather avoid vegetables that contain too many chemicals.

## 3. Do many people grow vegetables in your city?

Yes, quite a few people do, although it is less common than in rural
areas. Some families grow vegetables in small gardens, balconies, or
rooftop spaces. I think urban gardening is becoming more popular because
people want access to fresh and healthy food.

## 4. Do you think it's easy to grow vegetables?

I'd say it depends on the type of vegetable and the available space.
Some plants are relatively easy to grow, while others require more care,
sunlight, and regular watering. I think anyone can learn the basics if
they are patient and willing to practise.

## 5. Should schools teach students how to grow vegetables?

Yes, absolutely. I think gardening is a practical skill that can teach
children about nature, healthy eating, and responsibility. Schools could
even create small gardens where students could learn how plants grow and
understand where their food comes from.
