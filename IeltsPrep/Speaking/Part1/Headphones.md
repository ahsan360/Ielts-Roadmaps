# Part 1: Headphones

## 1. What type of headphones do you use?

I usually use wireless earbuds because they are compact, convenient, and
easy to carry around. They don't have any cables, so I can use them
comfortably while travelling, exercising, or working.

## 2. When would you use headphones?

I normally use headphones when I'm travelling, working in a noisy
environment, or listening to music at home. They're particularly useful
when I need to concentrate because they help block out background noise.

## 3. In what conditions would you not use headphones?

I avoid using headphones when I'm driving or walking in places where I
need to remain highly aware of my surroundings. I think it's important
to hear traffic, announcements, and other potential hazards in those
situations.

## 4. Do you use headphones every day?

Yes, I use them almost every day. They're an essential part of my daily
routine because I often listen to music, podcasts, online lessons, or
work-related content. However, I try not to use them continuously for
long periods.
