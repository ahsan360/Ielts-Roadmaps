# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. Who was your favourite teacher in high school?

### 💡 Ideas

- Mathematics/English teacher
- Supportive
- Encouraged learning
- Explained clearly

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

My favourite teacher was my mathematics teacher because he explained even the most difficult concepts in a simple and engaging way. He was always patient and encouraged us to think critically instead of memorising formulas. His teaching style inspired me to enjoy problem-solving, which later influenced my interest in software engineering.

---

## 2. What was your favourite subject in high school?

### 💡 Ideas

- Mathematics
- Computer science
- Logical thinking
- Problem-solving

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

My favourite subject was mathematics. I really enjoyed solving logical problems and challenging equations because they improved my analytical thinking. Looking back, I think mathematics laid a strong foundation for my career in software engineering.

> **Alternative:** If you studied computer science in high school, you can mention that instead.

---

## 3. Do you still remember what happened on your first day of high school?

### 💡 Ideas

- Nervous
- New classmates
- Teachers
- Excited

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I remember it quite clearly. I felt a mixture of excitement and nervousness because everything was new, including the teachers, classmates, and classrooms. Fortunately, my classmates were friendly, so I quickly settled in and started enjoying school.

---

## 4. Do you still keep in touch with your friends from high school?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Social media
- Meet occasionally
- Strong friendship

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. Although we're all busy with our careers and studies now, we stay connected through social media and messaging apps. We also meet occasionally whenever everyone is available, especially during holidays or special occasions.

---

## 5. Do you miss your life in high school?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Simple life
- Friends
- Fewer responsibilities

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, to some extent. High school was a memorable period of my life because I spent a lot of time with my friends and had far fewer responsibilities than I do now. Although I enjoy my current life, I sometimes miss the carefree atmosphere and unforgettable memories from those years.

# Band 8+ Tip

For questions about the past, use **past simple** for completed events and **present perfect** for ongoing relationships.

### ❌ Basic

> My teacher was good.

### ✅ Band 8+

> My mathematics teacher was incredibly supportive and patient. He always encouraged us to think independently, and his teaching style inspired me to develop a genuine interest in problem-solving.

# Extra Band 8+ Follow-up Ideas

### If the examiner asks:
**"Should schools focus more on practical skills?"**

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Communication
- Financial literacy
- Technology

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I think they should. While academic knowledge is important, students also need practical skills such as communication, teamwork, financial literacy, and digital skills. These abilities prepare them for real-life challenges and future careers.

---

### If the examiner asks:
**"What's the difference between school life and university life?"**

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think university life offers much more freedom and independence than school life. At university, students are expected to manage their own schedules and take greater responsibility for their learning. In contrast, schools usually provide more structure and guidance from teachers.
