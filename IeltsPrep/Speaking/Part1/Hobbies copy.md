# IELTS Speaking Band 7+ Sample Answers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part 1: Hobbies

## 1. Do you have any hobbies?

Yes, I do. I enjoy several hobbies, but travelling and reading are
probably my favourites. I also like playing sports occasionally because
it helps me stay active and gives me a break from my usual work routine.

## 2. Did you have any hobbies when you were a child?

Yes, I did. When I was a child, I enjoyed playing outdoor games with my
friends, particularly football and badminton. I also liked reading
storybooks, although I probably spent more time playing than reading.

## 3. Do you have a hobby that you've had since childhood?

Yes, reading is probably the hobby I've maintained for the longest time.
I started reading storybooks when I was young, and over the years, my
interests have gradually shifted towards technology, personal
development, and travel-related books and articles.

## 4. Do you have the same hobbies as your family members?

Not exactly. We share a few interests, such as watching films and
travelling, but our individual hobbies are quite different. I tend to
enjoy technology and reading, whereas some of my family members prefer
gardening, cooking, or watching television.
