# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. Do you have any hobbies?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Travelling
- Reading
- Programming
- Learning languages

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. I have several hobbies, but my favourites are travelling, reading books, and programming. I also enjoy learning new technologies and improving my English because I believe these activities help me grow both personally and professionally.

---

## 2. What hobbies are popular in your country?

### 💡 Ideas

- Cricket
- Football
- Watching movies
- Gardening
- Travelling

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In Bangladesh, sports such as cricket and football are extremely popular, especially among young people. Many people also enjoy travelling, gardening, watching films, and spending time on social media during their leisure time.

---

## 3. Did you have different hobbies when you were a child?

### 💡 Ideas

- Outdoor games
- Cycling
- Cricket
- Now more productive hobbies

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, definitely. When I was a child, I spent most of my free time playing cricket, football, and riding my bicycle with friends. As I've grown older, my hobbies have become more productive, such as reading, coding, and travelling.

---

## 4. Is it important for people to have hobbies?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Reduce stress
- Learn skills
- Better mental health

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. I think hobbies are essential because they help people relax, reduce stress, and maintain a healthy work-life balance. In addition, many hobbies allow people to learn new skills and become more creative.

---

## 5. Have your hobbies changed over time?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- More learning
- Career-related

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, they have. As my interests and responsibilities have changed, I've started focusing more on hobbies that contribute to my personal and professional development. For example, I now spend more time learning programming, reading non-fiction books, and practising English than I did a few years ago.

# Common Mistakes

❌ My hobby is play cricket.

✅ My hobby is playing cricket.

---

❌ I like do programming.

✅ I like programming.

---

❌ Hobby help people relax.

✅ Hobbies help people relax.

---

❌ I do hobby every day.

✅ I spend time on my hobbies every day.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

Explain **why** you enjoy a hobby instead of simply naming it.

### ❌ Basic

> I like travelling.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I love travelling because it allows me to experience different cultures, meet new people, and broaden my perspective. Every trip teaches me something valuable and creates unforgettable memories.
