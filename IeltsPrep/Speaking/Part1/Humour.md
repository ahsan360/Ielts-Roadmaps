# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. What type of programmes do you find funny on TV?

### 💡 Ideas

- Sitcoms
- Stand-up comedy
- Comedy shows
- Funny talk shows

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I usually enjoy sitcoms and stand-up comedy shows because they help me relax after a busy day. I particularly like programmes with witty dialogues and clever humour rather than jokes that rely on exaggeration. A well-written comedy can make me laugh while also highlighting everyday situations in a humorous way.

---

## 2. Which types of programmes are most popular in your country?

### 💡 Ideas

- Dramas
- Cricket
- Reality shows
- Comedy

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In Bangladesh, television dramas and reality shows are extremely popular. Sports programmes, especially cricket matches, also attract a huge audience. In recent years, comedy shows and online entertainment have become increasingly popular, particularly among younger viewers.

---

## 3. What kind of things make you laugh?

### 💡 Ideas

- Friends
- Funny videos
- Memes
- Daily life

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Simple things usually make me laugh, such as funny conversations with friends, clever jokes, or humorous videos on social media. I especially enjoy spontaneous humour because it feels more natural than scripted comedy. Sometimes even small misunderstandings can become memorable and amusing moments.

---

## 4. Do you like to make people laugh?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Positive atmosphere
- Friends
- Family

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. I enjoy making people laugh because it creates a friendly and relaxed atmosphere. Whenever my friends or family are feeling stressed, I sometimes tell a funny story or make a light-hearted joke to cheer them up. Seeing other people smile gives me a sense of satisfaction.

✅ I like funny programmes.

---

❌ My friend always make me laugh.

✅ My friend always makes me laugh.

---

❌ I laugh by meme.

✅ I laugh at funny memes.

---

❌ Comedy reduce stress.

✅ Comedy helps reduce stress.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

Don't simply say **"It's funny."**

Explain **why** it's funny and **how** it affects you.

### ❌ Basic

> I like comedy.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I enjoy comedy because it helps me unwind after a stressful day. I particularly appreciate clever jokes that are based on real-life situations rather than exaggerated humour.

# Extra Band 8+ Follow-up

### If the examiner asks:
**"Why is humour important?"**

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think humour plays an essential role in everyday life because it helps people relieve stress and maintain positive relationships. Sharing laughter with friends or colleagues creates a comfortable atmosphere and often makes difficult situations easier to deal with. In my opinion, a good sense of humour is one of the most valuable personal qualities.

---

### If the examiner asks:
**"Can humour solve problems?"**

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Not always, but it can certainly make problems easier to handle. Humour reduces tension during disagreements and helps people stay calm. Although it cannot solve serious issues directly, it often improves communication and encourages people to think more positively.
