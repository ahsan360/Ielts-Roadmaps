## Transportation

### 1. Do you prefer public transportation or private transportation?

#### 💡 Ideas

- Public transport
- Affordable
- Eco-friendly
- Avoid parking

#### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I generally prefer public transportation because it's more affordable and environmentally friendly. It also saves me the hassle of finding parking spaces and dealing with heavy traffic. However, if I need to travel long distances or carry a lot of सामान, I prefer using a private car because it's more comfortable and convenient.

---

### 2. What's the most popular means of transportation in your hometown?

#### 💡 Ideas

- Rickshaws
- Buses
- Motorcycles
- Ride-sharing

#### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In my hometown, buses and rickshaws are probably the most commonly used forms of transportation. Many people also use motorcycles because they're convenient for travelling through busy streets. In recent years, ride-sharing services have become increasingly popular as well.

---

### 3. Is it easy to catch a bus in your country?

#### 💡 Ideas

- Mostly yes
- Cities
- Rural areas harder

#### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, in most cities it's quite easy because buses operate throughout the day. However, during rush hour they're often overcrowded, so passengers sometimes have to wait for the next one. In rural areas, bus services are less frequent, which can make travelling more difficult.

---

### 4. Is driving to work popular in your country?

#### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Middle-class families
- Traffic problems

#### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, many people prefer driving to work, particularly those who own private cars. They appreciate the comfort and flexibility it offers. However, due to traffic congestion in large cities, some people choose public transport or ride-sharing services instead.

---

### 5. What do you think will become the most popular means of transportation in your country?

#### 💡 Ideas

- Electric vehicles
- Metro
- Public transport

#### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I believe electric vehicles and modern public transportation systems, such as metro rail, will become much more popular in the future. People are becoming more environmentally conscious, and governments are investing in cleaner and more efficient transport options.

---

## Indoor Activities

### 6. Do you like indoor activities?

#### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Reading
- Coding
- Movies

#### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. Since I work in the technology field, I naturally enjoy indoor activities such as reading books, programming, and watching educational videos. They help me relax while also improving my knowledge and skills.

---

### 7. What indoor activities do you like?

#### 💡 Ideas

- Reading
- Coding
- Chess
- Cooking

#### 🗣️ Sample Answer

My favourite indoor activities include reading non-fiction books, programming, learning new technologies, and occasionally cooking. I also enjoy playing chess because it improves my concentration and problem-solving skills.

---

### 8. How much time do you spend indoors every week?

#### 💡 Ideas

- Work
- Study
- About 40–50 hours

#### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I spend a considerable amount of time indoors, probably around 40 to 50 hours a week, mainly because of my work and study routine. However, I try to balance this by going for walks or spending time outdoors whenever I have free time.

---

### 9. What types of indoor activities are popular in your country?

#### 💡 Ideas

- Watching TV
- Social media
- Gaming
- Reading
- Gym

#### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Watching television, using social media, playing video games, reading books, and working out at indoor gyms are all quite popular in Bangladesh. Recently, many people have also started taking online courses and learning new skills from home.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I prefer public transport because cheap.

✅ I prefer public transportation because it's affordable.

---

❌ I spend many time indoor.

✅ I spend a lot of time indoors.

---

❌ Bus are crowded.

✅ Buses are often crowded.

---

❌ I like do indoor activities.

✅ I like doing indoor activities.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

Don't just answer **"yes"** or **"no."**

Always explain **why** and give an example.

### ❌ Basic

> I like public transport.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I prefer public transportation because it's economical, environmentally friendly, and allows me to avoid the stress of driving in heavy traffic. I usually take the bus whenever I travel within the city.
