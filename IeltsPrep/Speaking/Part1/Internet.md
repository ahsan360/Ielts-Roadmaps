# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. How important is the Internet to you?

### 💡 Ideas

- Very important
- Work
- Study
- Daily life

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

The Internet is an essential part of my daily life. I use it for work, learning, communication, and entertainment. As a software engineer, I rely on it to access documentation, collaborate with colleagues, and solve technical problems. Without a reliable internet connection, both my productivity and learning would be significantly affected.

---

## 2. Do you use the Internet more for work or in your free time?

### 💡 Ideas

- Mostly work
- Learning
- Entertainment sometimes

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I use the Internet far more for work and learning than for entertainment. Most of my online time is spent reading technical articles, watching programming tutorials, or participating in virtual meetings. In my free time, I occasionally watch YouTube videos or browse social media to relax.

---

## 3. What are your favourite websites?

### 💡 Ideas

- YouTube
- GitHub
- Stack Overflow
- LinkedIn

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

My favourite websites are YouTube, GitHub, and Stack Overflow because they help me improve my technical knowledge. I also use LinkedIn to keep up with industry trends and connect with professionals. Apart from that, I occasionally visit news websites to stay informed about current events.

---

## 4. Do you think you use the Internet too much?

### 💡 Ideas

- Sometimes
- Work requires it
- Try to limit screen time

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

To some extent, yes. Since my work and studies are closely related to technology, I spend several hours online every day. Although it's necessary, I try to maintain a healthy balance by taking regular breaks, exercising, and avoiding unnecessary screen time whenever possible.

---

## 5. How will the Internet develop in the future?

### 💡 Ideas

- Faster
- AI
- VR
- Better connectivity

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- automation
- artificial intelligence
- virtual reality
- smart technology

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I believe the Internet will become much faster, smarter, and more integrated into our everyday lives. Advances in artificial intelligence, virtual reality, and the Internet of Things will transform the way we work, study, and communicate. At the same time, cybersecurity will become increasingly important as more personal information is stored online.

---

## 6. What are the positive and negative things about the Internet?

### 💡 Ideas

- Positive: information, communication, education
- Negative: addiction, fake news, privacy

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- instant access
- misinformation
- online addiction
- data privacy

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

The Internet offers countless benefits, such as instant access to information, online education, and global communication. It has made learning and working much more convenient. However, there are also drawbacks, including misinformation, online scams, privacy concerns, and excessive screen time. In my opinion, the Internet is an incredibly valuable tool, but people need to use it responsibly.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I use internet every day.

✅ I use the Internet every day.

---

❌ Internet help people.

✅ The Internet helps people.

---

❌ I search in Google.

✅ I search on Google.

---

❌ Internet have many advantage.

✅ The Internet has many advantages.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

When discussing the Internet, mention **both advantages and disadvantages** to show balanced thinking.

### ❌ Basic

> The Internet is useful.

### ✅ Band 8+

> The Internet has transformed the way people communicate, learn, and work by providing instant access to information. However, it has also created challenges such as misinformation and online privacy concerns.
