# Part 1: Keys

## 1. Do you always bring a lot of keys with you?

No, I don't. I usually carry only the essential keys, such as my house
key and perhaps a key for my workplace. I prefer keeping things minimal
because carrying too many keys can be inconvenient.

## 2. Have you ever lost your keys?

Yes, I have, although it hasn't happened very often. I remember losing
my house keys once and having to retrace my steps to find them.
Fortunately, I eventually found them, so I didn't need to replace the
lock.

## 3. Do you often forget your keys and lock yourself out?

No, thankfully. I'm generally quite careful about checking that I have
my keys before leaving home. I usually keep them in the same pocket or
place, which makes it much less likely that I'll forget them.

## 4. Do you think it's a good idea to leave keys with a neighbour?

Yes, provided that you completely trust the neighbour. It can be
extremely useful in an emergency, particularly if you lose your keys or
accidentally lock yourself out. However, I'd only do this with someone I
know well and consider reliable.
