# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. What do you do in your free time?

### 💡 Ideas

- Reading
- Programming
- Travelling
- Learning English
- Coffee

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In my free time, I usually read books, practise English, and explore new technologies related to software engineering. I also enjoy travelling whenever I have the opportunity because it helps me relax and experience different cultures. If I only have a short break, I like to make a cup of coffee and watch educational videos.

---

## 2. Do you have a busy social life?

### 💡 Ideas

- Not very
- Close friends
- Family

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I wouldn't say I have a particularly busy social life because I'm usually occupied with work and self-improvement. However, I make time to meet my close friends and spend quality time with my family whenever possible. I prefer meaningful conversations with a small group of people rather than attending large social gatherings.

---

## 3. Do you lead an active life?

### 💡 Ideas

- Trying to
- Walking
- Exercise
- Desk job

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I try to lead an active lifestyle, although my work requires me to spend long hours in front of a computer. To stay healthy, I go for walks, exercise regularly, and try to avoid sitting for extended periods without taking breaks.

---

## 4. Has your life changed much in the last year?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- New goals
- Learning
- Career

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, it has changed quite a lot. Over the past year, I've become much more focused on improving my English and preparing for international career opportunities. I've also become more disciplined with my daily routine and now spend more time learning new technical skills than before.

---

## 5. What would you like to change about your lifestyle?

### 💡 Ideas

- More exercise
- Better sleep
- Less screen time

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I'd like to reduce my screen time and maintain a more consistent exercise routine. Since I work with computers every day, I sometimes spend too many hours sitting. I think improving my work-life balance would have a positive impact on both my physical and mental well-being.

---

## 6. What type of activities do you like to do in your free time?

### 💡 Ideas

- Reading
- Coding
- Travelling
- Photography

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I enjoy activities that are both relaxing and productive. For example, I like reading non-fiction books, learning new programming concepts, travelling to scenic places, and occasionally taking photographs during my trips. These activities help me develop new skills while also allowing me to unwind.

---

## 7. How long have you been interested in these activities?

### 💡 Ideas

- Several years
- University
- Career

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I've been interested in most of these activities for several years. I started programming during my university life, and my passion for travelling developed after I began exploring different parts of Bangladesh. Since then, both hobbies have become an important part of my lifestyle.

---

## 8. Do you like to do these activities alone or with other people? Why?

### 💡 Ideas

- Both
- Reading alone
- Travel with friends

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

It depends on the activity. I prefer reading and studying alone because I can concentrate better without distractions. However, I enjoy travelling with friends or family because sharing experiences and creating memories together makes the trip much more enjoyable.

---

## 9. Do you think people have enough free time?

### 💡 Ideas

- Not really
- Busy jobs
- Technology

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Not always. Many people have demanding jobs and family responsibilities, which leave them with very little leisure time. Although technology has made many tasks more efficient, it has also blurred the boundary between work and personal life. That's why I believe people should consciously make time for relaxation and hobbies.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I have many free times.

✅ I have a lot of free time.

---

❌ I like stay home.

✅ I like staying at home.

---

❌ My life changed a lot last year.

✅ My life has changed quite a lot over the past year.

---

❌ I spend time with my friend.

✅ I spend time with my friends.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

Avoid listing activities without explanation.

### ❌ Basic

> I like reading and travelling.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I enjoy reading because it broadens my knowledge, while travelling allows me to experience different cultures and escape from my daily routine. Together, these activities help me maintain a healthy work-life balance.
