# Part 1: Mirrors

## 1. How often do you look at yourself in the mirror every day?

I probably look at myself in the mirror several times a day, but usually only briefly. I tend to check my appearance before leaving home, particularly my hair and clothes. I might also glance in the mirror before an important meeting or social event to make sure I look presentable.

---

## 2. Do you usually carry a mirror with you?

No, I don't. I rarely feel the need to carry a separate mirror because I can use the front camera on my phone whenever I want to check my appearance. It's much more convenient and means I don't have to carry an extra item.

---

## 3. Have you ever bought a mirror?

Yes, I have. I bought a medium-sized mirror for my bedroom because I wanted to check my appearance before going out. I chose a simple design because I prefer practical things rather than overly decorative ones.

---

## 4. Do you think mirrors are good decorations for a room?

Yes, definitely. Apart from being practical, mirrors can make a room look more spacious and brighter by reflecting light. A large, well-designed mirror can also become an attractive focal point, particularly when it matches the overall interior design.

---

## 5. Did you look in the mirror a lot when you were a child?

Not really. When I was a child, I wasn't particularly concerned about my appearance, so I only used a mirror when necessary. I became more conscious of my appearance as I grew older, especially when I started paying more attention to my hairstyle and clothes.

---

## 6. Where do you usually look in the mirror: at home, work, or elsewhere?

I mostly look in the mirror at home, especially in the bedroom or bathroom. That's usually when I'm getting ready for the day or preparing to go somewhere. Occasionally, I'll check my appearance in a mirror at work or in a restaurant, particularly before a meeting or an important occasion.
