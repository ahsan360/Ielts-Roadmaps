# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. Do you have a mobile phone?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Every day
- Work
- Communication

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. In fact, my smartphone is an essential part of my daily life. I use it not only to communicate with family and friends but also for work, online learning, navigation, and even managing my personal finances. It has become an indispensable tool for both my professional and personal life.

---

## 2. At what age did you first get a mobile phone?

### 💡 Ideas

- Around 15–16
- School
- Parents

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I got my first mobile phone when I was around **15 years old**. Initially, I used it mainly to stay in touch with my parents whenever I was away from home. Compared with today's smartphones, it was very basic and could only make calls and send text messages.

> **Tip:** Replace the age with your real answer if necessary.

---

## 3. What do you mostly use it for?

### 💡 Ideas

- Work
- Programming
- Calls
- Learning
- Maps

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I mostly use my phone for work-related tasks and communication. I frequently check emails, participate in online meetings, use messaging apps, and read technical articles. Apart from work, I also use it for online banking, navigation, and occasionally watching educational videos on YouTube.

---

## 4. Is it a nuisance if people use mobile phones in public places such as trains and buses?

### 💡 Ideas

- Depends
- Loud calls
- Respect others

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

It depends on how they use their phones. I don't mind if someone is quietly texting or reading, but speaking loudly on the phone in a crowded place can be quite distracting and inconsiderate. I believe people should be mindful of others and keep their conversations brief and quiet in public spaces.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I use mobile for many work.

✅ I use my mobile phone for many work-related tasks.

---

❌ I got mobile at 15.

✅ I got my first mobile phone when I was 15 years old.

---

❌ I use phone all day.

✅ I spend a considerable amount of time using my phone each day.

---

❌ People use loudly mobile.

✅ Some people speak loudly on their mobile phones in public.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

Don't simply say **"I use it for everything."**

Explain **how** and **why** you use it.

### ❌ Basic

> I use my phone every day.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I rely on my smartphone for a wide range of tasks, including communication, online learning, navigation, and managing my work schedule. It has become an essential productivity tool in my daily routine.
