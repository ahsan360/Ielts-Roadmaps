# Part 1: Mobile Phone

## 1. What was your first mobile phone?

My first mobile phone was a basic Nokia phone. It was quite simple
compared with modern smartphones, and I mainly used it for making calls
and sending text messages. At that time, I didn't need many advanced
features.

## 2. Do you often use your mobile phone for texting or calling?

I use my phone for both, but I probably text more often than I make
calls. Messaging is more convenient when I'm busy because I can reply
whenever I have time. I usually make calls when something needs to be
discussed quickly.

## 3. Will you buy a new phone in the future?

Yes, but probably not immediately. I usually replace my phone when my
current one starts causing problems or becomes too slow. I don't think
it's necessary to buy a new phone simply because a newer model has been
released.

## 4. How has your mobile phone changed your life?

It has changed my life quite significantly. Nowadays, I use my phone for
communication, navigation, online payments, learning, entertainment, and
staying informed. It's become an essential part of my daily routine,
although I try to avoid spending too much time on it.
