# Part 1: Morning Time

## 1. Do you like getting up early in the morning?

Not really. I can get up early when I have something important to do,
but I generally prefer sleeping a little longer and starting my day
later. I feel more energetic when I get enough sleep.

## 2. What do you usually do in the morning?

I usually start my morning with a cup of coffee, check the news, and get
ready for the day. If I have enough time, I also try to do some light
exercise because it helps me feel more active.

## 3. What did you do in the mornings when you were little? Why?

When I was a child, I usually woke up early and spent a couple of hours
studying before going to school. My parents encouraged me to study in
the morning because they believed I could concentrate better at that
time.

## 4. Are there any differences between your mornings now and in the past?

Yes, there are some noticeable differences. When I was younger, I had a
fixed routine and usually followed my parents' schedule. Nowadays, I
have more freedom, so I can decide when to start my day and usually
spend more time checking the news or planning my work.

## 5. Do you spend your mornings doing the same things on weekends and weekdays? Why?

No, not really. On weekdays, I usually have a fairly structured morning
because I need to work. At weekends, I tend to wake up later and take
things more slowly. I might watch a film, have a relaxed breakfast, or
simply enjoy some quiet time.
