# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. How often do you go to the cinema?

### 💡 Ideas

- Not often
- Streaming services
- Special occasions

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I don't go to the cinema very often these days because streaming platforms are much more convenient. However, I still enjoy watching blockbuster films on the big screen, especially when they have impressive visual effects or excellent sound design. I probably visit the cinema three or four times a year.

---

## 2. Are cinema tickets expensive in your country?

### 💡 Ideas

- Moderate
- Affordable for some
- Premium screens cost more

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I would say cinema tickets are reasonably priced, although they can be quite expensive for students or people with a limited budget. Premium cinemas with larger screens and better sound systems usually charge higher prices, but many people are willing to pay for the improved viewing experience.

---

## 3. What are the advantages of seeing a film at the cinema?

### 💡 Ideas

- Big screen
- Better sound
- Atmosphere
- Friends

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

The biggest advantage is the immersive cinematic experience. Watching a film on a huge screen with high-quality surround sound makes it far more enjoyable than watching it at home. I also think going to the cinema with friends or family makes the experience more memorable because everyone shares the excitement together.

---

## 4. Do you usually watch films alone or with others?

### 💡 Ideas

- Mostly alone
- Friends for action films
- Family sometimes

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

It depends on the type of film. I usually watch documentaries and educational films on my own because I like to concentrate without distractions. However, I prefer watching action films or comedies with friends or family since it's much more enjoyable to share reactions and discuss the story afterwards.

---

## 5. Which actor would you like to play you in a film?

### 💡 Ideas

- Shah Rukh Khan
- Leonardo DiCaprio
- Natural acting
- Intelligent roles

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

If I had to choose, I'd probably pick **Leonardo DiCaprio** because I admire his versatility and the depth he brings to every role. He has portrayed a wide range of complex characters, and I think he could realistically represent someone who is ambitious, curious, and passionate about technology.

> **Tip:** Replace the actor with your personal favourite if you wish.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I like watch movie.

✅ I like watching movies.

---

❌ Cinema is more better.

✅ The cinema offers a better viewing experience.

---

❌ I watch movie in Netflix.

✅ I watch movies on Netflix.

---

❌ He is my favourite actor because he act good.

✅ He is my favourite actor because he acts exceptionally well.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

Don't only say that a film is **"good"** or **"interesting."**

Explain **why**.

### ❌ Basic

> I like action movies.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I enjoy action movies because they usually have fast-paced storylines, impressive visual effects, and exciting fight scenes that keep me engaged from beginning to end.
