# Questions & Answers

---

## 1. How do you listen to music?

### 💡 Ideas

- Smartphone
- Spotify
- YouTube Music
- Earphones

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I usually listen to music on my smartphone using streaming platforms like Spotify or YouTube Music. Most of the time, I use wireless earphones because they're convenient, especially when I'm travelling or exercising. Occasionally, I also play music through a Bluetooth speaker when I'm relaxing at home.

---

## 2. When do you listen to music?

### 💡 Ideas

- Travelling
- Exercising
- Relaxing
- Not while working

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I mainly listen to music when I'm travelling, exercising, or simply relaxing after a busy day. However, I rarely listen to music while working because I find it distracting and prefer to concentrate fully on my tasks.

---

## 3. What's your favourite kind of music?

### 💡 Ideas

- Soft
- Instrumental
- Acoustic
- Motivational

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

My favourite type of music is soft instrumental and acoustic music because it's calming and helps me unwind. I also enjoy motivational songs with meaningful lyrics, especially when I need a boost of energy or inspiration.

---

## 4. Is music an important subject at school in your country?

### 💡 Ideas

- Some schools
- Not a core subject
- Creativity

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Music is taught in some schools, but it's generally not considered a core academic subject in Bangladesh. I think it deserves more attention because learning music can enhance creativity, improve concentration, and help students express their emotions more effectively.

---

## 5. What kinds of music are most popular in your country?

### 💡 Ideas

- Bengali songs
- Folk
- Pop
- Modern

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Bengali pop songs, folk music, and modern romantic songs are extremely popular in Bangladesh. Traditional genres such as **Baul** and **Rabindra Sangeet** are also highly respected because they reflect the country's rich cultural heritage. In recent years, international pop music has become increasingly popular among younger people.

---

## 6. Do you like to listen to live music?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Better atmosphere
- Energy

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. Live music creates a unique atmosphere that recordings simply can't replicate. Watching talented musicians perform in person allows the audience to feel more connected to both the performers and the music.

---

## 7. Is live music popular in your country?

### 💡 Ideas

- Festivals
- Concerts
- University events

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, especially in major cities. Live concerts are often organised during national celebrations, cultural festivals, and university events. Many young people enjoy attending these performances because they offer both entertainment and a chance to socialise with friends.

---

## 8. Have you ever been to a concert or musical performance?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- University
- Enjoyed atmosphere

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I've attended a few concerts, mostly during university events and cultural festivals. I really enjoyed the lively atmosphere and the opportunity to hear talented musicians perform live. I think it's a completely different experience from simply listening to recorded music.

---

## 9. How much time do you spend listening to music every day?

### 💡 Ideas

- 30–60 minutes
- Relaxation

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I probably spend around half an hour to an hour listening to music each day. It mainly depends on my workload. On busy days, I only listen while commuting, whereas on weekends I have more time to explore new songs and artists.

---

## 10. Are your music tastes varied?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Different genres
- Depends on mood

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, my musical taste is quite diverse. Although I mainly enjoy soft instrumental and acoustic music, I also listen to pop, classical, and motivational songs depending on my mood. I believe listening to different genres keeps my playlist fresh and interesting.

---

## 11. What is your favourite song?

### 💡 Ideas

- Personal favourite

- Emotion

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

One of my favourite songs is **[replace with your favourite song]** because its lyrics are meaningful and the melody is very relaxing. I never get tired of listening to it, and it always helps me feel calm after a stressful day.

## 12. Do you like to sing along to your favourite songs?

### 💡 Ideas

- Sometimes
- Alone
- Relaxing

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, occasionally. Although I'm not a very good singer, I sometimes sing along when I'm alone or travelling. It's a fun way to relax and enjoy the music without worrying about how I sound.

## 13. Are you learning to play a musical instrument at the moment?

### 💡 Ideas

Not at the moment because I'm currently focused on my career and language learning. However, if I had more free time, I'd love to learn the guitar because it's a versatile instrument and relatively easy for beginners to start with.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I listen music every day.

✅ I listen **to** music every day.

---

❌ I like hear music.

✅ I like listening to music.

---

❌ Music make me relax.

✅ Music helps me relax.

---

❌ I don't know play guitar.

✅ I don't know how to play the guitar.

---

# Band 8+ Tip

Don't simply name a music genre.

Explain **why** you like it and **when** you listen to it.

### ❌ Basic

> I like pop music.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I enjoy pop music because it usually has catchy melodies and uplifting rhythms. I often listen to it while travelling or exercising because it keeps me motivated and improves my mood.
