# 1. Do you know the people who live next door to you?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Friendly relationship
- Help each other

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I know my neighbours quite well. We've lived in the same neighbourhood for many years, so we've built a friendly relationship over time. Although we're not extremely close, we always greet each other warmly and are willing to help whenever someone needs assistance.

---

# 2. How often do you see each other?

## 💡 Ideas

- Daily
- Outside home
- Weekends

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I usually see my neighbours almost every day, especially when leaving for work or returning home. Sometimes we stop for a short conversation, particularly during weekends or community events.

---

# 3. What kind of relationship do you have?

## 💡 Ideas

- Respectful
- Friendly
- Helpful

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I'd describe our relationship as friendly and respectful. We respect each other's privacy but are always willing to support one another. I think maintaining good relationships with neighbours contributes to a peaceful living environment.

---

# 4. How can neighbours be helpful?

## 💡 Ideas

- Emergencies
- Looking after house
- Sharing information

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Neighbours can be extremely helpful in many situations. For example, they can look after your house while you're away, receive deliveries on your behalf, or offer assistance during emergencies. Strong community relationships make neighbourhoods safer and more enjoyable places to live.

---

# 5. What kind of problems can people have with their neighbours in a big city?

## 💡 Ideas

- Noise
- Parking
- Privacy
- Pets

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

The most common problems include excessive noise, parking disputes, littering, and a lack of privacy. In large cities, people often lead busy lives, which can reduce communication and increase misunderstandings. Fortunately, most conflicts can be resolved through respectful discussion and mutual understanding.

# Common Mistakes

❌ My neighbour is very helping.

✅ My neighbour is very **helpful**.

---

❌ We have good relation.

✅ We have a good **relationship**.

---

❌ They always helps me.

✅ They always **help** me.

---

❌ Neighbours should helping each other.

✅ Neighbours should **help** each other.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Instead of saying:

❌ "Neighbours help each other."

Say:

✅ "Good neighbours create a strong sense of community by supporting one another during emergencies, respecting each other's privacy, and maintaining friendly communication."
