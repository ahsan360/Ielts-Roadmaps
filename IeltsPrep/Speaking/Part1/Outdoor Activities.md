# 1. Do you like outdoor activities?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Nature
- Relaxing
- Healthy

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I enjoy outdoor activities because they allow me to relax and escape from my daily routine. Spending time in nature helps reduce stress and improves both my physical and mental well-being. I particularly enjoy walking in parks and exploring scenic places whenever I have free time.

---

# 2. What outdoor sports do you like?

## 💡 Ideas

- Football
- Cricket
- Cycling
- Hiking

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I enjoy playing football and cycling because they're both enjoyable and beneficial for my health. Football helps improve teamwork and fitness, while cycling is an excellent way to stay active and explore different places at the same time.

---

# 3. How much time do you spend outdoors every week?

## 💡 Ideas

- 5–7 hours
- Walking
- Exercise

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

On average, I spend around five to seven hours outdoors each week. Most of that time is spent walking, exercising, or meeting friends. Whenever my schedule allows, I also like visiting parks or other peaceful outdoor locations.

---

# 4. What types of outdoor activities are popular in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- Cricket
- Football
- Picnics
- Boating
- Hiking

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In Bangladesh, people enjoy a wide variety of outdoor activities. Cricket and football are extremely popular, especially among young people. Families also enjoy picnics during the winter season, while many people visit beaches, tea gardens, or rivers for sightseeing and boating.

---

# 5. Why are outdoor activities important?

## 💡 Ideas

- Health
- Reduce stress
- Social interaction
- Nature

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Outdoor activities are important because they promote a healthy lifestyle and encourage people to stay physically active. They also improve mental health by reducing stress and providing opportunities to connect with nature. In addition, many outdoor activities strengthen friendships and family relationships through shared experiences.

---

# 6. Do you think people spend enough time outdoors nowadays?

## 💡 Ideas

- No
- Technology
- Busy lifestyle
- More screen time

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

No, I don't think so. Many people spend most of their time indoors because of work, studies, or excessive use of smartphones and computers. As a result, they often miss the physical and psychological benefits of spending time outside. I believe people should make outdoor activities a regular part of their routine.

---

# 7. Should schools encourage more outdoor activities?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Teamwork
- Health
- Confidence

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Outdoor activities help students develop teamwork, leadership, and communication skills while improving their physical fitness. They also provide a healthy break from classroom learning and encourage children to become more active and socially engaged.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I like do outdoor activities.

✅ I like **doing** outdoor activities.

---

❌ Outdoor activity make people healthy.

✅ Outdoor activities help people stay healthy.

---

❌ I go outside for fresh airs.

✅ I go outside for **fresh air**.

---

❌ Playing outdoor is important.

✅ Playing **outdoors** is important.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Don't just list activities.

Explain **why** you enjoy them and **what benefits** they provide.

### ❌ Basic

> I like football.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I enjoy playing football because it improves my fitness, teaches teamwork, and provides an excellent opportunity to socialise with friends while staying physically active.
