# IELTS Speaking Band 7+ Sample Answers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part 1: Outer Space and Stars

## 1. Have you ever learnt about outer space and stars?

Yes, I have. I learnt some basic information about the solar system and
stars at school, and later I became interested in the subject through
documentaries and online videos. I find it fascinating to think about
how enormous the universe is.

## 2. Do you like science fiction movies?

Yes, I do. I particularly enjoy science fiction films that explore space
travel, advanced technology, and the possibility of life on other
planets. I like them because they combine entertainment with
thought-provoking ideas about the future.

## 3. Do you want to know more about outer space?

Absolutely. There is still so much that we don't understand about the
universe, including black holes, distant galaxies, and the possibility
of extraterrestrial life. I'd love to learn more about these subjects
because they are both mysterious and intellectually fascinating.

## 4. Do you want to go into deep space in the future?

If it were completely safe and realistically possible, I'd love to
experience space travel. However, I don't think I'd want to take part in
a dangerous mission. Even a short journey into space would be an
extraordinary experience and something I'd probably remember for the
rest of my life.
