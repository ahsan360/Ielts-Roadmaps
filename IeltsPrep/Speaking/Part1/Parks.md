# Part 1: Parks

## 1. Did you like going to parks as a child?

Yes, I really enjoyed going to parks when I was a child. I often went
there with my friends or family, and we used to play games, ride
bicycles, and simply spend time outdoors. I think those visits were a
great way to stay active and have fun.

## 2. Do you still like going to parks now?

Yes, I do, although I don't visit them as frequently as I did when I was
younger. Nowadays, I mainly go to parks when I want to relax, take a
walk, or get some fresh air. I find spending time in a peaceful green
space quite refreshing, especially after a busy day.

## 3. Would you like to see more parks in your city?

Definitely. I think my city would benefit from having more public parks
and green spaces. As urban areas become increasingly crowded, people
need somewhere to escape from traffic, noise, and pollution. More parks
would also encourage people to exercise and spend more time outdoors.

## 4. Are there any parks you want to go to in the future?

Yes, I'd love to visit some of the large and well-designed parks in
other countries. In particular, I'd like to explore parks that combine
beautiful landscapes with recreational facilities. I think it would be
interesting to see how other cities create green spaces for their
residents.
