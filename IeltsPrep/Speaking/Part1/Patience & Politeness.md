# 1. What do you think patience is?

## 💡 Ideas

- Staying calm
- Waiting
- Self-control

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In my opinion, patience is the ability to stay calm and composed when facing delays, difficulties, or stressful situations. It involves controlling your emotions instead of reacting impulsively. I believe patience is an essential quality in both personal and professional life.

---

# 2. Do you think patience is important?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Better decisions
- Relationships
- Career

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Patient people usually make better decisions because they think carefully before acting. Patience also helps build stronger relationships, as it encourages understanding, empathy, and effective communication. In the workplace, it is particularly valuable when solving complex problems.

---

# 3. Do you think you are a patient person?

## 💡 Ideas

- Mostly yes
- Sometimes impatient
- Learning

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I would say I'm fairly patient, although no one is patient all the time. I can remain calm when solving technical problems or learning new skills, but I occasionally become impatient if someone is repeatedly irresponsible or wastes other people's time.

---

# 4. Have you ever lost your patience?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Heavy traffic
- Delays
- Learned lesson

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, of course. I remember becoming frustrated after waiting for several hours because of unexpected traffic congestion. However, that experience taught me that losing my temper doesn't solve problems, so I now try to stay calm and focus on finding practical solutions.

---

# 5. Are you a polite person?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Respect
- Greetings
- Listening

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I try my best to be polite in my daily interactions. I believe showing respect through simple actions, such as greeting people warmly, listening carefully, and saying "please" and "thank you," creates a positive atmosphere and strengthens relationships.

---

# 6. Who taught you to be polite?

## 💡 Ideas

- Parents
- Teachers
- Society

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

My parents played the biggest role in teaching me good manners. From an early age, they encouraged me to respect others regardless of their age or background. My teachers also reinforced these values by emphasising discipline and courteous behaviour at school.

---

# 7. Is it important to be polite?

## 💡 Ideas

- Essential
- Respect
- Professional success
- Trust

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I think politeness is extremely important because it promotes mutual respect and makes communication more effective. Whether in the workplace or in everyday life, polite behaviour helps build trust, avoid unnecessary conflicts, and leave a positive impression on others.

---

# 8. What do you do if others are not polite to you?

## 💡 Ideas

- Stay calm
- Respectful
- Ignore
- Avoid argument

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I try to remain calm and avoid responding with the same behaviour. If the situation is serious, I politely explain my point of view. In many cases, staying respectful helps prevent the disagreement from becoming even more stressful.

---

# 9. Why are some people becoming less patient nowadays?

## 💡 Ideas

- Technology
- Instant results
- Busy lives
- Stress

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think modern technology has made people expect immediate results. Fast internet, online shopping, and instant messaging have reduced people's willingness to wait. In addition, busy lifestyles and workplace pressure often increase stress, making people more impatient.

---

# 10. Can patience be learned?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Practice
- Experience
- Self-awareness

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Definitely. Patience is a skill that can be developed through practice and self-awareness. For example, mindfulness, time management, and learning to control emotions can gradually help people become more patient in challenging situations.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I lose my patient.

✅ I lose my **patience**.

---

❌ He is very politely.

✅ He is very **polite**.

---

❌ Patience help people.

✅ Patience **helps** people.

---

❌ I always keeping calm.

✅ I always **keep** calm.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Don't simply say:

❌ "Being polite is good."

Instead explain the wider impact.

### ✅ Band 8+

> "Politeness improves communication, builds trust, and reduces unnecessary conflict. In both professional and personal life, respectful behaviour often leads to stronger and more productive relationships."
