# Part 1: Pets and Animals

## 1. What's your favourite animal? Why?

My favourite animal is probably the dog. I like dogs because they are
loyal, intelligent, and excellent companions. They can also form a
strong bond with their owners, which I find quite special.

## 2. Where do you prefer to keep your pet, indoors or outdoors?

I'd prefer to keep a pet indoors, at least most of the time. I think
pets are safer and more comfortable when they live with their owners.
However, they should also have enough outdoor space to exercise and
play.

## 3. Have you ever had a pet before?

Yes, I have. I had a dog when I was younger, and it was a memorable part
of my childhood. I enjoyed playing with it and taking care of it, and I
think having a pet taught me to be more responsible.

## 4. What is the most popular animal in India?

I think dogs and cows are among the most familiar and popular animals in
India. Cows have particular cultural and religious significance in many
parts of the country, while dogs are also very common as pets and
companions.
