# Part 1: Public Parks

## 1. Are there many public parks in your country or city?

Yes, there are quite a few public parks in Bangladesh, particularly in major cities. However, I think the number of well-maintained parks is still insufficient compared with the growing population. In urban areas, people need more green spaces where they can relax and spend time outdoors.

---

## 2. How often do you visit a park?

I don't visit parks very frequently, but I try to go whenever I have some free time. I usually visit one once or twice a month, particularly at weekends. It's a nice way to take a break from work and enjoy some fresh air.

---

## 3. What do you usually do when you visit a park?

I usually take a leisurely walk and spend some time enjoying the surroundings. Sometimes I sit on a bench, listen to music, or have a conversation with friends. If I'm feeling stressed, simply being surrounded by trees and fresh air helps me relax and clear my mind.

---

## 4. Did you often go to parks when you were a child?

Yes, I did. I used to visit parks quite regularly with my family, especially during weekends and school holidays. I particularly enjoyed playing with other children and exploring the different areas of the park. Those visits are still some of my fondest childhood memories.

---

## 5. Do you think parks are important for a city? Why?

Absolutely. Parks are essential because they provide much-needed green spaces in densely populated urban areas. They improve the quality of the environment, encourage people to exercise, and provide a peaceful place where residents can relax. They also give children a safe environment to play outdoors.

---

## 6. How are the parks today different from the ones you visited as a child?

I think modern parks tend to have better facilities and more organised recreational areas. Many now have walking paths, playgrounds, exercise equipment, and designated spaces for children. However, some older parks felt more natural and less commercialised, whereas today's parks are often more structured and crowded.
