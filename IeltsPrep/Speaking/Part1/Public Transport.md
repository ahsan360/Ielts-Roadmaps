# IELTS Speaking Part 3
# Topic: Public Transport
**Target Band:** 7.5–8.5

---

# Band 8–9 Vocabulary

| Vocabulary | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| public transportation | buses, trains, metros, ferries, etc. |
| commuter | a person who regularly travels to work or school |
| traffic congestion | heavy traffic that slows movement |
| fare | the amount paid for a journey |
| mass transit system | a transport system serving many people |
| infrastructure | roads, railways, bridges, and transport facilities |
| environmentally friendly | causing little harm to the environment |
| carbon emissions | gases released by vehicles that contribute to pollution |
| rush hour | the busiest travel times of the day |
| accessibility | how easy it is for everyone to use a service |
| punctuality | arriving or departing on time |
| sustainable transport | transport that has a low environmental impact |

# 1. What kinds of public transport do you have in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- Buses
- Trains
- Rickshaws
- CNGs
- Metro
- Ferries

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Bangladesh has a wide range of public transportation, including buses, trains, ferries, CNG auto-rickshaws, and the recently introduced metro rail system in Dhaka. Each mode of transport serves different needs depending on the distance and location.

---

# 2. What kinds of public transport do most people use?

## 💡 Ideas

- Bus
- Rickshaw
- Metro

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Buses are still the most commonly used form of public transport because they are affordable and operate across most cities. In urban areas, many people also use rickshaws and the metro rail, while trains are popular for long-distance travel.

---

# 3. What is your favourite type of public transport?

## 💡 Ideas

- Metro
- Fast
- Comfortable
- Reliable

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

My favourite form of public transport is the metro because it is fast, punctual, and comfortable. Unlike buses, it is not affected by heavy traffic congestion, which makes travelling much more convenient, especially during rush hour.

---

# 4. What do you do while travelling on public transport?

## 💡 Ideas

- Read
- Listen to podcasts
- Check emails
- Observe people

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

During my journey, I usually read news articles, reply to emails, or listen to educational podcasts. Sometimes I simply look out of the window or observe people's daily routines, which I find quite interesting.

---

# 5. How could public transport in your country be improved?

## 💡 Ideas

- More buses
- Better roads
- Cleaner vehicles
- Digital tickets
- Better safety

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think the government should invest more in modern infrastructure by increasing the number of buses and trains, improving road conditions, and expanding metro services. Better punctuality, cleaner vehicles, and digital ticketing systems would also make public transportation more efficient and attractive.

---

# 6. Why should people use public transport instead of private cars?

## 💡 Ideas

- Reduce pollution
- Less traffic
- Save money

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Using public transportation helps reduce traffic congestion, lowers carbon emissions, and saves fuel costs. If more people relied on buses or trains, cities would become cleaner, quieter, and more environmentally sustainable.

---

# 7. What problems do people face when using public transport?

## 💡 Ideas

- Crowding
- Delays
- Heat
- Poor service

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Passengers often experience overcrowding, delays, and limited seating, especially during peak hours. In some areas, poor road conditions and insufficient transport services make commuting more stressful than it should be.

---

# 8. Will public transport become more important in the future?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Population growth
- Environment

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, absolutely. As cities continue to grow, efficient public transportation will become increasingly important. Governments around the world are investing in sustainable transport systems to reduce pollution and accommodate growing populations.

# Common Mistakes

❌ People uses buses.

✅ People **use** buses.

---

❌ Bus is cheaper than cars.

✅ Buses are generally more affordable than private cars.

---

❌ Public transport reduce pollution.

✅ Public transport **reduces** pollution.

---

❌ Metro is very fastly.

✅ The metro is very **fast**.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Avoid giving only one reason.

### ❌ Basic

> Public transport is good.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Public transport is beneficial because it reduces traffic congestion, lowers carbon emissions, and provides an affordable travel option for people from different economic backgrounds.
