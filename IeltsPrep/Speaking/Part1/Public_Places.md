# IELTS Speaking Band 7+ Sample Answers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part 1: Public Places

## 1. Have you ever talked to someone you don't know in public places?

Yes, many times. I often have short conversations with strangers when
I'm travelling on public transport. I've also occasionally spoken to new
people in parks, shopping malls, or other public places. Usually, it's a
simple conversation, such as asking for directions or discussing
something around us.

## 2. Do you wear headphones in public places?

Not very often. I don't particularly enjoy wearing headphones all the
time because I prefer to stay aware of my surroundings. However, I
sometimes use them when I'm travelling or when I want to listen to music
or watch something.

## 3. Would you like to see more public places near where you live?

Yes, absolutely. I'd like to have more parks, walking areas, and
community spaces near my home. These places would give people somewhere
to relax, exercise, and spend time with their families. I think they
would also make the neighbourhood more pleasant.

## 4. Do you often go to public places with your friends?

Not very often. I usually prefer meeting my friends at restaurants,
cafés, or someone's home rather than spending time in crowded public
places. However, we sometimes go to parks or shopping malls when we want
to spend a few hours together.
