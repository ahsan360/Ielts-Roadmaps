# IELTS Speaking Band 7+ Sample Answers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part 1: Reading

## 1. Do you like reading?

Yes, I do. I enjoy reading because it helps me switch off from screens
and expand my knowledge at the same time. I particularly like reading
books and articles about technology, personal development, and travel.

## 2. Do you prefer to read on paper or on a screen?

I generally prefer reading on paper because I find it easier on my eyes
and more comfortable for long periods. However, I often read on a screen
when I'm researching something because digital content is much more
convenient and accessible.

## 3. When do you need to read carefully, and when not?

I need to read particularly carefully when I'm dealing with technical
documentation, contracts, instructions, or important work-related
information. On the other hand, when I'm reading the news or browsing
social media, I usually skim the content unless something catches my
attention.

## 4. Do you prefer scanning or detailed reading?

It depends on the purpose. If I'm looking for a particular piece of
information, I prefer scanning because it's much faster. However, when
I'm studying a new subject or trying to understand a complicated idea, I
prefer detailed reading so that I don't miss any important information.
