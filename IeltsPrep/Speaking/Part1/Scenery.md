# IELTS Speaking Band 7+ Sample Answers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part 1: Scenery

## 1. Do you look out the window at the scenery when travelling by bus or car?

Yes, I usually do. Looking out of the window makes long journeys more
enjoyable, especially when I'm travelling through rural areas or places
with beautiful landscapes. It also gives me a chance to relax and take a
break from using my phone.

## 2. Do you prefer the mountains or the sea?

Personally, I prefer the mountains because I find them more peaceful and
refreshing. I enjoy the cool weather, green landscapes, and sense of
tranquillity that mountainous areas often provide. That said, I also
enjoy visiting the sea occasionally.

## 3. Do you like to take scenery pictures?

Yes, definitely. Whenever I visit a beautiful place, I like taking
photographs so that I can preserve the memory of the trip. I also enjoy
sharing particularly impressive landscapes with my friends and family.

## 4. What are the most beautiful sights you have seen while travelling?

One of the most beautiful sights I've seen was a beach at sunset. The
combination of the colourful sky, the sound of the waves, and the
reflection of the sunlight on the water created a breathtaking scene. It
was one of those moments that I wanted to capture and remember for a
long time.
