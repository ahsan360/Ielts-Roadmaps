# IELTS Speaking Band 7+ Sample Answers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part 1: Science

## 1. Do you like science?

Yes, I do. I find science fascinating because it helps us understand how
the world works and explains many things that we encounter in everyday
life. I'm particularly interested in areas that have practical
applications.

## 2. When did you start to learn about science?

I started learning about science in primary school. At first, I studied
basic topics such as plants, animals, the human body, and the
environment. As I got older, the subjects became more detailed and
interesting.

## 3. Which science subject is interesting to you?

I'm particularly interested in biology because it helps explain how
living organisms function. I find topics related to the human body,
genetics, and health especially fascinating because they have a direct
connection with our everyday lives.

## 4. What interesting things have you done with science?

Recently, I've become interested in urban gardening, so I've
experimented with growing a few plants at home. I've tried to understand
how sunlight, soil, and watering affect their growth. It's been a simple
but surprisingly educational experience.

## 5. Do you like watching science TV programmes?

Yes, occasionally. I particularly enjoy well-produced documentaries
because they can explain complicated scientific ideas in a simple and
engaging way. I find programmes about space, technology, and nature
especially interesting.

## 6. Do Indian people often visit science museums?

I imagine science museums are reasonably popular, particularly among
schoolchildren and families. They provide an interactive way to learn
about scientific concepts, so they can be more engaging than simply
studying them from textbooks.
