# 1. What is your favourite season?

## 💡 Ideas

- Winter
- Comfortable
- Outdoor activities

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

My favourite season is winter because the weather is cool and pleasant. It is much easier to concentrate on work and studies without the discomfort of excessive heat. I also enjoy travelling, having picnics, and spending time outdoors during this season.

---

# 2. Tell me about the different seasons in your country.

## 💡 Ideas

- Six seasons
- Summer
- Monsoon
- Winter

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Traditionally, Bangladesh has six seasons, although nowadays the most noticeable ones are summer, the monsoon, and winter. Summers are usually hot and humid, the monsoon brings heavy rainfall, and winter is relatively short but quite pleasant.

---

# 3. How do the clothes people wear change with the seasons?

## 💡 Ideas

- Cotton
- Light clothes
- Sweaters
- Raincoats

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People generally wear lightweight cotton clothing during the hot summer months to stay comfortable. During winter, they switch to jackets, sweaters, and warmer fabrics. In the rainy season, umbrellas and waterproof clothing become essential.

---

# 4. Do any types of jobs change with the seasons?

## 💡 Ideas

- Farming
- Tourism
- Construction

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, agriculture is heavily influenced by seasonal changes because farmers plant and harvest crops at different times of the year. Tourism and construction work are also affected, as favourable weather conditions usually increase productivity and attract more visitors.

---

# 5. Is tourism popular in a particular season in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- Winter
- Beaches
- Tea gardens

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, winter is generally the most popular season for tourism in Bangladesh. The pleasant weather encourages people to visit destinations such as Cox's Bazar, Sylhet, and the Sundarbans. Many families also organise picnics and outdoor gatherings during this time.

---

# 6. Which season is most suitable for work or study?

## 💡 Ideas

- Winter
- Comfortable
- Better concentration

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In my opinion, winter is the best season for work and study because the cooler temperatures make it easier to concentrate for long periods. During the hot summer months, people often feel tired and less productive due to the heat and humidity.

---

# 7. How do seasons affect people's lifestyles?

## 💡 Ideas

- Clothing
- Food
- Travel
- Activities

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Seasons influence many aspects of daily life, including clothing, food preferences, and recreational activities. For example, people tend to travel more during winter, while they often stay indoors during periods of heavy rainfall. Seasonal changes also affect people's moods and daily routines.

---

# 8. Do you think climate change is affecting the seasons?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Extreme weather
- Unpredictable

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I believe climate change is making seasonal weather more unpredictable. Heatwaves are becoming more intense, rainfall patterns are changing, and natural disasters are occurring more frequently. These changes have significant effects on agriculture, tourism, and people's everyday lives.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I like winter because less hot.

✅ I like winter because it is much cooler.

---

❌ Bangladesh have six season.

✅ Bangladesh has six seasons.

---

❌ Summer is too much hot.

✅ Summer is extremely hot.

---

❌ Winter help people work.

✅ Winter helps people work more efficiently.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Go beyond describing the weather. Explain **how the season affects people's lives, work, tourism, agriculture, or health**.

### ❌ Basic

> I like winter because it is cold.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I prefer winter because the pleasant weather improves my concentration, allows me to spend more time outdoors, and makes travelling far more enjoyable.
