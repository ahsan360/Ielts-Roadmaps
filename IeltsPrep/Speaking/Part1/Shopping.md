# Part 1: Shopping

## 1. How often do you go shopping?

I wouldn't say I'm a frequent shopper. I usually go grocery shopping
once or twice a month, while I buy clothes or other personal items only
when I actually need them. I'm not particularly interested in spending
hours browsing through shops.

## 2. Do you prefer online shopping or in-store shopping?

I generally prefer online shopping because it's convenient and saves a
considerable amount of time. I can compare prices, read customer
reviews, and order things from the comfort of my home. However, for
clothes or shoes, I sometimes prefer shopping in-store because I can
check the quality and size before purchasing them.

## 3. Have you ever returned anything you bought online?

Yes, I have. Once, I ordered a piece of clothing online, but the size
wasn't suitable for me, so I had to return it. The process was
relatively straightforward, although it was slightly inconvenient
because I had to wait for the replacement.

## 4. Do you like shopping?

To be honest, I'm not particularly enthusiastic about shopping. I don't
mind it when I need to buy something specific, but I wouldn't consider
it a hobby. I'd rather spend my free time doing something productive or
relaxing.
