# Part 1: Singing

## 1. Do you like singing? Why?

Yes, I do, although I'm not a particularly talented singer. I usually
sing when I'm alone or travelling because it helps me relax and improves
my mood. I think singing is a simple way to enjoy myself without needing
any special equipment.

## 2. Have you ever learnt how to sing?

No, I've never had formal singing lessons. I mostly learnt by listening
to songs and trying to imitate the singers. I wouldn't say I have
excellent vocal skills, but I can sing well enough to enjoy myself.

## 3. Do you sing well?

I'd say I'm an average singer at best. I can sing along to familiar
songs, but I certainly wouldn't perform in front of a large audience.
For me, singing is mainly a form of entertainment rather than something
I take seriously.

## 4. Do you think singing can bring happiness to people?

Yes, definitely. Singing can improve people's moods, reduce stress, and
create a sense of connection when people sing together. For example,
singing with friends at a social gathering can create a cheerful
atmosphere and strengthen relationships.
