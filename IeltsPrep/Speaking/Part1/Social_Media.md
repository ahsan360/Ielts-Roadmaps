# Part 1: Social Media

## 1. Have you ever posted anything on social media?

Yes, I have. I occasionally post photos from my travels, important
moments, or interesting things that I come across. However, I don't post
very frequently because I prefer to keep most aspects of my personal
life private.

## 2. What do you usually use social media for?

I mainly use social media to stay in touch with friends, follow current
events, and discover useful content. I also sometimes use it to learn
about technology and keep up with developments in my professional field.

## 3. Do you think you spend too much time on social media?

Sometimes, yes. I think it's quite easy to lose track of time when
scrolling through short videos or reading posts. I try to limit my
screen time, especially when I have important work to complete.

## 4. Do your friends use social media?

Yes, most of my friends use social media regularly. It's one of the
easiest ways for us to stay connected, particularly when we're busy or
living in different places. We often share news, photos, and interesting
videos with one another.

## 5. What do people often do on social media?

People use social media for a wide range of purposes. They share photos
and opinions, communicate with friends, follow celebrities, watch short
videos, and keep up with current affairs. Many people also use it for
business, networking, and promoting products or services.
