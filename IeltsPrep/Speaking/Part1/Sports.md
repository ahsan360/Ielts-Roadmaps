# Band 8–9 Vocabulary

| Vocabulary | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| physical fitness | good physical health and strength |
| endurance | the ability to continue an activity for a long time |
| teamwork | working effectively with others |
| sportsmanship | fair and respectful behaviour in sports |
| competitive spirit | a strong desire to succeed |
| sedentary lifestyle | a lifestyle with little physical activity |
| recreational sport | sport played for enjoyment rather than competition |
| professional athlete | someone who earns a living through sport |
| mental resilience | the ability to recover from difficulties |
| cardiovascular health | the health of the heart and blood vessels |
| discipline | self-control and consistent effort |
| spectator sport | a sport mainly watched rather than played |

# 1. Do you play any sports?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Football
- Badminton
- Exercise

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I enjoy playing football and badminton whenever I have free time. Besides being enjoyable, these sports help me stay physically fit, reduce stress, and improve my teamwork and communication skills.

---

# 2. Do you watch sports on TV?

## 💡 Ideas

- Cricket
- Football
- International tournaments

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I regularly watch football and cricket, particularly major international tournaments such as the FIFA World Cup and the ICC Cricket World Cup. Watching professional athletes inspires me to stay active and appreciate the importance of discipline and hard work.

---

# 3. What is the most popular sport in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- Cricket
- Football
- National passion

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Cricket is undoubtedly the most popular sport in Bangladesh. Whenever the national team plays, millions of people watch the matches and passionately support their favourite players. Football is also widely enjoyed, especially among younger generations.

---

# 4. How do people in your country stay fit?

## 💡 Ideas

- Gym
- Walking
- Sports
- Cycling

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People maintain their fitness in various ways. Some go to the gym, while others prefer jogging, cycling, or playing outdoor sports. Walking has also become increasingly popular because it is simple, affordable, and suitable for people of all ages.

---

# 5. Is it important for children to play sports?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Health
- Teamwork
- Confidence

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Sports help children develop physically, mentally, and socially. They learn teamwork, discipline, leadership, and problem-solving skills while improving their overall health. In addition, regular exercise helps reduce the risk of childhood obesity.

---

# 6. Is there a lot of sport on television in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Cricket
- Football
- Olympics

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, sports receive extensive television coverage in Bangladesh. Cricket and football are broadcast throughout the year, and international competitions such as the Olympic Games and the Asian Games also attract large audiences.

---

# 7. What sports do children normally do at school?

## 💡 Ideas

- Football
- Cricket
- Athletics

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Schoolchildren commonly participate in football, cricket, athletics, badminton, and volleyball. Many schools also organise annual sports competitions to encourage students to stay active and develop a competitive yet cooperative attitude.

---

# 8. Do you think people do enough sport these days?

## 💡 Ideas

- No
- Technology
- Busy lifestyle
- Health

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

No, I don't think so. Modern lifestyles have become increasingly sedentary due to long working hours and excessive screen time. As a result, many people struggle to find time for regular exercise. I believe governments and schools should promote sports more actively to encourage healthier lifestyles.

---

# 9. Should governments invest more in sports facilities?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Public health
- Young people
- Community

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, definitely. Investing in sports facilities encourages people of all ages to exercise regularly, which improves public health and reduces healthcare costs in the long run. Accessible playgrounds, parks, and sports centres also provide young people with positive recreational opportunities.

---

# 10. Can sport help bring people together?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Unity
- Friendship
- International events

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Sport has a unique ability to unite people regardless of their age, nationality, or background. International tournaments foster a sense of global community, while local sporting events strengthen friendships and encourage teamwork within neighbourhoods and schools.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I play football from childhood.

✅ I have played football since childhood.

---

❌ Sport is good for health.

✅ Sports are beneficial for both physical and mental health.

---

❌ People don't do exercise nowadays.

✅ Many people do not exercise regularly nowadays.

---

❌ Cricket are popular.

✅ Cricket is the most popular sport in my country.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Don't simply say that sports are "good." Explain **how** they benefit individuals and society.

### ❌ Basic

> Sports are good for health.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Regular participation in sports improves physical fitness, strengthens mental resilience, reduces stress, and teaches valuable life skills such as teamwork, discipline, and perseverance.
