# Part 1: Sports Team

## 1. Have you ever been part of a sports team?

Yes, I have. When I was at school, I was part of a football team with
some of my classmates. We used to practise after school and occasionally
took part in local competitions. It was a great way to stay active and
develop teamwork skills.

## 2. Are team sports popular in your culture?

Yes, team sports are extremely popular in Bangladesh. Cricket and
football, in particular, attract enormous numbers of fans, and people
often watch major matches together with their friends and families.
Sporting events can create a strong sense of community.

## 3. Did you like watching team games? Why?

Yes, I did. I find team games exciting because the result can change
very quickly, and there is usually a lot of suspense. I also enjoy
watching how players cooperate, develop strategies, and respond to
pressure during an important match.

## 4. What are the differences between team sports and individual sports?

The main difference is that team sports require cooperation and
coordination among several players, whereas individual sports depend
more heavily on a person's own skills and discipline. Team sports can
develop communication and leadership skills, while individual sports
often require greater self-motivation and personal responsibility.
