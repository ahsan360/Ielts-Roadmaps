# Part 1: Staying with Old People

## 1. Have you ever worked with people who are older than you?

Yes, I have. I've worked with people from different age groups,
including colleagues who were considerably older than me. I think
working with experienced people can be very useful because they often
have valuable knowledge and practical experience.

## 2. Are you happy to work with people who are older than you?

Yes, definitely. I don't see age as a problem in the workplace. In fact,
I enjoy working with older colleagues because I can learn from their
experience while also sharing some of my own knowledge and skills.

## 3. Do you enjoy spending time with old people?

Yes, I do, especially when they are members of my family. I enjoy
spending time with older relatives because they often have fascinating
stories about their childhood and the past. Their experiences can also
teach me valuable life lessons.

## 4. What are the benefits of being friends with or working with old people?

There are several benefits. Older people often have a great deal of life
experience, so they can offer practical advice when we face difficult
situations. They may also have a different perspective on life, which
can help younger people become more patient and thoughtful.
