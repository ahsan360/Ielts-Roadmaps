# 1. Do you like watching TV?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes, occasionally
- Relaxation
- News
- Documentaries

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do, but I don't watch television every day. I usually watch it to relax after work or to keep up with current affairs. I particularly enjoy documentaries and technology-related programmes because they are both informative and entertaining.

---

# 2. How often do you watch TV?

## 💡 Ideas

- 3–4 times a week
- Evening
- Short time

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I usually watch television three or four times a week, mostly in the evening. Since I spend a lot of time working on my computer, I try to limit my screen time and only watch programmes that genuinely interest me.

---

# 3. What kind of TV programmes do you like to watch?

## 💡 Ideas

- Documentaries
- News
- Travel
- Technology

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I enjoy watching documentaries, travel shows, and technology programmes. They allow me to learn about different cultures, scientific discoveries, and the latest innovations while also providing entertainment.

---

# 4. What is your favourite TV show now?

## 💡 Ideas

- Nature documentary
- Technology programme
- Educational

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

At the moment, I don't follow a particular TV series. Instead, I prefer educational programmes and documentaries, especially those related to technology, wildlife, and world history. I find them much more beneficial than reality shows.

---

# 5. What was your favourite show when you were a child?

## 💡 Ideas

- Cartoons
- Educational shows
- Fun

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

When I was a child, I loved watching cartoons because they were entertaining and imaginative. I also enjoyed educational programmes that introduced interesting facts about science and nature in a simple and engaging way.

---

# 6. What are the most popular TV shows in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- Cricket
- Dramas
- Reality shows
- News

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In Bangladesh, television dramas, cricket matches, reality shows, and news programmes are extremely popular. During major cricket tournaments, millions of people watch live broadcasts and enthusiastically support the national team.

---

# 7. Do you like watching TV shows from other countries?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Learn culture
- English

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. Foreign programmes expose me to different cultures, accents, and perspectives. Since many of them are in English, they also help me improve my listening skills and expand my vocabulary.

---

# 8. Has the Internet affected your viewing habits?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- YouTube
- Netflix
- Streaming

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Definitely. Nowadays, I spend more time watching content on YouTube and streaming platforms than on traditional television. Online services allow me to choose what I want to watch and when I want to watch it, which is much more convenient.

---

# 9. How many hours a day do you spend watching television?

## 💡 Ideas

- Less than one hour
- Busy
- Computer work

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

On average, I spend less than an hour a day watching television. Since I already spend many hours in front of a computer for work, I try to avoid excessive screen time during my free time.

---

# 10. Do you think children in your country watch too much television?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Entertainment
- Parents
- Balance

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I think many children spend too much time watching television and online videos. Although educational programmes can be beneficial, excessive screen time may reduce physical activity and affect their social development. Parents should encourage a healthy balance between entertainment, study, and outdoor activities.

---

# 11. Do you think television is useful for education in schools?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Visual learning
- Documentaries

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I believe television can be an effective educational tool when used appropriately. Documentaries, science programmes, and historical films make lessons more engaging and help students understand complex topics more easily through visual learning.

# Common Mistakes

✅ I watch TV every day.

---

❌ I like watch documentary.

---

❌ TV help students.

---

❌ I watch less TV because mobile.

✅ I watch less television because I prefer online streaming platforms.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Don't just say **what** you watch—explain **why** you watch it and **what benefit** you get from it.

### ❌ Basic

> I like documentaries.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I enjoy watching documentaries because they help me broaden my knowledge, improve my English listening skills, and learn about different cultures and scientific developments.
