# Part 1: Teachers

## 1. Do you have a favourite teacher?

Yes, I do. One of my favourite teachers was my English teacher at
school. What made her memorable was the way she explained difficult
topics in a simple and engaging manner. She also encouraged us to
express our opinions rather than simply memorising information.

## 2. In what way has your favourite teacher helped you?

She had a significant influence on my confidence and communication
skills. Instead of simply pointing out my mistakes, she encouraged me to
explain my ideas clearly and think independently. Those skills have been
extremely useful in both my studies and professional life.

## 3. Are you still in touch with your primary school teachers?

Unfortunately, I'm not. It's been quite a long time since I left primary
school, and people naturally lose touch as they move to different places
and become busy with their own lives. However, I still have very fond
memories of some of my teachers.

## 4. Do you have a teacher from your past that you still remember?

Yes, definitely. I still remember one of my mathematics teachers from
secondary school. He was quite strict, but he was also incredibly
dedicated and supportive. He taught me to approach difficult problems
patiently rather than giving up when I couldn't find an immediate
solution.

## 5. Do you want to be a teacher in the future?

Probably not. I have a great deal of respect for teachers because it's a
demanding profession that requires patience, communication skills, and a
strong sense of responsibility. However, I'd personally prefer to
continue developing my career in the technology sector, where I can
focus on software development and problem-solving.
