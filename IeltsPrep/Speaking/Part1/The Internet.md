# Topic: The Internet
**Target Band:** 7.5–8.5

# 1. Do you use the Internet?

## 💡 Ideas

- Every day
- Work
- Study
- Communication

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I use the Internet every day for both professional and personal purposes. As a software engineer, I rely on it for programming, research, and communication. I also use it to read news, watch educational videos, and stay in touch with friends and family.

---

# 2. Are children allowed to use the Internet at school in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Supervised
- Educational websites

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, many schools allow students to use the Internet under the supervision of teachers. It is mainly used for educational purposes, such as online research, interactive learning, and accessing digital resources. However, schools should also teach children how to use the Internet safely and responsibly.

---

# 3. Do you think the Internet is useful for study?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Unlimited resources
- Online courses
- Research

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. The Internet provides instant access to academic articles, online courses, video lectures, and digital libraries. It enables students to learn independently and explore topics in greater depth than traditional textbooks alone.

---

# 4. Have you ever used the Internet to buy something?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Electronics
- Books
- Convenience

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I frequently shop online because it is convenient and time-saving. I have purchased books, electronic devices, and household items through various e-commerce platforms. Online shopping also allows me to compare prices before making a purchase.

---

# 5. Are there any dangers in buying things online?

## 💡 Ideas

- Fraud
- Fake products
- Scams
- Privacy

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, there are several potential risks. Customers may receive counterfeit products or become victims of online scams. There are also concerns about data privacy and payment security. Therefore, people should only purchase from reputable websites and use secure payment methods.

---

# 6. What are the biggest advantages of the Internet?

## 💡 Ideas

- Communication
- Education
- Business
- Convenience

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In my opinion, the Internet has revolutionised communication and access to information. It allows people to study online, work remotely, conduct business internationally, and communicate instantly with anyone around the world. These benefits have significantly improved productivity and convenience.

---

# 7. What are the disadvantages of the Internet?

## 💡 Ideas

- Addiction
- Misinformation
- Privacy
- Cybercrime

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Despite its many advantages, the Internet also has drawbacks. Excessive screen time can affect people's physical and mental health, while misinformation spreads rapidly on social media. In addition, cybercrime and identity theft have become increasingly serious concerns.

---

# 8. Do you think people rely too much on the Internet?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Everyday life
- Less face-to-face communication

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I believe many people have become overly dependent on the Internet. While it has made life more convenient, excessive reliance on digital technology can reduce face-to-face interaction and make people less independent in solving everyday problems.

---

# 9. How will the Internet change in the future?

## 💡 Ideas

- AI
- Faster
- Smarter
- More automation

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think the Internet will become even faster, smarter, and more integrated with artificial intelligence. Technologies such as virtual reality, cloud computing, and the Internet of Things will transform education, healthcare, transportation, and many other industries.

---

# 10. Should governments regulate the Internet?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Cybercrime
- Privacy
- Freedom

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, governments should introduce reasonable regulations to combat cybercrime, protect personal data, and prevent the spread of harmful content. However, these regulations should be carefully balanced so that they do not unnecessarily restrict freedom of expression.

# Common Mistakes

❌ Internet make life easy.

✅ The Internet makes life much easier.

---

❌ I search in Google.

✅ I search **on** Google.

---

❌ I buy many thing online.

✅ I buy many **things** online.

---

❌ Internet have many advantages.

✅ The Internet has many advantages.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Always discuss **both advantages and disadvantages** when appropriate.

### ❌ Basic

> The Internet is useful.

### ✅ Band 8+

> The Internet has transformed modern life by making communication, education, and business far more accessible. Nevertheless, it has also introduced challenges such as cybercrime, misinformation, and excessive screen time.
