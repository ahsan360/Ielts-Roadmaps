# 1. Do you like to go on holiday by the sea?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Relaxing
- Fresh air
- Beautiful views

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I really enjoy seaside holidays because they are both relaxing and refreshing. Spending time by the sea allows me to escape the stress of everyday life, breathe fresh air, and enjoy the peaceful sound of the waves. I also appreciate the beautiful coastal scenery.

---

# 2. Are there many hotels by the sea in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Cox's Bazar
- Tourism
- Resorts

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, Bangladesh has many hotels and resorts along its coastline, particularly in Cox's Bazar, which is one of the country's most popular tourist destinations. These hotels cater to both domestic and international visitors, especially during the peak holiday season.

---

# 3. What kinds of activities can people do by the sea?

## 💡 Ideas

- Swimming
- Boating
- Photography
- Seafood
- Walking

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People can enjoy a wide range of activities, including swimming, boating, beach volleyball, photography, and simply walking along the shore. Many visitors also enjoy tasting fresh seafood and watching the sunrise or sunset over the ocean.

---

# 4. How long do people normally stay when they go on a seaside holiday?

## 💡 Ideas

- 2–5 days
- Weekends
- Family trips

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Most people usually spend two to five days at the seaside, depending on their budget and work commitments. Families often plan weekend trips, while others may stay longer during public holidays or annual vacations.

---

# 5. Why do people enjoy seaside holidays?

## 💡 Ideas

- Relaxation
- Nature
- Escape stress
- Family time

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People enjoy seaside holidays because they provide a perfect opportunity to relax and reconnect with nature. The peaceful environment, fresh sea breeze, and scenic views help reduce stress and improve mental well-being. In addition, beaches are ideal places for spending quality time with family and friends.

---

# 6. What are the benefits of living near the sea?

## 💡 Ideas

- Tourism
- Fishing
- Beautiful environment
- Fresh seafood

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Living near the sea offers several advantages. Residents can enjoy a pleasant environment, fresh seafood, and employment opportunities in tourism, fishing, and maritime industries. Coastal areas also tend to attract visitors, which benefits the local economy.

---

# 7. Are there any disadvantages to living near the sea?

## 💡 Ideas

- Cyclones
- Flooding
- Coastal erosion
- Humidity

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, coastal areas are often vulnerable to natural disasters such as cyclones, storm surges, and flooding. High humidity and coastal erosion can also create challenges for local communities. Therefore, effective disaster management and environmental protection are essential.

---

# 8. Should governments do more to protect the sea?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Plastic pollution
- Marine life
- Laws

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Governments should introduce stricter regulations to reduce plastic pollution, protect marine ecosystems, and prevent illegal fishing. Public awareness campaigns and international cooperation are also crucial for preserving the oceans for future generations.

---

# 9. How does the sea contribute to a country's economy?

## 💡 Ideas

- Tourism
- Fishing
- Trade
- Ports

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

The sea plays a vital role in a country's economy by supporting tourism, fishing, and international trade. Ports facilitate the import and export of goods, while coastal tourism creates employment opportunities and generates significant revenue for local businesses.

---

# 10. What are the biggest environmental problems affecting the sea?

## 💡 Ideas

- Plastic waste
- Oil spills
- Climate change
- Overfishing

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Some of the most serious environmental issues include plastic pollution, oil spills, overfishing, and climate change. These problems threaten marine biodiversity and damage fragile ecosystems. If governments and individuals fail to take action, the long-term consequences could be severe.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I like sea.

✅ I like **the sea**.

---

❌ Sea have many fish.

✅ The sea has a wide variety of marine life.

---

❌ People go sea for enjoy.

✅ People go to the sea to relax and enjoy themselves.

---

❌ Sea is important for country.

✅ The sea is important for a country's economy and environment.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Try to extend your answers beyond tourism. Mention **the economy, the environment, climate change, marine life, or international trade**.

### ❌ Basic

> The sea is beautiful.

### ✅ Band 8+

> The sea is not only a beautiful natural attraction but also an essential resource that supports tourism, international trade, marine biodiversity, and millions of livelihoods around the world.
