# IELTS Speaking Band 7+ Sample Answers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part 1: Tidiness

## 1. Do you like to keep things tidy?

Yes, absolutely. I prefer keeping my surroundings clean and organised
because I find it much easier to concentrate in a tidy environment. When
everything is in its proper place, I feel more comfortable and
productive.

## 2. Did you use to keep your room tidy as a child?

Not always. When I was a child, I wasn't particularly concerned about
keeping my room organised, so my parents often had to remind me to put
things away. As I grew older, I gradually became more responsible and
started developing better habits.
