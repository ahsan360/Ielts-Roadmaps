# 1. Is being late acceptable in your culture?

## 💡 Ideas

- Generally no
- Depends on situation
- Traffic

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In general, being late is not considered polite in my culture, especially for formal events, business meetings, or job interviews. However, people are usually understanding if someone is delayed due to unavoidable circumstances such as heavy traffic or bad weather.

---

# 2. Are you ever late for appointments?

## 💡 Ideas

- Rarely
- Plan ahead
- Traffic

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I rarely arrive late because I usually plan my schedule carefully and leave home early. Of course, unexpected traffic congestion can occasionally cause delays, but I always inform the other person in advance if that happens.

---

# 3. What type of excuses do you think are alright for lateness?

## 💡 Ideas

- Medical emergency
- Traffic accident
- Bad weather

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think excuses are acceptable only when the reason is genuine, such as a medical emergency, a traffic accident, or severe weather conditions. However, poor planning or oversleeping is generally not a valid excuse because those situations can often be avoided.

---

# 4. How do you feel when someone is late for an appointment with you?

## 💡 Ideas

- Depends
- Understandable
- Respect

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

It depends on the situation. If the person informs me beforehand and has a genuine reason, I don't mind waiting. However, if someone is repeatedly late without any explanation, I feel that they are not respecting my time, which can be quite frustrating.

---

# 5. Why is punctuality important?

## 💡 Ideas

- Professionalism
- Trust
- Respect

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Punctuality is important because it reflects responsibility, professionalism, and respect for other people's time. It also helps build trust, especially in workplaces where meeting deadlines and attending meetings on time are essential.

---

# 6. Do you think people are less punctual nowadays?

## 💡 Ideas

- Mixed
- Busy lifestyle
- Technology

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

It's a mixed picture. On one hand, digital calendars and reminder apps help people stay organised. On the other hand, busy lifestyles and heavy traffic often make it difficult for people to arrive exactly on time.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I am always in time.

✅ I am usually **on time**.

---

❌ Traffic make me late.

✅ Heavy traffic sometimes makes me late.

---

❌ I don't like late people.

✅ I don't like people who are frequently late.

---

❌ Being late show disrespect.

✅ Being late shows disrespect.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Instead of simply saying **"I don't like being late,"** explain **why**.

### ❌ Basic

> I don't like being late.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I dislike being late because punctuality reflects professionalism and respect. Arriving on time also helps me stay organised and reduces unnecessary stress.
