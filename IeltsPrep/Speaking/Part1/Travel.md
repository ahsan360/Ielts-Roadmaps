# 1. Where was the last place you visited on holiday?

## 💡 Ideas

- Sikkim, India
- Mountains
- Nature
- Friends

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

The last place I visited on holiday was **Sikkim, India**. I travelled there with a few friends, and it was one of the most memorable trips I've ever had. We visited places like Gangtok and Lachung, enjoyed the breathtaking mountain scenery, and experienced the local culture.

---

# 2. Would you like to go back there again?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Peaceful
- Beautiful
- More places

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. I'd love to return because there are still many places I didn't have the chance to explore. The peaceful environment, pleasant weather, and stunning natural landscapes made the trip truly unforgettable.

---

# 3. What kind of tourist destinations do you usually prefer?

## 💡 Ideas

- Mountains
- Nature
- Quiet
- Adventure

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I usually prefer natural destinations such as mountains, tea gardens, forests, and lakes rather than crowded cities. I find peaceful places much more relaxing because they allow me to unwind and appreciate the beauty of nature.

---

# 4. Has a foreign visitor ever stayed at your home?

## 💡 Ideas

- No
- Would welcome
- Hospitality

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

No, I haven't had the opportunity to host a foreign visitor yet. However, I would be delighted to do so because it would be a wonderful opportunity to exchange cultures and introduce them to Bangladeshi food and traditions.

---

# 5. What's the best way to save money while travelling?

## 💡 Ideas

- Plan ahead
- Budget hotels
- Public transport
- Local food

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think careful planning is the best way to save money while travelling. Booking accommodation and transport in advance, using public transportation, eating at local restaurants, and avoiding unnecessary shopping can significantly reduce travel expenses.

---

# 6. Do you prefer travelling alone or with others?

## 💡 Ideas

- With friends
- Safer
- Fun
- Shared memories

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I generally prefer travelling with friends because the journey becomes more enjoyable and memorable. We can share expenses, help each other during unexpected situations, and create lasting memories together.

---

# 7. Why do people enjoy travelling?

## 💡 Ideas

- Relaxation
- Learn cultures
- New experiences

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People enjoy travelling because it gives them a break from their daily routine and allows them to experience new cultures, cuisines, and lifestyles. It also broadens their perspective and helps them create unforgettable memories.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I like travel.

✅ I like **travelling**.

---

❌ I went Sikkim.

✅ I went **to Sikkim**.

---

❌ The place was very naturally.

✅ The place was very **beautiful and peaceful**.

---

❌ I enjoy with my friends.

✅ I enjoy travelling with my friends.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Always include a **specific example** from your own experience. Personal details make your answer sound more natural and authentic.

### ❌ Basic

> I like travelling.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I enjoy travelling because it allows me to experience different cultures and escape my daily routine. For example, my recent trip to Sikkim was unforgettable due to its breathtaking mountain views and peaceful atmosphere.
