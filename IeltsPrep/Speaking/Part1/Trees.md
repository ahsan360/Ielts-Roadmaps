# 1. Do you like trees?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Nature
- Fresh air
- Beauty

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. I love trees because they make the environment more beautiful and peaceful. They provide fresh air, reduce pollution, and create a relaxing atmosphere. I especially enjoy spending time in places surrounded by greenery, such as tea gardens and parks.

---

# 2. Are there many trees in your hometown?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Sylhet
- Tea gardens
- Green city

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, fortunately, my hometown, Sylhet, has plenty of trees and green spaces. It is well known for its tea gardens and beautiful natural scenery. Compared to many large cities, the environment is much greener and more refreshing.

---

# 3. Do you think there are enough trees in most cities?

## 💡 Ideas

- No
- Urbanisation
- Pollution

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

No, I don't think so. Rapid urbanisation has resulted in the removal of many trees to make room for roads and buildings. As a result, many cities suffer from poor air quality and higher temperatures. I believe local authorities should create more parks and green spaces.

---

# 4. What are the benefits of planting more trees?

## 💡 Ideas

- Cleaner air
- Climate
- Wildlife
- Beauty

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Planting more trees has numerous benefits. Trees absorb carbon dioxide, improve air quality, and help combat climate change. They also provide habitats for wildlife, reduce noise pollution, and make cities more attractive and comfortable places to live.

---

# 5. Should governments encourage tree planting?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Campaigns
- Schools
- Public awareness

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Governments should organise tree-planting campaigns and encourage schools, businesses, and local communities to participate. Raising public awareness about environmental conservation is essential for creating a greener and healthier future.

---

# 6. Why are trees important in cities?

## 💡 Ideas

- Air quality
- Shade
- Mental health
- Parks

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Trees are essential in cities because they improve air quality, provide shade during hot weather, and reduce urban temperatures. In addition, parks and green spaces offer residents a peaceful place to relax, exercise, and improve their mental well-being.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

For environmental topics, mention **both personal and global benefits**.

### ❌ Basic

> Trees are important because they give oxygen.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Trees play a vital role in improving air quality, reducing carbon emissions, preserving biodiversity, and creating healthier, more liveable cities. They also provide people with peaceful green spaces where they can relax and unwind.
