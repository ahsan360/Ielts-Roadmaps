# Part 1: Typing

## 1. Do you prefer typing or handwriting?

I definitely prefer typing because it's considerably faster and more
convenient for me. I can easily edit, reorganise, and share digital
documents, whereas handwriting is less practical for lengthy pieces of
work. However, I still use handwriting when I need to make quick notes.

## 2. Do you type on a desktop or laptop keyboard every day?

Yes, I do. I use a computer keyboard almost every day because most of my
work involves writing code, sending emails, and preparing documents.
Since typing is such an important part of my routine, I rely on a
keyboard for several hours a day.

## 3. When did you learn how to type on a keyboard?

I started learning to type when I was a teenager, when I began using
computers more regularly. At first, I typed quite slowly and had to look
at the keyboard frequently, but I gradually became faster through
regular practice.

## 4. How do you improve your typing?

I improve my typing mainly through regular practice. I try to type
without constantly looking at the keyboard and occasionally use online
typing exercises to improve my speed and accuracy. I think consistency
is more important than practising intensively for a short period.
