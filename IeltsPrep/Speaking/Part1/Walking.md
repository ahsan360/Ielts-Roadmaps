# Part 1: Walking

## 1. Do you walk a lot?

Yes, I walk quite a lot, especially in the evenings. I find walking a
simple way to clear my mind and stay physically active. If I have time,
I usually go for a short walk around my neighbourhood.

## 2. Did you often go outside to have a walk when you were a child?

Yes, I did. I often went for walks with my cousins and friends when I
was a child. We used to play outside, explore the neighbourhood, and
spend most of our free time outdoors.

## 3. Why do people like to walk in parks?

I think people enjoy walking in parks because the environment is usually
peaceful and refreshing. Parks also provide a break from busy streets,
traffic, and noise. In addition, walking in a green space can help
people relax and reduce stress.

## 4. Where would you like to take a long walk if you had the chance?

If I had the chance, I'd love to take a long walk along a beach. I think
walking beside the sea would be incredibly relaxing, especially with the
sound of the waves and the fresh air. It would also give me an
opportunity to enjoy the scenery and clear my mind.

## 5. Where did you go for a walk lately?

Recently, I went for a walk around a nearby park close to my house. It
was relatively quiet, so I could walk at a comfortable pace and enjoy
some peaceful time away from my phone and work.
