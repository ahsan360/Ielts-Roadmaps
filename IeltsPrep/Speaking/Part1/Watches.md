# Part 1: Watches

## 1. Do you wear a watch?

Yes, I do. I usually wear a watch when I'm going to work or attending a formal event because I find it both practical and stylish. Although I can easily check the time on my phone, wearing a watch is much more convenient and discreet.

---

## 2. Have you ever received a watch as a gift?

Yes, I have. I received a watch as a birthday present from one of my close friends a few years ago. It wasn't particularly expensive, but it had sentimental value because it reminded me of our friendship. I still wear it occasionally.

---

## 3. Do you think it is important to wear a watch? Why / Why not?

I don't think it's essential nowadays because smartphones allow us to check the time instantly. However, I think wearing a watch is useful because it helps people manage their time without constantly looking at their phones. Besides that, a well-designed watch can be a stylish accessory.

---

## 4. Why do some people like to wear expensive watches?

I think there are several reasons. Some people appreciate the craftsmanship, durability, and sophisticated design of luxury watches. Others see them as a symbol of status and financial success. In some cases, expensive watches are also considered valuable investments or collectibles.

---

## 5. Do you prefer a smartwatch or a regular/traditional watch?

Personally, I prefer a smartwatch because it offers far more functionality than a traditional watch. Apart from showing the time, it can track my fitness, display notifications, and help me manage my daily activities. However, I think traditional watches look more elegant on formal occasions.

---

## 6. Did you wear a watch when you were a child?

Yes, I did. I started wearing a simple watch when I was in primary school because my parents wanted me to learn how to tell the time and become more punctual. At that age, I wasn't particularly interested in its design; I simply found it useful for knowing when I needed to go to school or come home.
