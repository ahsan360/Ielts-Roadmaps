# Part 1: Websites and the Internet

## 1. What kinds of websites do you often visit?

I frequently visit websites related to technology, programming, news, and education. Since I'm interested in software development, I often use documentation websites, coding platforms, and technical forums to solve problems and learn new concepts. I also browse travel websites when I'm planning a trip.

---

## 2. How often do you use the internet?

I use the internet every day, probably for several hours. It's an essential part of both my professional and personal life. I use it for work, communication, learning, entertainment, online shopping, and keeping up with current events.

---

## 3. What is your favourite website?

One of my favourite websites is **YouTube** because it offers an enormous amount of useful and entertaining content. I can watch programming tutorials, travel videos, documentaries, and educational content whenever I have some free time. What I particularly like is that I can learn practical skills at my own pace.

---

## 4. Do you think the internet is useful for your work or studies?

Absolutely. The internet is indispensable for my work because I can access technical documentation, tutorials, research papers, and developer communities within seconds. Whenever I encounter a technical problem, I can usually find several possible solutions online. It has made learning and problem-solving considerably faster and more efficient.

---

## 5. Have you ever bought anything on a website?

Yes, I frequently buy things online. I've purchased electronic accessories, books, clothing, and various everyday items from online shops. I particularly appreciate the convenience because I can compare prices, read customer reviews, and place an order without having to visit several physical stores.

---

## 6. Do you think children should be allowed to browse the internet freely?

No, I don't think children should have completely unrestricted access to the internet. Although it is an excellent educational resource, there is also inappropriate content, misinformation, and potential online risks. Parents should supervise their children's online activities and teach them how to use the internet responsibly while still giving them enough freedom to learn and explore.
