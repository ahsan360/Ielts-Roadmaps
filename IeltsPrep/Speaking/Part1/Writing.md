# 1. Do you enjoy writing?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Emails
- Notes
- Programming documentation

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. Although I don't write stories or poems very often, I regularly write emails, technical documentation, and notes for my work and studies. I also enjoy writing because it helps me organise my thoughts and communicate more effectively.

---

# 2. What age do children normally learn to write in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- 4–6 years old
- Kindergarten
- Primary school

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In Bangladesh, most children begin learning to write when they are around four or five years old. They usually start with the alphabet and simple words in kindergarten before moving on to sentences in primary school.

---

# 3. Did you write a lot when you were a child?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Homework
- Diary
- Practice

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I did. I spent a considerable amount of time writing homework, practising handwriting, and completing school assignments. Occasionally, I also kept a small diary where I wrote about memorable events.

---

# 4. Do you ever write letters?

## 💡 Ideas

- Rarely
- Emails
- Messages

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Nowadays, I rarely write traditional letters because emails and instant messaging are much faster and more convenient. However, I think handwritten letters feel more personal and meaningful on special occasions.

---

# 5. Do you prefer writing letters or sending emails?

## 💡 Ideas

- Emails
- Fast
- Convenient
- Professional

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I definitely prefer sending emails because they are quick, efficient, and suitable for both personal and professional communication. They also allow me to attach documents and receive replies almost instantly, which saves a great deal of time.

# Common Mistakes

❌ I like write.

✅ I like **writing**.

---

❌ I write from childhood.

✅ I have enjoyed writing since childhood.

---

❌ I write email everyday.

✅ I write emails every day.

---

❌ Writing improve English.

✅ Writing improves English.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Don't simply say **"I like writing."** Explain **what you write, why you write it, and how it benefits you.**

### ❌ Basic

> I like writing because it's interesting.

### ✅ Band 8+

> I enjoy writing because it helps me organise my thoughts, improve my English, and communicate more effectively, especially in academic and professional situations.
