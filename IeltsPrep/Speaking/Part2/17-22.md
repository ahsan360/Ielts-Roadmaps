# IELTS Speaking Part 2 — Band 7+ Sample Answers

## 17. Describe a city you enjoyed visiting

### You should say:

- Where it is
- When you visited it
- How long you stayed there
- What you did there
- And explain why you enjoyed visiting it

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about **Sikkim**, a beautiful state in northeastern India, which I really enjoyed visiting. I went there with one of my closest friends during a holiday, and it was one of the most memorable trips I have ever had.

I visited Sikkim a few years ago and stayed there for around **five days**. We spent most of our time exploring places such as **Gangtok and Lachung**. We visited several scenic viewpoints, tried local food, took plenty of photographs, and walked around the city to experience the local atmosphere.

One of the highlights of the trip was travelling through the mountainous roads because the scenery kept changing throughout the journey. The mountains, winding roads, waterfalls, and cloudy weather made the entire journey incredibly enjoyable.

What I particularly enjoyed was the combination of **natural beauty and a peaceful atmosphere**. The weather was pleasant, the surroundings were breathtaking, and the local people were very welcoming.

Overall, I enjoyed visiting Sikkim because it allowed me to **escape from my usual routine, spend quality time with my friend, and reconnect with nature**. Even now, I have very fond memories of that trip.

## 18. Describe a short-term job you want to have in a foreign country

### You should say:

- Where it is
- How you know of it
- What the job is
- And explain why you want to do it

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about a short-term job that I would love to have in **Germany**. I have learned about these opportunities through online job platforms, professional networking websites, and discussions with people who have worked there.

Ideally, I would like to work as a **full-stack software engineer** for a technology company for several months. My responsibilities would probably include developing web applications, fixing technical issues, collaborating with other developers, and contributing to different stages of the software development process.

The main reason I want to do this job is that Germany has a strong technology sector and offers an international working environment. I believe working there, even for a limited period, would allow me to **gain international experience, improve my technical skills, and understand how software teams operate in a different professional culture**.

Another reason is that I would have the opportunity to interact with people from different backgrounds. This could improve my communication skills and help me become more confident when working in multicultural teams.

Overall, I think it would be a valuable short-term experience because it would combine **professional development with cultural exposure**, while also giving me a better understanding of the international technology industry.

## 19. Describe a boring place

### You should say:

- Where it is
- Who you went there with
- What you did there
- And explain why you think it is a boring place

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about a place that I found extremely boring. It was a **small local museum** in my hometown, which I visited with one of my close friends a few years ago.

We went there because we wanted to learn something new and spend our afternoon doing something different. However, once we arrived, we realised that the museum was quite small and had a very limited collection.

We spent around an hour there. We walked from one room to another, looked at some old photographs and historical objects, and read the descriptions next to the exhibits. We also took a few photographs, but unfortunately, there was not much else to do.

I found the place boring mainly because **it lacked variety and interaction**. There were no interactive displays, audio guides, or activities for visitors. Personally, I enjoy places where I can actively explore or participate in something, so simply looking at static exhibits for a long time did not hold my attention.

Having said that, I understand that people who are particularly interested in local history might find the museum fascinating. So, I would say it was boring **for me rather than objectively boring**.

## 20. Describe a new law you would like to introduce in your country

### You should say:

- What law it is
- What changes this law brings
- Whether this new law will be popular
- How you came up with the new law
- And explain how you feel about this new law

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about a law that I would introduce in my country to **reduce the excessive use of plastic bags**. Under this law, shops and supermarkets would be prohibited from providing single-use plastic bags and would instead have to offer environmentally friendly alternatives.

This law would bring several positive changes. Firstly, it would significantly reduce plastic waste in streets, rivers, and drainage systems. Secondly, it could encourage people to develop more environmentally responsible habits, such as carrying reusable shopping bags.

I believe this law would eventually become popular, although some people might initially oppose it because they are accustomed to using plastic bags. The government could make the transition easier by giving businesses and consumers enough time to adapt and by making affordable alternatives widely available.

I came up with this idea after seeing how much plastic waste is produced during everyday shopping. In many places, people use a plastic bag for only a few minutes and then throw it away, even though it can remain in the environment for decades.

Personally, I would feel **very positive and satisfied** if such a law were introduced. I believe governments have a responsibility to protect the environment for future generations, and reducing unnecessary plastic consumption would be a practical step in that direction.

## 21. Describe a time when you got up early

### You should say:

- When it was
- What you did
- Why you got up early
- And how you felt about it

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about a time when I had to **get up unusually early**. It was during a trip to Jaflong with one of my closest friends.

We decided to leave very early in the morning because we wanted to reach the destination before it became crowded. I woke up at around **5 a.m.**, got ready quickly, had a light breakfast, and met my friend at the agreed meeting point.

We started our journey shortly afterwards. Although I was quite sleepy at first, I gradually became more energetic as we travelled. One of the best parts of getting up early was seeing the peaceful morning scenery. The roads were relatively quiet, the air felt fresh, and the surroundings looked completely different from how they usually appeared during the day.

When we finally reached Jaflong, we had plenty of time to explore the area without dealing with large crowds. We walked around, took photographs, enjoyed the natural scenery, and spent several hours there.

At first, I felt **sleepy and slightly exhausted**, but later I felt extremely happy that I had made the effort to wake up early. It taught me that starting the day early can sometimes make a trip much more enjoyable and productive.

## 22. Describe a TV or online program you like to watch

### You should say:

- What it is
- What it is about
- Who you watch it with
- And explain why you like to watch it

### Sample Answer

I would like to talk about an online program that I really enjoy watching. It is a **travel and adventure program on YouTube** that focuses on different countries, cultures, and natural landscapes.

The program usually follows the presenter as they travel to different destinations. They explore local attractions, try traditional food, interact with local people, and share useful information about the places they visit. I particularly enjoy the episodes about remote areas because they show a side of a country that tourists do not always get to experience.

I usually watch it **by myself**, especially when I have some free time in the evening. Sometimes, however, I watch interesting episodes with my friends and we discuss the destinations afterwards.

The main reason I like this program is that it combines **entertainment with education**. I can learn about different cultures and destinations while also enjoying beautiful scenery. Since I am interested in travelling and exploring the world, I find this type of content particularly engaging.

Another reason is that it inspires me to travel to new places. Whenever I watch an interesting episode, I often start thinking about where I would like to go in the future.

Overall, I enjoy watching this program because it is **informative, entertaining, and closely connected to my personal interests**.
