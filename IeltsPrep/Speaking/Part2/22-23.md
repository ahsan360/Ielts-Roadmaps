# IELTS Speaking Part 2 — Cue Card Practice

## Cue Card 1: Describe a TV or online program you like to watch

### You should say:

- What it is
- What it is about
- Who you watch it with
- And explain why you like watching it

---

## 🎯 Band 7+ Sample Answer

I’d like to talk about a TV series called **Friends**, which I watched about two years ago. It is a popular American sitcom that revolves around the daily lives of four close friends.

The series mainly focuses on their **friendship, relationships, jobs, and everyday problems**. At the end of a busy day, they often meet at a café, where they talk about what has happened in their lives. Although the story is quite simple, it is entertaining because there are plenty of funny and relatable situations.

I usually watched it by myself whenever I had some free time. At first, I was a little worried that I would struggle to understand the English. However, since I had already watched several English-language series, I gradually became comfortable with the dialogue.

What I particularly liked about *Friends* was the way it **portrayed genuine friendship**. Whenever one of them faced a difficult situation, the others were there to support and encourage them. This made the characters feel realistic and helped me understand how important close friends can be in our lives.

Overall, I really enjoyed watching it because it was **both entertaining and meaningful**. It allowed me to relax after a busy day, while also helping me improve my English listening skills.

---

## 🧩 Useful Sentence Structures

### 1. Relative clause
> I’d like to talk about a TV series called *Friends*, **which I watched about two years ago**.

**Structure:**
> Noun + which/who + extra information

---

### 2. Although sentence
> **Although** the story is quite simple, it is entertaining.

**Structure:**
> Although + subject + verb, subject + verb.

---

### 3. Whenever sentence
> **Whenever I had some free time**, I watched it by myself.

**Structure:**
> Whenever + situation, main sentence.

---

### 4. Since sentence
> **Since I had already watched several English-language series**, I gradually became comfortable with the dialogue.

**Structure:**
> Since + reason, result.

---

### 5. What-clause
> **What I particularly liked about the series was** the way it portrayed genuine friendship.

**Structure:**
> What I liked about X was + noun/idea.

---

### 6. Whenever + past situation
> **Whenever one of them faced a difficult situation**, the others were there to support them.

This is a very useful structure for explaining repeated situations.

---

### 7. While + -ing
> It allowed me to relax after a busy day, **while also helping me improve my English**.

**Structure:**
> Main idea + while + verb-ing.

# Cue Card 2: Describe a city that you think is very interesting or famous

### You should say:

- What city it is
- What it is famous for
- How you know about this city
- And explain why you think it is interesting or famous

---

## 🎯 Band 7+ Sample Answer

I’d like to talk about **Sylhet, which is my hometown and one of the most well-known cities in Bangladesh**. I have been living there for over 20 years, so I know the city extremely well.

Sylhet is particularly famous for its **natural beauty**. The city is surrounded by lush green landscapes and numerous tea gardens, which give the area a peaceful and refreshing atmosphere. Whenever people want to escape from their busy lives, they can visit these places to relax and enjoy nature.

One of the most famous attractions in Sylhet is **Jaflong**. Although it is located quite far from the main city, it is definitely worth visiting. It is well known for its river, stones, and breathtaking natural scenery. What makes it even more interesting is that you can see mountains on the Indian side of the border while traveling there from Bangladesh.

I think Sylhet is particularly interesting because it offers **a combination of natural beauty and a peaceful lifestyle**. Unlike crowded cities, it has plenty of open spaces where people can spend time in nature. In addition, the journey to places such as Jaflong is an enjoyable experience in itself because you can see beautiful landscapes and mountains along the way.

Overall, I consider Sylhet a fascinating city because of its **scenic beauty, tea gardens, and unique attractions such as Jaflong**. Since it is my hometown, I am also personally attached to it, which makes the city even more special to me.

---

# 🧩 Useful Sentence Structures

### 1. Relative clause
> Sylhet, **which is my hometown**, is one of the most well-known cities in Bangladesh.

**Structure:**
> Noun + which/who + additional information

---

### 2. Present perfect
> I **have been living there for over 20 years**, so I know the city extremely well.

**Structure:**
> Subject + have/has been + verb-ing + for + period.

---

### 3. Which + result
> The city is surrounded by lush green landscapes, **which give the area a peaceful atmosphere**.

This helps you connect ideas naturally without repeatedly using **“and.”**

---

### 4. Whenever
> **Whenever people want to escape from their busy lives**, they can visit these places to relax.

**Structure:**
> Whenever + situation, result.

---

### 5. Although
> **Although Jaflong is located quite far from the main city, it is definitely worth visiting.**

**Structure:**
> Although + contrast, main idea.

---

### 6. What-clause
> **What makes it even more interesting is that** you can see mountains on the Indian side of the border.

This is an excellent structure for Band 7+ speaking.

---

### 7. Because
> I think Sylhet is particularly interesting **because it offers a combination of natural beauty and a peaceful lifestyle**.

**Structure:**
> Main idea + because + reason.

---

### 8. Unlike
> **Unlike crowded cities**, Sylhet has plenty of open spaces where people can spend time in nature.

**Structure:**
> Unlike + noun, subject + verb.

---

### 9. In addition
> **In addition**, the journey to places such as Jaflong is an enjoyable experience in itself.

Use this to add another idea without repeatedly saying **“and.”**

---

### 10. Since
> **Since it is my hometown**, I am also personally attached to it.

**Structure:**
> Since + reason, result.

---

# 🔥 High-Value Structures to Practise

Try to use these structures naturally rather than memorising entire answers.

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| **which + verb** | It is a city **which attracts many visitors**. |
| **Although + clause** | **Although** it is far away, it is worth visiting. |
| **Whenever + clause** | **Whenever** I have free time, I watch it. |
| **Since + clause** | **Since** I have lived there for years, I know it well. |
| **What + clause** | **What I particularly like is** its peaceful atmosphere. |
| **Unlike + noun** | **Unlike** crowded cities, it is relatively peaceful. |
| **because + clause** | I like it **because** it is entertaining. |
| **while + -ing** | I can relax **while improving my English**. |
| **which + result** | It has many tea gardens, **which attract tourists**. |
| **not only...but also** | It is **not only** beautiful **but also** peaceful. |

# 🗣️ My Speaking Rule for You

Don't try to use difficult vocabulary in every sentence.

Instead, focus on:

**Simple vocabulary + accurate grammar + varied sentence structures + natural fluency**

For example:

❌ **I like it and it is beautiful and it is famous and many people visit it.**

✅ **I particularly like it because it offers breathtaking scenery. Although it is relatively quiet, many visitors come there to enjoy its natural beauty.**

The second version is better because the **sentence structures are varied**, not because the vocabulary is unnecessarily complicated.

---

# ⭐ Your Band 7 Speaking Formula

For each cue card, try to include:

1. **Direct introduction**
2. **Relative clause**
3. **Reason with because**
4. **Although/while contrast**
5. **Whenever/when**
6. **What-clause**
7. **One personal example**
8. **Short conclusion**

### Remember:

> **Don't memorise the answer. Memorise the structures.**

If you can use these structures naturally across different topics, your **Grammatical Range and Accuracy** and **Coherence and Cohesion** can improve considerably.
