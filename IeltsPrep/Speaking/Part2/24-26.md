# IELTS Speaking Part 2 – Cue Cards

## 24. Describe an interesting video

### You should say:
- When and where you watched it
- What it was about
- Why you watched it
- And explain how you felt about it

### Band 7+ Answer

I would like to talk about an interesting video that I watched last weekend. Since I had some free time, I decided to stay at home and watch a movie on YouTube.

The video was about the movie **KGF**, which is quite popular in my country. It is an action movie with an interesting storyline. The story is mainly about a powerful man who wants to become successful and gain control over a large kingdom. What I liked most about it was the combination of action, drama, and emotion.

I decided to watch it because I was looking for something entertaining to watch during my free time. I had heard about the movie from my friends, so I was curious to find out why it was so popular.

What impressed me most was the acting and the background music. The main actor gave a very energetic performance, and the dialogue delivery was also impressive. The visual effects made the story more engaging, and at several points, I felt completely absorbed in the movie.

Overall, I really enjoyed watching it because it was entertaining and kept me interested from beginning to end. I would definitely recommend it to someone who enjoys action movies with a strong storyline.

## 25. Describe a tall building you like or dislike

### You should say:
- What it is used for
- Where it is
- What it looks like
- And explain why you like or dislike it

### Band 7+ Answer

I would like to talk about a tall building called **Sadik Tower**, which is one of the tallest buildings in my city. It is located in a busy area of the city and is mainly used for offices and other commercial purposes.

I visited the building last week with one of my closest friends. We went there because we wanted to spend some quality time together and have lunch. The building has around fifteen floors, and we went to the rooftop to enjoy the view.

What I liked most about the building was the amazing view from the top. We stayed there until the evening, and as it became dark, I could see the lights of the entire city. It was a beautiful and memorable experience.

The building itself looks modern and well-designed. However, there is one thing I do not particularly like about it. I am not very comfortable with heights, so when I went to the rooftop, I felt a little nervous and uncomfortable.

Despite that, I really enjoyed visiting the building. The view from the top was fascinating, and spending time there with my friend made the experience even more enjoyable.

## 26. Describe a time when you worked in a group

### You should say:
- What you did
- Who you worked with
- What problems you faced
- And explain why you worked in the group

### Band 7+ Answer

I would like to talk about a time when I worked in a group as a software engineer. Since software development usually requires teamwork, I have worked with different teams several times.

One experience that I remember particularly well was last year when I worked on a software project with my team. There were three members in the team, and I was responsible for leading the development work. We also had a manager who guided us and assigned different tasks.

At the beginning, we faced several challenges. For example, we sometimes had different ideas about how to solve a particular technical problem. In addition, we had to complete some difficult tasks within a limited amount of time.

However, instead of working separately, we discussed the problems together and tried to find practical solutions. Each team member had different skills, so we could learn from one another and divide the workload effectively.

The main reason we worked as a group was that the project was too complex for one person to handle efficiently. By working together, we could complete the tasks more quickly and maintain the quality of the project.

Overall, it was a very useful experience for me because I learned that effective communication, cooperation, and mutual support are essential for achieving a common goal.
