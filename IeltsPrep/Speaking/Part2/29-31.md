# 29. Describe an important decision that you made

## Question

- What the decision was
- How you made your decision
- What the results of the decision were
- Why it was important

## Answer

I would like to talk about an important decision I made recently, which was to apply for the German Opportunity Card and try to find a job in Germany.

I first became interested in this idea when I was watching some videos on YouTube about people who had moved to Germany through this visa. I found their experiences quite interesting, so I decided to look into the process further. I also contacted one of my former colleagues who was already there, and he shared some useful information with me.

After thinking about it carefully, I decided to take the opportunity because I believed it could give me better career prospects. I was particularly interested in the possibility of earning a higher salary, having a better work-life balance, and working in a strong economy.

The decision was important to me because it could have a major impact on my professional and personal life. Although I knew that moving to another country would involve several challenges, I felt that it was worth taking the risk.

Overall, I considered it an important decision because it gave me a new direction and encouraged me to step outside my comfort zone.

# 30. Describe a person who works in a successful company

## Question

- Who he/she is
- What he/she does in the company
- What business the company does
- Why you think it is a successful company

## Answer

I would like to talk about my former CTO, Parvez Bhai, who worked at my company for around five years.

He was a very kind, supportive, and experienced person. Whenever we faced a technical problem, we could ask him for help. What I particularly liked about him was that he did not simply give us the answer. Instead, he explained the solution and showed us how to deal with a similar problem in the future.

Apart from his technical knowledge, he also understood the business side of the company very well. For example, whenever we had to communicate with clients, he was able to understand their requirements and suggest suitable solutions. He was also good at managing different situations and maintaining a positive relationship with clients.

I think the company was successful partly because of people like him. His experience, leadership skills, and willingness to support other employees made a significant difference.

Overall, I consider him a successful professional because he had both strong technical knowledge and excellent communication skills.

# 31. Describe a place you would like to visit in your free time

## Question

- Where it is
- What you will do there
- How long you will stay there
- Why you would like to visit it

## Answer

I would like to talk about Jaflong, which is a beautiful tourist destination in Sylhet. It is relatively close to my home, so it is quite convenient for me to visit.

If I had some free time, I would probably go there in the morning and stay until the evening. I would spend my time enjoying the natural scenery, taking photographs, walking along the river, and simply relaxing in the peaceful environment.

One of the main reasons I would like to visit Jaflong is its breathtaking natural beauty. The river, the surrounding hills, and the stone-covered areas create a fascinating landscape. What I particularly enjoy is the mountain view near the Bangladesh-India border. The scenery from the Bangladeshi side is absolutely stunning.

Another reason is that the journey itself is enjoyable. While travelling there, I can see beautiful scenery along the road, which makes the trip more interesting.

Overall, I would love to visit Jaflong again because it is peaceful, beautiful, and relatively easy for me to reach.

# ⭐ Universal Cue Card Framework

## 1. Introduction

I would like to talk about [person/place/decision/event].

## 2. Background

I first came across it when...

At that time...

As far as I remember,...

## 3. Main Details

First of all,...

One of the main reasons was that...

What I particularly liked was...

## 4. Example

For example,...

To give you an example,...

I remember that...

## 5. Result

As a result,...

Because of this,...

This made me feel...

## 6. Personal Opinion

What I particularly liked about it was...

Another reason was that...

I found it particularly interesting because...

## 7. Conclusion

Overall, I would say that...

Looking back,...

It was important/special/memorable to me because...
