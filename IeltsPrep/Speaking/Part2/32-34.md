# IELTS Speaking Part 2 — Cue Cards 32, 33 & 34
## Band 6.5–7 Sample Answers

---

# 32. Describe a food that people eat on special occasions/events

### You should say:
- What it is
- What the special event/occasion is
- How it is cooked/made
- And explain why people eat it on that special occasion/event

## Sample Answer

I would like to talk about **biryani**, which is one of the most popular foods in Bangladesh. It is a rice dish usually made with meat, such as chicken or beef, along with spices, potatoes, and other ingredients.

People often eat biryani on **special occasions such as weddings, Eid celebrations, family gatherings, and birthday parties**. In fact, I would say that a celebration in Bangladesh often feels incomplete without a good plate of biryani.

To prepare it, first, the meat is cooked with different spices, onions, and yogurt. Then, rice is partly boiled separately. After that, the rice and meat are arranged in layers and cooked together on low heat. This allows the flavors to mix properly and gives the dish a wonderful smell.

The main reason people eat biryani on special occasions is that it is **rich, delicious, and suitable for serving a large number of people**. Moreover, preparing biryani can bring family members together because several people can help with the cooking.

For me, biryani is not just a tasty meal. **It is also connected with our celebrations, family gatherings, and happy memories**, which is why I think it is such a popular choice for special occasions.

# 33. Describe a live sports event you watched and liked

### You should say:
- What it was
- When and where you watched it
- Who you watched it with
- And explain why you liked it

## Sample Answer

I would like to talk about a **live cricket match** that I watched a few years ago. It was a local cricket tournament in my hometown, and although it was not a professional international match, I really enjoyed watching it.

I watched the match at a **local cricket ground with a few of my friends**. We went there in the afternoon and found a place near the boundary. There were quite a lot of people in the audience, so the atmosphere was lively and exciting.

The match was quite competitive. At first, one team was clearly ahead, but the other team started scoring runs quickly towards the end. In the final few overs, everyone became extremely excited because the result was still uncertain. Eventually, the team I was supporting won the match by a small margin.

I particularly liked the event because **watching a sport live is completely different from watching it on television**. I could hear the crowd cheering, see the players celebrating, and feel the excitement whenever someone scored a boundary or took a wicket.

Another reason I enjoyed it was that I was with my friends. We were constantly discussing the match and supporting our favorite team.

Overall, it was a **simple but memorable experience**, and it made me realize how enjoyable live sports can be.

# 34. Describe a person you know who would like to choose a career in the medical field

### You should say:
- Who this person is / When you knew him/her
- When he/she started to think about that
- What he/she would like to do
- And explain why he/she would like to choose this career

## Sample Answer

I would like to talk about **one of my close friends, named Rahim**, who has always been interested in the medical profession. I have known him since our school days, so I have seen how his career plans have developed over the years.

He started thinking seriously about becoming a doctor when he was in **secondary school**. His interest became stronger after one of his family members became seriously ill. During that period, he spent quite a lot of time in the hospital and saw how doctors helped patients. I think this experience had a strong influence on his career choice.

He would like to become a **doctor, particularly a physician**, because he wants to help people and do something meaningful for society. He is also interested in biology and enjoys learning about the human body and different diseases.

Another reason he wants to choose this career is that he believes doctors have a **direct impact on people's lives**. He told me that helping someone recover from an illness would give him a great sense of satisfaction.

I think he would be suitable for this profession because he is **patient, responsible, and hardworking**. He also communicates well with other people, which I believe is very important for a doctor.

Overall, I admire his ambition because becoming a doctor requires years of study and a great deal of dedication, but he is genuinely motivated to pursue it.

# Reusable IELTS Speaking Part 2 Phrases

## Introduction

- I would like to talk about...
- I am going to describe...
- The first thing that comes to mind is...
- One experience/person/place that I clearly remember is...

## Giving Background

- I have known about it for...
- I first came across it when...
- It happened a few years ago...
- It is particularly popular in...

## Giving Reasons

- The main reason is that...
- Another reason is that...
- One of the main factors is...
- What makes it special is that...

## Describing Feelings

- I found it really memorable because...
- I was extremely excited because...
- I felt really happy and satisfied...
- It was an unforgettable experience for me.

## Adding Details

- For example,...
- In fact,...
- To be more specific,...
- What I particularly remember is...
- Another thing worth mentioning is...

## Ending

- Overall, I would say that...
- All in all, it was a memorable experience.
- That is why it is so special to me.
- Looking back, I still remember it very clearly.

---

# Simple Part 2 Structure

## 1. Introduction
**I would like to talk about + topic.**

## 2. Background
**When / Where / Who / What**

## 3. Main Details
**What happened / How it happened / What it was like**

## 4. Reasons
**The main reason + Another reason + Example**

## 5. Feelings
**How you felt + Why**

## 6. Conclusion
**Overall, I would say that...**

# Band 6.5–7 Strategy

### Focus on:

1. **Grammar accuracy**
2. **Fluent delivery**
3. **Clear ideas**
4. **Relevant examples**
5. **Natural collocations**
6. **A mixture of simple and complex sentences**

### Avoid:

- Trying to use extremely difficult vocabulary
- Memorizing every sentence word-for-word
- Repeating "and" too frequently
- Using too many fillers such as "um", "uh", and "you know"
- Giving a very short answer

### Target Speaking Time

**1 minute 30 seconds – 2 minutes**

### Golden Rule

> **Simple + accurate + fluent is better than complicated + inaccurate.**
