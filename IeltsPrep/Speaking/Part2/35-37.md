# IELTS Speaking Part 2 — Cue Cards

## 35. Describe a child who loves drawing/painting

### You should say:

* Who he/she is
* How/when you knew him/her
* How often he/she draws/paints
* And explain why you think he/she loves drawing/painting

### Sample Answer

I’d like to talk about my cousin, **Rafi**, who is really interested in drawing and painting. He is about ten years old, and I have known him since he was a little child because we are quite close.

I first noticed his interest in drawing when I visited his house a few years ago. He showed me some of his drawings, and I was actually surprised because they were quite good for his age. He mainly draws **animals, natural scenery, houses, and cartoon characters**.

As far as I know, he draws almost every day, especially in the afternoon after finishing his schoolwork. Sometimes he spends an hour or two drawing. At weekends, he usually has more free time, so he may spend several hours on a painting.

I think he loves drawing because **it gives him a way to express his imagination**. He is a rather quiet child, so instead of talking a lot, he likes to express his ideas through pictures. Another reason is that his parents always encourage him and appreciate his work. They have even bought him some good-quality pencils and colours.

Overall, I think drawing is not just a hobby for him. **It is something that makes him happy and allows him to be creative.**

## 36. Describe one of your friends who learned something without a teacher

### You should say:

* Who he/she is
* What he/she learned
* Why he/she learned this
* And explain whether it would be easier to learn from a teacher

### Sample Answer

I’d like to talk about one of my close friends, **Sakib**, who taught himself how to play the guitar without taking lessons from a professional teacher.

He became interested in the guitar when he was at university. One of his friends used to play the guitar, and after watching him, Sakib also wanted to learn it. However, he didn't have enough money or time to join a music school, so he decided to learn it by himself.

He started by watching **YouTube tutorials** and practising basic chords. At first, it was quite difficult for him because he didn't know how to hold the guitar properly or change between chords. However, he was very patient and practised almost every day.

He learned the guitar mainly because **he wanted to have a new hobby and entertain his friends**. He also enjoyed listening to songs and wanted to play some of his favourite songs himself.

As for whether it would be easier to learn from a teacher, I think it probably would be. A teacher could immediately point out his mistakes and show him the correct technique. However, learning by himself also taught him to be patient and independent.

So, I think **having a teacher is faster, but self-learning can make a person more independent and motivated**.

## 37. Talk about a time when you gave advice to someone

### You should say:

* Who you gave advice to
* What the advice was
* Why you gave the advice
* And how you felt about the advice

### Sample Answer

I’d like to talk about a time when I gave some advice to one of my close friends. It happened a few months ago when he was feeling stressed about his career.

At that time, he was thinking about leaving his job because he wasn't happy with his work. However, he wasn't completely sure whether leaving was the right decision. He asked me what I thought he should do.

I advised him **not to make a quick decision**. I told him that before leaving his current job, he should look for another opportunity and make a proper plan. I also suggested that he should improve some of his professional skills because that would give him more options in the future.

I gave him this advice because I had known him for several years, and I knew that he was making the decision mainly because he was frustrated. I thought that if he waited for a while and considered all the possible consequences, he could make a better decision.

After giving him the advice, I felt quite happy because I felt that I had been able to support my friend. A few weeks later, he told me that he had followed my advice and started looking for a new job before resigning.

So, overall, **I felt satisfied because my advice was useful and helped my friend make a more sensible decision.**

# Useful Speaking Structure

You can use this simple structure for almost any Part 2 cue card:

### 1. Introduction

> I’d like to talk about...

### 2. Background

> I first knew/met/noticed him/her when...

### 3. Main details

> As far as I know, he/she usually...

> One thing I remember is that...

### 4. Reason

> I think the main reason is that...

> Another reason is that...

### 5. Personal feeling

> I felt quite happy/surprised/excited because...

### 6. Conclusion

> Overall, I think...
