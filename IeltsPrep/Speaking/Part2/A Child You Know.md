# IELTS Speaking Cue Card — A Child You Know

## Topic

**Describe a child that you know and like a lot.**

## AAA Method

### A — Answer

I'd like to talk about **my little cousin, Rafi**, whom I know quite well and really like. He is around eight years old, and I have known him since he was very young. He is a cheerful, curious, and energetic child.

### A — Add Details

What I like most about him is his personality. He is extremely friendly and loves talking to people. He is also very curious and always asks questions whenever he sees something new. Sometimes, his questions are actually quite funny because he wants to know the reason behind everything.

Another thing I like about him is that he is very helpful. Whenever someone in the family needs something, he is usually willing to help. He also enjoys playing football and spending time with other children.

### A — Anecdote

I particularly remember one day when I was working on my laptop and he came and asked me what I was doing. I explained that I was working on a programming problem. He became very curious and started asking me how computers understand instructions. I tried to explain it in a simple way, and he listened very carefully.

What made me laugh was that after a few minutes, he said that he wanted to become a programmer when he grew up. I found that really cute because he was genuinely interested in what I was doing.

I really like him because **he is cheerful, curious, and kind-hearted**. I also enjoy spending time with him because his energy and positive attitude always make me feel happy.
