# IELTS Speaking Part 2: A Person Who Likes to Look After the Natural World

## Cue Card

### Describe a person who likes to look after the natural world.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- What he or she does
- How he or she does it
- How often he or she does it
- And explain how you feel about this person

---

# Band 7+ Sample Answer

## A — Answer

I'd like to talk about **my uncle, who really cares about nature and likes looking after the environment**. He is a calm, responsible, and kind-hearted person, and I've known him since I was a child.

---

## A — Add Details

What I like most about him is that **he is very serious about protecting nature**. He has a small garden around his house where he grows different kinds of flowers, vegetables, and fruit trees.

He makes sure that the plants get enough water and sunlight. He also tries not to waste water and avoids using too many chemicals because he believes they can damage the soil and the environment.

Whenever he sees plastic or rubbish around his neighbourhood, he usually picks it up and puts it in the proper place. He also encourages other family members to keep their surroundings clean.

He normally spends **a few hours every weekend** taking care of his garden and doing other small things to help the environment.

---

## A — Anecdote

I particularly remember **one weekend when I visited his house**. He asked me to help him plant a few small trees in his garden. At first, I wasn't very interested because I thought it would be tiring.

However, while we were planting them, he explained how trees provide oxygen, reduce heat, and create a better environment for birds and other animals.

He also told me that **even small actions can make a difference if many people do them**. I found that idea quite interesting, and it really stayed with me.

---

## How I Feel About This Person

I really admire my uncle because **he doesn't just talk about protecting nature; he actually takes action**.

I think his lifestyle has influenced me to become more careful about the environment. Overall, I feel inspired by him, and I hope more people develop the same sense of responsibility towards nature.

---

# IELTS Speaking Part 3

## 1. Do you think parents should teach their children how to protect the environment?

Yes, definitely. I think parents are the first teachers in a child's life, so they should teach children simple environmental habits from an early age. For example, they can teach them not to waste water, to avoid littering, and to recycle things when possible. These small habits can become part of their daily lives.

---

## 2. Do you think protecting the environment should be the responsibility of governments?

Yes, I think governments have a major responsibility because they have the power to make laws and introduce large-scale environmental policies. For example, they can control pollution, protect forests, and improve waste management.

However, I don't think governments can solve everything alone. Individuals and businesses also need to play their part.

---

## 3. What are the most effective ways to protect the environment in your country?

I think there are several effective ways. First, the government should improve waste management and reduce plastic pollution. Second, people should be encouraged to use public transport instead of private cars.

Another important step is planting more trees and protecting existing forests. In my opinion, education is also essential because people are more likely to protect nature when they understand its importance.

---

## 4. Which do you think people prefer, rewards or punishment, when it comes to government intervention in environmental protection?

I think people generally prefer **rewards**, because positive encouragement can motivate them to develop good habits. For example, the government could provide incentives to people who recycle or use environmentally friendly products.

However, punishment is also necessary in serious cases, such as illegal dumping or destroying forests. So, I think a combination of rewards and reasonable punishment would be most effective.

---

## 5. Is it easy for children in cities to get close to the natural world?

Not always. Children in cities often spend a lot of time indoors using phones, computers, or watching television. They may also have fewer opportunities to see forests, farms, and wildlife.

However, parents and schools can take them to parks, gardens, or natural areas. Even small activities such as growing plants at home can help children develop a connection with nature.

---

## 6. What can people do to protect the natural world?

There are many simple things people can do. They can reduce plastic use, avoid wasting water and electricity, recycle whenever possible, and use public transport more often.

They can also plant trees and keep their neighbourhoods clean. These actions may seem small, but if millions of people follow them, they can have a significant impact on the environment.

---

## 7. Is it important to teach students environmental protection at school?

Yes, absolutely. I think schools have an important role because children spend a large part of their lives there.

Students can learn about pollution, recycling, climate change, and the importance of protecting wildlife. Schools can also organise practical activities, such as planting trees or cleaning local areas.

In my opinion, practical education is particularly effective because students can understand the importance of environmental protection through real experience.
