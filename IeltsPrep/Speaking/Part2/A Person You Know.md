# IELTS Speaking Cue Card — A Person You Know

## Topic

**Describe a person you know.**

## AAA Method

### A — Answer

I'd like to talk about one of my closest friends, **Tanju**. We met at Metropolitan University back in 2019, and we've been friends ever since. He is a really friendly, honest and talented person.

### A — Add Details

When I first met him, I was immediately impressed by his personality and the way he treated other people. He was always friendly and willing to help his classmates. **What I admired most about him was his programming ability.** He was very good at solving programming problems, and whenever someone was struggling, he would take the time to help.

He also helped us with our studies. Whenever we got stuck on an assignment or a difficult task, he would sit with us and explain it step by step. **The best thing was that he didn't just give us the answer. He explained why the solution worked, so we could solve similar problems by ourselves in the future.**

### A — Anecdote

I particularly remember one programming contest that we participated in as a team. We had to solve several difficult problems within a limited amount of time, so there was quite a lot of pressure. At one point, I was struggling with a particularly difficult problem, and I was starting to lose confidence.

Tanju noticed that I was struggling, so he sat beside me and explained the main idea step by step. He didn't solve the problem for me. Instead, he guided me towards the solution and encouraged me to figure it out myself. **Eventually, I managed to solve the problem, and I felt incredibly relieved and proud of myself.**

**That experience made me realise that Tanju wasn't just a talented programmer; he was also a genuinely supportive friend who wanted to see other people succeed. That's one of the main reasons I really respect and appreciate him.**

## Useful Phrases

- **one of my closest friends**
- **we've been friends ever since**
- **I was immediately impressed by...**
- **what I admired most was...**
- **he was always willing to help**
- **whenever we got stuck...**
- **step by step**
- **there was quite a lot of pressure**
- **I was starting to lose confidence**
- **he genuinely wanted to see other people succeed**

## Speaking Strategy

Don't memorise the entire answer. Instead, remember the **three A's**:

1. **Answer** → Who is the person?
2. **Add Details** → Describe the person and your relationship.
3. **Anecdote** → Tell a specific story that explains why you like or respect them.

> **Goal:** Speak naturally for approximately **1.5–2 minutes** without memorising every sentence.
