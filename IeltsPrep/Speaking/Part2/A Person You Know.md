# IELTS Speaking Cue Card

## Topic: A Person You Know

### Cue Card

**Describe an old person that you know.**

You should say:

- What your relationship is with this person
- How often you see them
- What people think about this person

**And explain why you like them.**

---

## 🧠 AAA Structure

### A — Answer

> I'd like to talk about one of my closest friends, **Tanju**, whom I met when we were studying together at a military college back in **2018 or 2019**. We've been friends ever since, and over the years, I've come to know him as a genuinely honest, loyal, and talented person.

### A — Add Details

> When I first met him, I was immediately impressed by his personality and the way he interacted with other people. He was extremely friendly and always willing to help his classmates. What I particularly admired about him was his ability to solve programming problems. He was really good at programming, and he never hesitated to teach others whenever they were struggling with something.
>
> Apart from programming, he was also very supportive when it came to our studies. Whenever we got stuck on a particular assignment or task, he would willingly sit with us and explain the problem step by step. What I really appreciated was that he didn't simply give us the answer. Instead, he tried to make us understand **why** a particular solution worked and how we could approach similar problems in the future.

### A — Anecdote

> I particularly remember participating in several programming contests with him as part of a team. We had to solve complicated problems within a limited amount of time, so naturally, there was quite a lot of pressure. On one occasion, I was struggling with a particularly difficult problem and was beginning to lose confidence. Tanju noticed this and encouraged me not to give up.
>
> He sat beside me and explained the underlying concept step by step. Rather than solving the problem for me, he guided me towards the solution and allowed me to figure it out myself. Eventually, I managed to solve it, and I felt incredibly relieved and proud of myself. That experience showed me that he wasn't just a talented programmer; he was also someone who genuinely wanted other people to succeed.

### ⭐ Why I Like Him

> That's probably the main reason why I value his friendship so much. He's not only intelligent and talented, but he's also incredibly supportive, generous with his knowledge, and encouraging towards the people around him. I think having a friend like Tanju has had a very positive influence on me, both academically and personally, and that's why I consider him one of the most valuable friends I've ever had.

## 📌 Useful Vocabulary

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| genuinely honest | sincerely honest |
| extremely approachable | easy to talk to |
| willing to help | ready to assist others |
| struggle with something | have difficulty doing something |
| underlying concept | the fundamental idea behind something |
| guide someone towards | help someone find the answer |
| lose confidence | become less confident |
| generous with his knowledge | willing to share what he knows |
| have a positive influence | affect someone in a beneficial way |
| value someone's friendship | consider someone's friendship important |

---

## 🗣️ Speaking Strategy

**A — Answer:** Introduce the person.

**A — Add Details:** Describe the person, your relationship, and relevant characteristics.

**A — Anecdote:** Tell one specific story or experience.

**Final:** Explain why the person is important or memorable.

### Quick Formula

> **Who → When/Where → Characteristics → Example/Story → Feelings → Why**

---

## 🎯 Target

- **Target Band:** 7+
- **Speaking Time:** 1:45–2:00 minutes
- **Method:** AAA — Answer → Add Details → Anecdote
- **Focus:** Fluency, natural vocabulary, grammatical accuracy, and storytelling
