# IELTS Speaking Part 2: A Problem Using an Electronic Device

## Cue Card

### Describe a time when you had a problem using an electronic device.

You should say:

- When it happened
- Where it happened
- What the problem was
- And explain how you solved the problem at last

---

# Band 7+ Sample Answer

## A — Answer

I'd like to talk about **a problem I had with my laptop**. It happened about a year ago when I was working on an important software project. I was at home and had to finish some work that evening.

---

## A — Add Details

The problem started quite suddenly. My laptop became extremely slow, and some applications stopped responding. After a few minutes, the screen froze completely, and I couldn't use the mouse or keyboard properly.

At first, I thought it was just a temporary problem, so I waited for a few minutes and tried restarting the laptop. Unfortunately, it didn't solve the problem. I became quite worried because I had some important work to finish.

I checked the storage and noticed that my laptop was almost completely full. I also had many applications running in the background.

---

## A — Anecdote

I particularly remember **how nervous I felt when the laptop suddenly froze**. I was afraid that I might lose the work I had already completed.

Fortunately, I had saved most of my files, so I didn't lose anything important. I searched online for some possible solutions and found that freeing up storage space could improve the laptop's performance.

I deleted some unnecessary files, removed a few programs I no longer used, and restarted the laptop. After that, it worked much better.

---

## How I Felt

I felt **really relieved** when the laptop started working normally again. The experience also taught me to keep my device organised and back up important files regularly.

Overall, it was a frustrating experience at first, but it was also useful because I learned how to deal with a common technical problem without immediately asking someone else for help.

---

# IELTS Speaking Part 3: Electronic Devices

## 1. Why are people keen on buying new electronic devices?

I think there are several reasons. First, technology develops very quickly, so new devices often have better features and improved performance. People may also want to replace their old devices because they become slow or outdated.

Another reason is advertising. Companies often use attractive advertisements to convince people that they need the latest products. For some people, buying a new device is also a way of keeping up with modern trends.

---

## 2. What impact do electronic devices have on people?

Electronic devices have both positive and negative effects. On the positive side, they make communication, education, and work much easier. For example, people can attend online classes, work remotely, and communicate with others instantly.

However, excessive use can cause problems. People may become too dependent on their phones or computers and spend less time interacting face-to-face. It can also reduce physical activity and make it harder for people to concentrate.

So, I think electronic devices are extremely useful, but people need to use them **in moderation**.
