# IELTS Speaking Part 2: A Story or Book with Animals in It

## Cue Card

### Describe a story or book with animals in it.

You should say:

- What animals are in it
- What the story/book is about
- Why you read the story/book
- And explain what you think of this story/book

---

# Band 7+ Sample Answer

## A — Answer

I'd like to talk about **The Jungle Book**, which is a famous story with several animals in it. I read it when I was a child, and I still remember some parts of the story quite clearly.

---

## A — Add Details

The main character is **Mowgli**, a young boy who grows up in the jungle. There are several important animals in the story, such as **Baloo the bear, Bagheera the panther, and Shere Khan the tiger**.

The story is mainly about Mowgli's life in the jungle and his relationship with different animals. Baloo and Bagheera take care of him and try to protect him from Shere Khan.

I first read the story because my parents recommended it to me. I was interested in animals at that age, so the story immediately caught my attention. I also liked the idea of a child living in the jungle and communicating with animals.

---

## A — Anecdote

I particularly remember **reading the story before going to bed when I was a child**. I was always curious to find out what would happen to Mowgli next.

One thing I really liked was the friendship between Mowgli, Baloo, and Bagheera. They were very different from each other, but they still supported and protected one another.

I also remember feeling a little nervous whenever Shere Khan appeared because he was presented as a dangerous and powerful animal.

---

## What I Think About the Story

Overall, I really enjoyed the story because **it was entertaining but also taught me some useful lessons about friendship, courage, loyalty, and responsibility**.

I think it is a great story for children because it combines adventure with meaningful life lessons. Even though I read it many years ago, I still remember it quite well.

---

# IELTS Speaking Part 3: Animals

## 1. Should schools teach children about animals?

Yes, definitely. I think children should learn about animals because it helps them understand nature and develop respect for living creatures. Schools can teach them about different species, their habitats, and the importance of protecting wildlife.

I also think practical activities, such as visiting zoos or nature centres, could make these lessons more interesting.

---

## 2. Some people think pets should not be kept in cities. What do you think?

I don't completely agree with this view. I think people can keep pets in cities as long as they have enough space and can take proper care of them.

However, people living in small apartments should consider whether they can provide enough exercise, food, and attention. So, I think responsible ownership is more important than simply banning pets in cities.

---

## 3. Many people regard pets as members of their family. What do you think?

I completely understand why people feel that way. Pets can become very close to their owners because they provide companionship and emotional support.

People often spend years with their pets, so naturally they can develop a strong emotional connection with them. In my opinion, treating a pet like a family member is fine as long as people also understand that animals have their own needs.

---

## 4. Do many people keep pets in your country?

Yes, many people do, although it is probably more common in cities and among younger people. Cats and birds are particularly popular because they are relatively easy to keep.

However, some families prefer not to keep pets because of the cost, limited space, or cultural and practical reasons.

---

## 5. What are the advantages of keeping a pet?

There are several advantages. First, pets can provide **companionship**, especially for people who live alone. They can also help people reduce stress and feel less lonely.

Another benefit is that taking care of a pet can teach children responsibility. For example, they can learn to feed the animal, clean its living area, and look after its health.

---

## 6. Why do people always tell children stories with animals?

I think animals make stories more **interesting and easier for children to understand**. Children naturally find animals fascinating, so they are more likely to pay attention to a story when animals are involved.

Also, animals can represent different qualities. For example, a lion might represent courage, while a fox might represent cleverness. This allows children to learn moral lessons in an entertaining way.
