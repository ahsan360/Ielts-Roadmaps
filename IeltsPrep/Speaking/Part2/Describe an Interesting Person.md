# IELTS Speaking Part 2 — Describe an Interesting Person

## Cue Card

**Describe a person that you think is very interesting.**

You should say:
- Who this person is
- How you met them
- What they like to talk about
- And explain why you think this person is interesting

---

## AAA Method

**AAA = Answer → Add Details → Add an Example**

### 1. Answer — Who is this person?

I would like to talk about my friend, **Taju**, whom I met when we were at university studying **Computer Science and Engineering**.

He is a very **knowledgeable, supportive, and hardworking** person. From the very beginning, I found him quite interesting because he had a different way of thinking and was always willing to help others.

### 2. Add Details — How did you meet him?

I first met him on the **very first day of university**. I was sitting at a desk when he came and sat right beside me, and that was how we first met.

As we gradually became friends, I discovered that he was particularly good at **problem-solving and competitive programming**. He had been solving difficult algorithmic problems since his first year.

Whenever someone was struggling with a programming problem, he would patiently explain the solution and help them understand where they had gone wrong.

### 3. Add Details — What does he like to talk about?

He particularly likes talking about **technology, artificial intelligence, software development, and competitive programming**.

He can talk about these subjects for hours because he is genuinely curious about how technology works and how it might develop in the future.

What I particularly admire is that he can explain complicated technical ideas in a **simple and understandable way**.

### 4. Add an Example — A Memorable Conversation

I particularly remember a conversation we had about **artificial intelligence**.

At that time, I only had a basic understanding of AI, but Taju explained several concepts in great detail. He also talked about how AI might develop in the future and how it could potentially change our lives.

I was genuinely impressed by his knowledge because he could discuss a complicated subject so confidently and explain his ideas with relevant examples.

### 5. Conclusion — Why is he interesting?

Overall, I find Taju very interesting because he is **intelligent, curious, knowledgeable, and supportive**.

He is not only a good friend but also someone I can learn a lot from. Whenever I have a conversation with him, I usually learn something new.

**I hope that one day I can become as talented and supportive as he is.**
