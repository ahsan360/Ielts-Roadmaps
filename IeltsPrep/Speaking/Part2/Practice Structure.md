# IELTS Speaking: Replacing “And” — Practice Structure

## How to use this practice

When you are about to say **“and”**, pause briefly and ask:

> **What is the relationship between these two ideas?**

Then choose the appropriate linker.

---

# 1. Adding Another Idea

### Useful linkers
- also
- besides that
- in addition
- another thing is that

### Examples

1. I usually travel with my friends. **Besides that,** I sometimes travel with my family.
2. Jaflong has beautiful scenery. **In addition,** it has several attractive tourist spots.
3. I enjoy travelling by car. **Another thing is that** I can stop whenever I see an interesting place.
4. I like visiting new places. **Also,** I enjoy trying local food.

### Practice

- I enjoy travelling, and I like trying local food.
- I often practise English, and I watch English videos.
- My uncle speaks English, and he can speak German.

---

# 2. Giving a Reason

### Useful linkers
- because
- since
- as
- mainly because

### Examples

1. I recommend Jaflong **because** it has breathtaking natural scenery.
2. I enjoy travelling with my friends **since** we always have a great time together.
3. I want to learn German **mainly because** I may need it when I go to Germany.
4. I practise English every day **as** I want to become a more confident speaker.

### Practice

- I recommend this place, and it is very peaceful.
- I practise speaking every day, and I want to improve my fluency.
- I want to work for a technology company, and I enjoy solving difficult problems.

---

# 3. Giving an Example

### Useful linkers
- for example
- for instance
- particularly
- one example is

### Examples

1. Jaflong has many beautiful natural attractions. **For example,** you can see tea gardens and lush greenery.
2. I enjoy learning languages. **For instance,** I recently started learning German.
3. There are many ways to improve your English. **One example is** practising with a speaking partner.
4. I have several long-term goals. **One example is** working for a major technology company.

### Practice

- There are many beautiful places in Bangladesh, and Jaflong is one of them.
- I enjoy learning new skills, and programming is a good example.
- There are several ways to improve English, and speaking practice is one of them.

---

# 4. Showing a Result

### Useful linkers
- so
- therefore
- as a result
- because of this
- that's why

### Examples

1. The journey was extremely scenic. **As a result,** I really enjoyed the trip.
2. I practise speaking English every day. **Because of this,** I have become more confident.
3. The place was incredibly peaceful. **Therefore,** I felt completely relaxed.
4. I made several mistakes while speaking. **That's why** I decided to practise more regularly.

### Practice

- The scenery was breathtaking, and I felt relaxed.
- I practise every day, and my fluency has improved.
- The journey was long, and I was tired when I arrived.

---

# 5. Showing Contrast

### Useful linkers
- however
- although
- whereas
- while
- on the other hand

### Examples

1. Jaflong is quite far from my home. **However,** I still enjoy visiting it.
2. **Although** learning German is challenging, I am determined to continue.
3. I prefer travelling with friends, **whereas** my brother prefers travelling alone.
4. **On the other hand,** travelling alone gives you more freedom.

### Practice

- Jaflong is far, and I still enjoy going there.
- Learning German is difficult, and I want to continue.
- I like travelling with friends, and my brother prefers travelling alone.

---

# 6. Showing Sequence

### Useful linkers
- first
- then
- after that
- eventually
- finally

### Examples

1. **First,** we travelled to Jaflong by car.
2. **Then,** we stopped at a beautiful viewpoint.
3. **After that,** we explored the surrounding area.
4. **Eventually,** we reached our final destination.
5. **Finally,** we returned home in the evening.

### Practice

- We arrived, and we explored the area, and we took photographs.
- I woke up, and I had breakfast, and I went to work.
- We reached the hotel, and we checked in, and we went outside.

---

# The Golden Rule

Do **not** try to eliminate **“and”** completely.

Instead, avoid using it repeatedly when the ideas have a different logical relationship.

## Weak

> I went to Jaflong with my friends **and** we saw tea gardens **and** we took photographs **and** I enjoyed the journey.

## Better

> **First,** I travelled to Jaflong with my friends. **During the journey,** we saw beautiful tea gardens and lush greenery. **After that,** we explored the area and took several photographs. **As a result,** I really enjoyed the entire experience.

---

# Quick Decision Guide

When you want to say **“and”**, ask:

| Relationship | Use |
|---|---|
| Add information | also / besides that / in addition |
| Give a reason | because / since / mainly because |
| Give an example | for example / for instance |
| Show a result | so / therefore / as a result |
| Show contrast | however / although / whereas |
| Show sequence | first / then / after that / finally |

---

# IELTS Speaking Practice

## Step 1 — Find “and”

Say:

> “I went to Jaflong with my friends, **and** we took lots of photographs.”

## Step 2 — Identify the relationship

Ask:

> Are the two ideas simply being added?

Yes → use an **adding linker**.

## Step 3 — Replace it

> “I went to Jaflong with my friends. **Besides that,** we took lots of photographs.”

## Step 4 — Practise naturally

Do not memorise the entire sentence. Focus on the **relationship between the ideas**.

---

# Your Target

During IELTS Speaking, aim for:

**Idea → Relationship → Appropriate linker → Next idea**

Instead of:

**Idea → and → idea → and → idea → and → idea**

That will make your speech more **coherent, logically organised, and natural**.
