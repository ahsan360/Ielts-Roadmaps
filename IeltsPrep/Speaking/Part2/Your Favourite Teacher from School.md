# Describe Your Favourite Teacher from School

## A — Answer

I'd like to talk about my favourite teacher, **Mr. Hajur**, whom I met when I was studying at university. He was a very intelligent, friendly, and supportive teacher.

## A — Add Details

He was quite tall and handsome, and he always looked smart and professional. He taught us **Artificial Intelligence**, which was quite a difficult subject for us.

However, he made the subject much easier because he explained everything in a simple way and used practical examples. What I liked most about him was his supportive nature. Whenever we got stuck on an assignment or faced a difficult problem, he would patiently listen to us and guide us towards a solution.

The best thing was that he didn't simply give us the answer. Instead, he explained how the solution worked and how we could deal with similar problems in the future.

## A — Anecdote

I particularly remember one occasion when a group of students were struggling to find suitable data for their project. They asked our teacher for help, and he patiently listened to their problem.

He then suggested a practical solution and explained how they could approach similar problems in the future. His explanation helped them understand what they needed to do.

That experience made me realise that he was not only knowledgeable about his subject, but he was also genuinely supportive. **He really wanted his students to learn and succeed, and that's why he remains one of my favourite teachers.**
