# Topic

**Describe an advertisement with a famous person in it.**

Who the person is

Where you can see it

What the advertisement is about

And explain how you feel about the advertisement

## AAA Method

### A — Answer

I'd like to talk about an advertisement featuring **Shakib Al Hasan**, one of the most well-known cricketers in Bangladesh. I first came across the advertisement on **YouTube and Facebook**, and it was promoting a mobile financial service. I remember it because the advertisement was simple, catchy and featured someone whom almost everyone in Bangladesh recognises.

### A — Add Details

The advertisement was mainly about **encouraging people to use the company's digital payment and money-transfer services**. Shakib appeared in different situations and explained how convenient the service was for everyday transactions.

What I found particularly interesting was the way the company used his popularity to establish **trust and credibility**. Since he is a highly recognisable public figure, people are naturally more likely to pay attention when he appears in an advertisement.

Another thing I liked was that the advertisement wasn't excessively long. **It delivered its message clearly without providing too much unnecessary information.** The visuals were also quite attractive, and the overall presentation made the service appear modern and convenient.

### A — Anecdote

I particularly remember seeing this advertisement several times while I was watching videos online. Initially, I didn't pay much attention to it, but after seeing Shakib in it, I became curious about what the company was offering.

What made me remember the advertisement was actually a conversation I had with one of my friends. We were discussing whether celebrities genuinely influence people's purchasing decisions, and we realised that we could both remember several products simply because a famous person had endorsed them.

**That experience made me realise how powerful celebrity endorsement can be in advertising.** Personally, I think the advertisement was effective, although I don't believe people should trust a product solely because a celebrity promotes it.
