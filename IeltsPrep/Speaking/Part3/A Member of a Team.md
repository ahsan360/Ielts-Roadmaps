# Question 1

## In a team, is it more important to pursue individual development or to achieve team targets?

### 💡 Ideas

- Team goals come first
- Individual growth is still important
- Balance both

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- collective success
- shared objective
- individual strengths
- personal development
- common goal

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think achieving team targets should be the top priority because every member is working towards a shared objective. If individuals focus only on their personal success, the overall performance of the team may suffer. However, personal development is also important because improving individual skills ultimately benefits the whole team. In my opinion, the most successful teams are those that strike a balance between collective success and individual growth.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Balanced opinion
- Well-developed explanation
- Advanced vocabulary

# Question 2

## Do you think it's important for children to join teams to learn to cooperate with others?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Learn communication
- Respect
- Leadership

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- interpersonal skills
- social development
- cooperation
- responsibility
- empathy

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Team activities teach children valuable life skills that cannot always be learned in the classroom. By working with others, they learn how to communicate effectively, respect different opinions, and share responsibilities. They also develop leadership qualities and empathy, which are essential for both academic and professional success later in life.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Clear opinion
- Logical development
- Strong vocabulary

# Question 3

## Do you think disagreements among team members have a great influence on teamwork?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Positive if managed well
- Negative if personal

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- constructive criticism
- healthy debate
- conflict resolution
- diverse perspectives
- mutual understanding

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, disagreements can have a significant impact on teamwork, but they are not always negative. Healthy debates often encourage people to consider different perspectives and make better decisions. However, if disagreements become personal or remain unresolved, they can damage trust and reduce productivity. Therefore, effective communication and conflict resolution are essential for maintaining a positive team environment.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Discusses both advantages and disadvantages
- Uses sophisticated vocabulary
- Balanced conclusion

# Question 4

## Can you suggest how teamwork could be cultivated in classes at school?

### 💡 Ideas

- Group projects
- Sports
- Class discussions
- Leadership roles

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- collaborative learning
- project-based learning
- extracurricular activities
- communication skills
- practical experience

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I believe schools should organise more group projects, debates, and sports competitions because these activities naturally encourage students to cooperate. Teachers could also rotate leadership roles so that every student has an opportunity to lead a team. In addition, project-based learning helps students improve their communication, problem-solving, and decision-making skills, all of which are essential in the workplace.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Multiple practical suggestions
- Clear explanation
- Strong conclusion

# Common Mistakes

❌ Teamwork help people.

✅ Teamwork helps people.

---

❌ Every members should help.

✅ Every member should contribute.

---

❌ Children learn cooperate.

✅ Children learn how to cooperate.

---

❌ Team have same goal.

✅ Team members work towards a common goal.

---

# Examiner's Tips (Band 8+)

✔ Present a balanced opinion when appropriate.

✔ Support your ideas with examples.

✔ Use topic-specific vocabulary such as **collaboration**, **leadership**, and **conflict resolution**.

✔ Avoid repeating the words *team* and *people* too frequently. Use alternatives like **group**, **members**, **colleagues**, or **participants**.

✔ Finish with a concise concluding statement.
