# Question 1

## What challenges do young people face today?

### 💡 Ideas

- Fierce competition
- Unemployment
- Mental health
- Social media pressure
- Financial problems

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- competitive job market
- financial independence
- peer pressure
- unrealistic expectations
- mental well-being

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think young people face a wide range of challenges nowadays. One of the biggest is the highly competitive job market, where employers expect candidates to possess both academic qualifications and practical experience. On top of that, many young adults struggle with financial independence because the cost of living has risen significantly. Social media also creates unrealistic expectations, making people compare themselves with others, which can negatively affect their mental well-being. In my opinion, developing resilience and continuously improving one's skills are the best ways to cope with these challenges.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Gives multiple ideas
- Explains each point
- Uses advanced vocabulary
- Includes a balanced conclusion

# Question 2

## How do young people handle difficult or challenging tasks?

### 💡 Ideas

- Internet
- Friends
- Teachers
- Practice
- Planning

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- seek guidance
- break tasks into smaller steps
- stay motivated
- effective planning
- collaborative learning

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

It depends on the individual, but most young people try to solve problems by gathering information and seeking advice from experienced people. Thanks to the internet, they can easily access online courses, tutorials, and discussion forums. Many also prefer working with friends because exchanging ideas often leads to better solutions. Personally, I believe dividing a difficult task into smaller, manageable steps is one of the most effective ways to stay motivated and eventually achieve the desired outcome.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Gives several methods
- Explains why they work
- Includes a personal opinion

# Question 3

## Which do you think is better: to face challenges alone or to seek help from others?

### 💡 Ideas

- Balance
- Learn independently
- Ask for help if necessary

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- independent thinking
- valuable insights
- different perspectives
- constructive feedback
- collaboration

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I believe the best approach is to strike a balance between the two. Trying to solve a problem independently helps people develop critical thinking and confidence. However, if the challenge becomes too complex, seeking help from others is a sensible decision rather than a sign of weakness. Teachers, mentors, or experienced colleagues can offer valuable insights that save both time and effort. In my opinion, successful people know when to rely on themselves and when to ask for support.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Balanced opinion
- Uses comparison
- Well-developed explanation

# Question 4

## Do you think people need to be challenged?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Personal growth
- Learn new skills
- Build confidence

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- fulfil potential
- continuous improvement
- broaden experience
- self-development
- comfort zone

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Without challenges, people are unlikely to develop new skills or discover their true potential. Difficult situations encourage us to think creatively, adapt to change, and become more resilient. For example, someone who takes on a demanding project at work may initially struggle, but the experience will improve both their confidence and professional abilities. I believe stepping outside one's comfort zone is essential for long-term personal and career development.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Strong opinion
- Logical explanation
- Relevant example
- Excellent conclusion

# Common Mistakes

❌ Young people has many challenge.

✅ Young people face many challenges.

---

❌ They can solve by internet.

✅ They can solve problems by using the internet.

---

❌ Challenge make people strong.

✅ Challenges make people stronger.

---

❌ Ask help is weakness.

✅ Asking for help is not a sign of weakness.

---

# Examiner's Tips (Band 8+)

✔ Explain **why**, not just **what**.

✔ Give an example whenever possible.

✔ Compare the past and the present if relevant.

✔ Use advanced vocabulary naturally.

✔ Finish with a concluding opinion.
