# Question 1

## What are the most popular types of advertising in today's world?

### 💡 Ideas

- Social media
- TV
- YouTube
- Billboards
- Search engines

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- digital platforms
- personalised advertisements
- online marketing
- traditional media
- sponsored posts

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Nowadays, digital advertising is by far the most popular form of marketing. Companies heavily promote their products through social media platforms, search engines, YouTube, and mobile apps because these platforms allow them to reach a specific target audience. Although traditional advertising, such as television commercials and billboards, still exists, it has become less influential than online marketing due to the widespread use of smartphones and the internet.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Compares traditional and modern advertising.
- Uses topic-specific vocabulary.
- Gives clear reasons.

## What type of media advertising do you like most?

- Informative
- Easy to skip
- Product reviews

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

# Question 3

## Do you think advertising influences what people buy?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Creates desire
- Discounts
- Influencers

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- consumer psychology
- purchasing decisions
- emotional appeal
- promotional offers

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I believe advertising has a significant influence on consumer behaviour. Well-designed advertisements can create a strong emotional connection with viewers and make products appear more attractive than they actually are. Promotional discounts, celebrity endorsements, and social media influencers also encourage people to make impulse purchases, even when they don't genuinely need the product.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Explains *how* advertising influences people.
- Uses precise vocabulary.
- Includes multiple examples.

# Question 4

- Honesty
- Creativity
- Audience
- Simplicity

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- ethical advertising
- target market
- visual appeal
- truthful information

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

# Question 5

## Is advertising really necessary in modern society?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Information
- Competition
- Business growth

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- market competition
- consumer awareness
- informed choices

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I think advertising is essential in today's competitive market. It helps businesses introduce new products and allows consumers to compare different brands before making a purchase. Without advertising, many innovative products might never reach potential customers. However, companies should ensure their advertisements are honest and do not mislead consumers.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Balanced opinion.
- Explains both benefits and responsibility.

# Question 6

## How does advertising influence children?

### 💡 Ideas

- Cartoon characters
- Junk food
- Toys
- Brand preference

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- impressionable audience
- consumer habits
- persuasive marketing
- materialistic attitudes

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Children are particularly vulnerable to advertising because they are easily influenced by colourful visuals, cartoon characters, and catchy slogans. As a result, they often develop preferences for certain toys, snacks, or brands without considering whether those products are beneficial. In my opinion, parents should help children understand that advertisements are designed to persuade rather than simply provide information.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Explains cause and effect.
- Includes a practical solution.

# Question 7

## Is there any advertising that can be harmful to children?

### 💡 Ideas

- Junk food
- Sugary drinks
- Violence
- Unrealistic lifestyles

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- unhealthy lifestyle
- unrealistic expectations
- misleading marketing
- harmful content

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I think advertisements promoting junk food, sugary drinks, or age-inappropriate products can be harmful to children. They may encourage unhealthy eating habits or create unrealistic expectations about life and success. Therefore, governments should introduce stricter regulations to ensure that advertisements targeting children are responsible and educational rather than purely commercial.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Gives examples.
- Offers a practical recommendation.
- Uses advanced vocabulary naturally.

# Common Mistakes

❌ Advertisement influence people.

✅ Advertisements influence people.

---

❌ Company should make honest advertisement.

✅ Companies should create honest advertisements.

---

❌ Children believe every advertisement.

✅ Many children tend to believe advertisements easily.

---

❌ Advertisement make people buy.

✅ Advertisements often encourage people to make purchases.

---

# Examiner's Tips (Band 8+)

✔ Discuss **both positive and negative effects** where possible.

✔ Support opinions with **real-life examples**.

✔ Use topic-specific vocabulary like **consumer behaviour**, **target audience**, and **brand awareness**.

✔ Avoid repeating the word **advertisement** too often. Replace it with **commercial**, **promotion**, **campaign**, or **marketing**.

✔ Finish with a balanced conclusion.
