# Question 1

## How do people in your country feel about art?

### 💡 Ideas

- Respect art
- Music
- Painting
- Festivals

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- cultural heritage
- artistic appreciation
- traditional craftsmanship
- creative expression

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In Bangladesh, people generally have a positive attitude towards art, especially traditional forms such as folk music, handicrafts, and painting. Art is closely connected to our cultural heritage and is often showcased during festivals and national celebrations. Although not everyone visits art galleries regularly, many people appreciate artistic expression in their daily lives through music, literature, and decorative crafts.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Discusses culture.
- Gives examples.
- Uses advanced vocabulary.

# Question 2

## Do people in your country prefer music over art?

### 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Easier to access
- Entertainment

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- performing arts
- visual arts
- wider audience
- entertainment industry

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I think music is more popular than visual art because it is much easier to access. People can listen to music while travelling, working, or relaxing, whereas paintings and sculptures are usually displayed only in galleries or museums. As a result, music reaches a much wider audience and has a greater influence on everyday life.

- Explains the reason.
- Uses precise vocabulary.

# Question 3

## What are some traditional art forms in your country?

### 💡 Ideas

- Nakshi Kantha
- Pottery
- Folk painting
- Weaving

### 🧠 Band 8 Vocabulary

- folk art
- handicrafts
- textile art
- cultural traditions

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Bangladesh has a rich artistic tradition. Some well-known examples include **Nakshi Kantha** embroidery, terracotta pottery, traditional weaving, and folk paintings. These art forms have been passed down through generations and reflect the country's history, culture, and rural lifestyle. I think preserving them is extremely important because they represent our national identity.

### ⭐ Why this gets Band 8

- Specific examples.
- Cultural knowledge.
- Strong conclusion.

# Examiner's Tips (Band 8+)

✔ Compare **traditional art** with **modern or digital art**.

✔ Give **specific examples** from your country (e.g., *Nakshi Kantha*, terracotta, folk music).

✔ Discuss **both cultural and economic benefits** of art.

✔ Use topic-specific vocabulary like **cultural heritage**, **creativity**, **public funding**, and **artistic expression**.

✔ Conclude your answer with a clear opinion to sound confident and well-organised.
