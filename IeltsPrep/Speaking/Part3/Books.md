# 1. Do people read more nowadays?

## 💡 Ideas

- Read online more than printed books
- Articles, blogs, e-books
- Reading habits have changed

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I believe people read just as much as before, but the format has changed significantly. Instead of reading printed books, many people now consume digital content such as online articles, blogs, and e-books. Smartphones have made reading more accessible, although people tend to read shorter pieces of content rather than lengthy novels.

---

# 2. Do you read before going to bed?

## 💡 Ideas

- Helps relaxation
- Better sleep
- Avoid social media

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I often read for about twenty or thirty minutes before going to bed. I find that reading helps me unwind after a busy day and reduces my screen time. Unlike scrolling through social media, reading allows me to relax and usually improves the quality of my sleep.

---

# 3. How will e-books affect paper books?

## 💡 Ideas

- Convenience
- Lower cost
- Paper books remain valuable

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

E-books will probably become even more popular because they are portable, affordable, and environmentally friendly. However, I don't think printed books will disappear completely. Many readers still appreciate the experience of holding a physical book, turning the pages, and even collecting their favourite titles.

---

# 4. What's the difference between films and books?

## 💡 Ideas

- Books require imagination
- Movies provide visuals
- Books offer more detail

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

The biggest difference is that books encourage readers to imagine the characters and settings, whereas films present everything visually. Books usually provide more detailed descriptions and deeper character development, while films are more time-efficient and visually engaging. Personally, I think both have their own strengths.

---

# 5. What is one example of traditional literature in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- Rabindranath Tagore
- Kazi Nazrul Islam
- Folk stories

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

One famous example is the literary works of Rabindranath Tagore, whose poetry and stories are admired throughout Bangladesh. Another important example is the poetry of Kazi Nazrul Islam. Their works reflect our history, culture, and social values, and they continue to inspire readers today.

---

# 6. Do you like reading the traditional literature of your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes occasionally
- Learn history
- Rich language

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, although I don't read traditional literature very often, I enjoy it from time to time. It helps me understand the history, culture, and values of my country. In addition, the language used in these works is often elegant and thought-provoking.

---

# 7. Do you prefer books or movies?

## 💡 Ideas

- Depends
- Books for knowledge
- Movies for relaxation

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

It depends on my purpose. If I want to gain knowledge or understand a topic in depth, I prefer books because they provide more comprehensive information. However, if I simply want to relax after a long day, I'd rather watch a movie because it's less demanding and more entertaining.

---

# 8. Is it important to read the book before watching the movie?

## 💡 Ideas

- Better understanding
- Compare adaptation
- Not essential

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

I don't think it's essential, but it certainly enhances the experience. Reading the book first allows people to understand the characters and storyline more deeply. It also makes it interesting to compare the original story with the film adaptation and notice any differences.

---

# 9. Do boys and girls like the same kinds of books?

## 💡 Ideas

- Individual preference
- No gender rule
- Interests differ

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Not necessarily. I think reading preferences depend more on personality than gender. Some boys enjoy romance novels, while some girls are interested in science fiction or history. Nowadays, people choose books based on their interests rather than traditional stereotypes.

---

# 10. What kind of books do people in Bangladesh like to read?

## 💡 Ideas

- Novels
- Religious books
- Self-help
- Academic books

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Many people in Bangladesh enjoy reading novels, religious books, motivational books, and books related to education or career development. Young people are increasingly interested in self-improvement and business books, while older readers often prefer historical or religious literature.

# Examiner's Tips ⭐

## Don't simply say:

❌ "Books are better."

Instead say:

✅ "I believe books provide a deeper understanding of a topic because they offer detailed explanations and encourage readers to use their imagination."

---

## Extend every answer using:

Opinion

↓

Reason

↓

Example

↓

Mini conclusion

---

# Universal Band 8 Structure

> **Opinion → Explanation → Example → Conclusion**

Example:

> I believe reading is extremely important because it improves both knowledge and vocabulary. For example, many successful people read every day to keep learning. Therefore, developing a regular reading habit can benefit people throughout their lives.
