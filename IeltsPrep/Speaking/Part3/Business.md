# Topic: Business
**Target Band:** 7.5–8.5

# Band 8–9 Vocabulary

| Vocabulary | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| entrepreneur | a person who starts and manages a business |
| start-up | a newly established business |
| innovation | introducing new ideas, products, or methods |
| leadership | the ability to guide and motivate others |
| competitiveness | the ability to compete successfully |
| profitability | the ability to generate profit |
| investment | money put into a business to help it grow |
| customer satisfaction | meeting customers' expectations |
| market demand | the level of consumer interest in a product |
| globalisation | the increasing connection of world economies |
| digital transformation | using technology to improve business |
| corporate social responsibility (CSR) | a company's responsibility to society |

# 1. In your opinion, do business people have to work long hours?

## 💡 Ideas

- Often yes
- Responsibility
- Deadlines
- Especially entrepreneurs

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I think many business people work long hours, especially entrepreneurs who are responsible for every aspect of their company. They often have to manage employees, communicate with clients, and make important decisions. However, maintaining a healthy work-life balance is equally important because excessive working hours can eventually reduce productivity.

---

# 2. How do business people relax?

## 💡 Ideas

- Family
- Travel
- Sports
- Hobbies

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Most business people try to relax by spending time with their families, travelling, exercising, or pursuing hobbies. Some also enjoy reading books or practising meditation to reduce stress. I believe taking regular breaks helps them stay motivated and make better decisions.

---

# 3. How can a small business grow into a large one?

## 💡 Ideas

- Good product
- Customer service
- Marketing
- Technology

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

A small business can grow by offering high-quality products or services and focusing on customer satisfaction. Effective marketing, adopting modern technology, and managing finances wisely are also essential. As the business gains a strong reputation, it can attract more customers and gradually expand into new markets.

---

# 4. What kind of small businesses will young people have in the future?

## 💡 Ideas

- Online businesses
- AI
- E-commerce
- Digital services

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think young people will increasingly establish online businesses such as e-commerce stores, software companies, digital marketing agencies, and AI-based services. These businesses require relatively low initial investment and have the potential to reach customers around the world through the internet.

---

# 5. What skills are required to start a small business?

## 💡 Ideas

- Leadership
- Communication
- Finance
- Problem-solving

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Several skills are essential, including leadership, communication, financial management, and problem-solving. Entrepreneurs should also be adaptable because markets change rapidly. In my opinion, resilience is equally important because every business faces challenges before achieving success.

---

# 6. What are the impacts of globalisation on small and large businesses?

## 💡 Ideas

- More opportunities
- More competition
- International markets

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Globalisation has created both opportunities and challenges. On the one hand, businesses can access international markets and reach a much larger customer base. On the other hand, they face greater competition from companies around the world. Therefore, businesses need to innovate continuously to remain competitive.

---

# 7. What qualities are required to become a company leader?

## 💡 Ideas

- Vision
- Confidence
- Decision-making
- Integrity

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

A successful leader should be honest, confident, and capable of making well-informed decisions. They should also communicate effectively, motivate their team, and have a clear vision for the company's future. In my opinion, emotional intelligence is just as important as technical expertise.

---

# 8. What factors determine whether a small company becomes successful?

## 💡 Ideas

- Product quality
- Market demand
- Team
- Innovation

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Success depends on several factors, including the quality of the product, customer satisfaction, effective leadership, and strong financial management. Businesses also need to understand market demand and respond quickly to changing customer preferences. Innovation often gives companies a significant competitive advantage.

---

# 9. Does the latest technology play an important role in a company's development?

## 💡 Ideas

- Absolutely
- Automation
- AI
- Productivity

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Modern technology enables businesses to automate routine tasks, improve communication, analyse customer data, and increase productivity. Companies that invest in digital transformation are generally more competitive because they can provide faster and more efficient services.

---

# 10. What do you think of charitable organisations?

## 💡 Ideas

- Positive
- Help society
- Transparency

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think charitable organisations play an important role in supporting disadvantaged communities and addressing social issues. However, they should operate transparently and ensure that donations are used effectively. Public trust is essential for any charitable organisation to succeed.

---

# 11. Which is better: starting your own business or working for someone else?

## 💡 Ideas

- Depends
- Business = freedom + risk
- Job = stability

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

It depends on an individual's personality and goals. Running a business offers greater independence and potentially higher financial rewards, but it also involves significant risks. Working for an established company provides more stability, regular income, and valuable professional experience. Personally, I think both options have their own advantages.

# Examiner's Tips ⭐

## Don't say:

❌ "Business is difficult."

Instead say:

✅ "Running a business can be challenging because entrepreneurs must manage finances, employees, marketing, and customer expectations simultaneously."
