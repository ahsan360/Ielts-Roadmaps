# 1. Do you think change is good?

## 💡 Ideas

- Mostly yes
- Improvement
- New opportunities
- Some changes difficult

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Overall, I believe change is beneficial because it encourages people to learn new skills and adapt to different situations. Without change, there would be little progress in society. Of course, not every change is positive, but even difficult experiences often help people grow both personally and professionally.

---

# 2. What are some of the major changes that occur to people throughout their lives?

## 💡 Ideas

- Education
- Career
- Marriage
- Family
- Retirement

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

People experience many important changes throughout their lives. For example, they move from school to university, start their careers, get married, become parents, and eventually retire. Each stage brings new responsibilities and opportunities for personal development.

---

# 3. Is your country changing rapidly?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Technology
- Infrastructure
- Economy

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, Bangladesh has been developing quite rapidly over the past decade. There have been significant improvements in infrastructure, digital services, transportation, and the technology sector. Although some challenges remain, the country is progressing in many areas.

---

# 4. In what ways has technology changed people's lives?

## 💡 Ideas

- Communication
- Education
- Healthcare
- Business

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Technology has transformed almost every aspect of our lives. It has made communication faster, improved access to education through online learning, and increased productivity in workplaces. In addition, advances in healthcare and artificial intelligence are improving people's quality of life in many different ways.

---

# 5. Why do some older people not accept change?

## 💡 Ideas

- Habits
- Fear
- Less technology
- Comfort zone

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Many older people are accustomed to traditional ways of living, so they may feel uncomfortable with rapid changes, particularly technological ones. Learning new systems can seem overwhelming, and they may worry about making mistakes. However, with proper support and training, many older adults are able to adapt successfully.

# Examiner's Tips ⭐

## Don't say:

❌ "Technology changed everything."

Instead say:

✅ "Technological advancements have revolutionised communication, education, healthcare, and business, making everyday life considerably more efficient."

**Opinion**

↓

**Reason**

↓

**Conclusion**

Example:

> I think change is essential because it drives innovation and personal growth. For instance, digital technology has transformed education by making online learning accessible to millions of people. Therefore, although change can sometimes be challenging, it usually leads to long-term benefits.

# Common Mistakes

❌ Change is always good.

✅ I believe most changes are beneficial, although some can be difficult initially.

---

❌ Old people don't like technology.

✅ Some older people find it difficult to adapt to new technology because they are more familiar with traditional methods.

---

❌ Technology make life easy.

✅ Technology has made life much easier and more convenient.

---

❌ Country changing fast.

✅ My country is changing rapidly in terms of infrastructure and technology.
