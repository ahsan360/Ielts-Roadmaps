# IELTS Speaking Part 3
# Topic: City
**Target Band:** 7.5–8.5

---

# Band 8–9 Vocabulary

| Vocabulary | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| urbanisation | the growth and expansion of cities |
| infrastructure | roads, bridges, transport, utilities, etc. |
| metropolitan area | a large city and its surrounding areas |
| population density | the number of people living in a specific area |
| public amenities | facilities such as parks, libraries, and hospitals |
| cost of living | the amount of money needed to live in a place |
| sustainable development | development that protects the environment while meeting current needs |
| traffic congestion | heavy traffic that slows movement |
| green spaces | parks, gardens, and other natural areas within cities |
| air pollution | contamination of the air by harmful substances |
| urban planning | designing and organising the development of cities |
| high-rise building | a tall building with many floors |

# 1. In your opinion, what makes a city a good place to live?

## 💡 Ideas

- Good transport
- Healthcare
- Education
- Safety
- Green spaces

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

In my opinion, a good city should offer reliable public transportation, quality healthcare, excellent educational institutions, and a safe environment. In addition, parks and recreational facilities are essential because they improve residents' physical and mental well-being. A balance between economic opportunities and a high quality of life is what truly makes a city attractive.

---

# 2. What are the advantages of living in a city?

## 💡 Ideas

- Jobs
- Education
- Entertainment
- Healthcare

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Living in a city provides numerous opportunities. People have better access to employment, universities, hospitals, and entertainment facilities. Cities also offer greater cultural diversity and faster public services. These advantages make urban areas particularly attractive to young professionals and students.

---

# 3. What are the negative aspects of crowded cities?

## 💡 Ideas

- Traffic
- Pollution
- Noise
- Stress
- High costs

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Overcrowded cities often suffer from traffic congestion, air pollution, noise, and a high cost of living. These problems can increase stress levels and reduce people's overall quality of life. In addition, excessive population growth places enormous pressure on housing, healthcare, and public transportation systems.

---

# 4. How can governments improve living standards in crowded cities?

## 💡 Ideas

- Better transport
- Affordable housing
- Parks
- Smart planning

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Governments should invest in efficient public transportation, affordable housing, and modern healthcare facilities. They should also create more green spaces and implement effective urban planning to reduce congestion. Smart city technologies can further improve traffic management and public services.

---

# 5. What can people do to improve air quality in cities?

## 💡 Ideas

- Public transport
- Plant trees
- Electric vehicles
- Recycling

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Individuals can contribute by using public transportation, cycling, walking, and reducing unnecessary car journeys. Planting more trees and supporting environmentally friendly policies can also make a significant difference. In my opinion, protecting air quality requires cooperation between both governments and citizens.

---

# 6. What are the advantages and disadvantages of living in tall buildings?

## 💡 Ideas

### Advantages
- Save land
- Better security
- Modern facilities

### Disadvantages
- Crowded
- Limited privacy
- Emergency risks

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Tall buildings allow cities to accommodate more people while using less land, which is especially important in densely populated areas. Many apartment complexes also provide security, parking, and recreational facilities. However, living in high-rise buildings may reduce privacy, increase dependence on lifts, and create challenges during emergencies such as fires or earthquakes.

---

# 7. Do you think there will be more tall buildings in the future?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Urbanisation
- Population growth

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I believe so. As urban populations continue to grow, cities will need to make more efficient use of limited land. High-rise buildings can provide housing and office space for thousands of people, making them a practical solution for future urban development.

---

# 8. Why aren't there many tall buildings in the countryside?

## 💡 Ideas

- Less population
- Land available
- Lower demand

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

The countryside generally has a much lower population density, so there is less demand for high-rise buildings. Land is usually more affordable and widely available, allowing people to build larger houses instead of apartments.

---

# 9. Why do some people like to live in tall buildings nowadays?

## 💡 Ideas

- Modern lifestyle
- Facilities
- Location
- Security

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Many people prefer tall buildings because they are often located near workplaces, shopping centres, and public transportation. Modern apartment complexes also offer facilities such as gyms, security services, and parking, making urban life more convenient.

---

# 10. Are tall buildings more beneficial than small buildings?

## 💡 Ideas

- Depends
- Space efficiency
- Community

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

It depends on the situation. Tall buildings are more efficient in densely populated cities because they save space and accommodate more residents. However, smaller buildings often provide a quieter environment, greater privacy, and a stronger sense of community.

---

# 11. Why do many people enjoy going to crowded places?

## 💡 Ideas

- Entertainment
- Shopping
- Festivals
- Social interaction

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

People often enjoy crowded places because they offer excitement and a lively atmosphere. Shopping malls, festivals, and concerts allow people to socialise, enjoy entertainment, and experience the energy of a large crowd. However, some people prefer quieter places to avoid stress.

---

# 12. What is the most crowded place in your city?

## 💡 Ideas

- Shopping centre
- Bus terminal
- Market

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

In my city, the busiest places are usually the main shopping centres and bus terminals, especially during weekends and public holidays. Traditional markets are also extremely crowded because people visit them to buy fresh food and household items.

---

# 13. Is it important for a city to have recreational facilities?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Health
- Families
- Tourism

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Parks, sports centres, and public libraries improve people's quality of life by promoting physical activity and social interaction. They also provide families with places to relax and contribute to the overall attractiveness of a city.

---

# 14. Why do people choose to live in big cities despite the problems?

## 💡 Ideas

- Better jobs
- Education
- Lifestyle

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Most people move to large cities because they offer better career opportunities, higher salaries, and access to high-quality education and healthcare. Although city life can be stressful, many people believe the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.

---

# 15. How are cities overseas different from those in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- Better planning
- Cleaner
- Transport

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Many developed cities overseas tend to have better urban planning, more efficient public transportation, and cleaner environments. However, cities in Bangladesh are developing rapidly, and many infrastructure projects are gradually improving urban life.

---

# 16. Is urban planning important?

## 💡 Ideas

- Essential
- Sustainability
- Traffic
- Housing

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, urban planning is extremely important because it determines how efficiently a city functions. Good planning helps reduce traffic congestion, protects green spaces, improves public transportation, and ensures sustainable development for future generations.
