# 1. Can clothing tell you much about a person in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- First impression
- Profession
- Lifestyle
- Not personality

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

To some extent, yes. A person's clothing can reveal their profession, lifestyle, or personal taste. For example, someone wearing a business suit is often associated with a corporate job. However, I don't think clothes accurately reflect a person's personality or values because appearances can sometimes be misleading.

---

# 2. Do people still wear traditional clothing in your country?

## 💡 Ideas

- Festivals
- Weddings
- Religious occasions

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, traditional clothing remains very popular in Bangladesh, particularly during weddings, religious festivals, and national celebrations. Men often wear a *Panjabi*, while women usually wear a *Saree* or *Salwar Kameez*. On ordinary working days, however, most people prefer modern, comfortable clothing.

---

# 3. How has clothing fashion changed over the last few decades?

## 💡 Ideas

- Western influence
- Online shopping
- Social media

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Fashion has changed dramatically due to globalisation and social media. Younger generations are increasingly influenced by international fashion trends, and online shopping has made stylish clothing much more accessible. At the same time, traditional designs have evolved by incorporating modern elements.

---

# 4. Why do some companies ask their staff to wear uniforms?

## 💡 Ideas

- Professional image
- Equality
- Easy identification

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Many companies require uniforms because they create a professional image and make employees easily identifiable. Uniforms also promote equality by reducing visible differences in clothing, allowing people to focus more on their work than on fashion.

---

# 5. What are the advantages and disadvantages of having uniforms at work?

## 💡 Ideas

### Advantages
- Equality
- Professionalism
- Saves time

### Disadvantages
- Less freedom
- Uncomfortable

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Uniforms have several advantages. They strengthen a company's professional image, save employees time when choosing what to wear, and promote equality. However, they can also limit personal expression, and some uniforms may not be comfortable enough for long working hours.

---

# 6. For which jobs are people required to wear uniforms?

## 💡 Ideas

- Police
- Military
- Doctors
- Nurses
- Pilots
- Security
- Hotel staff

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Many professions require uniforms, including police officers, military personnel, doctors, nurses, firefighters, pilots, security guards, and hotel staff. Uniforms help people recognise professionals quickly and reinforce trust and discipline.

---

# 7. Do you think people are treated differently when they are in uniform?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Respect
- Trust
- Responsibility

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I believe so. People often associate uniforms with authority, responsibility, and professionalism. For example, doctors and police officers usually receive greater respect because their uniforms represent important public services.

---

# 8. Where do people in your country usually buy clothes?

## 💡 Ideas

- Shopping malls
- Local markets
- Online

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

People purchase clothes from a variety of places, including shopping malls, local markets, boutiques, and online stores. In recent years, online shopping has become increasingly popular because it offers convenience, competitive prices, and a wider range of choices.

---

# 9. What's the difference between men's and women's choices of clothes?

## 💡 Ideas

- Women: more variety
- Men: comfort
- Fashion trends

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Generally speaking, women's fashion offers a wider variety of colours, styles, and designs, whereas men's clothing tends to be simpler and more practical. However, these differences are gradually becoming less noticeable as fashion becomes more diverse and inclusive.

---

# 10. Do clothes affect people's mood?

## 💡 Ideas

- Confidence
- Comfort
- Colours

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I think clothing can significantly influence a person's mood. Wearing comfortable and well-fitting clothes often boosts confidence and creates a positive mindset. Bright colours may also improve people's emotions, while uncomfortable clothing can make them feel irritated or distracted.

---

# 11. What do people consider when buying clothes?

## 💡 Ideas

- Price
- Quality
- Comfort
- Brand

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Most people consider factors such as price, quality, comfort, and style before purchasing clothes. Some customers are also influenced by well-known brands, while others focus more on durability and whether the clothing is suitable for everyday use.

---

# 12. What are the advantages and disadvantages of wearing a uniform?

### 💡 Ideas

- Same as Question 5

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

One major advantage is that uniforms create a sense of unity and professionalism. They also eliminate the pressure to wear fashionable clothes every day. On the downside, uniforms may restrict individuality and can become uncomfortable if they are poorly designed.

---

# 13. Do schools in your country provide similar uniforms?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- Equality
- Discipline

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes. Most schools in Bangladesh require students to wear uniforms, although the colours and designs vary from one institution to another. School uniforms encourage discipline, reduce social pressure, and help create a sense of belonging among students.

---

# 14. What kinds of professionals need to wear uniforms?

## 💡 Ideas

- Medical staff
- Police
- Military
- Airlines
- Hospitality

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Professionals such as doctors, nurses, police officers, soldiers, airline crew, firefighters, chefs, and hotel staff usually wear uniforms. Their uniforms help identify their roles and enhance public confidence in their professionalism.

---

# 15. Do you think people wear clothes that reflect their personality?

## 💡 Ideas

- Partly
- Style
- Colours
- Interests

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

To a certain extent, yes. Some people choose clothing that reflects their interests, lifestyle, or creativity. For example, someone with a minimalist personality may prefer simple designs and neutral colours. However, practical considerations such as workplace dress codes also influence what people wear.

---

# 16. Do you think women's clothes show more variety than men's clothes?

## 💡 Ideas

- Yes
- More colours
- More styles

## 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I believe women's fashion generally offers greater variety in terms of colours, fabrics, patterns, and designs. Men's fashion has become more diverse over time, but it is still comparatively less varied than women's clothing.

# Examiner's Tips ⭐

## Avoid short answers like:

❌ "Uniforms are good."

### Instead say:

✅ "I believe uniforms are beneficial because they promote equality, strengthen a company's professional image, and make employees easily recognisable. However, they should also be comfortable and practical."
