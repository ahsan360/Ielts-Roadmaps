# 1. What is the difference between big companies and small companies?

## 💡 Ideas
- Resources
- Flexibility
- Management
- Opportunities

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

The main difference lies in their size, resources, and organisational structure. Large companies usually have greater financial resources, advanced technology, and more opportunities for career progression. In contrast, small businesses tend to be more flexible, allowing employees to take on a wider range of responsibilities and make decisions more quickly.

---

# 2. Are there many big companies in your country?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Banking
- Telecom
- Garments

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, Bangladesh has several large companies, particularly in the banking, telecommunications, pharmaceutical, and garment industries. These companies play a significant role in creating employment opportunities and contributing to the country's economic growth.

---

# 3. What are the good things about working for a big company?

## 💡 Ideas
- Salary
- Training
- Promotion
- Stability

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Working for a large company offers several advantages. Employees often receive competitive salaries, comprehensive training programmes, and clear opportunities for promotion. In addition, large organisations usually provide better job security and attractive employee benefits such as health insurance and bonuses.

---

# 4. Should big companies be punished more seriously than small companies?

## 💡 Ideas
- Depends
- Greater responsibility
- Larger impact

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I believe the punishment should depend on the seriousness of the offence rather than the size of the company. However, if a large corporation causes significant environmental damage or violates consumer rights, stricter penalties may be justified because its actions affect a much larger number of people.

---

# 5. Why do some people choose to work at an international company?

## 💡 Ideas
- Higher salary
- Career growth
- Global exposure

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Many people prefer international companies because they offer better salaries, professional development, and exposure to global business practices. Employees also have the opportunity to work with colleagues from different cultural backgrounds, which broadens their experience and improves their communication skills.

---

# 6. How can a company maintain the quality of the service that it gives to the public?

## 💡 Ideas
- Training
- Feedback
- Quality control

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

A company can maintain service quality by regularly training its employees, monitoring customer feedback, and implementing strict quality-control measures. It should also respond promptly to complaints and continuously improve its products and services based on customer needs.

---

# 7. Do you think it's important for a company to provide after-sales service?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Trust
- Loyalty
- Reputation

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. After-sales service is essential because it helps customers solve problems after purchasing a product. Good customer support increases satisfaction, builds trust, and encourages repeat business. In today's competitive market, excellent after-sales service can significantly enhance a company's reputation.

# Common Mistakes

✅ Big companies generally offer higher salaries.

---

❌ Small company have less facilities.

---

❌ Customer happy.

✅ Customer satisfaction is extremely important.

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

In Part 3, avoid giving only one advantage or disadvantage. Compare both sides before expressing your opinion.

### ❌ Basic

> Big companies are better.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Large companies generally offer better salaries, structured career paths, and greater job security. However, smaller businesses often provide a more flexible working environment and allow employees to develop a broader range of skills.
