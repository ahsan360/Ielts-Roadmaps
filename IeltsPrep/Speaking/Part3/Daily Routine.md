# 1. What are a few things that make you happy?

## 💡 Ideas
- Family
- Friends
- Achievements
- Travelling
- Reading

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Several things make me happy, such as spending quality time with my family, achieving personal goals, and travelling to new places. I also enjoy reading books because it helps me relax and broaden my knowledge. In my opinion, happiness often comes from meaningful experiences rather than material possessions.

---

# 2. Do you believe that engaging in artistic activities might make people happier? Why/Why not?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Reduce stress
- Creativity
- Self-expression

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I do. Artistic activities such as painting, music, or photography allow people to express their emotions and relieve stress. They also stimulate creativity and provide a sense of accomplishment, which can significantly improve mental well-being.

---

# 3. Do you think people who have more talent are happier than others?

## 💡 Ideas
- Not always
- Hard work
- Pressure

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Not necessarily. Although talented people may have more opportunities, they often face greater expectations and pressure. I believe long-term happiness depends more on personal relationships, good health, and life satisfaction than on talent alone.

---

# 4. Do you think money makes people happy?

## 💡 Ideas
- To some extent
- Basic needs
- Not everything

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Money can certainly improve people's quality of life because it helps meet basic needs such as food, housing, healthcare, and education. However, beyond a certain point, factors like meaningful relationships, good health, and personal fulfilment become much more important for genuine happiness.

---

# 5. Are the things that make people happy today the same as several decades ago?

## 💡 Ideas
- Similar
- Technology
- Relationships

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Some aspects remain the same, such as family, friendship, and good health. However, modern lifestyles have introduced new sources of happiness, including technology, international travel, and online entertainment. People's priorities have become more diverse than they were in the past.

---

# 6. What are the benefits of getting up early?

## 💡 Ideas
- Productivity
- Exercise
- Less stress

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Waking up early allows people to start the day calmly and plan their tasks effectively. It also provides time for exercise, reading, or meditation before work or study. As a result, many early risers feel more productive and less stressed throughout the day.

---

# 7. Why do some people like to stay up late?

## 💡 Ideas
- Entertainment
- Quiet environment
- Work

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Some people are naturally more productive at night because there are fewer distractions. Others stay up late to watch films, play games, or complete work that requires concentration. However, consistently sleeping late may affect their health if they don't get enough rest.

---

# 8. Do you think it is important to be punctual?

## 💡 Ideas
- Respect
- Professionalism
- Trust

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Punctuality demonstrates responsibility, professionalism, and respect for other people's time. It also helps build trust in both personal and professional relationships, making communication and teamwork more effective.

---

# 9. What kind of situations need people to arrive early?

## 💡 Ideas
- Interviews
- Exams
- Flights
- Meetings

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People should arrive early for important events such as job interviews, examinations, flights, business meetings, and medical appointments. Arriving early reduces stress and allows enough time to prepare mentally.

---

# 10. Why do children and youngsters generally wake up late?

## 💡 Ideas
- School pressure
- Mobile phones
- Biological clock

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

There are several reasons. Many young people stay up late using social media or completing homework, while teenagers also experience natural changes in their biological clock that make it difficult to wake up early. As a result, they often sleep later than adults.

---

# 11. Do you know anyone who likes to get up early?

## 💡 Ideas
- Parents
- Friend
- Exercise

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, my father is an early riser. He usually wakes up before sunrise to pray, exercise, and prepare for the day. He believes that starting the day early improves both productivity and mental clarity, and I think his routine has been very beneficial.

# Common Mistakes

❌ Money give happiness.

✅ Money can contribute to happiness, but it is not the only factor.

---

❌ I sleep late because mobile.

✅ I sometimes stay up late because I spend time using my phone.

---

❌ Early wake up is good.

✅ Waking up early has many benefits.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Avoid giving one-sided answers. Acknowledge different perspectives before stating your own opinion.

### ❌ Basic

> Money makes people happy.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Money certainly improves people's standard of living, but lasting happiness also depends on health, meaningful relationships, and a sense of purpose.
