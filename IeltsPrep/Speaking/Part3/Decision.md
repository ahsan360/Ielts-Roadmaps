# 1. Why do some people find it hard to make decisions?

## 💡 Ideas
- Fear of failure
- Too many choices
- Lack of confidence

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Many people struggle to make decisions because they are afraid of making mistakes or facing negative consequences. In addition, having too many options can make people feel overwhelmed. A lack of confidence also prevents some individuals from making quick and independent decisions.

---

# 2. How important is it to get advice from other people when making decisions?

## 💡 Ideas
- Helpful
- Experience
- Final decision should be yours

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think seeking advice is extremely valuable, especially when making important decisions such as choosing a career or making financial investments. Experienced people can offer useful perspectives. However, the final decision should always be made by the individual because they are the one who will live with the consequences.

---

# 3. Why is it sometimes difficult to accept advice?

## 💡 Ideas
- Ego
- Different opinions
- Independence

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Some people find it difficult to accept advice because they want to remain independent or believe they already know the best solution. Others may feel that the advice doesn't match their personal circumstances or values. In some cases, pride also plays a role.

---

# 4. What are some of the most important decisions young people have to make?

## 💡 Ideas
- Education
- Career
- Marriage
- Financial planning

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Young people usually have to make several life-changing decisions, such as selecting a university major, choosing a career path, deciding where to live, and managing their finances. These decisions often have long-term effects on their personal and professional lives.

---

# 5. Do you agree that parents should make important decisions for their children?

## 💡 Ideas
- Young children: Yes
- Adults: Independence

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

It depends on the child's age. For young children, parents should make important decisions because they have more experience and can ensure their safety. However, as children grow older, parents should encourage them to make their own choices while providing guidance and support.

---

# 6. Is it better to make a decision based on what you want or what other people want?

## 💡 Ideas
- Balance
- Personal goals
- Consider others

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I believe a balanced approach is the best. People should consider their own goals and values while also thinking about how their decisions affect others. For example, career choices are usually personal, but family-related decisions often require compromise and mutual understanding.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

In Part 3, avoid giving absolute answers such as **"always"** or **"never."** Show balance by discussing both perspectives before expressing your opinion.

### ❌ Basic

> Parents should decide everything.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Parents should certainly guide their children when they are young. However, as children become more mature, they should gradually learn to make independent decisions, as this helps them develop confidence and responsibility.
