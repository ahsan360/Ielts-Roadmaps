**Target Band:** 7.5–8.5

# 1. Why do old people not like changes?

## 💡 Ideas
- Comfortable with routine
- Fear of uncertainty
- Hard to adapt

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Many older people prefer stability because they have followed the same routines for many years. Rapid changes, especially in technology and society, can make them feel uncertain or uncomfortable. However, this doesn't mean all elderly people dislike change; many are willing to adapt if they receive enough support.

---

# 2. Do you think it's important for people to socialise with their neighbours?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Trust
- Safety
- Support

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Building good relationships with neighbours creates a stronger sense of community and improves people's quality of life. Neighbours can support each other during emergencies, look after each other's homes, and create a safer and friendlier environment.

---

# 3. How do people socialise with their neighbours?

## 💡 Ideas
- Festivals
- Community events
- Daily conversations
- Helping each other

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People usually socialise through casual conversations, community gatherings, religious festivals, and family celebrations. In many neighbourhoods, residents also help each other during difficult times, such as illness or natural disasters, which strengthens community spirit.

---

# 4. What changes would improve your local area?

## 💡 Ideas
- Better roads
- More parks
- Public transport
- Waste management

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think my local area would benefit from better public transport, cleaner streets, and more green spaces. Building parks and improving waste management would make the neighbourhood healthier and more attractive for residents.

---

# 5. Why are parks and public spaces important?

## 💡 Ideas
- Exercise
- Children
- Mental health

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Public parks provide people with a place to exercise, relax, and spend time with their families. They also improve mental well-being by offering a peaceful environment away from traffic and noise. In addition, children need safe outdoor spaces for recreation and social development.

---

# 6. Should local people be involved in improving their community?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Responsibility
- Teamwork
- Volunteering

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Definitely. While governments are responsible for providing infrastructure, local residents should also contribute by keeping their neighbourhood clean, participating in community projects, and supporting local initiatives. A strong community depends on cooperation between citizens and authorities.

---

# 7. What problems do modern neighbourhoods face?

## 💡 Ideas
- Pollution
- Traffic
- Less communication
- Crime

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Modern neighbourhoods often face problems such as traffic congestion, pollution, and a lack of social interaction. Nowadays, many people are busy with work and spend more time online than communicating with their neighbours, which can weaken community relationships.

# Common Mistakes

❌ Old people don't like technology.

✅ Some older people find it difficult to adapt to new technology.

---

❌ Government should do everything.

✅ Both the government and local residents share responsibility for improving communities.

---

❌ Neighbours not talk nowadays.

✅ Nowadays, many neighbours communicate less frequently than in the past.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Always provide **both individual and government responsibilities** when discussing community issues.

### ❌ Basic

> The government should build parks.

### ✅ Band 8+

> The government should invest in public parks and improve local infrastructure, while residents should help maintain these facilities and actively participate in community activities.
