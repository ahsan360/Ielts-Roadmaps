# 1. Tell me about the types of food that people eat in your country.

## 💡 Ideas
- Rice
- Fish
- Meat
- Vegetables
- Traditional dishes

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In Bangladesh, rice is the staple food and is usually served with fish, meat, lentils, and a variety of vegetables. Traditional dishes such as biryani, khichuri, and different kinds of curries are extremely popular. In recent years, fast food has also become increasingly common, especially among young people.

---

# 2. How are the eating habits now in your country different from eating habits in the past?

## 💡 Ideas
- More fast food
- Busy lifestyle
- Less home cooking

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People today lead much busier lives, so many rely on fast food or ready-made meals instead of cooking at home. In the past, families usually ate together and consumed more fresh, home-cooked food. Nowadays, convenience often takes priority over nutrition.

---

# 3. How healthy is your country's food?

## 💡 Ideas
- Traditional food is healthy
- Fast food is increasing

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Traditional Bangladeshi food can be quite healthy because it includes rice, vegetables, fish, and lentils. However, some dishes contain a considerable amount of oil and spices. Moreover, the growing popularity of processed and fast food has made unhealthy eating more common.

---

# 4. Why do you think different cultures have different table manners?

## 💡 Ideas
- History
- Religion
- Customs

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Table manners vary because they are influenced by history, religion, and cultural traditions. For example, some cultures eat with chopsticks, while others use forks and knives or even their hands. These customs reflect each society's identity and values.

---

# 5. How may eating habits change in the coming decades?

## 💡 Ideas
- Health awareness
- Technology
- Plant-based food

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I believe people will become more health-conscious and choose foods with higher nutritional value. Plant-based diets, organic products, and personalised nutrition plans may become more popular. At the same time, technology could make healthy food more accessible and convenient.

---

# 6. Do you think our diet is important?

## 💡 Ideas
- Health
- Energy
- Disease prevention

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. A healthy diet is essential because it provides energy, strengthens the immune system, and reduces the risk of many diseases. In my opinion, regular exercise and a balanced diet are the foundations of a healthy lifestyle.

---

# 7. What is a balanced diet?

## 💡 Ideas
- Fruits
- Vegetables
- Protein
- Carbohydrates
- Healthy fats

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

A balanced diet includes an appropriate combination of carbohydrates, proteins, healthy fats, vitamins, and minerals. It should contain plenty of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean protein while limiting excessive sugar, salt, and processed food.

---

# 8. How might eating habits change in the coming decades?

## 💡 Ideas
- AI
- Sustainable food
- Less meat

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In the future, eating habits may become more personalised with the help of technology and health data. People may consume more sustainable foods, such as plant-based proteins, and pay greater attention to environmental issues when choosing what to eat.

# Common Mistakes

❌ People eat more fast foods.

✅ People eat more fast food nowadays.

---

❌ Healthy food give energy.

---

❌ Diet is very important for health.

✅ Maintaining a balanced diet is essential for long-term health and well-being.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

When discussing food, compare **traditional eating habits** with **modern lifestyles**. This demonstrates your ability to analyse social change.

### ❌ Basic

> People eat fast food now.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Compared with the past, people nowadays consume far more convenience food because they have busier lifestyles. However, this shift has contributed to rising health problems such as obesity and diabetes.
