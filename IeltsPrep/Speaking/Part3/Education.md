# 1. How are education priorities today different from those in the past?

## 💡 Ideas
- Technology
- Practical skills
- Creativity
- Communication

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Education today focuses much more on practical skills, critical thinking, and digital literacy than it did in the past. Previously, schools mainly emphasised memorisation and examination results. Nowadays, students are encouraged to solve problems, work collaboratively, and develop creativity alongside academic knowledge.

---

# 2. What is your opinion on the way languages are taught in schools?

## 💡 Ideas
- Improve speaking
- Real-life communication
- Less memorisation

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think language teaching should place greater emphasis on communication rather than memorising grammar rules. Students learn more effectively when they practise speaking, listening, and interacting in real-life situations. Technology, such as language-learning apps and online conversations, can also make lessons more engaging.

---

# 3. How can the type of school you go to affect career success?

## 💡 Ideas
- Resources
- Teachers
- Networking
- Opportunities

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

The type of school can influence career success by providing different levels of educational resources, experienced teachers, and extracurricular opportunities. Well-equipped schools often expose students to better guidance and networking opportunities. However, personal motivation and hard work are ultimately more important than the school itself.

---

# 4. What changes do you think will happen in the classroom in the near future?

## 💡 Ideas
- AI
- Online learning
- Interactive lessons
- Smart classrooms

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I believe classrooms will become increasingly technology-driven. Artificial intelligence, virtual reality, and interactive digital tools will make learning more personalised and engaging. Teachers will probably spend less time delivering lectures and more time guiding students through discussions and practical activities.

---

# 5. Should schools focus more on practical skills than academic knowledge?

## 💡 Ideas
- Balance
- Theory + Practice
- Real-world skills

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I don't think schools should choose one over the other. Academic knowledge provides a strong theoretical foundation, while practical skills prepare students for real-life challenges. A balanced education that combines both is the most effective approach.

---

# 6. Is online learning as effective as classroom learning?

## 💡 Ideas
- Flexible
- Convenient
- Less interaction

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Online learning offers flexibility and access to a wide range of resources, making it ideal for independent learners. However, face-to-face classes encourage greater interaction, teamwork, and immediate feedback from teachers. Therefore, I believe blended learning, which combines both methods, is the most beneficial.

---

# 7. What skills should schools teach besides academic subjects?

## 💡 Ideas
- Communication
- Financial literacy
- Teamwork
- Leadership
- Problem-solving

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In addition to academic subjects, schools should teach communication, financial literacy, leadership, teamwork, and emotional intelligence. These skills help students succeed not only in their careers but also in their personal lives.

# Common Mistakes

❌ Students should only study books.

✅ Students should develop both academic knowledge and practical skills.

---

❌ Online class is better.

✅ Online learning offers flexibility, but classroom learning provides greater interaction.

---

❌ School decide future.

✅ Schools can influence students' futures, but personal effort plays an equally important role.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

When discussing education, compare **traditional** and **modern** approaches instead of giving a one-sided answer. Showing balance demonstrates higher-level thinking.

### ❌ Basic

> Technology is good for education.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Technology has transformed education by making learning more accessible and interactive. Nevertheless, teachers remain essential because they provide guidance, motivation, and personalised feedback that technology alone cannot replace.
