# Topic: Electronic Devices
**Target Band:** 7.5–8.5

---

| smart device | an electronic device connected to the internet |
| digital dependency | excessive reliance on technology |

# 1. What are the most popular electronic devices in today's world?

## 💡 Ideas
- Smartphones
- Laptops
- Tablets
- Smartwatches

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Smartphones are undoubtedly the most popular electronic devices because they combine communication, entertainment, education, and work in one compact device. Laptops, tablets, and smartwatches are also widely used, especially by students and professionals.

---

# 2. What devices do you think will be popular in the future?

## 💡 Ideas
- AI devices
- Smart glasses
- Robots
- Wearables

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I believe AI-powered devices, smart glasses, wearable technology, and home robots will become increasingly common. As technology continues to evolve, these devices will make daily tasks more convenient and improve efficiency in both personal and professional life.

---

# 3. Do you think people spend too much money on electronic devices?

## 💡 Ideas
- Sometimes
- Latest models
- Social status

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, to some extent. Many people upgrade their devices even when their existing ones work perfectly well. This is often influenced by advertising, social trends, and the desire to own the latest technology. However, purchasing high-quality devices can also be a worthwhile investment if they improve productivity.

---

# 4. In what ways can electronic devices make our lives harder?

## 💡 Ideas
- Addiction
- Distraction
- Privacy
- Less communication

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Although electronic devices make life more convenient, they can also create problems such as screen addiction, digital distractions, and privacy concerns. Excessive use may reduce face-to-face communication and negatively affect both physical and mental health.

---

# 5. What would the world be like without computers?

## 💡 Ideas
- Slower
- Less efficient
- Limited communication

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Without computers, many aspects of modern life would become significantly slower and less efficient. Businesses, healthcare, education, and scientific research all depend heavily on computers. Communication would also be much more limited, and many jobs would require considerably more manual effort.

---

# 6. Should children be taught to use computers at school?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Digital skills
- Responsible use

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Digital literacy has become an essential life skill. Schools should teach children how to use computers effectively for learning, research, and communication while also educating them about online safety, cybersecurity, and responsible internet use.

---

# 7. Will electronic devices replace teachers in the future?

## 💡 Ideas
- No
- AI assists
- Human guidance

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I don't think electronic devices will completely replace teachers. AI can provide personalised learning and instant feedback, but teachers offer emotional support, motivation, and critical thinking skills that technology cannot fully replicate. I believe technology should complement teachers rather than replace them.

---

# 8. How can people reduce their dependence on electronic devices?

## 💡 Ideas
- Set limits
- Outdoor activities
- Family time

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People can reduce their dependence on electronic devices by setting daily screen-time limits, spending more time outdoors, reading books, and participating in social activities. Creating technology-free periods during meals or before bedtime can also help maintain a healthier lifestyle.

# Common Mistakes

✅ Smartphones have become an indispensable part of modern life.

---

❌ Computer make life easy.

---

❌ Children use computer too much.

✅ Many children spend an excessive amount of time using digital devices.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Technology questions are ideal for showing **balanced thinking**. Mention both the advantages and disadvantages before giving your opinion.

### ❌ Basic

> Technology is good.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Technology has transformed the way we work, study, and communicate. However, excessive reliance on electronic devices can lead to addiction, privacy concerns, and reduced face-to-face interaction. Therefore, technology should be used wisely rather than excessively.
