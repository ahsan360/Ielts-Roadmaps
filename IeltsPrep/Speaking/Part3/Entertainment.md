# 1. Do you think traditional performances are important?

## 💡 Ideas
- Preserve culture
- Teach history
- Attract tourists

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I believe traditional performances are extremely important because they preserve a country's cultural heritage and pass traditions on to younger generations. They also attract tourists and promote national identity. Without such performances, many valuable customs could gradually disappear.

---

# 2. What do you think is the difference between watching a live performance and watching it on TV?

## 💡 Ideas
- Atmosphere
- Convenience
- Interaction

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Watching a live performance offers a unique atmosphere that television cannot fully replicate. The audience can interact with the performers and experience the excitement firsthand. On the other hand, television is far more convenient and affordable because people can enjoy performances from the comfort of their homes.

---

# 3. How do you think watching a dance performance or a stage play influences children?

## 💡 Ideas
- Creativity
- Confidence
- Culture

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think these performances have a positive influence on children because they stimulate creativity and imagination. They also introduce children to different cultures, improve their appreciation of the arts, and may even inspire them to participate in music, drama, or dance themselves.

---

# 4. Why is entertainment important in modern society?

## 💡 Ideas
- Relaxation
- Mental health
- Social connection

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Entertainment plays an important role because it helps people relax after busy days and reduces stress. It also brings people together through shared experiences, whether they are watching films, attending concerts, or participating in cultural festivals. In many cases, entertainment can even educate people while keeping them engaged.

---

# 5. Has technology changed the entertainment industry?

## 💡 Ideas
- Streaming
- Online concerts
- Easy access

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Technology has transformed the entertainment industry by making films, music, and live performances accessible through streaming platforms and social media. Nowadays, people can enjoy high-quality entertainment anytime and anywhere, which was almost impossible a few decades ago.

---

# 6. Should governments support artists and performers?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Culture
- Economy
- Tourism

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I think governments should provide financial and institutional support to artists because they play a vital role in preserving culture and enriching society. Supporting the arts can also boost tourism and create employment opportunities within the creative industry.

---

# 7. Do you think entertainment today is better than it was in the past?

## 💡 Ideas
- More choices
- Better technology
- Less originality

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In some ways, yes. People now have access to a much wider variety of entertainment thanks to digital technology. However, some argue that older films and performances were more original and emotionally engaging. Therefore, both generations have their own strengths.

# Common Mistakes

❌ TV is better than theatre.

✅ Television is more convenient, whereas live performances provide a unique atmosphere.

---

❌ Entertainment only for fun.

✅ Entertainment not only provides enjoyment but also promotes education and cultural awareness.

---

❌ Traditional performance is old.

✅ Traditional performances preserve valuable cultural heritage and historical traditions.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Entertainment questions often require **comparison**. Compare **past vs present**, **live vs recorded**, or **traditional vs modern** to demonstrate analytical thinking.

### ❌ Basic

> Live performances are better.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Live performances create a unique atmosphere and allow audiences to connect directly with performers. However, television and streaming services make entertainment far more accessible and affordable for the general public.
