# Band 8–9 Vocabulary

| Vocabulary | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| climate change | long-term changes in global weather patterns |
| biodiversity | the variety of plants and animals |
| renewable energy | energy from sustainable sources such as solar and wind |
| deforestation | the large-scale removal of forests |
| water contamination | pollution of rivers, lakes, or oceans |
| carbon emissions | greenhouse gases released into the atmosphere |
| conservation | the protection of natural resources |
| ecosystem | a community of living organisms and their environment |
| sustainable development | growth that meets present needs without harming future generations |
| environmental awareness | understanding environmental issues |

# 1. What are some of the main environmental problems in your country?

## 💡 Ideas
- Air pollution
- Water pollution
- Plastic waste
- Deforestation

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Bangladesh faces several environmental challenges, including air pollution, water contamination, plastic waste, and deforestation. Rapid urbanisation and industrialisation have worsened these problems, especially in large cities. If they are not addressed effectively, they could seriously affect public health and biodiversity.

---

# 2. Why should people be concerned about the environment?

## 💡 Ideas
- Future generations
- Health
- Climate

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People should care about the environment because it directly affects our health, quality of life, and future generations. Environmental degradation contributes to climate change, extreme weather events, and the loss of biodiversity. Protecting nature is essential for sustainable development.

---

# 3. Is water pollution a problem in your country?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Rivers
- Factories
- Waste

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, water pollution is a significant issue in Bangladesh. Industrial waste, untreated sewage, and plastic pollution contaminate many rivers and lakes. This not only harms aquatic life but also threatens people's access to safe drinking water.

---

# 4. What are some of the causes of water pollution?

## 💡 Ideas
- Industrial waste
- Plastic
- Chemicals
- Agriculture

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

The major causes include industrial discharge, agricultural chemicals, household waste, and plastic pollution. In many areas, untreated wastewater is released directly into rivers, which damages ecosystems and creates serious health risks.

---

# 5. Do you think problems with the cleanliness of water will improve in the future?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes, with action
- Laws
- Technology

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I am cautiously optimistic. If governments enforce stricter environmental regulations and invest in modern wastewater treatment facilities, water quality can improve. However, public awareness and individual responsibility are equally important.

---

# 6. How can people protect the environment?

## 💡 Ideas
- Recycle
- Save energy
- Public transport
- Trees

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Individuals can make a difference by recycling, reducing plastic use, conserving water and electricity, planting trees, and using public transport whenever possible. Although these actions may seem small, they can have a significant collective impact.

---

# 7. Do you think money should be spent on protecting animals?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Biodiversity
- Ecosystem

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Protecting wildlife is essential because every species plays an important role in maintaining ecological balance. Government funding for conservation projects, wildlife reserves, and anti-poaching programmes is a worthwhile long-term investment.

---

# 8. Do you think more should be done to protect natural scenic spots in your country?

## 💡 Ideas
- Tourism
- Nature
- Laws

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes. Natural attractions should be protected through stricter environmental laws, sustainable tourism, and better waste management. Preserving these places not only protects biodiversity but also supports the tourism industry and benefits local communities.

---

# 9. What can individuals do to ensure water is kept clean?

## 💡 Ideas
- Avoid littering
- Save water
- Dispose of chemicals properly

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Individuals should avoid throwing rubbish into rivers, reduce plastic consumption, and dispose of chemicals responsibly. They should also conserve water and participate in community clean-up campaigns. Public awareness is just as important as government action.

# Common Mistakes

❌ People should not throw garbage.

✅ People should dispose of waste responsibly.

---

❌ Government only responsible.

✅ Protecting the environment requires cooperation between governments, businesses, and individuals.

---

❌ Pollution increase everyday.

✅ Pollution is increasing due to rapid industrialisation and urbanisation.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Environmental questions often require discussing **both individual and government responsibilities**. Showing both perspectives demonstrates mature and balanced thinking.

### ❌ Basic

> People should recycle.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Individuals should certainly recycle and reduce plastic consumption, but governments must also introduce stricter environmental policies and invest in sustainable infrastructure to create long-term improvements.
