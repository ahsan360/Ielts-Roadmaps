# 1. What rewards can children get from school?

## 💡 Ideas
- Certificates
- Medals
- Praise
- Scholarships

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Schools reward children in various ways, including certificates, medals, trophies, scholarships, and verbal praise. These rewards recognise academic achievement, good behaviour, sports performance, and creativity. Such recognition can motivate students to work harder and build their confidence.

---

# 2. Should parents push their children to get prizes?

## 💡 Ideas
- Encourage, not pressure
- Mental health
- Personal growth

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think parents should encourage their children to do their best rather than pressure them to win prizes. Excessive pressure can lead to stress and anxiety, whereas positive encouragement helps children develop confidence, resilience, and a genuine love of learning.

---

# 3. Is it good for children to compete for prizes at school?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes, with balance
- Motivation
- Teamwork

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Healthy competition can motivate children to improve their skills and develop perseverance. However, schools should ensure that competition remains fair and enjoyable rather than creating unnecessary stress. Learning and personal growth should always be the main priorities.

---

# 4. What kinds of rewards can companies offer to their outstanding employees?

## 💡 Ideas
- Bonus
- Promotion
- Awards
- Flexible working

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Companies can reward high-performing employees with bonuses, salary increases, promotions, additional leave, and public recognition. Some organisations also provide professional development opportunities, which encourage employees to continue improving their skills.

---

# 5. Is it good to have competition?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Innovation
- Improvement
- Balance

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I believe healthy competition is beneficial because it encourages people to improve their performance, develop new skills, and become more innovative. However, excessive competition may create stress and damage teamwork, so maintaining a balance is essential.

---

# 6. What are the most common resolutions in your country?

## 💡 Ideas
- Exercise
- Save money
- Study
- Career

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Many people in Bangladesh make New Year's resolutions such as exercising regularly, eating healthier, saving money, improving their academic performance, or advancing their careers. Although people begin with enthusiasm, many struggle to maintain these habits throughout the year.

---

# 7. How difficult would it be for you to save money this year?

## 💡 Ideas
- Moderate
- Budget
- Discipline

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

It would require discipline, but I think it is achievable. By budgeting carefully and avoiding unnecessary expenses, I should be able to save a reasonable amount. Financial planning is becoming increasingly important because of rising living costs.

---

# 8. How difficult would it be for you to get fit this year?

## 💡 Ideas
- Busy schedule
- Consistency
- Exercise

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

The biggest challenge would be maintaining consistency. Although I have a busy schedule, I believe regular exercise and a healthy diet would help me achieve my fitness goals if I remain committed.

---

# 9. What do you think this year will be like for you?

## 💡 Ideas
- Opportunities
- Learning
- Career

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I expect this year to be both challenging and rewarding. I hope to improve my professional skills, continue learning, and achieve some important personal goals. Although there may be obstacles, I see them as opportunities for growth.

---

# 10. Do you personally think that resolutions help us achieve our goals?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Planning
- Discipline

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, provided they are realistic and supported by consistent effort. Simply making a resolution is not enough; people need a clear plan and the self-discipline to follow it. Small, achievable goals are usually more effective than overly ambitious ones.

---

# 11. What are the benefits of taking New Year's resolutions?

## 💡 Ideas
- Motivation
- Planning
- Reflection

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

New Year's resolutions encourage people to reflect on their lives and set meaningful goals. They provide motivation, promote self-improvement, and help people focus on positive changes. Even if not every goal is achieved, the process itself can be valuable.

# Common Mistakes

❌ Competition is always good.

✅ Healthy competition can be beneficial, but excessive competition may create unnecessary stress.

---

❌ Prize make children happy.

✅ Rewards can motivate children and boost their self-confidence.

---

❌ Resolution easy to follow.

✅ Maintaining a resolution requires consistency and self-discipline.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

Part 3 answers score highly when you explain **both benefits and drawbacks** before expressing your opinion.

### ❌ Basic

> Competition is good.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Healthy competition encourages people to improve and work harder. However, if it becomes excessive, it can increase stress and reduce cooperation. Therefore, maintaining a balance is the most effective approach.
