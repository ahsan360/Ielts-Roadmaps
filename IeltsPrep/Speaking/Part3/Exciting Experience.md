# IELTS Speaking Part 3
# Topic: Exciting Experience
**Target Band:** 7.5–8.5

---

# Band 8–9 Vocabulary

| Vocabulary | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| adrenaline rush | a feeling of excitement caused by risk or adventure |
| comfort zone | a situation where someone feels safe and familiar |
| thrill-seeker | a person who enjoys exciting or risky activities |
| calculated risk | a risk taken after careful consideration |
| unforgettable experience | an experience that is difficult to forget |
| personal growth | improvement in one's character or abilities |
| life-changing experience | an event that significantly changes someone's life |
| cultural celebration | an event celebrating traditions and culture |
| social gathering | an event where people meet and interact |
| memorable occasion | an event remembered for a long time |

# 1. Can you compare some exciting activities people do now with activities people did 20 years ago?

## 💡 Ideas
- Adventure sports
- Technology
- Social media

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Nowadays, people have access to a much wider range of exciting activities, such as bungee jumping, skydiving, and virtual reality gaming. Twenty years ago, entertainment was generally simpler and involved outdoor games or local cultural events. Technology has significantly expanded people's leisure options.

---

# 2. Why do some people enjoy doing dangerous sports?

## 💡 Ideas
- Adrenaline
- Challenge
- Achievement

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Many people enjoy dangerous sports because they provide an adrenaline rush and a sense of achievement. Challenging activities such as rock climbing or skydiving help people overcome fear and build confidence. For some individuals, these experiences make life feel more exciting.

---

# 3. Do you think some dangerous activities should be banned?

## 💡 Ideas
- Regulate
- Safety
- Freedom

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I don't think they should be completely banned, provided they are properly regulated. Adults should have the freedom to participate in risky activities if they understand the dangers and follow appropriate safety measures. Strict regulations are generally more effective than outright bans.

---

# 4. Should people try doing new things?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Learning
- Confidence

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Absolutely. Trying new things allows people to develop new skills, broaden their perspectives, and become more adaptable. Even if they fail at first, the experience often contributes to personal growth and greater self-confidence.

---

# 5. What problems can people have when they try new activities for the first time?

## 💡 Ideas
- Fear
- Lack of experience
- Mistakes

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People often feel nervous because they lack experience and fear making mistakes. They may also need time to develop the necessary skills. However, with patience and practice, most people gradually become more confident.

---

# 6. Do you think it's best to do new things on your own or with other people?

## 💡 Ideas
- Together
- Support
- Alone for independence

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think it depends on the activity. Doing something new with friends can provide encouragement and make the experience more enjoyable. However, trying certain activities alone can also help people become more independent and self-reliant.

---

# 7. Do people in your country spend a lot of money on celebrating birthdays?

## 💡 Ideas
- Sometimes
- Family
- Social media

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

It varies from family to family. Some people organise large birthday parties with expensive decorations and catering, while others prefer simple family gatherings. Recently, social media has encouraged some people to spend more on celebrations.

---

# 8. Do you think it's necessary to spend a lot of money on holding a party?

## 💡 Ideas
- No
- Relationships
- Memories

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

No, I don't think so. The purpose of a party is to enjoy quality time with friends and family rather than impress others. A simple celebration with loved ones can often be more meaningful than an expensive event.

---

# 9. Do people in your country usually go out to celebrate traditional festivals with others?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Family
- Community

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, many people celebrate traditional festivals with relatives, neighbours, and friends. Festivals bring communities together, strengthen relationships, and help preserve cultural traditions. They are an important part of our social life.

# Common Mistakes

❌ Dangerous sports should stop.

✅ Dangerous sports should be carefully regulated rather than completely banned.

---

❌ New experience always good.

✅ Trying new experiences is generally beneficial, although some activities involve certain risks.

---

❌ Party need much money.

✅ A memorable celebration does not necessarily require a large budget.

---

# Examiner's Tip ⭐

For questions about adventure or celebrations, explain **why** people behave that way and discuss **both advantages and disadvantages** before giving your own opinion.

### ❌ Basic

> Dangerous sports are exciting.

### ✅ Band 8+

> Dangerous sports offer an adrenaline rush and help people build confidence. However, they also involve significant risks, so participants should receive proper training and follow strict safety procedures.
