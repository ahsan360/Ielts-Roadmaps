# IELTS Speaking Part 3
# Topic: Family
**Target Band:** 7.5–8.5

---

# Band 8–9 Vocabulary

| Vocabulary | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| nuclear family | parents and their children |
| extended family | grandparents, uncles, aunts, cousins, etc. |
| generation gap | differences in opinions between young and old people |
| family values | beliefs and principles shared by a family |
| mutual respect | respect shared by everyone |
| emotional support | care and encouragement from family |
| caregiving | looking after children or elderly people |
| household responsibilities | daily duties within a family |
| work-life balance | balancing career and family life |
| intergenerational | involving different generations |

---

- strengthen family bonds

# 1. Is family important in your country?

## 💡 Ideas
- Strong family ties
- Support
- Culture

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, family plays a central role in Bangladeshi society. People usually maintain close relationships with their relatives and rely on family members for emotional, financial, and practical support. Family values are deeply rooted in our culture.

---

# 2. Who should be responsible for caring for the elderly—the family or the government?

## 💡 Ideas
- Shared responsibility
- Family + Government

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I believe it should be a shared responsibility. Families should provide emotional support and daily care, while governments should ensure access to quality healthcare, pensions, and social services. Working together is the most effective approach.

---

# 3. How has the size of the average family changed in your country?

## 💡 Ideas
- Smaller families
- Urbanisation
- Living costs

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Over the last few decades, the average family size has become smaller. Many young couples prefer having one or two children because of rising living costs, urban lifestyles, and career commitments.

---

# 4. How do you think families will change in the future?

## 💡 Ideas
- Technology
- Smaller families
- Equality

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I think families will become smaller and more independent. Technology will make it easier for relatives to stay connected even if they live far apart. I also expect husbands and wives to share responsibilities more equally than before.

---

# 5. Should husbands and wives have different roles within the family?

## 💡 Ideas
- Equality
- Ability
- Cooperation

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In my opinion, family responsibilities should be shared according to each person's abilities and circumstances rather than traditional gender roles. Equal cooperation creates a healthier and happier family environment.

---

# 6. What role do grandparents play in families?

## 💡 Ideas
- Childcare
- Advice
- Tradition

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Grandparents often provide childcare, emotional support, and valuable life advice. They also help preserve family traditions by passing on cultural values and personal experiences to younger generations.

---

# 7. What qualities are needed to take care of elderly people?

## 💡 Ideas
- Patience
- Kindness
- Responsibility

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People who care for the elderly should be patient, compassionate, responsible, and understanding. They also need good communication skills because older people often require emotional support as much as physical care.

---

# 8. Should elderly people be cared for at home?

## 💡 Ideas
- Usually yes
- Family support
- Care homes if needed

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Whenever possible, I believe older people should be cared for at home because being surrounded by family provides emotional comfort. However, professional care homes may be more appropriate if specialised medical care is required.

---

# 9. How can neighbours help elderly people during an epidemic?

## 💡 Ideas
- Shopping
- Medicine
- Emotional support

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Neighbours can help by delivering groceries and medicines, checking on elderly residents regularly, and offering emotional support. Even small acts of kindness can make a significant difference during difficult times.

---

# 10. Do you think teenagers should help build a community for elderly people?

## 💡 Ideas
- Yes
- Volunteering
- Responsibility

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes. Volunteering helps teenagers develop empathy and social responsibility while making older people feel valued and less isolated. It also strengthens relationships between different generations.

---

# 11. What kind of jobs require physical activity?

## 💡 Ideas
- Construction
- Farming
- Delivery
- Sports

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Many jobs require physical effort, including construction work, farming, delivery services, manufacturing, and professional sports. These occupations demand strength, endurance, and practical skills.

---

# 12. Can physical workers earn higher salaries in the future?

## 💡 Ideas
- Skilled workers
- Labour shortage

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, especially skilled workers such as electricians, plumbers, and technicians. As demand for these professions increases and labour shortages become more common, their salaries are likely to rise.

---

# 13. Do you think machines could replace manual labour?

## 💡 Ideas
- Partly
- AI
- Human skills

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Machines will replace some repetitive and dangerous tasks, but I don't think they can completely replace human workers. Many jobs require creativity, judgement, and adaptability, which machines still struggle to replicate.

---

# 14. What's the difference between payment for physical work and mental work?

## 💡 Ideas
- Skills
- Demand
- Responsibility

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Pay usually depends more on the level of skill, responsibility, and market demand than on whether the work is physical or mental. Highly skilled workers in either category can earn competitive salaries.

---

# 15. How can a person's energy affect others?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Positive and enthusiastic people often motivate those around them. Their attitude can improve teamwork, increase productivity, and create a pleasant atmosphere. On the other hand, negative behaviour can reduce morale.

---

# 16. Are children born smart or do they learn to become smart?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

I believe intelligence is influenced by both genetics and environment. Natural ability is important, but education, practice, and encouragement play an equally significant role in helping children reach their full potential.

---

# 17. How do children become smart at school?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Children become smarter through effective teaching, regular practice, curiosity, and active participation in classroom activities. Supportive teachers and parents also contribute greatly to their intellectual development.

---

# 18. Why are some people well-rounded while others excel in only one area?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

People have different interests, personalities, and opportunities. Some prefer developing a wide range of skills, while others focus intensely on one field to achieve expertise. Both approaches can lead to success.

---

# 19. Why does modern society need talent of all kinds?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Modern society depends on professionals from many different fields, including healthcare, technology, education, business, and the arts. Diverse talents encourage innovation, economic growth, and social progress.

---

# 20. How can we help young people realise their potential?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Young people need quality education, supportive families, good mentors, and opportunities to explore their interests. Encouragement and constructive feedback can greatly increase their confidence and motivation.

---

# 21. Who is the head of the family in your culture?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Traditionally, fathers were often regarded as the head of the family. However, nowadays many families make important decisions together, reflecting greater equality between husbands and wives.

---

# 22. Are men better at decision-making in a family?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

No, I don't think decision-making depends on gender. Good decisions come from communication, experience, and mutual respect. Both men and women can be excellent decision-makers.

---

# 23. How do you see leadership qualities now and in the future?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Leadership today requires communication, adaptability, empathy, and critical thinking. In the future, leaders will also need strong technological knowledge and the ability to manage diverse teams.

---

# 24. Do you think there will be more women leaders in the future?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes. As educational and career opportunities become more equal, I expect to see more women in leadership positions across business, politics, and science.

---

# 25. Should a leader discuss decisions with team members?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes. Consulting team members encourages collaboration, generates better ideas, and makes employees feel respected. However, leaders should still be able to make final decisions when necessary.

---

# 26. What are the advantages of strong family relationships?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Strong family relationships provide emotional security, reduce stress, and create a sense of belonging. They also help people overcome difficulties by offering continuous support and encouragement.

---

# 27. How many generations usually live together in your country?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

In Bangladesh, both nuclear and extended families are common. In rural areas, it is still quite common for three generations to live under one roof, whereas nuclear families are becoming more common in cities.

---

# 28. What are the benefits and drawbacks of multi-generational families?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Living together allows family members to support one another financially and emotionally, and grandparents can help care for children. However, differences in lifestyle and opinions may sometimes lead to conflicts or a lack of privacy.

---

# 29. Do both parents have equal responsibilities in raising children?

### 🗣️ Sample Answer

Yes, I believe both parents should share responsibility equally. Children benefit when both parents are actively involved in their education, emotional development, and daily care. Sharing responsibilities also helps maintain a healthier work-life balance.

---
