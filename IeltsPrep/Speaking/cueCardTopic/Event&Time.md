# IELTS Speaking Part 2 — Three Improved Cue Cards

## 1. Describe an occasion when you were not allowed to use your mobile phone

**Answer:**

I would like to talk about an occasion when I was not allowed to use my mobile phone. It happened at my office, where I was working as a software engineer. My boss instructed us not to use our mobile phones during working hours, so I had to follow that rule.

I usually worked from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and during that time, I had to write code, fix bugs, and complete various tasks. Although I understood the reason behind the rule, I found it quite strict because working continuously for several hours could become monotonous and mentally tiring.

Sometimes, I wanted to check my phone, communicate with my family, or watch a short video for a few minutes. I felt that these small activities could help me unwind and feel refreshed before returning to my work.

Initially, I found the restriction frustrating, but eventually, I got used to it. In my opinion, employees should be allowed to use their phones during short breaks because this could help them relax and maintain their concentration.

Overall, it was a slightly restrictive experience, but I understood that the rule was intended to improve productivity.

## 2. Describe a piece of technology (not a phone) that you would like to own

**Answer:**

I would like to talk about a piece of technology that I would really like to own, which is an Apple MacBook. As I have been working as a software engineer for more than two years, I am quite familiar with different types of laptops and their capabilities.

I learned about MacBooks through my work and by researching different laptops online. At present, I use a non-Apple laptop, but I sometimes find it quite slow when I run several demanding applications at the same time. For example, I regularly use VS Code and other development tools, which require a considerable amount of processing power and memory.

One of the main features that attracts me to a MacBook is its powerful M-series chip. It can handle demanding tasks smoothly, which is particularly useful for software development. It also has fast unified memory and SSD storage, allowing applications to run efficiently without much delay.

I would like to own one because it could make my daily work more efficient and provide me with a smoother development experience. Since I spend a significant amount of time coding, I think a powerful and reliable laptop would be a worthwhile investment for my career.

Overall, a MacBook would be an ideal piece of technology for me because it could improve both my productivity and my overall working experience.

## 3. Describe a time when you had a problem using an electronic device

**Answer:**

I would like to talk about a time when I had a problem with my laptop. It happened last year when I was trying to prepare and submit a document to my teacher.

When I started using my laptop, I noticed that it was unusually slow. After a few minutes, it completely froze and stopped responding. At first, I was not too worried because I thought the problem might have been caused by the heavy tasks I was performing. I restarted the computer, but after a few minutes, the same problem occurred again.

At that point, I became quite worried because I needed the laptop to complete an important task. Instead of asking someone for help immediately, I searched online and looked for possible solutions on Google and technology communities. I eventually found that the problem could have been related to insufficient storage space because my laptop contained a large number of unnecessary files.

I deleted some unused files and freed up some storage space. Then I restarted the laptop once again, and fortunately, it started functioning normally.

I felt extremely relieved and satisfied because I had managed to solve the problem independently through online research. It was a valuable experience because it taught me that I can often troubleshoot technical problems by staying calm and looking for appropriate solutions.

Overall, it was a challenging situation, but I was pleased that I managed to resolve it without anyone's assistance.
# IELTS Speaking Part 2 — Two Improved Answers

## 1. Describe a piece of technology you own that you feel is difficult to use

**Answer:**

I would like to talk about a piece of technology that I own and find quite difficult to use, which is my laptop. I got it last year as a gift from my brother.

I mainly use it for coding, watching videos, and working on different software projects. As a software engineer, I regularly use my laptop for programming and other technical tasks. For example, I use it to watch tutorials on YouTube and build different kinds of projects, such as web applications and mobile software.

However, I find this laptop quite difficult to use because some tasks require a lot of effort and technical knowledge. When I work on demanding projects, I sometimes have to deal with different tools and applications, which can make the laptop feel complicated and difficult to manage.

Despite these difficulties, I have learned many useful things by using it. I have become more familiar with different software and have also improved my technical skills through practical projects.

Overall, although I sometimes find my laptop difficult to use, it is an important device for my work and learning, so I continue to use it regularly.

## 2. Describe something that you can't live without (not a computer or phone)

**Answer:**

I would like to talk about an object that is extremely important in my daily life, which is my Wi-Fi router. I have been using a router for about two years, and I consider it an essential part of my daily routine.

I use it mainly to access the internet on different devices, such as my laptop, mobile phone, and other electronic devices. Since I need the internet for many activities, including working, studying, watching videos, and communicating with other people, having a reliable internet connection is very important to me.

Over the past two years, I have realised how essential my router is. It provides an internet connection to my devices and usually works smoothly without major problems. Whenever the internet connection becomes slow or stops working, I immediately notice how much I depend on it.

I cannot imagine my daily life without a router because so many of my activities require an internet connection. As a software engineer, I particularly need the internet for coding, research, communication, and accessing online resources.

Overall, I would say that my Wi-Fi router is something I cannot live without because it keeps me connected to the internet and allows me to carry out many essential activities every day.
