# IELTS Speaking Part 2 — Improved Answers
## Target Band: 6.5

---

# 1. Describe a special day out that cost you little money/didn't cost you much

I would like to talk about a special day out that cost me very little money. Last week, I went to Jaflong with two of my close friends, which is quite close to my home. We had planned the trip beforehand, so everything was quite simple and affordable.

We mainly went there to explore the tea gardens and enjoy the beautiful mountain views from the Bangladesh-India border. The mountains were particularly impressive because they are actually located in India. Most of the time, we walked around the area, which helped us save money on transportation.

We did not have any expensive food or activities. We just drank a couple of cups of tea and spent most of our time talking, exploring the area, and taking in the natural scenery.

Although we spent very little money, the day was really special because I was able to spend quality time with some of my closest friends. In my opinion, a memorable day does not necessarily have to be expensive, as long as you are with people you enjoy being around. Overall, we had a great time, and I would definitely like to visit Jaflong with them again.

---

# 2. Describe a special cake you received from others

I would like to talk about a special cake that I received on my birthday last year. I did not organize any party or celebration because I wanted to spend the day normally. However, one of my close friends suddenly came to my house with a cake, which was a complete surprise for me.

The cake was quite simple, but it was a chocolate cake, which is one of my favorite flavors. My friend knew that I really liked chocolate, so he specifically chose that type of cake for me. When I saw him standing at my door with the cake, I was genuinely surprised and happy because I had not expected anyone to bring me anything.

First, we cut the cake together, and then we spent the rest of the day doing some activities. We played games, watched some videos, and talked about different things.

Although the cake itself was not particularly expensive or luxurious, it was very meaningful to me because it showed that my friend remembered my birthday and made an effort to make my day special. For me, the emotional value of the cake was much greater than its price. That is why I still remember it as one of the most special cakes I have ever received.

# 3. Describe a time when you were stuck in a traffic jam for a very long time

I would like to talk about a terrible traffic jam that I experienced last year while I was traveling to Jaflong with some of my close friends. We were on our way to our destination when we suddenly got stuck in heavy traffic near a local bazaar.

At first, I thought it was just a normal traffic jam and that the traffic would start moving within a few minutes. However, the situation became much worse, and we remained stuck there for almost two or three hours.

During the first hour, we were quite relaxed because we thought the problem would be solved soon. We spent our time chatting with each other, drinking some water, and using our phones. However, as time passed, I became extremely frustrated because there was almost no movement on the road.

Eventually, we decided to walk a short distance because we were tired of sitting in the vehicle. After some time, the traffic gradually started moving, and we were finally able to continue our journey.

Overall, it was a really unpleasant experience because we wasted several hours on the road. At the same time, it became a memorable experience because I had never been stuck in such a long traffic jam before. It was definitely one of the worst traffic jams I have ever experienced.

# 1. Describe a short-term job you want to have in a foreign country

I would like to talk about a short-term job that I would like to have in a foreign country, probably Germany. I am planning to move to Germany in the near future, so I have been researching different types of jobs, including part-time and temporary positions.

Since I have more than two years of professional experience as a software engineer, I would ideally like to work as a software engineer or a backend developer. I learned about these opportunities mainly through different job portals and by researching the German job market online.

I am particularly interested in this type of job because it is closely related to my professional experience and technical skills. As I already have experience in software development, I would not have to start from scratch, and I could contribute to a company while gaining international work experience.

Another reason I would like to have this job is that software engineering is generally well paid, which would help me manage my living expenses while I am abroad. At the same time, working in a foreign country would allow me to improve my professional skills and become familiar with a different working environment.

Overall, although I would consider other short-term jobs if necessary, a software engineering position would be my first choice because it matches both my experience and my long-term career goals.

# 2. Describe a time when you sent a message or an email to someone but received no reply for a long time

I would like to talk about a time when I sent a message to one of my close friends but did not receive a reply for several days. At that time, I was planning a tour with some of my friends, so I messaged him to ask whether he wanted to join us.

At first, he did not even see my message, which was unusual because we normally talk to each other almost every day. However, I did not become worried immediately because I assumed that he might have been busy with his work or studies.

After about three days, I started to feel a little frustrated, so I sent him a follow-up message. Even then, I did not receive a response immediately. A few days later, he finally contacted me and explained that he had been extremely busy with some personal matters.

Initially, I was quite annoyed because I had been waiting for his response for several days. However, after listening to his explanation, I understood his situation and realized that I had judged him too quickly.

Fortunately, he still wanted to join the tour, so everything worked out in the end. This experience taught me that we should not judge people without knowing what they are going through. Sometimes, there may be a genuine reason why someone cannot respond immediately.

# 3. Describe a time when you organized a happy event successfully

I would like to talk about a tour to Jaflong that I organized with some of my close friends. We had been planning the trip for almost a month, and eventually, we managed to make it happen successfully.

I was mainly responsible for organizing the tour, so I contributed to almost every aspect of the planning. First, I hired a car and decided on the date and meeting point. I also contacted my friends individually and made sure that everyone knew when and where we needed to gather.

On the day of the trip, all of my friends arrived at the meeting point on time, which made the organization much easier. Once everyone was there, we started our journey towards Jaflong. After reaching the destination, we explored the area, enjoyed the natural scenery, and spent quality time together.

I consider the event a success because everything went according to our plan and we did not face any major problems. More importantly, everyone enjoyed themselves, and we were able to complete the entire trip safely.

I felt particularly happy because I had been the main organizer. Although organizing a group trip can sometimes be stressful, seeing everyone enjoy the day made all the effort worthwhile. It was therefore one of the most successful and enjoyable events that I have organized.
