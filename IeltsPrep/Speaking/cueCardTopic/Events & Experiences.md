# 1. Describe a special day out that cost you little money/didn't cost you much

I would like to talk about a special day out that cost me very little money. Last week, I went to Jaflong with two of my close friends, which is quite close to my home. We had planned the trip beforehand, so everything was quite simple and affordable.

We mainly went there to explore the tea gardens and enjoy the beautiful mountain views from the Bangladesh-India border. The mountains were particularly impressive because they are actually located in India. Most of the time, we walked around the area, which helped us save money on transportation.

We did not have any expensive food or activities. We just drank a couple of cups of tea and spent most of our time talking, exploring the area, and taking in the natural scenery.

Although we spent very little money, the day was really special because I was able to spend quality time with some of my closest friends. In my opinion, a memorable day does not necessarily have to be expensive, as long as you are with people you enjoy being around. Overall, we had a great time, and I would definitely like to visit Jaflong with them again.

# 2. Describe a special cake you received from others

I would like to talk about a special cake that I received on my birthday last year. I did not organize any party or celebration because I wanted to spend the day normally. However, one of my close friends suddenly came to my house with a cake, which was a complete surprise for me.

The cake was quite simple, but it was a chocolate cake, which is one of my favorite flavors. My friend knew that I really liked chocolate, so he specifically chose that type of cake for me. When I saw him standing at my door with the cake, I was genuinely surprised and happy because I had not expected anyone to bring me anything.

First, we cut the cake together, and then we spent the rest of the day doing some activities. We played games, watched some videos, and talked about different things.

Although the cake itself was not particularly expensive or luxurious, it was very meaningful to me because it showed that my friend remembered my birthday and made an effort to make my day special. For me, the emotional value of the cake was much greater than its price. That is why I still remember it as one of the most special cakes I have ever received.

# 3. Describe a time when you were stuck in a traffic jam for a very long time

I would like to talk about a terrible traffic jam that I experienced last year while I was traveling to Jaflong with some of my close friends. We were on our way to our destination when we suddenly got stuck in heavy traffic near a local bazaar.

At first, I thought it was just a normal traffic jam and that the traffic would start moving within a few minutes. However, the situation became much worse, and we remained stuck there for almost two or three hours.

During the first hour, we were quite relaxed because we thought the problem would be solved soon. We spent our time chatting with each other, drinking some water, and using our phones. However, as time passed, I became extremely frustrated because there was almost no movement on the road.

Eventually, we decided to walk a short distance because we were tired of sitting in the vehicle. After some time, the traffic gradually started moving, and we were finally able to continue our journey.

Overall, it was a really unpleasant experience because we wasted several hours on the road. At the same time, it became a memorable experience because I had never been stuck in such a long traffic jam before. It was definitely one of the worst traffic jams I have ever experienced.
