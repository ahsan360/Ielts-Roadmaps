# UNIVERSAL PERSON CUE CARD — YANIS

## 1. WHO — Always the same

I’d like to talk about Yanis, someone I have known since I was a child. He is a very important person in my life, and over the years, I have got to know him quite well.

## 2. PERSONALITY — Always the same

He is a calm, practical and hardworking person who always tries to improve himself. He is also quite curious and open-minded. Whenever he becomes interested in something, he usually spends time learning about it and trying to improve.

Although he is often busy with his work, he always finds time for the things that matter to him. I also really appreciate the fact that he is supportive and willing to help other people.

## 3. CUE CARD — CHANGE THIS PART

There is one particular thing about Yanis that makes him especially relevant to this topic, and that is **[WHAT THE CUE CARD ASKS FOR]**.

I remember one particular occasion when **[SPECIFIC STORY RELATED TO THE CUE CARD]**.

At that time, **[WHAT HAPPENED / WHAT HE DID]**. Although **[SMALL DIFFICULTY]**, he **[HOW HE DEALT WITH IT]**.

As a result, **[RESULT]**. This made me realise that **[LESSON / OPINION]**.

## 4. FEELING — Almost always the same

Personally, I really admire and respect Yanis because of this quality. I have learned quite a lot from him over the years, and I think he has had a positive influence on the way I think and behave.

That’s why I chose to talk about him, and that’s also why I remember this particular experience so clearly.

---

# HOW TO USE IT

When you receive ANY PERSON cue card:

### Step 1 — Start automatically

> I’d like to talk about Yanis, someone I have known since I was a child...

### Step 2 — Give the same personality

> He is a calm, practical and hardworking person...

### Step 3 — Find the ONE thing the examiner wants

Ask yourself:

> **“What does this cue card want me to talk about?”**

Then insert that topic.

### Step 4 — Tell ONE small story

Use:

> I remember one particular occasion when...

### Step 5 — Explain the result

> As a result...

### Step 6 — Finish with your feeling

> Personally, I really admire and respect Yanis because...

# EXAMPLES OF THE 30% SWITCH

## Nature

**Cue:** Describe a person who likes to look after the natural world.

> ...what makes him especially relevant to this topic is that he genuinely cares about nature. I remember one weekend when I visited him and saw him planting several trees and taking care of the plants in his garden. He also told me that he tries to reduce plastic waste. As a result, I became more conscious of environmental issues.

### Keywords:

`nature → trees → garden → plastic → environment`

---

## Languages

**Cue:** Describe a person who is good at learning and speaking new languages.

> ...what makes him especially relevant to this topic is his ability to learn languages independently. I remember when he started learning German using online videos, apps and regular conversation practice. Although he didn't have a personal teacher, he gradually improved his vocabulary and pronunciation. As a result, he became much more confident when speaking.

### Keywords:

`languages → German → online → practice → confidence`

---

## Gardening

**Cue:** Describe a person who likes to grow plants.

> ...what makes him especially relevant to this topic is his passion for gardening. He has a small garden where he grows flowers, vegetables and different plants. I remember visiting him one weekend when he was watering the plants and checking their growth. He seemed genuinely happy while doing it. As a result, I understood why gardening is such a relaxing hobby for him.

### Keywords:

`gardening → flowers → vegetables → watering → relaxing`

---

## Helping people

**Cue:** Describe a person who often helps others.

> ...what makes him especially relevant to this topic is his willingness to help other people. I remember one occasion when our neighbour was having a computer problem. Although Yanis was quite busy, he visited the neighbour and patiently helped him solve it. As a result, the problem was fixed, and the neighbour was really grateful.

### Keywords:

`help → neighbour → computer → busy → solution`

---

## Problem solving

**Cue:** Describe a person who solved a problem smartly.

> ...what makes him especially relevant to this topic is his problem-solving ability. I remember one occasion when he was working on a technical project and something suddenly stopped working. Instead of trying random solutions, he analysed the situation and divided the problem into smaller parts. Eventually, he identified the cause and fixed it.

### Keywords:

`problem → analyse → smaller parts → cause → solution`

---

## Planning

**Cue:** Describe a person who is good at planning.

> ...what makes him especially relevant to this topic is his ability to plan things carefully. I remember when we were preparing for a trip together. Yanis prepared a simple plan for transportation, accommodation and activities. He even had a backup plan in case the weather changed. As a result, everything went quite smoothly.

### Keywords:

`planning → trip → transport → accommodation → backup → smooth`

---

## Inspiration

**Cue:** Describe a person who inspired you to do something interesting.

> ...what makes him especially relevant to this topic is the influence he has had on me. I remember watching him learn new skills independently, and this encouraged me to become more curious and disciplined. I eventually started learning new things through online resources myself. As a result, I became much more interested in self-learning.

### Keywords:

`inspiration → Yanis → self-learning → online → curiosity → change`

---

## Creative person

**Cue:** Describe a creative person you admire.

> ...what makes him especially relevant to this topic is his creativity. I remember one occasion when he faced a difficult problem at work and the usual solution wasn't working. Instead of giving up, he came up with a different approach and managed to simplify the whole process. As a result, the problem was solved more efficiently.

### Keywords:

`creative → problem → usual method → new idea → solution`

# THE GOLDEN RULE

Don't memorize:

**24 cue cards × 2 minutes = 48 minutes of speeches ❌**

Memorize:

**ONE PERSON + ONE STRUCTURE + SMALL STORY SWITCHES ✅**

Your brain should work like this:

> **Person cue card → YANIS → common description → identify topic → choose one story → speak**

That means if the examiner gives you an unfamiliar **person-related cue card**, you don't need to remember a prepared answer for that exact card. You can **build the answer on the spot using the same structure**.
