# IELTS Speaking Part 2 — Three Cue Cards

## 1. Describe a place you have travelled to that you would like to recommend to others

I would like to talk about a place called **Jaflong**, which is one of the most beautiful tourist destinations in my area. I went there last year with some of my close friends, and it was a really enjoyable experience.

During our trip, we did a lot of things. For example, we wandered around the tea gardens and explored the beautiful natural scenery. One of the most impressive things was the view of the mountains from the Bangladesh border, although the mountains themselves are located in India. We also enjoyed the peaceful environment and spent a lot of time taking pictures and talking with each other.

The journey from Sylhet to Jaflong was quite long, but it was relatively smooth and enjoyable. When we arrived, we were quite hungry, so we first tried some local cuisine. The food was delicious, and it was a nice way to start our visit.

I would definitely recommend Jaflong to other people because of its breathtaking views and natural beauty. There are mountains, tea gardens, rivers and plenty of greenery, so visitors can relax and enjoy the scenery. Although travelling there can sometimes take quite a long time, I think it is definitely worth visiting because the experience is memorable.

## 2. Describe a home that you like to visit but do not want to live in

I would like to talk about my uncle's home, which immediately comes to mind when I think about a place that I enjoy visiting but would not want to live in permanently. His house is located in Jaflong, and I try to visit it whenever I have some free time.

What I like most about this home is its peaceful atmosphere and beautiful natural surroundings. It is surrounded by greenery, and the environment is much quieter than the area where I normally live. Whenever I go there, I feel relaxed because I can enjoy nature and spend some quality time with my family.

The house itself is quite simple, but it has a pleasant environment both inside and outside. There is plenty of open space around it, and the surrounding natural scenery makes the place particularly attractive. Another advantage is that it is not extremely far from my home, so I can visit my uncle without too much difficulty.

However, I would not want to live there permanently because it is located in a rural area. One major problem is transportation. Public transport is not as convenient as it is in the city, so travelling to different places can sometimes be difficult. In addition, my studies and work are based in a more developed area, so living there would make my daily commute quite challenging.

Overall, I really enjoy visiting my uncle's home because of its peaceful atmosphere and natural beauty, but I would prefer to live somewhere with better transportation and easier access to work and other facilities.

## 3. Describe a city you enjoyed visiting

I would like to talk about **Jaflong**, a scenic tourist area in the Sylhet region of Bangladesh, which I really enjoyed visiting. I went there last year with some of my close friends and had a memorable experience.

Jaflong is famous for its natural beauty and tourist attractions. During our visit, we explored several beautiful places and enjoyed the surrounding scenery. There are large tea gardens and plenty of green spaces where people can relax and enjoy the views. In addition, visitors can see rivers and mountains, which make the area particularly attractive.

Another thing I enjoyed was the local culture and food. The local people were friendly towards tourists, and we had the opportunity to try some traditional cuisine. I really enjoyed spending time with my friends, taking photographs and exploring the area together.

What I particularly liked about Jaflong was that there were many different things to do. Whether someone wants to enjoy nature, explore tourist attractions or simply spend some peaceful time with friends and family, there is something for everyone.

The only disadvantage was the distance from my home. It sometimes takes quite a long time to get there, but the journey itself was not boring because I was travelling with my friends. Overall, I really enjoyed visiting Jaflong, and I would love to go there again in the future.
