# IELTS Speaking Part 2: My Ambition to Become a Successful Software Engineer

## My Corrected Answer — Band 6.5

I would like to talk about my ambition to become a successful software engineer, which I developed when I was studying at university.

At that time, I had several reasons for choosing this career. First of all, I was interested in technology, and I believed that the software industry offered excellent career opportunities and a relatively high salary. **Because I wanted to build a successful career and become financially independent, I decided to work hard to achieve this goal.**

To improve my skills, I started solving programming problems on different platforms, such as Codeforces. This helped me develop my problem-solving abilities and become more confident in programming. After graduating from university, I received a job offer from a software company, where I started working as a full-stack software engineer.

Since then, I have continued to improve my skills by working on real-world projects and learning new technologies. **Although the journey required a great deal of time and effort, I found it rewarding because I was able to apply my knowledge to practical problems.**

Overall, I believe that becoming a successful software engineer is not just about earning a good salary; it is also about continuously learning and improving myself. **If I remain consistent and continue developing my technical skills, I believe I will be able to achieve my long-term ambition.** In the future, I hope to work for a well-established international technology company and contribute to meaningful projects.

## Complex Sentences to Practise

1. **Because + reason:** Because I wanted to build a successful career, I decided to work hard to achieve my goal.
2. **Although + contrast:** Although the journey required considerable effort, I found it rewarding.
3. **If + condition:** If I continue developing my skills, I believe I will achieve my ambition.
4. **Relative clause:** My ambition is to become a successful software engineer, which I developed at university.
