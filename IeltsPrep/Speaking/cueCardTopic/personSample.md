# ⭐ ONE UNIVERSAL ENDING

When you become stuck, you can safely finish with:

> **Overall, I really admire this person because of their positive qualities, and I think they have had a meaningful influence on me.**

For a cue card where "admire" is not appropriate, use:

> **Overall, I remember this person clearly because of the experience I had with them, and that is why this person immediately comes to mind.**

# ⭐ ONE UNIVERSAL COMPLEX SENTENCE

Practise this until it becomes automatic:

> **Although I have met many interesting people in my life, this person stands out to me because of the way they deal with different situations.**

# ⭐ ONE UNIVERSAL STORY LINK

> **I remember one occasion when I saw this quality in action, and it made a strong impression on me.**

These three sentences can give you a reliable way to **connect your ideas naturally instead of memorising isolated sentences**.
