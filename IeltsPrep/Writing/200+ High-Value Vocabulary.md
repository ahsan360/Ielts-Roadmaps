# 19. The Most Important Rule

Do **not** try to replace every simple word with an advanced synonym.

For example:

❌ People **utilise** cars instead of **use** cars.

✅ People **use** cars.

❌ The government should **ameliorate** the problem.

✅ The government should **address** the problem.

Your goal is:

> **Simple + accurate + natural + precise**

rather than:

> **Complicated + unnatural + inaccurate**

# 20. Daily Practice Method

## Step 1 — Learn 10 words

Choose 10 words from this list.

## Step 2 — Learn the collocation

Don't learn:

> **impact = effect**

Learn:

> **have an impact on**

## Step 3 — Write one sentence

> Technology has a significant impact on modern education.

## Step 4 — Change the topic

> Tourism has a significant impact on the local economy.

## Step 5 — Use it in an IELTS paragraph

> Tourism plays a crucial role in many developing economies. It generates substantial revenue and creates employment opportunities for local residents. As a result, it can improve living standards and contribute to long-term economic development.
