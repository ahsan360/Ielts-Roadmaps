# IELTS Writing Task 1 — Process Vocabulary

## Introduction

- illustrate
- show
- describe
- depict
- present
- outline

Useful structures:
- The diagram illustrates the process of...
- The diagram shows how...
- The diagram depicts the various stages involved in...

## Overview

- consist of
- comprise
- involve
- begin with
- end with
- consist of four main stages
- can be divided into...

Useful structures:
- Overall, the process consists of four main stages.
- The process begins with... and ends with...
- The process can be divided into four main stages.

## Beginning

- Initially
- To begin with
- At the beginning of the process
- The process begins with...
- The first stage involves...

## Middle

- Next
- Then
- Following this
- Subsequently
- Thereafter
- In the next stage
- At this point
- Once this is complete

## Ending

- Finally
- Ultimately
- In the final stage
- The final step involves...
- The process ends with...
