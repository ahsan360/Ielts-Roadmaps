# IELTS Academic Task 1: Line Graph Model Answers (Band 6.5–7)

---

## 📌 Standardized Essay Template & Structure

Every model answer below strictly follows this 4-paragraph layout:

* **Paragraph 1: Introduction**  
  *Simple paraphrase using:* `"The line graph illustrates/shows the [measured data] in [location/context] from [start year] to [end year]."`
* **Paragraph 2: Overview**  
  *Summary of overall key trends using:* `"Overall, [Item 1] increased/decreased significantly, while [Item 2]..."`
* **Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 1**  
  *Detailed presentation of data/trends for the primary 1–2 items with specific figures.*
* **Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 2**  
  *Detailed presentation of data/trends for the remaining items with comparative analysis.*

## 1. Serious Crimes in London (2003–2012)

### Prompt
> *The line graph below shows the number of serious crimes that occurred in London between 2003 and 2012.*

### Model Answer
The line graph illustrates the number of serious crimes, namely armed robbery, assault, and murder, that occurred in London from 2003 to 2012.

Overall, the number of armed robberies decreased significantly over the period, whereas assault cases experienced a steady increase. Meanwhile, the figure for murders remained relatively stable throughout the given timeframe.

Looking at the details, armed robbery started at just under 3,500 cases in 2003 and rose slightly to around 3,700 in 2004. However, this number dropped dramatically over the next four years to roughly 1,000 cases in 2008. After that, it increased slightly to 1,500 in 2009 and stayed at a similar level until 2012.

In contrast, the number of assaults began at around 2,300 in 2003 and fluctuated slightly until 2008. It then grew steadily to reach nearly 2,800 cases by the end of the period. The figure for murder remained the lowest throughout the ten years, starting at just over 500, peaking at nearly 1,000 in 2005, and returning to around 500 by 2012.

Overall, all four Asian countries experienced an increase in spending on cars over the ten-year period. Additionally, China had the highest expenditure throughout the whole timeframe.

Looking at the details, spending in China started at approximately $80 million in 1995 and fluctuated over the next eight years before rising to nearly $100 million in 2005. Spending in Vietnam grew steadily throughout the entire period, starting at around $55 million in 1995 and reaching nearly $75 million by 2005.

Overall, sales of Strawberry, Raspberry, and Grape jams experienced an upward trend, while Plum jam sales declined steadily over the period. Meanwhile, Cherry jam sales showed moderate fluctuations before slightly decreasing toward the end.

Looking at the details, Strawberry jam recorded the highest sales growth, starting at around 3.2 in 2009 and rising continuously to reach a peak of 7 by 2014. Similarly, Raspberry jam sales grew steadily from approximately 2.6 in 2009 to 5.5 in 2014. Grape jam saw a constant rise throughout the entire period, starting at the lowest point of 0.5 in 2009 and climbing to 4.5 by 2014.

In contrast, Plum jam sales began at 2.5 in 2009 and continuously dropped, eventually falling to zero in 2014. Cherry jam started at roughly 1.8, rose to a peak of about 3.2 between 2012 and 2013, and then decreased slightly to under 3 in 2014.

## 4. Australian Exports (1990–2012)

### Prompt
> *The graph below shows the percentage of Australian exports to 4 countries from 1990 to 2012. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.*

### Model Answer
The line graph illustrates the percentage of total Australian exports sent to Japan, the US, China, and India between 1990 and 2012.

Overall, the proportion of exports to China grew dramatically, making it Australia's largest export destination by the end of the period. Conversely, exports to Japan decreased significantly over the years, while figures for the US and India remained relatively low with minor fluctuations.

Looking at the details, Japan was initially Australia's largest export partner, accounting for around 26% of exports in 1990. However, this figure dropped steadily over the next two decades, stabilizing at roughly 16% from 2010 to 2012. In stark contrast, China began with a very low share of under 5% in 1990, but experienced rapid growth after 2000, surging to a peak of nearly 28% in 2012.

Meanwhile, exports to the US started at nearly 11% in 1990, fluctuated slightly over the years, and ended at around 6% in 2012. Exports to India remained the lowest for most of the timeframe, starting at just above 2% in 1990, peaking at around 8% in 2010, and dropping to about 4% by 2012.

## 5. Tourist Enquiries (Jan–Jun 2011)

### Prompt

### Model Answer
The line graph demonstrates the number of enquiries made in person, by letter/email, and by telephone to a Tourist Information Office in a specific city from January to June 2011.

Overall, enquiries made in person and via telephone showed a significant upward trend, whereas enquiries received by letter or email experienced a steady decline over the six-month period.

Looking at the details, in-person enquiries started at the lowest point of around 450 in January. However, this figure grew dramatically over the period, surpassing all other methods to peak at nearly 1,900 in June. Telephone enquiries began at 900 in January, increased to 1,000 in March, remained stable until April, and then climbed steadily to finish at 1,600 in June.

In contrast, enquiries by letter or email started at roughly 750 in January and gradually fell over the following months. By May, this figure dropped to around 350 and stayed constant at this level through June, making it the least popular option by the end of the period.

## 6. Gross Domestic Product (2010–2015)

### Prompt
> *The line graph below compares the Gross Domestic Product (GDP) in four countries in billions of US dollars. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.*

### Model Answer
The line graph illustrates the Gross Domestic Product (GDP) of Bangladesh, Pakistan, Nepal, and Malaysia in billions of US dollars from 2010 to 2015.

Overall, Malaysia maintained the highest GDP for most of the period, despite a drop in the final year. In contrast, Bangladesh and Pakistan showed a continuous increase, while Nepal recorded the lowest GDP throughout the entire timeframe.

Looking at the details, Malaysia's GDP started at roughly $255 billion in 2010 and grew steadily to peak at nearly $340 billion in 2014, before declining to around $300 billion in 2015. Pakistan had the second-highest figures, starting at approximately $180 billion in 2010 and rising steadily to reach nearly $270 billion by 2015.

Meanwhile, the GDP of Bangladesh grew consistently over the six-year period, starting at around $115 billion in 2010 and reaching nearly $200 billion in 2015. Nepal's GDP remained almost flat and was significantly lower than the other three nations, hovering around $20 billion from 2010 to 2015.
