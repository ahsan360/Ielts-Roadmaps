# IELTS Writing Task 1: Line Charts (Band 6.5–7.0 Table-Style Standardized Answers)

---

## Standardized Sentence Template & Structure

To make all Task 1 reports identical in structure, every essay follows this exact 4-paragraph layout:

* **Paragraph 1: Introduction**
  `The line graph compares information regarding [Topic] across [Categories] over a [X]-year period from [Start Year] to [End Year].`
* **Paragraph 2: Overview**
  `Overall, what stands out from the graph is that [Category A] and [Category B] showed an overall upward trend/higher figures, while [Category C] recorded lower numbers/experienced a decline. Additionally, [Highest Category] registered the highest figures throughout the period.`
* **Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 1 (Primary Items & Trends)**
  `In terms of [Category A], [Item 1] recorded the highest figure at [Data], followed by [Item 2] at [Data] and [Item 3] at [Data]. Following this, [Item 1 / Sub-category] increased/decreased steadily to reach [Data] in [Mid Year].`
* **Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 2 (Secondary Items & Comparisons)**
  `Regarding [Category B], [Item 1] stood at [Data], whereas [Item 2] reached [Data]. In contrast, [Item 3] recorded the lowest figure at [Data], while [Final Category] finished at [Data] by the end of the timeframe.`

## 1. Price of Four Types of Bread (2001–2005)

### Model Answer

The line graph compares information regarding the price of four types of bread per kilogram in a European country over a 5-year period from 2001 to 2005.

Overall, what stands out from the graph is that Brown, White, and Rye bread showed an overall upward trend, while Wholegrain bread experienced noticeable fluctuations. Additionally, Brown bread became the most expensive type of bread by the end of the period.

In terms of bread prices, Wholegrain bread recorded the highest figure in 2001 at 0.8 Euros, followed by Brown, White, and Rye bread, which were lower at 0.6 Euros each. Following this, Wholegrain bread increased sharply to reach a peak of 1.8 Euros in 2002 before falling back to 1.0 Euro in 2003.

Regarding the remaining types, Brown bread stood at nearly 1.5 Euros in 2002, whereas White and Rye bread reached 1.3 Euros and 0.7 Euros respectively by 2005. In contrast, Wholegrain bread finished at its initial level of 0.8 Euros by the end of the timeframe.
