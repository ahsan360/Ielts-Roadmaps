# IELTS Academic Writing Task 1
# Band 7.5–8 Sample Answer (Line Graph)

## Question

The line graph illustrates the number of participants in five different activities at a social centre in Melbourne between **2000 and 2020**.

---

## Introduction

The line graph illustrates how the number of participants in five different activities at a social centre in Melbourne changed between 2000 and 2020.

---

## Overview

Overall, the film club remained the most popular activity throughout the period despite experiencing a slight decline during the first half of the timeline. In contrast, table tennis and musical performances recorded substantial growth, with table tennis showing the most dramatic increase. Meanwhile, amateur dramatics declined steadily and became the least popular activity by 2020, whereas participation in martial arts fluctuated only slightly over the period.

---

## Body Paragraph 1

In 2000, the film club attracted approximately **64 participants**, making it by far the most popular activity. This figure declined gradually to around **60** in 2010 before recovering to approximately **66** by 2020. Martial arts began with about **36 participants**, fell slightly to **32** in 2005, then fluctuated between **34** and **38** before ending the period at roughly **36** participants.

---

## Body Paragraph 2

Table tennis followed a strong upward trend, increasing from **16 participants** in 2000 to **20** in 2010 before rising sharply to approximately **54** by 2020. Musical performances had no participants in both 2000 and 2005, but the number increased steadily to around **12** in 2010, **16** in 2015 and **18** in 2020. By contrast, amateur dramatics declined consistently from about **26 participants** in 2000 to only **7** by the end of the period.
