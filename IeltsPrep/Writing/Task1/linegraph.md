# 1. What is a Line Graph?

A line graph shows how data changes over time.

It usually presents

- increase
- decrease
- fluctuation
- stability
- comparison

Your job is NOT to explain WHY.

Your job is ONLY to describe WHAT you see.

Never give your opinion.

# 4. Overview ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

The overview is the MOST IMPORTANT paragraph.

Do NOT include many numbers.

Mention only

Overall trend

Highest

Lowest

Largest increase

Largest decrease

Example

Overall, manufacturing employed the largest number of people for most of the period despite declining after 1980. By contrast, retail and healthcare experienced considerable growth, whereas agriculture steadily decreased and became the smallest sector.

✓ Biggest increase

# 14. Sample Answer

The line graph illustrates the number of jobs, measured in millions, across four sectors of the US economy between 1960 and 2020.

Overall, manufacturing remained the largest employer for most of the period despite experiencing a gradual decline after 1980. By contrast, retail and healthcare recorded substantial growth, whereas agriculture consistently decreased and became the smallest employment sector.

In 1960, manufacturing employed approximately 15 million people, compared with 6 million in both retail and agriculture. Healthcare accounted for the fewest jobs, at only 2 million. Manufacturing then rose to a peak of 20 million in 1980 before declining steadily to around 13 million by 2020.

Retail showed a continuous upward trend, increasing from 6 million to approximately 16 million over the sixty-year period. Healthcare experienced the most dramatic growth, climbing from just 2 million jobs to around 16 million by the end of the period. Conversely, agriculture declined steadily from 6 million to only 2 million jobs.

# 15. Common Mistakes

❌ Describe every year.

✔ Describe overall trends.

---

❌ Forget the overview.

✔ Always write one.

---

❌ Copy the question.

✔ Paraphrase it.

---

❌ Give opinions.

✔ Describe only the information.

---

❌ Ignore comparisons.

✔ Compare categories whenever possible.

---

❌ Repeat "increase" many times.

✔ Use a wide variety of synonyms.
