# IELTS Writing Task 1: Map Descriptions (Band 6.5 Standard)

This document contains standardized sample responses for three typical IELTS Writing Task 1 map prompts. Each response follows a consistent structural framework suitable for achieving a Band 6.5 target.

---

## Standard Structural Framework

1. **Introduction:** Rephrase the prompt clearly (1 sentence).
2. **Overview:** Highlight two main overall trends or changes (2 sentences).
3. **Body Paragraph 1:** Focus on key details/changes in one main section/feature (3–4 sentences).
4. **Body Paragraph 2:** Focus on key details/changes in the remaining section/feature (3–4 sentences).

---

## Task 1: Area Before and After Hydroelectric Power Plant Construction

### Question
The maps below describe an area before and after the construction of a hydroelectric power plant. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

### Sample Response (Band 6.5)

The maps illustrate the main changes that occurred in a rural area following the construction of a hydroelectric power plant.

Overall, what stands out from the maps is that the area was significantly industrialized over the period. Another noticeable point is that natural and historical features were replaced by industrial infrastructure and tourism facilities.

Looking at the details, the river running through the center was dammed to form a large lake in the northern section. To the east of this new reservoir, a hotel was constructed where farmland used to be located. However, the mountains in the northeast corner remained unchanged, as did the village located in the southeast corner.

In terms of other developments, the forest in the northwest corner retained its original location. In contrast, the national park in the west was completely removed to make room for a power plant. Further south, the historic castle was demolished, and transmission towers were erected in the southwest area to distribute electricity.

## Task 2: Stokeford Village (1930 vs. 2010)

### Question
The maps below show the village of Stokeford in 1930 and 2010. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

### Sample Response (Band 6.5)

The maps illustrate how the village of Stokeford developed over an 80-year period from 1930 to 2010.

Overall, what stands out from the maps is that the village became much more residential over the period. Another noticeable point is that former agricultural land and private gardens were repurposed to accommodate new housing.

Looking at the details, in 1930, there was a large area of farmland to the east of the main road. By 2010, this farmland was completely removed, and several new houses were built along new side roads. In addition, the primary school located nearby was extended to include two additional wings. Furthermore, the large house and its surrounding gardens in the southeast were converted into a retirement home.

In terms of other developments, the shops situated along the main road in the west were demolished and replaced by houses. On the other hand, the post office remained in its original position near the river. The bridge crossing the River Stoke in the north also remained unchanged throughout the entire period.

## Task 3: College Site Plan (Present vs. Future Development)

### Question
The diagrams below show the present building of a college and the plan for changes to the college site in the future. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

### Sample Response (Band 6.5)

The diagrams illustrate the current layout of a college campus and the proposed plan for its future redevelopment.

Overall, what stands out from the maps is that the internal layout of the college building will be significantly restructured. Another noticeable point is that the total capacity for classrooms and parking facilities will be expanded.

Looking at the details, the outdoor garden and footpath located in the northern section will be completely removed. The road on the western side will also be removed, allowing the adjacent car park to be enlarged to accommodate more vehicles. However, the bus stop along the main road in the south will remain in its present location.

In terms of internal changes, two new classrooms will be added on the eastern side, bringing the total number of classrooms to four in that wing. The reception area will be relocated to a central position, while the library will move to the southwest corner. Finally, the lunch area will be positioned in the center, next to a newly added shop and relocated toilets in the northwest corner.

### Key Highlights of this Standardized Template:
- **Consistent Openings:** Every introductory paragraph uses `The maps/diagrams illustrate...`
- **Uniform Overview Format:** Uses the exact structure `Overall, what stands out from the maps is that... Another noticeable point is that...` across all responses.
- **Consistent Body Paragraph Transitions:** Paragraph 1 begins with `Looking at the details...`, and Paragraph 2 transitions with `In terms of other developments/changes...`
- **Grammatical Accuracy (Band 6.5):** Uses a mixture of present/future tenses (for plans) and past/present perfect passive forms (`was constructed`, `were removed`, `will be expanded`, `has been modernized`).
