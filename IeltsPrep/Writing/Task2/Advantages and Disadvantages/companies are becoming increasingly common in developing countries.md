# IELTS Writing Task 2

## Question

**Multinational companies are becoming increasingly common in developing countries.
What are the advantages and disadvantages of this?**

---

## Introduction

Multinational corporations are becoming increasingly common in developing countries. 
This trend offers several benefits, particularly economic growth and increased employment opportunities. 
However, it can also reduce demand for local products and create difficulties for small domestic businesses.

---

## Body Paragraph 1 — Advantages

One major advantage of multinational companies is that they can stimulate economic growth.

This is because international corporations often bring foreign investment, advanced technology and expertise to developing countries.

As a result, they can contribute to industrial development, increase trade and generate additional income for the local economy.

Another advantage is that they create employment opportunities for local people.

This is because multinational companies require employees with different levels of skills and expertise.

For example, when an international technology company establishes a branch in a developing country, it may create hundreds of jobs for local graduates and experienced professionals.

---

## Body Paragraph 2 — Disadvantages

However, the growing presence of multinational companies also has some significant disadvantages.

One major drawback is that local businesses may struggle to compete with international brands.

This is because multinational corporations often have greater financial resources, advanced technology and well-established reputations.

As a result, consumers may prefer internationally recognised products, which can reduce demand for locally produced goods.

Another disadvantage is that some small domestic businesses may be forced to close.

For example, small textile companies may struggle to survive when they face intense competition from large international corporations with greater financial resources and stronger marketing strategies.

---

## Conclusion

In conclusion, the growing presence of multinational companies can provide significant benefits, particularly by stimulating economic growth and creating employment opportunities.

Nevertheless, it can also reduce demand for local products and put considerable pressure on small domestic businesses.
