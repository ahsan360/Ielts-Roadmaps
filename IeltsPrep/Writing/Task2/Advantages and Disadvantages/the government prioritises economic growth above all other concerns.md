# IELTS Writing Task 2

## Question

**In many countries, the government prioritises economic growth above all other concerns. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of this.**

---

## Introduction

Economic growth receives greater attention than any other national priority in many countries around the world. The principal advantages of this approach are lower unemployment and increased wealth for citizens, while its main disadvantages are rising living costs and insufficient support for the most vulnerable members of society.

---

## Body Paragraph 1 — Advantages

On the one hand, prioritising economic growth can be highly beneficial for the public because it can reduce unemployment.

This is because governments that focus on economic expansion are more likely to encourage the establishment and development of businesses, which creates demand for workers.

As a result, more people can find employment and earn a stable income.

In addition, economic growth can increase people's overall wealth and improve their standard of living.

When businesses become more profitable, they can expand their operations and offer higher salaries to their employees.

For example, in Poland, average salaries for middle-management positions have increased substantially since the collapse of communism, partly as a result of the country's economic development.

---

## Body Paragraph 2 — Disadvantages

On the other hand, rapid economic growth can also lead to higher living costs.

One reason for this is that when workers earn higher salaries, businesses may face increased labour costs, which can eventually be reflected in the prices of goods and services.

Consequently, although people's incomes may rise, they may not necessarily experience a corresponding improvement in their purchasing power.

Moreover, governments that prioritise economic growth above other concerns may provide insufficient support for disadvantaged groups.

If authorities place excessive emphasis on economic productivity, they may be reluctant to allocate sufficient resources to people who are unemployed, disabled or otherwise unable to contribute significantly to economic activity.

As a result, vulnerable members of society may become increasingly dependent on limited social welfare programmes and face greater economic hardship.

---

## Conclusion

In conclusion, prioritising economic growth can bring significant benefits, particularly by creating employment opportunities and increasing people's incomes. Nevertheless, excessive emphasis on economic expansion may also contribute to rising living costs and insufficient support for vulnerable members of society. Therefore, governments should pursue economic growth while also maintaining adequate social protection.
