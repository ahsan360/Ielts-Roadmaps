# IELTS Writing Task 2 — Agree/Disagree & Positive/Negative Development

## Essay 7 — Communication

### Question

Modern technology has made communication faster and easier, but it has reduced the quality of personal relationships. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

Modern technology has completely changed the way people communicate with each other. Although it has made communication considerably faster and more convenient, some people believe that it has reduced the quality of personal relationships. I partly agree with this view because excessive use of technology can weaken face-to-face relationships, while it can also help people remain close when they live far away.

On the one hand, technology can negatively affect personal relationships. Nowadays, many people spend a significant amount of time using smartphones, social media and messaging applications. Instead of having a proper conversation with family members or friends, they may simply send short messages or react to posts online. This can make relationships less personal because online communication does not always provide the same emotional connection as face-to-face interaction. For example, family members may sit together at home but remain busy with their own phones rather than talking to each other. Over time, this habit can create emotional distance.

On the other hand, technology has made it much easier for people to maintain relationships across long distances. Video calls, instant messaging and social networking sites allow individuals to communicate with relatives and friends who live in other cities or countries. For instance, someone working abroad can regularly speak to their parents through video calls without having to travel home. This can help families maintain strong emotional connections despite being physically separated.

In conclusion, I partly agree that modern technology has reduced the quality of some personal relationships, particularly when people use it excessively. However, I believe that it can also strengthen relationships by allowing people to communicate regularly over long distances.

# Essay 8 — Social Media and News

### Question

Social media has become the primary source of news for many people. Do you think this is a positive or negative development?

### Answer

Social media has become an increasingly important source of news for people around the world. Many individuals now use platforms such as Facebook, Instagram and X to learn about local and international events. Although this development offers several benefits, I believe that it is mainly negative because social media can spread inaccurate information very quickly.

There are some clear advantages to receiving news through social media. The most obvious benefit is speed. People can often find out about an important event within minutes of it happening. Traditional newspapers and television channels may take longer to report breaking news. Furthermore, social media allows users to access information about a wide range of subjects in one place. For example, a person can follow pages about politics, sports, technology and international affairs without buying several newspapers or visiting different websites. This makes obtaining information convenient and inexpensive.

However, the disadvantages of relying heavily on social media for news are more serious. The main concern is that information posted online is not always accurate. Almost anyone can publish a story, even if they have no professional knowledge or reliable evidence. False information can therefore spread rapidly, especially when users share sensational stories without checking their sources. Another problem is that social media algorithms may repeatedly show people content that matches their existing opinions. Consequently, individuals may develop a one-sided understanding of important issues.

In conclusion, social media provides fast and convenient access to news, but I consider its growing role as the main source of information to be a negative development. People should use reliable news organisations and verify important information before accepting or sharing it.

# Essay 12 — Crime, Poverty and Education

### Question

The increase in crime rates is mainly caused by poverty and lack of education. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

It is often argued that the increase in crime rates is mainly the result of poverty and a lack of education. I largely agree with this opinion because financial difficulties and limited educational opportunities can encourage criminal behaviour. However, I also believe that several other social and personal factors contribute to crime.

Firstly, poverty can increase the likelihood of criminal activity. People who struggle to afford food, housing and other basic necessities may become desperate and choose illegal ways to obtain money. This does not mean that every poor person becomes a criminal, but financial pressure can create difficult circumstances. In addition, people from disadvantaged backgrounds may have fewer opportunities to find stable and well-paid employment. For example, a young person who leaves school without useful qualifications may find it difficult to get a decent job and could eventually become involved in theft or other illegal activities.

Secondly, a lack of education can also contribute to higher crime rates. Education not only provides academic knowledge but also helps people develop useful skills and understand social responsibilities. People who receive a good education generally have better employment opportunities and may be less likely to depend on illegal activities. Governments can therefore reduce crime by improving access to schools and vocational training, especially in poorer communities.

Nevertheless, poverty and education are not the only causes of crime. Family problems, drug addiction, peer pressure and poor parenting can also influence people's behaviour. For instance, teenagers who associate with criminal groups may commit offences because they want acceptance from their peers.

In conclusion, I agree that poverty and lack of education are major causes of crime, but they are not the only factors. Governments should address these issues while also dealing with family problems, drug abuse and negative social influences.

# Essay 14 — Police Officers and Firearms

### Question

Police officers should carry firearms at all times to ensure public safety. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

Some people believe that police officers should carry firearms at all times in order to protect the public. I partly disagree with this view because firearms are necessary in certain dangerous situations, but requiring every officer to carry a gun constantly could also create unnecessary risks.

There are situations in which firearms can be extremely useful for police officers. Criminals involved in serious crimes may possess dangerous weapons, and police need effective equipment to protect themselves and innocent members of the public. For example, during an armed robbery or a terrorist attack, an officer with a firearm may be able to respond quickly and prevent further injuries. Therefore, having access to guns can be an important part of maintaining public safety in high-risk situations.

However, carrying firearms at all times can also have negative consequences. Not every police situation involves serious violence, and most officers deal with ordinary incidents such as traffic violations, disputes and minor crimes. If an officer is always armed, there may be a greater risk of accidental shootings or unnecessary use of force. In addition, some members of the public may feel nervous or threatened when they regularly encounter heavily armed officers. Less dangerous equipment may be sufficient for many routine situations.

A better approach would therefore be to give firearms to officers who work in areas or situations where serious violence is more likely. These officers should receive extensive training so that they understand when and how weapons should be used. Other officers could use non-lethal equipment when appropriate.

In conclusion, I do not believe that all police officers should carry firearms at all times. Although guns can be essential for dealing with serious threats, they should mainly be available to properly trained officers who face high-risk situations.

# Essay 15 — Historical Buildings

### Question

Historical buildings should be preserved even if it costs a lot of money to maintain them. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

Historical buildings are an important part of a country's cultural heritage, and some people believe that they should be preserved regardless of the cost. I largely agree with this view because such buildings can provide valuable historical knowledge and attract tourists. However, I believe that governments should consider the importance and condition of each building before spending large amounts of public money.

One major reason for preserving historical buildings is their cultural and educational value. Old buildings can provide future generations with a direct connection to the past. They can show how previous societies lived, worked and developed their communities. This is particularly important because historical information cannot always be fully understood through books alone. For example, an ancient palace, mosque or traditional house can help young people understand the architecture and lifestyle of earlier generations.

Another advantage is that historical buildings can support the tourism industry. Well-preserved sites often attract both domestic and international visitors. Tourists spend money on hotels, restaurants, transport and local products, which can create employment and support local businesses. Therefore, the cost of maintaining an important historical building may eventually be partly recovered through tourism.

Nevertheless, it would be unreasonable to preserve every old building regardless of its condition. Some structures may have very little historical importance while requiring millions of dollars for repair. Governments also have limited budgets and need to provide essential services such as healthcare, education and public transport. In such cases, authorities should carefully decide which buildings deserve protection.

In conclusion, I largely agree that historically and culturally significant buildings should be preserved even when maintenance is expensive. However, governments should assess their historical value, condition and potential benefits before allocating substantial public funds.

# Essay 24 — Examinations

### Question

Examinations are the best way to measure a student's academic performance. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

Examinations have traditionally been used to assess students' academic performance, and some people believe that they are the best method of doing so. I disagree with this view because examinations can effectively test academic knowledge, but they cannot measure many other important abilities that students need in education and employment.

There are several reasons why examinations remain useful. First, they provide a relatively standard way of assessing a large number of students. Every student can be given similar questions and the same amount of time, which makes comparison easier. Second, examinations encourage students to study seriously and review what they have learned. For example, a final examination can show whether a student understands the main theories and concepts taught throughout a course. This information can be useful for teachers and educational institutions.

However, examinations do not provide a complete picture of a student's abilities. Some students become extremely nervous during exams and therefore perform below their actual level. More importantly, written tests are often unable to assess skills such as creativity, teamwork, communication, leadership and practical problem-solving. These abilities are particularly important in modern workplaces. For example, a student studying computer science may have excellent programming and teamwork skills but perform only moderately well in a traditional written examination.

For this reason, schools and universities should use several assessment methods rather than relying entirely on examinations. Projects, presentations, practical tasks, group work and regular classroom activities can provide additional information about students' strengths and weaknesses.

In conclusion, I disagree that examinations are the best way to measure academic performance. Although they are useful for testing knowledge, combining exams with practical and continuous assessment would provide a more accurate and balanced evaluation of students.

# Essay 29 — Celebrities as Role Models

### Question

Celebrities have a responsibility to act as good role models for young people. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

Celebrities have a significant influence on modern society, particularly through television and social media. Some people therefore believe that they have a responsibility to act as positive role models for young people. I largely agree with this view because celebrities can strongly influence children's behaviour, although parents and schools also have important responsibilities.

One reason celebrities should behave responsibly is that young people often admire and imitate them. Famous actors, athletes and online personalities can influence the way teenagers dress, speak and behave. If celebrities promote healthy habits, hard work and respectful behaviour, young followers may be encouraged to adopt similar attitudes. For example, a famous athlete who regularly promotes exercise and a healthy lifestyle can motivate thousands of young people to become more physically active.

In contrast, irresponsible behaviour by celebrities can have a harmful influence. If a famous person promotes dangerous activities, excessive drinking or aggressive behaviour, some young followers may consider such actions acceptable. This is especially concerning because teenagers may not have enough experience to distinguish between responsible and irresponsible behaviour. Therefore, celebrities should think carefully about the messages they communicate to the public.

However, celebrities cannot be held completely responsible for the behaviour of young people. Parents have the primary responsibility for teaching children values and helping them understand right and wrong. Schools also play an important role by providing moral, social and emotional education. Furthermore, young people should gradually learn to think critically rather than copying everything they see online.

In conclusion, I largely agree that celebrities have a responsibility to behave as good role models because they have considerable influence over young people. Nevertheless, parents, teachers and young people themselves must also share responsibility for developing healthy and responsible behaviour.

# Essay 32 — Television and Children

### Question

Watching television is a waste of time for children. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

Some people argue that watching television is a waste of time for children. I partly disagree with this statement because excessive television viewing can have several negative effects, but suitable programmes can also provide educational and entertaining experiences for young people.

There are clear disadvantages to children spending too much time watching television. Firstly, excessive screen time can reduce the amount of time children spend exercising, reading and playing with other children. Physical activity is important for their health and development, while social interaction helps them develop communication skills. Secondly, watching television for long periods may affect children's concentration and sleeping habits, particularly when they watch programmes late at night. For example, a child who spends several hours watching television after school may have less time to complete homework or participate in outdoor activities.

However, it would be unfair to say that all television viewing is useless. Many programmes are specifically designed to educate children. Documentaries about nature, science and history can increase their knowledge and curiosity about the world. Educational programmes can also help younger children learn new words, basic numbers and social skills. In addition, watching television together as a family can sometimes provide an opportunity for parents and children to discuss different topics.

The main issue is therefore the amount and type of television children watch. Parents should set reasonable limits on screen time and encourage children to watch programmes that are appropriate for their age. They should also make sure that children have enough time for studying, sleeping, exercising and interacting with family and friends.

In conclusion, I partly disagree that watching television is a waste of time for children. While excessive viewing can be harmful, carefully selected programmes can provide useful knowledge and entertainment. Therefore, balanced television use is more appropriate than completely preventing children from watching it.

# Essay 35 — Plastic Pollution

### Question

Plastic pollution is the most serious environmental problem facing the world today. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

Plastic pollution has become a major environmental issue in many parts of the world. Some people believe that it is the most serious environmental problem facing humanity today. I partly agree because plastic waste causes widespread damage to nature, but I believe that problems such as climate change and deforestation are equally, or even more, serious.

One important reason why plastic pollution is a serious problem is that plastic can remain in the environment for many years. Large quantities of plastic waste are thrown into rivers, seas and other natural areas. This can seriously harm wildlife because animals may eat plastic or become trapped in it. In addition, plastic can break down into very small particles that may enter the food chain. For example, sea animals can mistake plastic waste for food, which can cause injury or death.

Nevertheless, plastic pollution is not the only major environmental threat. Climate change is particularly dangerous because it can affect the entire planet. Rising temperatures can contribute to extreme weather, melting ice and rising sea levels. These changes can threaten agriculture, wildlife and human settlements. Deforestation is another serious concern because forests provide habitats for animals and absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. Destroying forests can therefore make climate change worse.

For these reasons, it is difficult to identify one environmental issue as the single most serious problem. Different regions may also face different priorities. Governments should therefore develop a broad environmental strategy instead of focusing on plastic pollution alone. They should reduce plastic waste while also investing in renewable energy and protecting forests.

In conclusion, I agree that plastic pollution is one of the world's most serious environmental challenges, but I do not believe it is necessarily the most serious one. Climate change and deforestation also require urgent international action.

# Essay 39 — Elderly People and Retirement Homes

### Question

Elderly people should live with their family members rather than in retirement homes. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

Some people believe that elderly people should live with their family members instead of moving to retirement homes. I partly agree with this view because living with relatives can provide emotional support and companionship. However, retirement homes may be more suitable for elderly people who require professional care or whose families cannot provide sufficient support.

Living with family members has several important advantages. Elderly people can maintain close relationships with their children and grandchildren, which may reduce loneliness and improve their emotional well-being. Family members can also help them with everyday activities such as cooking, shopping and taking medicine. For example, an elderly parent living with their children may feel more secure because someone is available to assist them when necessary. In many cultures, living with older relatives is also considered an important way of showing respect and appreciation.

However, not every family is able to provide appropriate care. Many adults have full-time jobs and may spend most of the day outside their homes. Some elderly people also have serious health problems and require trained nurses or medical assistance. In such circumstances, a good retirement home can provide professional care, regular meals and suitable medical support. These facilities may also organise social activities, allowing elderly residents to communicate with people of a similar age.

Therefore, the best living arrangement depends on the individual's health, personality and family circumstances. Families should discuss the needs and preferences of elderly relatives rather than assuming that one solution is suitable for everyone.

In conclusion, I partly agree that elderly people should live with their families because this can provide valuable emotional and social support. However, retirement homes can be a better option when professional care is necessary or when families cannot provide sufficient daily assistance.

# Essay 45 — Parents and Childcare

### Question

Both parents should share equal responsibility for childcare and housework. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

Some people believe that both parents should share equal responsibility for childcare and housework. I strongly agree with this view because sharing these responsibilities can reduce pressure on individual family members and create a fairer and more supportive home environment.

Firstly, sharing household duties can reduce stress for both parents. In many modern families, both parents work full-time, so it can be extremely difficult for one person to manage all the cooking, cleaning and childcare alone. If responsibilities are divided fairly, both parents can have more time to rest and pursue their careers. For example, one parent could prepare dinner while the other helps the children with their homework. They could then change these responsibilities on different days.

Secondly, equal participation in household activities can have a positive influence on children. Children learn many behaviours by observing their parents. If they see both parents cleaning, cooking and looking after the family, they are more likely to understand that these responsibilities should not belong to one gender. This can help them develop a more balanced attitude towards family life when they become adults.

Equal responsibility can also strengthen relationships between parents. When both partners contribute to daily tasks, neither person is likely to feel that they are doing most of the work. This can reduce arguments and create greater mutual respect. Furthermore, sharing childcare allows both parents to develop a close relationship with their children.

In conclusion, I strongly agree that both parents should share responsibility for childcare and housework. This approach can reduce stress, promote equality, strengthen relationships and teach children valuable lessons about cooperation and responsibility.

# Essay 52 — Globalisation and National Identity

### Question

Globalization has led to the loss of national identities. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

Globalisation has brought countries and people closer together than ever before. However, some people argue that this process has caused the loss of national identities. I partly agree because international culture can weaken some traditional customs, but globalisation can also provide countries with new opportunities to promote their cultural heritage.

On the one hand, globalisation can influence people's lifestyles and reduce interest in traditional customs. People in different countries increasingly watch the same films, listen to similar types of entertainment and use the same social media platforms. International brands are also becoming common in many cities. As a result, young people may become more interested in foreign lifestyles than in traditional clothing, food or customs. If this trend continues for many years, some local traditions may gradually become less common.

On the other hand, globalisation can actually help countries preserve and promote their cultures. The internet allows people to introduce their traditional food, music, festivals and crafts to international audiences. Tourism can have a similar effect because visitors often want to experience the unique culture of a particular country. For example, traditional food can become popular internationally when tourists share their experiences online. This may encourage local communities to protect their cultural traditions.

Furthermore, national identity does not necessarily disappear simply because people adopt some foreign habits. A person can enjoy international films and products while still speaking their native language and following their traditional customs.

In conclusion, I partly agree that globalisation can weaken national identity, especially when younger people lose interest in traditional practices. However, it can also help countries promote their culture internationally. Therefore, the effect of globalisation depends largely on how societies manage and protect their cultural heritage.

# Essay 55 — Refugees from War-Torn Countries

### Question

Rich countries should accept more refugees from war-torn nations. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

Some people believe that wealthy countries should accept more refugees from nations affected by war. I largely agree with this opinion because refugees often have no safe place to live and rich countries generally have greater resources to help them. However, refugee programmes should be carefully managed so that host countries can also cope with the additional population.

The strongest argument for accepting refugees is humanitarian. People living in war zones may lose their homes, jobs and family members, while their lives can be threatened by violence. Children may also lose access to education and healthcare. Wealthier countries are usually in a better position to provide safe accommodation, medical care and education. For example, a refugee family escaping a dangerous conflict may be able to rebuild its life if it is given temporary protection and access to employment in a safe country.

Accepting refugees can also provide long-term benefits if they are properly integrated into society. Many refugees have useful professional skills and are willing to work. If they receive language training and employment opportunities, they can contribute to the economy and become independent rather than depending permanently on government support.

Nevertheless, wealthy countries cannot accept an unlimited number of refugees without considering their own resources. A rapid increase in population can put pressure on housing, schools, hospitals and employment markets. There may also be challenges related to cultural integration. Governments should therefore establish a controlled system based on their capacity and should provide suitable support for both refugees and local communities.

In conclusion, I largely agree that rich countries should accept more refugees from war-torn nations because they have greater resources and a humanitarian responsibility. However, this should be done through well-planned policies that ensure refugees can integrate successfully without placing excessive pressure on host communities.

# Essay 58 — Tourism and Local Communities

### Question

Tourism has more negative impacts than positive effects on local communities. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### Answer

Tourism has become an important source of income for many communities around the world. Although it can cause environmental and social problems, I partly disagree with the statement because I believe that tourism generally brings more benefits than disadvantages when it is properly managed.

One of the main benefits of tourism is that it creates employment opportunities for local people. Tourists need hotels, restaurants, transport and entertainment, which creates demand for workers in these industries. Tourism also provides income for small businesses that sell food, clothes, souvenirs and traditional products. For example, a village that becomes a popular tourist destination may see new hotels and restaurants opening, which can provide jobs for local residents. Governments may also improve roads, public transport and other facilities to support visitors.

However, tourism can have several negative consequences. Large numbers of visitors can produce considerable amounts of waste and put pressure on natural resources. Popular destinations may also suffer from traffic congestion, noise and damage to natural areas. Furthermore, the cost of housing and other services can increase when there is strong demand from tourists and investors. In some places, traditional buildings and cultural practices may also be changed to satisfy visitors.

Despite these disadvantages, many of them can be reduced through effective government policies. Authorities can limit the number of visitors in environmentally sensitive areas, introduce waste-management systems and protect important cultural sites. Local communities should also receive a fair share of tourism income.

In conclusion, tourism can create environmental and social problems, but I believe its positive effects are greater when it is carefully controlled. It can provide employment, support local businesses and improve infrastructure while still protecting the environment and local culture.
