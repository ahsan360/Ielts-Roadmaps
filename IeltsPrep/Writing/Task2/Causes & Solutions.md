## Essay 1 — Art & Digital Entertainment

### Question
> Many young people today show little interest in traditional art forms such as painting and sculpture. What are the reasons for this, and how can interest in traditional art be revived?

### Sample Answer

Nowadays, many young people are becoming less interested in traditional forms of art, such as painting and sculpture. This shift is mainly driven by the overwhelming popularity of digital entertainment and a lack of exposure to classical arts in school curricula. However, there are several practical measures that governments, schools, and cultural organisations can take to encourage young people to appreciate these art forms.

One major reason for this decline is the rapid development of digital entertainment. Young people spend a considerable amount of time using smartphones, playing video games, and watching online content. Because these activities provide instant entertainment and are highly interactive, visiting a traditional museum or looking at static paintings may seem less exciting to many adolescents. Furthermore, traditional art is often not given enough attention in schools. When educational systems prioritize academic subjects over creative disciplines, students rarely have the opportunity to develop an appreciation for cultural heritage.

There are several effective measures that can be taken to revive interest in traditional art. First, schools should integrate regular art-related activities into their programs, such as guided museum visits, painting competitions, and hands-on workshops. These activities allow students to experience art directly rather than simply studying it from textbooks. Second, museums and galleries could use modern technology to make exhibitions more appealing. For instance, incorporating interactive displays, virtual reality tours, and multimedia presentations can make classical art more engaging for younger, tech-savvy audiences.

In conclusion, young people's declining interest in traditional art is mainly caused by the prevalence of digital entertainment and limited exposure in school education. By making art education more practical and utilizing modern technology in exhibition spaces, society can foster a renewed appreciation for traditional art among the youth.

## Essay 2 — Climate Change & Environmental Protection

### Question
> Climate change is leading to extreme weather conditions worldwide. What are the primary causes of climate change, and what actions can be taken to mitigate its impact?

### Sample Answer

Climate change has become one of the most serious environmental challenges globally, leading to extreme weather events such as severe floods, heatwaves, and prolonged droughts. The main causes of this problem are the heavy burning of fossil fuels and widespread deforestation. However, governments and individuals can take several effective actions to reduce its overall impact.

The primary cause of climate change is the increasing amount of greenhouse gases released into the atmosphere. Industries, power plants, and motor vehicles burn large quantities of coal, oil, and gas, which produce high levels of carbon dioxide. As global economies grow, energy consumption increases significantly, making atmospheric pollution worse. Another major factor is deforestation. Because trees absorb carbon dioxide from the air, cutting down large forest areas for agriculture and urban development severely reduces the Earth's natural ability to remove harmful greenhouse gases.

To address this issue, several coordinated steps must be taken. First, governments should increase investment in renewable energy sources, such as solar and wind power, which produce far fewer carbon emissions than fossil fuels. Additionally, stricter environmental regulations should be introduced for factories, and public transportation systems should be improved to encourage people to reduce private car usage. On an individual level, citizens can contribute by saving energy at home, using public transit, and supporting local tree-planting initiatives to help restore damaged ecosystems.

In conclusion, climate change is primarily driven by fossil fuel consumption and large-scale deforestation. By transitioning to clean energy sources, enforcing stricter industrial controls, and protecting natural forests, society can effectively mitigate the impacts of global warming.

## Essay 3 — Education & Workplace Readiness

### Question
> Many high school graduates are unprepared for university or the workplace. What are the causes of this problem, and what solutions can be proposed?

### Sample Answer

Many high school graduates enter university or the workforce without the necessary practical skills and foundational knowledge. This problem is mainly caused by an excessive focus on standardized testing and a lack of connection between school curricula and real-world requirements. However, schools and governments can implement several changes to resolve this issue effectively.

One major cause is that modern education systems rely heavily on examinations. Students often spend most of their time memorizing theoretical information to achieve high test scores, leaving little room to develop essential life skills. While theoretical knowledge is important, memorization alone does not prepare students for the demands of higher education or employment, which require critical thinking, communication, and problem-solving abilities. Furthermore, schools rarely collaborate with industries, meaning students graduate without understanding how to apply their knowledge in real work environments.

To overcome this problem, educational institutions should adopt a more balanced approach to learning. First, schools ought to introduce more practical, project-based assignments where students work in groups to solve real-world problems. This approach helps students develop both academic knowledge and practical skills. Second, schools should partner with universities and businesses to provide career guidance, vocational workshops, and short-term internships. These opportunities give students direct exposure to professional environments before they graduate.

In conclusion, high school graduates are often unprepared for the future because education systems focus too much on exams and theoretical memorization. By introducing practical learning methods and fostering partnerships with industries, schools can ensure that students are well-prepared for both university and employment.

## Essay 4 — Technology & Communication

### Question
> Fake news is spreading rapidly on social media platforms. What are the reasons for this, and what steps can be taken to control it?

### Sample Answer

The rapid spread of fake news on social media platforms has become a serious issue, threatening public trust and social stability. This trend is mainly caused by the ease of sharing content online and people's tendency to react to sensational headlines. However, technology companies, governments, and individuals can work together to control this problem effectively.

One major reason fake news spreads quickly is the way social media platforms function. Users can share information with thousands of people within seconds, often without verifying whether the content is accurate. In addition, false news stories are frequently designed with shocking or emotional headlines to attract attention, encouraging users to click and share them immediately. Some individuals and organizations deliberately create deceptive content to generate ad revenue, manipulate public opinion, or increase website traffic.

Several measures can be implemented to address this growing problem. First, social media companies should improve their automated systems and collaborate with independent fact-checkers to detect and flag false information. Content identified as misleading should have its visibility reduced or be clearly labeled. Second, governments should enforce appropriate laws against those who intentionally create and distribute harmful disinformation, while ensuring these laws do not restrict freedom of speech. Finally, schools should teach media literacy so that individuals learn how to verify information before sharing it online.

In conclusion, fake news spreads rapidly because social media makes sharing effortless and sensational stories attract high engagement. Through better content moderation by tech companies, proper legal frameworks, and improved media literacy, society can significantly reduce the impact of online misinformation.
