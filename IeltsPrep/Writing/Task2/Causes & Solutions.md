# Essay 2 — Art

## Question

Many young people today show little interest in traditional art forms such as painting and sculpture. What are the reasons for this, and how can interest in traditional art be revived?

## Sample Answer

Nowadays, many young people are becoming less interested in traditional forms of art, such as painting and sculpture. This is mainly because they are more attracted to modern forms of entertainment and have limited exposure to traditional art. However, there are several ways in which governments, schools and cultural organisations can encourage young people to appreciate these art forms.

One major reason for this decline is the rapid development of digital entertainment. Young people spend a considerable amount of time using smartphones, watching videos and playing games. These activities provide instant entertainment and are often more interactive than traditional art. As a result, visiting a museum or looking at paintings may seem less exciting to many young people. Another reason is that traditional art is not always given enough attention in schools. If students are rarely introduced to painting, sculpture and other cultural activities, they may not develop an interest in them.

There are several measures that can be taken to revive young people's interest. First, schools could organise regular art-related activities, such as painting competitions, museum visits and workshops. These activities would allow students to experience art rather than simply studying it from textbooks. Second, museums and galleries could use modern technology to make exhibitions more attractive. For example, they could provide interactive displays, virtual tours and multimedia presentations. Social media could also be used to promote traditional artists and their work to younger audiences.

In conclusion, young people's declining interest in traditional art is mainly caused by the popularity of digital entertainment and limited exposure to art education. Schools, museums and governments should make traditional art more interactive, accessible and relevant to young people.

# Essay 3 — Art

## Question

Museums and art galleries are facing a decline in visitor numbers. What are the causes of this trend, and what measures can be taken to attract more visitors?

## Sample Answer

Many museums and art galleries around the world are experiencing a decline in the number of visitors. This is mainly due to the growing popularity of digital entertainment and the perception that museums are boring or outdated. However, museums and galleries can take several measures to attract more people.

One important cause of this problem is the rapid growth of online entertainment. People can now watch films, documentaries and virtual exhibitions from the comfort of their homes. As a result, they may feel that there is little reason to visit a museum physically. Another factor is that some museums use traditional methods of displaying their collections. If visitors simply walk around rooms and read long descriptions, they may find the experience uninteresting, particularly younger people.

Another reason is the cost and inconvenience of visiting some museums. In large cities, people may have to travel a long distance or pay relatively high entrance fees. Therefore, they may prefer cheaper forms of entertainment.

Several solutions could help reverse this trend. First, museums should make exhibitions more interactive. For example, they could use virtual reality, touch screens and audio guides to make historical and artistic information easier to understand. Second, museums could organise special events, such as workshops, temporary exhibitions and cultural festivals. These events could encourage people to visit repeatedly. Offering free or discounted admission to students could also attract younger visitors.

Furthermore, museums should make better use of social media. Short videos and attractive online content could introduce interesting objects and upcoming exhibitions to a wider audience.

In conclusion, museums and galleries are losing visitors mainly because of digital entertainment, outdated presentation methods and accessibility issues. By adopting modern technology, organising engaging events and improving their online presence, they can attract a much larger audience.

# Essay 6 — Communication

## Question

Face-to-face communication is becoming less common among young people. What are the causes of this issue, and what solutions can be implemented?

## Sample Answer

Face-to-face communication has become less common among young people in many societies. This is mainly because of the widespread use of smartphones and social media, as well as the increasingly busy lifestyles of young people. However, parents, schools and technology companies can take several steps to encourage more direct communication.

One major cause is the rapid development of social media and messaging applications. Young people can communicate with friends instantly through text messages, voice messages and video calls without meeting them in person. Although these technologies are convenient, they can reduce the need for physical interaction. Another factor is that many young people spend a large amount of their free time playing online games, watching videos or browsing social media. Consequently, they may have fewer opportunities to communicate directly with others.

This trend can be addressed in several ways. First, parents should encourage children to spend more time interacting with family members. For example, families could have regular meals together without using smartphones. This would give family members an opportunity to talk about their experiences and strengthen their relationships.

Schools also have an important role to play. Teachers could organise group discussions, team projects and outdoor activities that require students to communicate directly with their classmates. Such activities would help young people develop interpersonal and communication skills.

In addition, young people should be educated about responsible technology use. They do not necessarily need to stop using social media, but they should learn to maintain a healthy balance between online and offline communication.

In conclusion, face-to-face communication is declining mainly because of smartphones, social media and busy lifestyles. Parents and schools can help solve this problem by encouraging family interaction, group activities and responsible use of technology.

# Essay 9 — Communication

## Question

Fake news is spreading rapidly on social media platforms. What are the reasons for this, and what steps can be taken to control it?

## Sample Answer

Fake news has become a serious problem because it can spread extremely quickly through social media platforms. There are several reasons for this trend, including the ease of sharing information and people's tendency to believe sensational stories. Governments, technology companies and individuals all have a role to play in controlling the problem.

One major reason fake news spreads rapidly is that social media allows users to share information with thousands of people within seconds. In many cases, users share articles without checking whether the information is accurate. Another reason is that false stories are often designed to attract attention. They may contain shocking headlines or emotional claims, which encourage people to click on and share them. In addition, some individuals deliberately create fake news to make money, influence public opinion or increase the number of visitors to their websites.

Several measures can be introduced to address this problem. First, social media companies should improve their systems for detecting false information. They could use advanced technology to identify suspicious content and reduce its visibility. They should also clearly label information that has been identified as false.

Second, governments should introduce appropriate laws against people who deliberately create and distribute harmful false information. However, these laws should be carefully designed so that they do not unnecessarily restrict freedom of expression.

Finally, individuals should learn how to check information before sharing it. Schools could teach students basic media literacy, including how to identify unreliable websites and compare information from different sources.

In conclusion, fake news spreads mainly because social media makes information easy to share and sensational stories attract attention. Technology companies, governments and individuals should work together to improve fact-checking and media literacy.

# Essay 10 — Crime

## Question

Youth crime is on the rise in many urban areas. What are the main causes of this problem, and what measures can be taken to reduce youth crime?

## Sample Answer

Youth crime is a growing concern in many cities around the world. Several factors contribute to this problem, including family difficulties, poverty, peer pressure and a lack of positive activities. However, governments, schools and families can take effective measures to reduce criminal behaviour among young people.

One important cause of youth crime is a difficult family environment. Children who receive little attention or supervision from their parents may be more likely to become involved in harmful activities. Poverty can also contribute to criminal behaviour because some young people may commit crimes to obtain money or basic necessities. Another major factor is peer pressure. Teenagers may become involved in crime because they want to impress their friends or be accepted by a particular group.

A lack of recreational opportunities can make the situation worse. In some urban areas, young people have limited access to sports facilities, libraries and community centres. Consequently, they may spend their free time with people who encourage negative behaviour.

There are several possible solutions. First, governments should invest in youth centres, sports facilities and educational programmes. These places could provide young people with safe environments where they can develop useful skills and spend their free time productively. Second, schools should provide guidance and counselling to students who show behavioural problems.

Parents also have an important responsibility. They should communicate regularly with their children and monitor their activities without being excessively controlling. Finally, young offenders should receive rehabilitation and education rather than only punishment. This could help them change their behaviour and return to society as responsible citizens.

In conclusion, youth crime is mainly caused by family problems, poverty, peer pressure and a lack of positive activities. Providing education, family support, recreational opportunities and rehabilitation can significantly reduce this problem.

# Essay 17 — Culture and Society

## Question

Many traditional languages are disappearing around the world. What are the reasons for this, and what solutions can help preserve endangered languages?

## Sample Answer

Many traditional and minority languages are gradually disappearing around the world. This is mainly because younger generations increasingly use major international languages for education, employment and communication. Governments and local communities, however, can take several steps to preserve endangered languages.

One major reason for language loss is the growing importance of widely spoken languages. For example, young people may prefer to learn a major language because it provides better educational and employment opportunities. As a result, they may use their traditional language less frequently. Another reason is migration. When families move from rural areas to cities, they often communicate using the dominant language of the new community. Over time, children may stop learning the language spoken by their grandparents.

The influence of modern media is another important factor. Most films, television programmes, websites and social media content are produced in a limited number of major languages. Therefore, young people may have fewer opportunities to hear or use their traditional language.

Several measures can help preserve endangered languages. First, governments should include local languages in school education. Children could be taught basic subjects or cultural studies in their traditional language. This would increase its everyday use. Second, governments could provide financial support for books, dictionaries, films and online content in minority languages.

Families also have an important role. Parents and grandparents should speak the traditional language with children at home. Community organisations could organise cultural events where people use the language in songs, stories and traditional activities.

In conclusion, traditional languages are disappearing mainly because of globalisation, migration, education and the dominance of major languages. Governments, schools and families should work together to ensure that young people continue to learn and use these languages.

# Essay 23 — Education

## Question

Many high school graduates are unprepared for university or the workplace. What are the causes of this problem, and what solutions can be proposed?

## Sample Answer

Many high school graduates enter university or the workplace without having the necessary knowledge and practical skills. This problem is mainly caused by an excessive focus on academic theory and insufficient preparation for real-life situations. However, schools and governments can make several changes to address this issue.

One major cause is that many education systems focus heavily on examinations. Students often spend most of their time memorising information because they need to achieve high marks in tests. Although academic knowledge is important, memorisation alone does not prepare students for university or employment. They also need skills such as communication, teamwork, critical thinking and problem-solving.

Another problem is the limited connection between schools and employers. Students may graduate without understanding what skills are required in the workplace. For example, they may know theoretical concepts but have little experience working on practical projects or solving real-world problems.

There are several solutions to this issue. First, schools should introduce more practical learning. Students could complete projects, presentations and group assignments that require them to apply their knowledge. This would help them develop both academic and practical abilities.

Second, schools could cooperate with universities and businesses to provide career guidance, internships and workplace visits. These experiences would give students a clearer understanding of their future options and responsibilities.

Teachers should also place greater emphasis on skills such as critical thinking and communication rather than relying mainly on memorisation. Governments can support this change by updating national curricula and providing training for teachers.

In conclusion, high school graduates are often unprepared for university and employment because education systems focus too much on examinations and theoretical knowledge. More practical education, career guidance, internships and skill-based learning could help students become better prepared for their future.

# Essay 26 — Education

## Question

Bullying in schools has become a serious problem in many countries. What are the main causes, and what steps can schools take to address this issue?

## Sample Answer

Bullying has become a serious problem in schools in many countries. It can negatively affect students' confidence, academic performance and emotional well-being. The main causes include family problems, peer pressure and a lack of effective supervision. Schools can take several measures to reduce this problem.

One major cause of bullying is the environment in which children grow up. Some children may copy aggressive behaviour that they observe at home or in their communities. They may believe that using physical or verbal aggression is an acceptable way to deal with others. Peer pressure is another important factor. Some students bully others because they want to appear powerful or gain acceptance from their friends.

Another cause is insufficient supervision at school. Bullying may occur in places such as school corridors, playgrounds or online spaces where teachers are not always present. If students believe that they will not be punished, they may continue their behaviour.

Schools can take several steps to address this issue. First, they should establish clear anti-bullying policies. Students should understand what behaviour is unacceptable and what consequences may follow. Teachers should also receive training so that they can identify signs of bullying and respond appropriately.

Second, schools should provide counselling for both victims and students who bully others. Victims need emotional support, while bullies may need help understanding the consequences of their actions and changing their behaviour.

Schools could also organise awareness programmes that teach students about respect, empathy and peaceful conflict resolution. Encouraging students to report bullying safely can also help teachers identify problems at an early stage.

In conclusion, bullying is often caused by family influences, peer pressure and inadequate supervision. Schools should introduce clear policies, provide counselling, train teachers and promote respect among students in order to create a safer learning environment.

# Essay 33 — Environment

## Question

Climate change is leading to extreme weather conditions worldwide. What are the primary causes of climate change, and what actions can be taken to mitigate its impact?

## Sample Answer

Climate change has become one of the most serious environmental challenges in the world. It is contributing to extreme weather events such as floods, heatwaves and droughts. The main causes are the burning of fossil fuels and deforestation, while governments and individuals can take several actions to reduce its effects.

The primary cause of climate change is the increasing amount of greenhouse gases released into the atmosphere. Industries, power plants and vehicles burn coal, oil and gas, which produce large amounts of carbon dioxide. As countries become more industrialised, energy consumption often increases, making the problem worse.

Deforestation is another major cause. Trees absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere, so cutting down large areas of forest reduces the Earth's ability to remove this gas. In addition, forests are sometimes destroyed to create farmland, roads and housing.

There are several measures that can be taken to address climate change. First, governments should increase investment in renewable energy sources such as solar and wind power. These sources produce much less greenhouse gas than fossil fuels. Governments could also improve public transport and encourage people to use buses, trains and bicycles instead of private cars.

Second, stricter environmental regulations should be introduced for industries. Companies that produce large amounts of pollution should be required to reduce their emissions. Governments should also protect forests and encourage tree planting.

Individuals can contribute as well. People can reduce unnecessary energy consumption, use public transport, recycle waste and avoid excessive consumption of products.

In conclusion, climate change is mainly caused by fossil fuel consumption and deforestation. Governments should promote renewable energy, improve public transport and protect forests, while individuals should adopt more environmentally friendly lifestyles.

# Essay 34 — Environment

## Question

Deforestation is occurring at an alarming rate in many parts of the world. What are the reasons for deforestation, and how can this problem be solved?

## Sample Answer

Deforestation is a serious environmental problem that is occurring rapidly in many parts of the world. Forests are being destroyed mainly because of agricultural expansion, logging and urban development. However, governments, businesses and individuals can take several measures to reduce this problem.

One of the main causes of deforestation is the increasing demand for agricultural land. As the world's population grows, more land is required to produce food. Therefore, forests are often cleared to create farms and areas for livestock. Another important cause is commercial logging. Trees are cut down to produce furniture, paper and other products. In some countries, illegal logging is also a major problem because forests are destroyed without proper control.

Urbanisation contributes to deforestation as well. As cities expand, forests may be cleared to build houses, roads and factories. In addition, some people depend on wood as a source of fuel, particularly in poorer communities.

Several solutions can be implemented. First, governments should introduce and enforce strict laws against illegal logging. Companies that destroy forests without permission should face significant penalties. Second, governments should establish protected forest areas where tree cutting is prohibited.

Another solution is to promote sustainable farming. Farmers should be encouraged to increase food production on existing agricultural land rather than clearing new forests. Governments could provide training and modern farming technology to help them achieve higher yields.

Reforestation is also essential. Governments and environmental organisations should plant trees in areas where forests have already been destroyed. Finally, consumers can help by choosing products made from sustainable materials and reducing unnecessary paper consumption.

In conclusion, deforestation is mainly caused by agriculture, logging and urban expansion. Stronger laws, sustainable farming, protected forests and large-scale tree planting are necessary to slow down and eventually reduce this problem.

# Essay 54 — Environment

## Question

Overpopulation in urban areas is causing severe environmental problems. What are the main causes, and what measures can be taken to solve this issue?

## Sample Answer

Many cities around the world are experiencing rapid population growth, which is creating serious environmental problems. The main causes include rural-to-urban migration, better employment opportunities and natural population growth. Governments can address these problems through better urban planning and by developing smaller cities and rural areas.

One major cause of urban overpopulation is migration from rural areas. People often move to cities because they believe they can find better jobs, education and healthcare there. Large cities usually have more businesses and educational institutions, which makes them attractive to young people. Another factor is population growth within cities themselves. When the number of residents increases rapidly, housing, transport and public services may become inadequate.

Urban overpopulation can lead to several environmental problems. A larger population produces more waste and increases demand for water and electricity. It can also result in traffic congestion and higher levels of air pollution. In some cities, the rapid construction of buildings reduces green spaces and puts pressure on natural resources.

Several measures can help solve this problem. First, governments should develop smaller cities and rural areas by creating employment opportunities, universities, hospitals and other services. If people can access good jobs and facilities outside major cities, they may have less reason to migrate.

Second, city authorities should improve public transport and waste-management systems. More parks and green spaces should also be created to improve urban environmental conditions. In addition, governments should carefully control construction and encourage environmentally friendly buildings.

In conclusion, urban overpopulation is mainly caused by migration and population growth. Governments should reduce pressure on major cities by developing rural areas and smaller towns while improving public transport, waste management and urban planning.

# Essay 44 — Family

## Question

The average size of families is decreasing in many modern societies. What are the causes of this shift, and what impact does it have on society?

## Sample Answer

The average size of families is becoming smaller in many modern societies. This trend is mainly caused by the increasing cost of raising children, higher levels of education and the growing participation of women in the workforce. Although smaller families can provide some benefits, they can also create challenges for society.

One major reason for smaller families is the rising cost of living. Housing, education, healthcare and childcare can be expensive, so many couples believe that having fewer children allows them to provide a better standard of living for their families. Another reason is the increasing importance of education and careers. Many young adults spend several years studying and establishing their careers before having children. Consequently, they may have less time or desire to raise a large family.

Women's changing role in society is another important factor. More women now pursue higher education and full-time careers. Balancing professional responsibilities with childcare can be difficult, so some couples choose to have fewer children.

There are both positive and negative effects of this trend. One advantage is that parents can spend more money and time on each child. This may allow children to receive better education, healthcare and emotional support. Smaller families may also reduce pressure on household resources.

However, there can be serious long-term consequences. If birth rates remain low, countries may experience an ageing population. This can create pressure on healthcare systems and pension programmes. A smaller working-age population may also make it more difficult to support elderly citizens.

In conclusion, families are becoming smaller mainly because of high living costs, longer education and changing career patterns. Although smaller families can improve the quality of life for individual children, very low birth rates may create economic and social difficulties in the future.

# Essay 47 — Food and Farming

## Question

Fast food consumption is increasing worldwide, leading to health issues. What are the reasons for this trend, and how can healthy eating habits be promoted?

## Sample Answer

Fast food has become increasingly popular around the world, and excessive consumption of it is contributing to various health problems. This trend is mainly caused by busy lifestyles, affordable prices and effective advertising. However, governments, schools and individuals can take several measures to encourage healthier eating habits.

One major reason for the popularity of fast food is the modern lifestyle. Many people work long hours and have limited time to prepare meals at home. Fast food is convenient because it can be purchased and eaten quickly. Another reason is its relatively low price. For people with limited incomes, fast food may appear to be an affordable option. In addition, fast-food companies spend large amounts of money on advertising, especially on social media. Attractive advertisements can encourage people, particularly young consumers, to choose unhealthy meals.

Regular consumption of fast food can contribute to obesity, heart disease and other health problems because many fast-food products contain high levels of salt, sugar and unhealthy fats.

There are several ways to promote healthy eating. First, governments could introduce stronger regulations on food advertising aimed at children. They could also require restaurants to display clear nutritional information on menus. This would help consumers make more informed choices.

Schools should also teach students about healthy nutrition. Providing healthy meals in school cafeterias could help children develop good eating habits from an early age. Furthermore, governments could organise public health campaigns explaining the benefits of a balanced diet.

Individuals also have a responsibility. They should try to prepare meals at home using fresh ingredients and limit their consumption of processed food.

In conclusion, fast food is becoming more popular because it is convenient, inexpensive and heavily advertised. Governments, schools and individuals should work together to promote nutritious food and encourage healthier lifestyles.

# Essay 56 — Global Issues

## Question

Many people in developing nations are migrating to developed countries. What are the main causes of this migration, and what steps can governments take to manage it?

## Sample Answer

A large number of people from developing countries migrate to developed nations in search of better opportunities. The main reasons for this movement are limited employment opportunities, lower wages and inadequate public services in developing countries. Governments can manage migration by improving local conditions and creating better systems for legal migration.

One major reason people move abroad is the lack of well-paid employment in their home countries. Many workers believe that developed countries can offer higher salaries and better career opportunities. Another factor is the quality of education and healthcare. Some families migrate because they want their children to attend better schools or because they need access to better medical services.

Political and social instability can also encourage migration. People may leave their countries if they feel unsafe or believe that their future is uncertain. In addition, information about life abroad is now easily available through social media, making migration appear more attractive to some people.

Governments can take several steps to manage this trend. First, developing countries should invest in education, healthcare and job creation. If people can find stable employment and good public services at home, fewer individuals may feel the need to migrate.

Second, governments should establish clear and legal migration systems. Developed countries can create appropriate work visa programmes based on labour-market needs. This can reduce illegal migration and ensure that migrants receive proper legal protection.

Governments should also cooperate internationally. Developing and developed countries can work together on employment programmes, skills training and migration agreements.

In conclusion, people from developing nations mainly migrate because of better employment, higher wages and improved public services abroad. Governments can manage migration by improving conditions in developing countries, creating legal migration channels and strengthening international cooperation.

# Essay 59 — Health

## Question

Childhood obesity is becoming a major health concern in many countries. What are the primary causes of this problem, and what measures can be taken to combat it?

## Sample Answer

Childhood obesity has become a serious health problem in many countries. It is mainly caused by unhealthy diets, a lack of physical activity and excessive screen time. Governments, schools and parents can take several measures to help children maintain a healthy weight.

One major cause of childhood obesity is the increasing consumption of unhealthy food. Many children regularly eat fast food, sweets and processed snacks that contain high amounts of sugar, salt and unhealthy fats. These foods are often heavily advertised, making them particularly attractive to young people.

Another important cause is a lack of physical activity. Many children spend a considerable amount of time watching television, playing video games or using smartphones. As a result, they may spend less time playing outdoors or participating in sports. Busy lifestyles and a lack of safe recreational areas can make this problem worse.

There are several ways to combat childhood obesity. First, parents should encourage children to eat balanced meals containing vegetables, fruits, whole grains and suitable sources of protein. They should also limit the amount of sugary drinks and processed food that children consume.

Schools have an important role as well. They should provide nutritious meals and ensure that students participate in regular physical education. Schools could also organise sports clubs and outdoor activities to make exercise more enjoyable.

Governments can contribute by regulating advertisements for unhealthy food aimed at children. They could also create more parks, playgrounds and cycling areas so that children have safe places to exercise.

In conclusion, childhood obesity is mainly caused by unhealthy diets, insufficient physical activity and excessive screen time. Parents, schools and governments should work together to encourage healthy eating, regular exercise and a more active lifestyle among children.
