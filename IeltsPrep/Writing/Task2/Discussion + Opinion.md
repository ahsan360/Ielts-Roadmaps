## 1. Art in Modern Society

### Prompt
People have different views about the importance of art in modern society. Some believe that art is an essential part of culture, while others consider it a waste of time and money. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
People have different views about the importance of art in modern society. Some believe that art is an essential part of culture, while others consider it a waste of time and money. In my opinion, art is valuable because it preserves culture and also improves people's quality of life.

On the one hand, some people believe that art is not a priority. Governments and individuals have many practical problems to deal with, such as unemployment, poor healthcare and a lack of good schools. From this perspective, spending large amounts of money on museums, paintings or public performances may seem unnecessary. For example, a city with poor roads may receive more benefit from repairing them than from building an expensive art centre. In addition, some people do not have a strong interest in art, so they may feel that public money should be used for services that everyone needs.

On the other hand, art plays an important role in culture and society. Paintings, music, literature and traditional crafts can show the history and values of a community. They can also help younger generations understand their cultural background. Furthermore, art can make cities more attractive and create opportunities for artists and other workers. It can also provide entertainment and reduce stress after a busy day.

In conclusion, although essential public services should receive sufficient funding, I believe art should not be regarded as a waste of resources. A balanced society needs both practical services and cultural activities, and reasonable investment in art can bring long-term social benefits.

## 2. Public Art vs. Essential Services

### Prompt
There is an ongoing debate about whether governments should spend public money on art installations or essential services. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
There is an ongoing debate about whether governments should spend public money on art installations or essential services. While public art can improve the appearance of cities, I believe basic services should receive greater priority, especially when public needs are not being met.

On the one hand, investing in public art can have several advantages. Large sculptures, murals and other installations can make public spaces more attractive and give cities a unique identity. They may also encourage tourism because visitors often enjoy taking photographs of interesting landmarks. In addition, public art can create a sense of pride among local residents and provide opportunities for artists. For instance, a well-designed artwork in a city centre can turn an ordinary area into a popular meeting place.

On the other hand, essential services have a more direct effect on people's daily lives. Governments are responsible for providing hospitals, schools, roads, clean water and public transport. If these services are poor, spending a large amount of money on decorative projects could be difficult to justify. For example, building a new hospital or improving an unsafe road can protect people's lives, whereas an art installation usually provides mainly cultural and visual benefits.

In my opinion, governments should first ensure that essential public services are properly funded. Once basic needs are being met, a reasonable amount of money can be allocated to public art. This approach would allow cities to develop culturally without ignoring urgent social problems.

In conclusion, public art can improve cities and support culture, but essential services should come first. Governments should therefore maintain a sensible balance between cultural investment and basic public needs.

## 3. Life Imprisonment vs. Rehabilitation

### Prompt
People have different opinions about the best way to deal with violent criminals. Some believe that life imprisonment is necessary to protect society, whereas others think rehabilitation is more effective. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
People have different opinions about the best way to deal with violent criminals. Some believe that life imprisonment is necessary to protect society, whereas others think rehabilitation is more effective. In my view, serious offenders should face strong punishment, but rehabilitation should also be included whenever it is possible.

On the one hand, life imprisonment can protect the public from dangerous criminals. A person who has committed a serious violent crime may continue to be a threat if they are released too quickly. Keeping such offenders in prison can therefore reduce the risk of further crimes. It may also give victims and their families a sense that justice has been done. In addition, strict punishment can send a clear message that violent behaviour has serious consequences.

On the other hand, rehabilitation can help some offenders change their behaviour. Criminals may commit crimes because of poor education, drug problems, family difficulties or bad social environments. Education, counselling and job training can help them develop better habits and prepare for a responsible life. If a prisoner successfully changes, society may benefit because the person is less likely to commit another crime after release.

I believe the best solution is a combination of punishment and rehabilitation. Extremely dangerous criminals may need to remain in prison for a very long time, but suitable prisoners should have access to education and psychological support. The aim should not only be to punish people but also to reduce future crime.

In conclusion, life imprisonment is useful for protecting society from the most dangerous offenders, while rehabilitation can reduce repeat crime. Therefore, both approaches should be used according to the seriousness of the crime and the possibility of reform.

## 4. Capital Punishment

### Prompt
Capital punishment remains a controversial issue in many countries. Some people argue that the death penalty is necessary to discourage serious crimes, while others believe that it is cruel and should be abolished. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Capital punishment remains a controversial issue in many countries. Some people argue that the death penalty is necessary to discourage serious crimes, while others believe that it is cruel and should be abolished. In my opinion, capital punishment should not be used because there are serious risks of injustice.

On the one hand, supporters of the death penalty argue that it can be a strong punishment for extremely serious crimes. They believe that people who deliberately murder others should face the most severe possible consequence. Some also claim that the possibility of execution may discourage criminals from committing violent acts. Furthermore, the families of victims may feel that a severe punishment provides a form of justice.

On the other hand, opponents argue that the death penalty is inhumane. The most serious problem is that courts can make mistakes. If an innocent person is executed, the decision cannot be corrected. Another concern is that governments should focus on protecting society rather than taking a person's life. Life imprisonment can keep dangerous criminals away from the public without creating the possibility of an irreversible mistake.

I believe that capital punishment should be abolished. Although severe crimes deserve severe punishment, life imprisonment can protect society and provide justice without ending a person's life. Governments should also invest in better investigations and legal systems so that serious criminals are properly punished.

In conclusion, supporters see capital punishment as a powerful deterrent and a form of justice, whereas opponents consider it cruel and risky. I believe life imprisonment is a safer and more humane alternative.

## 5. Globalization and Culture

### Prompt
Globalization has changed the way people communicate, work and live, and it has also affected traditional cultures. Some people believe that globalization causes cultural loss, while others think it creates cultural enrichment. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Globalization has changed the way people communicate, work and live, and it has also affected traditional cultures. Some people believe that globalization causes cultural loss, while others think it creates cultural enrichment. In my opinion, globalization can bring many cultural benefits, but local traditions need deliberate protection.

On the one hand, globalization can weaken traditional culture. People, especially young people, are increasingly exposed to international films, fashion, food and lifestyles through the internet. As a result, they may become less interested in traditional clothing, local languages or customs. For example, young people in many countries may prefer international brands and foreign entertainment to traditional products and cultural activities. If this continues for several generations, some local traditions could gradually disappear.

On the other hand, globalization allows people to learn about cultures from around the world. International travel, social media and education make it easier for people to understand different lifestyles and ideas. This can reduce cultural misunderstanding and encourage respect between communities. Moreover, globalization can help local traditions reach an international audience. Traditional food, crafts and festivals can attract tourists and create income for local people.

I believe globalization itself is not the main problem. The real challenge is failing to protect valuable traditions while accepting useful international influences. Schools, families and governments can teach young people about local history, language and customs while still allowing them to enjoy global culture.

In conclusion, globalization can cause some traditional practices to decline, but it can also create greater cultural understanding. I believe societies should accept the benefits of globalization while actively preserving their unique cultural identity.

## 6. Social Media and Relationships

### Prompt
Social media has become an important part of modern communication. Some people believe that these platforms damage social relationships, while others argue that they help people stay connected. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Social media has become an important part of modern communication. Some people believe that these platforms damage social relationships, while others argue that they help people stay connected. In my opinion, social media is beneficial when it is used in a balanced and responsible way.

On the one hand, excessive use of social media can harm relationships. People may spend more time looking at their phones than talking to family members or friends in person. This can reduce the quality of face-to-face communication. Social media can also create misunderstandings because written messages do not always show a person's real feelings. In addition, comparing one's life with carefully selected posts from others may cause jealousy or dissatisfaction.

On the other hand, social media makes communication much easier. People can stay in contact with relatives and friends who live in different cities or countries. This is particularly useful for families who are separated by work or education. Social media can also help people find communities with similar interests. For example, a student can join an online group to discuss a subject, share ideas and receive support from other learners.

I believe the effect of social media depends mainly on how people use it. If someone spends several hours online and ignores people around them, relationships may suffer. However, using social media to maintain contact and arrange real-life meetings can strengthen relationships.

In conclusion, social media can create problems when it replaces real communication, but it can also connect people across long distances. Therefore, people should use these platforms moderately and make sure that online communication does not replace meaningful personal interaction.

## 7. Free University Education

### Prompt
There is considerable debate about whether university education should be free for everyone or whether students should pay for it. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
There is considerable debate about whether university education should be free for everyone or whether students should pay for it. Although free education can provide greater equality, I believe students should contribute at least part of the cost when they are financially able to do so.

On the one hand, free university education can give talented students from poor families a better opportunity to succeed. Many capable young people cannot attend university because tuition fees are too high. If higher education is free, students can choose subjects according to their abilities rather than their financial situation. A well-educated population can also benefit the whole country because graduates can become teachers, engineers, doctors and other skilled professionals.

On the other hand, universities require a large amount of money to provide qualified teachers, modern equipment and good facilities. If governments pay the entire cost for every student, taxpayers may have to carry a significant financial burden. Moreover, graduates often earn higher salaries than people without university qualifications. Therefore, it can be argued that they should contribute to the cost of their education, particularly after they begin earning a stable income.

In my opinion, a mixed system would be the fairest solution. Governments should provide free or heavily subsidised education for students from low-income families and offer scholarships to high-performing students. Other students could pay reasonable fees or repay part of the cost after graduation.

In conclusion, free university education can improve equality, while student fees can reduce pressure on public budgets. I believe a flexible system based on family income and academic performance would provide the best balance.

## 8. Academic vs. Vocational Education

### Prompt
People have different views about what students should study at school. Some believe academic subjects should be the main focus, whereas others argue that vocational training is equally important. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
People have different views about what students should study at school. Some believe academic subjects should be the main focus, whereas others argue that vocational training is equally important. In my opinion, both forms of education are valuable because students have different abilities and career goals.

On the one hand, academic education provides students with theoretical knowledge and develops important thinking skills. Subjects such as mathematics, science and languages are necessary for many professional careers. For example, doctors, engineers and researchers need strong academic backgrounds before they can receive professional training. Academic subjects can also teach students how to analyse information, solve problems and communicate clearly.

On the other hand, vocational education prepares students for practical jobs. Many industries need skilled electricians, mechanics, builders, cooks and technicians. These careers often require hands-on experience rather than extensive academic study. Vocational courses can also help young people enter the workforce more quickly and reduce unemployment. In some cases, skilled workers can earn a good income without attending university.

I believe schools should provide a balance between academic and vocational education. Students should have opportunities to learn basic academic subjects while also exploring practical skills. This would allow them to choose a suitable path according to their interests and abilities. For instance, a student who enjoys repairing machines should not be forced to follow a purely academic route.

In conclusion, academic subjects are essential for many careers, but vocational training is equally useful for developing practical skills. Therefore, education systems should offer both options and help students choose the path that best matches their future plans.

## 9. Online vs. Classroom Learning

### Prompt
Online learning has become increasingly common, and people have different opinions about its effectiveness. Some believe it is better than traditional classroom education, while others prefer face-to-face instruction. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Online learning has become increasingly common, and people have different opinions about its effectiveness. Some believe it is better than traditional classroom education, while others prefer face-to-face instruction. In my opinion, online learning is highly useful, but classroom learning remains more effective for many students.

On the one hand, online education offers flexibility and convenience. Students can attend lessons from home and often choose a suitable time for studying. This is particularly helpful for people who have jobs or live far from educational institutions. Online courses can also provide access to teachers and learning materials from different parts of the world. In addition, students can watch recorded lessons several times if they do not understand a topic.

On the other hand, traditional classrooms provide direct interaction between teachers and students. A teacher can immediately notice when a student is confused and explain the topic in another way. Classroom learning also encourages students to communicate with classmates and work together. Furthermore, studying in a classroom can reduce distractions because students are in an environment designed for learning.

I believe a combination of both methods is the most effective approach. Online resources can provide flexibility and additional practice, while face-to-face lessons can provide discipline, interaction and personal guidance. For younger students in particular, regular classroom instruction may be more suitable because they often need direct supervision.

In conclusion, online learning is convenient and gives students access to a wide range of resources, while traditional education offers stronger personal interaction. I believe combining the two methods can provide students with the greatest number of advantages.

## 10. School Uniforms

### Prompt
Whether students should be required to wear school uniforms is a subject of debate. Some people believe uniforms create discipline and equality, while others think children should have freedom to choose their clothes. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Whether students should be required to wear school uniforms is a subject of debate. Some people believe uniforms create discipline and equality, while others think children should have freedom to choose their clothes. In my opinion, school uniforms are generally beneficial, although schools should allow some flexibility.

On the one hand, uniforms can create a sense of equality among students. When everyone wears similar clothes, there may be less pressure to buy expensive brands or fashionable outfits. This can be particularly helpful for families with limited incomes. Uniforms can also make students look more organised and help schools maintain a professional image. In addition, teachers can easily identify students during school trips or other activities.

On the other hand, allowing students to choose their own clothes can encourage independence and self-expression. Clothing is one way for young people to show their personality and preferences. Some students may also feel more comfortable and confident when they can decide what to wear. Furthermore, buying a compulsory uniform can create an additional cost for parents, especially when children grow quickly.

I believe uniforms should be required in most schools because their benefits are greater than their disadvantages. However, schools should choose reasonably priced and comfortable designs rather than expensive uniforms. They could also allow students to wear different clothes on special days or during certain activities.

In conclusion, free clothing choices can encourage individuality, but school uniforms can reduce social pressure and promote a sense of unity. Therefore, I support compulsory uniforms with reasonable rules and some limited freedom.

## 11. Video Games

### Prompt
Video games are extremely popular among young people, but their effects are widely debated. Some people believe they have harmful effects, while others argue that they develop useful skills. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Video games are extremely popular among young people, but their effects are widely debated. Some people believe they have harmful effects, while others argue that they develop useful skills. In my opinion, video games can be beneficial when they are played in moderation, but excessive gaming can cause serious problems.

On the one hand, spending too much time playing games can affect young people's health and education. Children who play for many hours may exercise less and sleep poorly. They may also neglect homework and family activities. Some games contain violent content, which some people believe can influence children's behaviour. Moreover, excessive gaming can become a habit that is difficult to control.

On the other hand, video games can develop several useful abilities. Many games require players to solve problems, remember information and make quick decisions. Strategy games, for example, can encourage players to plan carefully and think about the results of their actions. Some multiplayer games also require communication and teamwork. In addition, educational games can make learning more enjoyable for children.

I believe the main issue is not gaming itself but the amount of time spent on it. Parents should set reasonable limits and make sure that children also study, exercise and spend time with their families. Schools can also teach students how to use digital entertainment responsibly.

In conclusion, excessive gaming can damage health, education and social life, while controlled gaming can develop useful mental and social skills. I therefore believe video games can be a positive activity if young people use them sensibly and maintain a balanced lifestyle.

## 12. Environmental Responsibility

### Prompt
Environmental problems such as climate change, pollution and deforestation affect countries around the world. Some people believe international organizations should take primary responsibility, while others think individual actions are more effective. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Environmental problems such as climate change, pollution and deforestation affect countries around the world. Some people believe international organizations should take primary responsibility, while others think individual actions are more effective. In my opinion, both levels are necessary, but governments and international organizations have greater power to create large-scale change.

On the one hand, international organizations can coordinate countries and create common environmental policies. Problems such as climate change cannot be solved by one country alone because pollution and greenhouse gases cross national borders. International agreements can encourage governments to reduce emissions and protect forests. Large organizations can also provide money and technology to poorer countries so that they can adopt cleaner methods.

On the other hand, individual behaviour also has an important effect. Millions of people make daily choices about transport, food, electricity and waste. If individuals use public transport, reduce plastic use, save energy and recycle, the total effect can be significant. Consumers can also influence companies by choosing environmentally friendly products.

I believe individual action is important, but it is not enough on its own. Governments and international bodies can introduce laws that influence millions of people at the same time. For example, a government can improve public transport and limit industrial pollution, while individuals can choose to use those services responsibly.

In conclusion, international organizations can provide coordination and large-scale solutions, while individuals can support environmental protection through daily choices. I believe the most effective approach is cooperation between governments, international organizations, businesses and ordinary citizens.

## 13. Parents and Children's Choices

### Prompt
Parents have a major influence on their children's development, but there is disagreement about how much control they should have. Some people believe parents should make important decisions for their children, while others think children need freedom. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Parents have a major influence on their children's development, but there is disagreement about how much control they should have. Some people believe parents should make important decisions for their children, while others think children need freedom. In my opinion, parents should provide guidance and set reasonable limits, but children should gradually be allowed to make their own choices.

On the one hand, parents have more life experience and can protect children from harmful decisions. Young children may not understand the long-term consequences of their actions. Parents can therefore guide them in areas such as education, friendships, internet use and health. For example, a parent may prevent a child from spending too much time online because it could affect sleep and schoolwork.

On the other hand, children need independence to develop confidence and responsibility. If parents control every decision, children may become dependent on others and struggle to solve problems themselves. Allowing them to choose hobbies, friends or some educational activities can teach them how to make decisions and learn from mistakes.

I believe the right level of control depends on the child's age. Young children require more supervision, while teenagers should receive greater freedom. Parents should explain the reasons behind important rules instead of simply giving orders. This can create trust and help children understand how to make sensible decisions.

In conclusion, parental guidance is essential for children's safety and development, but excessive control can prevent them from becoming independent. I believe parents should gradually reduce their control as children become older and more responsible.

## 14. Organic vs. Modern Farming

### Prompt
There is debate about whether organic farming or modern agricultural technology is the best way to produce food. Some consider organic farming healthier and more sustainable, while others argue modern technology is needed for large-scale production. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
There is debate about whether organic farming or modern agricultural technology is the best way to produce food. Organic farming is considered healthier and more environmentally friendly, while modern techniques can produce food on a much larger scale. In my opinion, modern methods are necessary, but they should be used in a safer and more sustainable way.

On the one hand, organic farming has several advantages. Farmers avoid many artificial chemicals, which can reduce the amount of harmful substances entering the soil and water. Organic methods can also protect biodiversity and improve soil quality. Some consumers believe that organic food is healthier, and the growing demand for such products can provide farmers with a good market.

On the other hand, modern farming techniques can produce much larger quantities of food. Improved seeds, irrigation systems, machinery and scientific methods allow farmers to grow crops more efficiently. This is particularly important because the world's population is increasing and food demand is also rising. Without modern technology, some countries might struggle to produce enough food at affordable prices.

I believe the best solution is not to completely choose one method over the other. Farmers should use modern technology while reducing unnecessary chemical use and protecting natural resources. For example, precision farming can help farmers apply water and fertiliser only where they are needed. This can increase production while reducing environmental damage.

In conclusion, organic farming offers environmental and health benefits, whereas modern agriculture is essential for large-scale food production. I believe combining scientific technology with sustainable farming practices is the most practical way to meet future food needs.

## 15. International Aid

### Prompt
International aid is often provided to poorer countries after natural disasters or during periods of economic difficulty. Some people believe this support is essential for development, while others argue that it creates dependency. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
International aid is often provided to poorer countries after natural disasters or during periods of economic difficulty. Some people believe this support is essential for development, while others argue that it creates dependency. In my opinion, international aid can be very helpful when it is carefully managed and used to build long-term independence.

On the one hand, foreign aid can provide immediate support during serious crises. Developing countries may not have enough money to respond to floods, earthquakes, disease outbreaks or food shortages. International assistance can provide medicine, food, clean water and temporary housing. Aid can also support schools, hospitals and infrastructure, which can improve people's living conditions and economic opportunities.

On the other hand, continuous aid may create dependency if countries rely on foreign support instead of developing their own economies. Poor management can also cause money to be wasted or misused. If aid is given without clear goals, governments may have less motivation to improve taxation, public services and local industries. In some cases, long-term dependence can make a country less able to solve its own problems.

I believe international aid is beneficial when it focuses on development rather than permanent assistance. Donor countries should support education, healthcare, infrastructure and job creation. They should also work with local governments and communities to ensure that projects are transparent and useful. The ultimate aim should be to help countries become self-sufficient.

In conclusion, international aid can save lives and support economic development, but poorly designed aid can create dependency. I believe aid should therefore be temporary, transparent and focused on helping developing countries build their own capacity.

## 16. Free Healthcare

### Prompt
Healthcare is an essential part of a healthy society, but there is disagreement about who should pay for it. Some people believe medical treatment should be free for everyone, while others think individuals should pay their own costs. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Healthcare is an essential part of a healthy society, but there is disagreement about who should pay for it. Some people believe medical treatment should be free for everyone, while others think individuals should pay their own costs. In my opinion, basic healthcare should be publicly funded, although people who can afford additional services may reasonably contribute.

On the one hand, free healthcare can ensure that everyone receives treatment when they need it. Poor people may avoid visiting doctors if medical fees are too high, which can allow illnesses to become more serious. A public healthcare system can therefore reduce inequality and protect vulnerable members of society. It can also benefit the whole population because preventing and treating diseases can reduce the spread of illness.

On the other hand, completely free healthcare can place a heavy burden on governments. Hospitals require doctors, nurses, medicines, equipment and regular maintenance, all of which are expensive. If healthcare is provided without any limits, some people may use medical services unnecessarily. Supporters of private payment also argue that individuals should take some responsibility for their own health expenses.

I believe essential healthcare should be funded mainly through taxes so that people can receive necessary treatment regardless of their income. However, governments can charge reasonable fees for non-essential services or provide private options for people who want faster or more specialised treatment. This system could maintain access while controlling public spending.

In conclusion, asking individuals to pay all medical costs can create serious inequality, while completely free healthcare can be expensive for governments. I believe a publicly funded basic healthcare system, combined with optional private services, is the fairest solution.
