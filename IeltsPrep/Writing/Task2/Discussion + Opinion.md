## 1. Art in Modern Society

### Prompt
People have different views about the importance of art in modern society. Some believe that art is an essential part of culture, while others consider it a waste of time and money. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
People have different views about the importance of art in modern society. Some believe that art is an essential part of culture, while others consider it a waste of time and money. In my opinion, art is valuable because it preserves culture, but essential public services must be prioritized.

On the one hand, some people believe that art is not a priority because governments have many practical problems to deal with, such as unemployment and healthcare. Spending large amounts of money on museums or paintings may seem unnecessary when a city needs basic infrastructure improvements. In addition, people who do not have an interest in art often feel that public money should be used for services that benefit everyone directly.

On the other hand, art plays an important role in preserving culture and society. Paintings, music, and traditional crafts show the history and values of a community, helping younger generations understand their roots. Furthermore, public art can make cities attractive, boost tourism, and give people entertainment that reduces stress after work.

In conclusion, although practical public services should receive sufficient funding, I believe art should not be considered a complete waste of resources. A balanced society needs both essential services and cultural activities to improve people's quality of life.

## 2. Public Art vs. Essential Services

### Prompt
There is an ongoing debate about whether governments should spend public money on art installations or essential services. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
There is an ongoing debate about whether governments should spend public money on art installations or essential services. While public art can improve the appearance of cities, I believe basic services should receive greater priority, especially when public needs are not being met.

On the one hand, investing in public art offers several visual and economic advantages. Large sculptures and murals make public spaces more attractive and give cities a unique identity, which can encourage tourism. In addition, public art creates a sense of pride among local residents and provides opportunities for local artists to showcase their talents.

On the other hand, essential services have a direct effect on people's daily health and safety. Governments are responsible for providing hospitals, schools, clean water, and roads. If these services are poor, spending public funds on decorative projects is difficult to justify because an art installation cannot protect lives or solve urgent social problems.

In conclusion, although public art supports local culture and urban design, basic services should always come first. Governments should ensure essential needs are fully met before allocating funds to decorative projects.

## 3. Life Imprisonment vs. Rehabilitation

### Prompt
People have different opinions about the best way to deal with violent criminals. Some believe that life imprisonment is necessary to protect society, whereas others think rehabilitation is more effective. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
People have different opinions about the best way to deal with violent criminals. Some believe that life imprisonment is necessary to protect society, whereas others think rehabilitation is more effective. In my view, serious offenders should face strong punishment, but rehabilitation should also be included whenever possible.

On the one hand, life imprisonment protects the public from dangerous criminals who might commit further crimes if released too quickly. Keeping violent offenders in prison provides safety to the community and gives victims a sense of justice. In addition, strict prison sentences send a clear message that violent behavior brings severe consequences.

On the other hand, rehabilitation helps offenders reform and address the root causes of crime, such as poor education, drug addiction, or difficult backgrounds. Educational programs, counseling, and job training help prisoners develop practical skills and habits. When a reformed criminal returns to society without reoffending, the whole community benefits.

In conclusion, while life imprisonment is necessary for the most dangerous individuals to ensure public safety, rehabilitation should be offered to suitable prisoners. Combining punishment with reform is the best way to reduce future crime rates.

## 4. Capital Punishment

### Prompt
Capital punishment remains a controversial issue in many countries. Some people argue that the death penalty is necessary to discourage serious crimes, while others believe that it is cruel and should be abolished. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Capital punishment remains a controversial issue in many countries. Some people argue that the death penalty is necessary to discourage serious crimes, while others believe that it is cruel and should be abolished. In my opinion, capital punishment should not be used because of the serious risks of executing innocent people.

On the one hand, supporters argue that the death penalty acts as a powerful warning against extreme crimes like murder. They believe that severe consequences prevent future criminals from committing violent acts. Furthermore, executing dangerous criminals gives a feeling of final justice to the victims' families.

On the other hand, opponents argue that the death penalty is inhumane and risks irreversible mistakes. Legal systems are run by humans and can make errors, meaning an innocent person could be wrongly executed. In contrast, life imprisonment keeps dangerous individuals away from society safely without taking a human life.

In conclusion, although severe crimes require serious consequences, I believe capital punishment should be abolished. Life imprisonment offers a safer and more humane alternative that protects society without the risk of irreversible injustice.

## 5. Globalization and Culture

### Prompt
Globalization has changed the way people communicate, work and live, and it has also affected traditional cultures. Some people believe that globalization causes cultural loss, while others think it creates cultural enrichment. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Globalization has changed how people communicate and live, affecting traditional cultures worldwide. Some people believe that globalization causes cultural loss, while others think it creates cultural enrichment. In my opinion, globalization brings many benefits, but local traditions need active protection.

On the one hand, global media and foreign entertainment can weaken local customs. Young people frequently consume international films, fashion, and food through the internet, which can make them lose interest in traditional clothing or native languages. Over several generations, unique local practices may gradually disappear as global culture dominates.

On the other hand, globalization allows people to share and learn about different cultures easily. International travel and social media build cultural understanding and respect between nations. Additionally, global exposure allows local traditions, crafts, and food to reach wider international audiences and boost local tourism.

In conclusion, while globalization can cause some traditional practices to decline, it also encourages cross-cultural learning. Societies should embrace the advantages of global connections while taking clear steps to preserve their unique cultural identity.

## 6. Social Media and Relationships

### Prompt
Social media has become an important part of modern communication. Some people believe that these platforms damage social relationships, while others argue that they help people stay connected. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Social media has become an important part of modern communication. Some people believe that these platforms damage social relationships, while others argue that they help people stay connected. In my opinion, social media is beneficial when used in a balanced and responsible way.

On the one hand, excessive screen time can harm face-to-face interactions. When people spend more time checking their phones than talking to family and friends in person, the quality of their real-life relationships drops. Additionally, written messages can easily lead to misunderstandings, and comparing oneself to online posts can cause envy.

On the other hand, social media helps people maintain connections over long distances. It allows individuals to stay in touch with relatives living in different cities or countries easily. Furthermore, these platforms enable users to join online groups with shared interests, providing valuable peer support and learning opportunities.

In conclusion, social media can harm personal connections if it replaces real-life interaction, but it remains a great tool for long-distance communication. People should use these platforms moderately to maintain strong, healthy relationships.

## 7. Free University Education

### Prompt
There is considerable debate about whether university education should be free for everyone or whether students should pay for it. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
There is considerable debate about whether university education should be free for everyone or whether students should pay tuition fees. Although free education creates equal opportunities, I believe students who can afford it should contribute to their tuition costs.

On the one hand, free university education allows talented students from low-income families to study without financial stress. When tuition costs are removed, young people can choose degrees based on academic ability rather than money. This leads to a well-educated workforce, which benefits the overall economy through skilled professionals.

On the other hand, running higher education institutions requires significant funding for modern equipment, research facilities, and qualified staff. If governments cover all tuition fees, taxpayers face a heavy financial burden. Moreover, when students pay for their education, they are often more focused and motivated to complete their studies successfully.

In conclusion, while free higher education promotes social equality, requiring tuition fees helps maintain university standards and reduces public spending. A flexible system offering financial aid to poorer students alongside paid tuition for others provides the best balance.

## 8. Academic vs. Vocational Education

### Prompt
People have different views about what students should study at school. Some believe academic subjects should be the main focus, whereas others argue that vocational training is equally important. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
People have different views about what students should study at school. Some believe academic subjects should be the main focus, whereas others argue that vocational training is equally important. In my opinion, both forms of education are valuable because students have different abilities and career goals.

On the one hand, academic education provides foundational knowledge and critical thinking skills needed for professional careers. Subjects like mathematics, science, and literature are essential prerequisites for fields such as medicine, engineering, and law. Studying academic topics helps students analyze information and build strong analytical thinking abilities.

On the other hand, vocational education prepares students directly for practical jobs in fields like construction, plumbing, and mechanics. These hands-on careers are vital for society and do not require university degrees. Vocational training helps young people enter the workforce quickly, reducing youth unemployment and meeting labor demands.

In conclusion, academic subjects are necessary for professional paths, but vocational training is equally important for practical trades. Schools should offer both options so students can choose the path that matches their personal strengths.

## 9. Online vs. Classroom Learning

### Prompt
Online learning has become increasingly common, and people have different opinions about its effectiveness. Some believe it is better than traditional classroom education, while others prefer face-to-face instruction. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Online learning has become increasingly common, leading to discussions about its effectiveness compared to traditional education. While online learning offers flexibility, I believe classroom learning remains more effective for most students.

On the one hand, online education provides convenience by allowing students to study from home at their own pace. This flexibility benefits working adults or people living far from educational institutions. Furthermore, online platforms offer access to international courses and recorded lectures that can be reviewed multiple times.

On the other hand, classroom learning provides direct interaction with teachers and classmates. Teachers can quickly identify when students are confused and give immediate help, while group activities foster teamwork and social skills. Studying in a structured school environment also minimizes personal distractions.

In conclusion, although online education is convenient and accessible, traditional classroom instruction offers essential personal guidance and social interaction. Combining both approaches gives students the best overall learning experience.

## 10. School Uniforms

### Prompt
Whether students should be required to wear school uniforms is a subject of debate. Some people believe uniforms create discipline and equality, while others think children should have freedom to choose their clothes. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Whether students should be required to wear school uniforms remains a popular topic of debate. Some people believe uniforms create discipline and equality, while others think children should choose their own clothing. In my opinion, mandatory uniforms are beneficial for maintaining equality at school.

On the one hand, uniforms reduce social inequality among students. When everyone wears the same clothing, there is less peer pressure to buy expensive brand-name clothes, which helps families with lower incomes. Uniforms also give schools a professional image and help teachers identify students easily during field trips.

On the other hand, allowing students to wear casual clothes encourages personal freedom and self-expression. Choosing their own outfits lets young people display their personality and feel comfortable. In addition, purchasing specialized uniforms creates an extra expense for parents when children grow rapidly.

In conclusion, while casual clothing promotes individuality, mandatory uniforms help create a disciplined environment and reduce social pressure among students. Therefore, schools should enforce uniform rules while ensuring prices remain reasonable for families.

## 11. Video Games

### Prompt
Video games are extremely popular among young people, but their effects are widely debated. Some people believe they have harmful effects, while others argue that they develop useful skills. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Video games are very popular among young people today, but their overall effect is widely debated. While excessive gaming causes health and academic problems, I believe playing video games in moderation helps develop useful mental skills.

On the one hand, spending too much time playing video games leads to a sedentary lifestyle, poor sleep, and reduced physical activity. Students who play games for many hours often neglect their homework and family responsibilities. Furthermore, continuous exposure to violent game content may negatively affect behavior.

On the other hand, video games can improve problem-solving skills, memory, and hand-eye coordination. Strategy and puzzle games require players to think logically and make quick decisions under pressure. Additionally, online multiplayer games teach players how to communicate and cooperate effectively in teams.

In conclusion, excessive video gaming harms young people's physical health and studies, but controlled gaming builds valuable cognitive and social skills. Parents should set reasonable limits so children maintain a healthy, balanced lifestyle.

## 12. Environmental Responsibility

### Prompt
Environmental problems such as climate change, pollution and deforestation affect countries around the world. Some people believe international organizations should take primary responsibility, while others think individual actions are more effective. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Environmental issues like climate change and pollution pose global challenges today. While some argue that international organizations should take primary responsibility, others believe individual daily actions are more effective. In my opinion, both are necessary, but international organizations have greater power to create large-scale change.

On the one hand, international organizations can coordinate policies across national borders. Environmental issues like global warming affect all countries, so global agreements are required to limit industrial emissions and protect forests. Furthermore, international bodies can provide funding and technology to help developing nations adopt green energy.

On the other hand, individual choices directly impact resource consumption and waste production. When millions of people choose to recycle, reduce single-use plastics, save electricity, and use public transport, the collective environmental impact is massive. Individual consumer habits also encourage businesses to produce eco-friendly products.

In conclusion, individual efforts are essential for daily conservation, but international organizations are needed to enforce large-scale policies. A combination of global governance and personal effort offers the best solution to global environmental problems.

## 13. Parents and Children's Choices

### Prompt
Parents have a major influence on their children's development, but there is disagreement about how much control they should have. Some people believe parents should make important decisions for their children, while others think children need freedom. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Parents play a major role in raising children, but people disagree on how much control they should exercise. While some believe parents should make key decisions, others feel children need personal freedom. In my opinion, parents should provide guidance while gradually allowing children to make their own choices.

On the one hand, parents have life experience and can prevent children from making harmful choices. Young people often lack long-term thinking and may not understand the consequences of poor habits regarding schoolwork, diet, or screen time. Strong parental guidance keeps children safe and focused on their future.

On the other hand, giving children personal freedom builds independence and problem-solving skills. If parents control every aspect of a child's life, the child may struggle to make decisions independently as an adult. Allowing children to choose their hobbies, friends, and interests helps them build self-confidence.

In conclusion, parental control is essential for safety in early childhood, but excessive control limits personal growth. Parents should guide young children closely and grant them more decision-making freedom as they grow older.

## 14. Organic vs. Modern Farming

### Prompt
There is debate about whether organic farming or modern agricultural technology is the best way to produce food. Some consider organic farming healthier and more sustainable, while others argue modern technology is needed for large-scale production. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
There is ongoing debate about whether organic farming or modern technology is the best approach for food production. While organic farming is environmentally friendly, I believe modern agricultural technology is necessary to feed the world's growing population.

On the one hand, organic farming avoids synthetic chemicals, pesticides, and artificial fertilizers, protecting soil health and water quality. It supports biodiversity and produces food that many consumers consider safer and healthier. However, organic methods yield smaller harvests and require more land and labor.

On the other hand, modern farming uses high-yield seeds, machinery, and advanced irrigation to produce food efficiently on a massive scale. With global population numbers rising, these technological methods ensure food supplies remain stable and affordable, preventing widespread food shortages in poorer regions.

In conclusion, organic farming offers environmental benefits, but modern agricultural technology is essential for large-scale food security. Combining modern machinery with sustainable farming practices provides the most practical long-term solution.

## 15. International Aid

### Prompt
International aid is often provided to poorer countries after natural disasters or during periods of economic difficulty. Some people believe this support is essential for development, while others argue that it creates dependency. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
International aid is frequently sent to developing countries during economic crises or after natural disasters. While foreign aid provides essential emergency relief, some argue it creates long-term dependency. In my opinion, international aid is vital during emergencies but should focus on building local independence.

On the one hand, foreign aid delivers immediate life-saving support during disasters like earthquakes, floods, or disease outbreaks. Poor nations often lack the resources to provide emergency medical supplies, food, and clean water. Furthermore, financial aid helps rebuild damaged schools, hospitals, and infrastructure.

On the other hand, continuous aid can make receiving governments dependent on foreign funding instead of developing local industries and tax systems. Mismanaged aid may also lead to corruption and waste. When a country relies heavily on outside help, local economic development can slow down significantly.

In conclusion, emergency aid is necessary to save lives during disasters, but long-term aid must focus on building self-sufficiency. Donor nations should support local education, infrastructure, and business development so receiving countries can eventually support themselves.

## 16. Free Healthcare

### Prompt
Healthcare is an essential part of a healthy society, but there is disagreement about who should pay for it. Some people believe medical treatment should be free for everyone, while others think individuals should pay their own costs. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Sample Answer
Healthcare is essential for every society, but opinions differ on how medical services should be funded. While some believe medical care should be free for all, others think individuals should cover their own expenses. In my opinion, basic healthcare should be publicly funded, while non-essential treatments can be paid privately.

On the one hand, free healthcare ensures that every citizen receives medical treatment regardless of income level. When medical care is expensive, low-income families may avoid seeing doctors, causing minor health issues to become severe illnesses. Publicly funded healthcare reduces social inequality and protects overall public health.

On the other hand, providing completely free healthcare places a massive financial strain on government budgets and taxpayers. Maintaining modern hospitals, paying medical staff, and buying equipment requires immense funding. If services are entirely free, some people might misuse emergency rooms for minor health issues, causing long wait times.

In conclusion, relying entirely on private payments leaves poor citizens vulnerable, while fully free healthcare burdens public finances. A system where the government provides free basic medical care alongside optional private options offers the fairest approach.
