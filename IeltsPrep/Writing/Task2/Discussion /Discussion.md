# Essay 1 — Art: The Importance of the Arts

## Question

> Some people believe that the arts (such as painting, music, and theatre) are essential to society, while others think they are a waste of money that could be spent on more practical areas like health and education. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

## Answer

The arts, such as painting, music and theatre, play an important role in many societies. While some people believe that governments should spend money on more practical areas such as health and education, others think that the arts are also necessary. This essay will discuss both views and explain why I believe that the arts should continue to receive financial support.

One of the main reasons why some people consider the arts a waste of money is that health and education are more important for people's daily lives. Governments have limited budgets, so they need to give priority to essential services. For example, spending money on hospitals can provide better medical treatment, while investment in schools can improve the quality of education. As a result, some people believe that public money should mainly be used for these areas.

However, there are several reasons why the arts are important to society. First, art can improve people's quality of life and provide entertainment. For example, people can enjoy music, visit art galleries or watch theatre performances in their free time. In addition, the arts can help preserve a country's history and culture. Traditional paintings, music and performances can teach younger generations about their cultural background. Therefore, supporting the arts can have important social and cultural benefits.

In conclusion, although health and education are essential areas that deserve significant government funding, I believe that the arts should not be considered a waste of money. They provide entertainment, protect cultural traditions and contribute to people's quality of life.

# Essay 16 — Culture and Tradition: International Tourism

## Question

> Some people think that international tourism creates friction between local people and visitors, while others believe that it promotes international understanding. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

## Answer

International tourism has become increasingly common in many countries. While some people believe that it can create problems between local residents and foreign visitors, others think that it helps people understand different cultures. This essay will discuss both views and explain why I believe that international tourism has more benefits than drawbacks.

One of the main reasons why tourism may create friction is that visitors may not understand local customs and traditions. For example, tourists may behave in ways that are considered inappropriate by local people because they are unfamiliar with the culture. Another problem is that large numbers of tourists can create overcrowding and increase prices in popular areas. As a result, local residents may become unhappy with the negative effects of tourism.

However, there are several reasons why international tourism can promote better understanding between people. First, tourists can learn about the culture, traditions and lifestyle of other countries by visiting them. For example, they can try local food, communicate with residents and visit historical places. In addition, local people can learn about foreign cultures by interacting with visitors. This can reduce cultural misunderstandings and help people become more open-minded.

In conclusion, although international tourism can sometimes cause cultural misunderstandings and problems for local residents, I believe that it generally promotes international understanding. If tourists respect local customs and governments manage tourism properly, its benefits can be greater than its disadvantages.

# Essay 19 — Culture and Tradition: Local Culture vs Global Culture

## Question

> Some people believe that local culture should be preserved, while others think that embracing global culture is inevitable and beneficial. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

## Answer

Local and global cultures have become closely connected because of international travel, technology and social media. While some people believe that traditional cultures should be protected, others think that adopting global culture is unavoidable and beneficial. This essay will discuss both views and explain why I believe that local culture should be preserved while people remain open to positive global influences.

One of the main reasons for embracing global culture is that it allows people to learn from other societies. For example, people can discover new ideas, lifestyles and forms of entertainment through the internet and international travel. In addition, global culture can help people communicate more easily with others from different countries. As a result, people may become more open-minded and better understand different ways of life.

However, there are several reasons why local culture should be protected. First, traditional customs and languages are important parts of a country's identity. For example, traditional festivals, clothing and food can connect younger generations with their history. Another concern is that global culture may gradually replace local traditions, especially among young people. If this continues for a long time, some unique customs and languages may disappear. Therefore, governments and communities should make efforts to protect their cultural heritage.

In conclusion, although global culture can provide people with new ideas and improve international communication, I believe that local culture should be preserved. Countries can benefit from global influences without abandoning their own traditions and cultural identity.

# Essay 21 — Education: Parents vs Schools

## Question

> Some people believe that parents are responsible for teaching children how to be good members of society, while others think this is the responsibility of schools. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

## Answer

Parents and schools both play important roles in children's development. While some people believe that parents are mainly responsible for teaching children how to behave well in society, others think that schools should take this responsibility. This essay will discuss both views and explain why I believe that parents and schools should share this responsibility.

One of the main reasons why parents are considered responsible is that children usually learn their first behaviours at home. Parents can teach them basic values such as honesty, respect and kindness from an early age. For example, children can learn to respect older people by observing how their parents behave. In addition, parents spend a significant amount of time with their children, so they can correct inappropriate behaviour and provide guidance.

However, there are several reasons why schools also have an important role in this area. First, schools provide children with opportunities to interact with people from different backgrounds. For example, students learn to cooperate with classmates, follow rules and solve disagreements peacefully. In addition, teachers can teach important social values through classroom activities and group projects. As a result, children can develop the skills they need to become responsible members of society.

In conclusion, although parents have a major influence on children's behaviour and values, I believe that schools also play an essential role. The best approach is for parents and schools to work together to teach children good behaviour and social responsibility.

# Essay 25 — Education: University Education for Everyone

## Question

> Some people think that university education should be available to everyone, regardless of their financial status. Others believe that higher education should only be for those who can afford it or excel academically. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

## Answer

University education can provide people with valuable knowledge and skills for their future careers. While some people believe that higher education should be available to everyone regardless of their financial situation, others think that it should be limited to those who can afford it or perform well academically. This essay will discuss both views and explain why I believe that university education should be accessible to capable students from all financial backgrounds.

One of the main reasons for limiting university education is that universities have limited resources. If everyone could enter university without meeting any academic requirements, classrooms could become overcrowded and the quality of education might decline. For example, universities may not have enough teachers, laboratories or other facilities to support a very large number of students. Therefore, some people believe that academic ability should be considered when offering university places.

However, there are several reasons why financial status should not prevent capable students from attending university. First, talented students from poor families may have the ability to succeed but cannot afford tuition fees. For example, a student with excellent academic results may be unable to attend university because their family has a low income. In addition, educated people can contribute valuable skills to the economy. Therefore, governments should provide scholarships or financial support to students who cannot afford higher education.

In conclusion, although universities need to maintain academic standards because their resources are limited, I believe that financial difficulties should not prevent capable students from receiving higher education. Governments should provide financial assistance to ensure that talented students have equal opportunities.

# Essay 28 — Employment and Work: Staying in One Job vs Changing Jobs

## Question

> Some people prefer to stay in the same job or company for their entire working life. Others choose to change jobs or careers frequently. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

## Answer

People have different attitudes towards their careers. While some people prefer to remain in the same company or job throughout their working lives, others choose to change jobs or careers regularly. This essay will discuss both views and explain why I believe that changing jobs can be beneficial when it provides better opportunities.

One of the main advantages of staying in the same job is that it provides stability and security. Employees who remain in one company for many years can develop strong relationships with their colleagues and gain extensive experience in their field. For example, a long-term employee may receive promotions and additional responsibilities because of their experience. In addition, having a stable job can provide people with a predictable income and reduce the stress of finding new employment.

However, there are several reasons why changing jobs can also be beneficial. First, people may gain new skills and experience by working for different companies. For example, an employee may learn new technologies or management methods after moving to another organisation. In addition, changing jobs can provide better salaries and career opportunities. As a result, people may be able to achieve greater professional growth by changing positions when necessary.

In conclusion, although staying in one company can provide stability and allow employees to develop strong experience, I believe that changing jobs can be beneficial for career development. People should consider changing jobs when they can gain better skills, opportunities or working conditions.

# Essay 41 — Family: Grandparents vs Formal Childcare

## Question

> Some people think that grandmothers and grandfathers should look after grandchildren when parents are working, while others believe that formal childcare (like nurseries) is a better choice. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

## Answer

Working parents need reliable childcare while they are at work. While some people believe that grandparents should look after their grandchildren, others think that formal childcare such as nurseries is a better option. This essay will discuss both views and explain why I believe that both options can be suitable depending on the family's situation.

One of the main advantages of grandparents providing childcare is that children can stay with familiar family members. Grandparents usually have a close emotional relationship with their grandchildren and may provide personal attention. For example, they can teach children family traditions and spend time playing with them. In addition, this option may be cheaper than paying for a nursery, which can be expensive for working parents.

However, there are several reasons why formal childcare can be a better choice. First, professional childcare workers are trained to look after young children and can provide structured activities. For example, nurseries may organise educational games that help children develop communication and social skills. In addition, children can interact with other children of a similar age, which can help them become more confident and cooperative. Therefore, formal childcare can support both children's education and social development.

In conclusion, although grandparents can provide loving and affordable childcare, I believe that formal childcare can offer important educational and social benefits. The best choice depends on the family's financial situation, the grandparents' availability and the needs of the child.

# Essay 55 — Global Issues: International Aid

## Question

> Some people believe that international aid to developing countries is essential for global progress, while others argue that it creates dependency and causes more problems than solutions. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

## Answer

International aid is an important issue in discussions about global development. While some people believe that wealthy countries should provide financial and other forms of assistance to developing nations, others argue that aid can create dependency. This essay will discuss both views and explain why I believe that international aid can be beneficial if it is provided in an effective way.

One of the main reasons for providing international aid is that developing countries may lack the resources needed to improve essential services. For example, financial support can be used to build hospitals, schools and clean water systems. In addition, aid can be particularly important after natural disasters such as floods or earthquakes. As a result, international assistance can improve living conditions and help developing countries deal with serious problems.

However, there are several concerns about international aid. First, countries that receive large amounts of assistance may become dependent on foreign support instead of developing their own economies. For example, governments may continue to rely on financial aid rather than improving their tax systems or creating new industries. Another problem is that aid money can sometimes be poorly managed or lost through corruption. Therefore, simply providing money may not solve the deeper problems faced by developing countries.

In conclusion, although international aid can create dependency and may be poorly managed in some cases, I believe that it is essential for helping developing countries. However, aid should focus on long-term development, education and infrastructure rather than simply providing financial assistance.
