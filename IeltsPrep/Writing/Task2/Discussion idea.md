# CATEGORY 1: ART, CULTURE AND SOCIETY

## 1. Art in Modern Society

### Question

People have different views about the importance of art in modern society. Some believe that art is an essential part of culture, while others consider it a waste of time and money. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### 1. Simple Ideas

**Why do some people consider art a waste of money?**

1. **Essential services:** Governments need money for hospitals, schools and roads.
2. **Limited public interest:** Not everyone enjoys paintings, museums or artistic performances.
3. **More urgent problems:** Poor communities may need better healthcare and employment opportunities.

**Why is art important?**

- **Cultural preservation:** Art preserves a country's traditions, history and identity.
- **Tourism:** Museums and public art attract visitors and support local businesses.
- **Mental well-being:** Artistic activities help people relax and reduce stress.

**My opinion:** Art is important, but governments should prioritise essential services when public resources are limited.

| 5. Limited resources | A shortage of available money or materials |
| 6. Preserve traditions | Protect customs and cultural practices |
| 7. Tourist attractions | Places that attract visitors |
| 8. Local businesses | Businesses operating in a particular area |

**Essential services + Limited budgets → Cultural preservation + Tourism + Relaxation**

## 9. Capital Punishment

### Question

Capital punishment remains a controversial issue in many countries. Some people argue that the death penalty is necessary to discourage serious crimes, while others believe that it is cruel and should be abolished. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### 1. Simple Ideas

**Why do some people support the death penalty?**

1. **Crime deterrence:** Fear of execution may discourage people from committing serious crimes.
2. **Justice for victims:** Some believe the most severe crimes deserve the harshest punishment.
3. **Permanent incapacitation:** An executed criminal cannot commit another crime.

**Why should capital punishment be abolished?**

- **Wrongful convictions:** Innocent people may be convicted because of mistakes in investigations or trials.
- **Irreversible consequences:** An execution cannot be undone if new evidence proves innocence.
- **Human dignity:** Opponents believe that the state should protect life rather than deliberately take it.

**My opinion:** The death penalty should be abolished because wrongful execution is irreversible, and life imprisonment can protect society.

### 2. Ten Vocabulary Words and Phrases

| Vocabulary | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| 1. Capital punishment | The death penalty |
| 2. Crime deterrence | Discouraging people from committing crimes |
| 3. Severe offences | Very serious crimes |

| 5. Wrongful conviction | Finding an innocent person guilty |
| 6. Irreversible decision | A decision that cannot be undone |
| 7. Judicial system | The system of courts and justice |
| 8. Human dignity | The value and worth of every person |
| 9. Life imprisonment | Imprisonment for the remainder of a person's life |
| 10. Abolish the death penalty | Officially end the practice of execution |

### 3. Memory Formula

# FINAL IELTS REVISION STRATEGY

## The 3-Step Method

### Step 1: Memorise the ideas, not the essays

Learn two or three main reasons for each viewpoint. Practise explaining each idea in your own words.

### Step 2: Learn five vocabulary items at a time

Write your own sentences using the vocabulary instead of memorising definitions alone.

### Step 3: Practise writing without looking at your notes

Use the memory formula to recall your arguments, then write an introduction, two body paragraphs and a conclusion.

## Recommended Essay Structure

1. **Introduction:** Paraphrase the question and state your opinion.
2. **Body Paragraph 1:** Explain the first viewpoint with reasons and an example.
3. **Body Paragraph 2:** Explain the opposing viewpoint and make your own position clear.
4. **Conclusion:** Summarise both perspectives and restate your opinion.

## Important Reminder

You do not need to memorise all 160 vocabulary items immediately. Start with five words per day and use them in original sentences. Focus on developing clear ideas, accurate grammar and logical paragraph structure rather than using complicated vocabulary unnecessarily.

**Your ultimate goal:** Learn to generate ideas independently and express them accurately under exam conditions. This will help you improve your IELTS Writing Task 2 performance more effectively than memorising complete essays.
