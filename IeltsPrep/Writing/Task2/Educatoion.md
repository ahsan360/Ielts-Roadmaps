## Corrected Answers — Target Band 6.5

# Essay 1: Free University Education

**Question:** Some people believe that university education should be free for all students, while others think that students should pay for their higher education. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Introduction

It is commonly argued that university education should be free for all students, whereas others believe that students should pay for their higher education. I believe that university education should be free, particularly for students from low-income families.

### Body Paragraph 1: Why University Education Should Be Free

On the one hand, people who support free university education believe that it can provide equal opportunities for students from different economic backgrounds, especially those from disadvantaged families. This is because students who have access to higher education can develop valuable skills and obtain a university degree, which may help them secure suitable jobs. As a result, free education can not only improve their quality of life but also contribute to the national economy by creating a more skilled workforce.

# Essay 2: Academic Subjects vs Vocational Training

**Question:** Some people argue that students should focus on academic subjects, while others believe that vocational training is equally important. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Introduction

Some people believe that students should focus on academic subjects, whereas others argue that vocational training is equally important. I believe that both are valuable, but academic qualifications can provide more opportunities, including the chance to work abroad.

### Body Paragraph 1: Why Academic Subjects Are Important

On the one hand, academic subjects are important because they can create a wide range of career opportunities. For example, students with university degrees may find it easier to work abroad or obtain job offers in their relevant fields. Although it can be difficult for fresh graduates to find jobs related to their degrees, academic qualifications still allow them to explore different career paths. As a result, they may have greater flexibility when changing jobs or pursuing new opportunities.

# Essay 3: Online Learning vs Classroom Learning

**Question:** Some people think that online learning is more effective than traditional classroom learning, while others prefer face-to-face instruction. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### Introduction

It is commonly argued that online learning is more effective than traditional classroom learning, whereas others believe that face-to-face instruction is better.

### Body Paragraph 1: Advantages of Online Learning

On the one hand, online learning has several advantages over traditional classroom learning. One of the main reasons is that it offers greater flexibility. For example, students can attend classes from anywhere in the world if they have a suitable device and an internet connection. In addition, lessons can be recorded, allowing students to watch them later. As a result, they can review the content whenever they need to, which helps them understand the lessons more effectively.

## Grammar Rules to Remember

1. **Use complete clauses:**  
   Incorrect: Others believe that should pay.  
   Correct: Others believe that students should pay.

2. **Use articles correctly:**  
   Incorrect: Pursue university degree.  
   Correct: Pursue a university degree.

3. **Check pronoun agreement:**  
   Incorrect: Academic subjects are important because it creates opportunities.  
   Correct: Academic subjects are important because they create opportunities.

4. **Use the correct verb pattern:**  
   Incorrect: It allows them explore different jobs.  
   Correct: It allows them to explore different jobs.

5. **Maintain parallel structures:**  
   Incorrect: Education not only improves students' lives but also they contribute to the economy.  
   Correct: Education not only improves students' lives but also contributes to the economy.

6. **Use commas after introductory phrases:**  
   For example, students can gain practical experience.  
   As a result, they may obtain better jobs.

7. **Develop each body paragraph logically:**  
   Main idea → Explanation → Example → Result.

## My Practice Strategy

- Practise one essay at a time.
- Write the introduction and Body Paragraph 1 independently.
- Check articles, verb forms, pronouns, and sentence structure.
- Use simple, accurate complex sentences rather than unnecessarily complicated ones.
- Aim to develop each main idea clearly before adding another idea.
