## Essay 5: Remote Working

### Minimal Ideas

**Advantages**
- Saves time and money because employees do not commute.
- Provides flexibility and improves work-life balance.

**Disadvantages**
- Employees may feel lonely because they have less face-to-face interaction.
- Household distractions may reduce concentration.

### 10 Useful Vocabulary Items

1. Remote working — working outside a traditional office
2. Commute — travel between home and work
3. Transportation costs — money spent travelling
4. Flexible schedule — a timetable that can be changed
5. Work-life balance — a balance between work and personal life
6. Social isolation — having limited social contact
7. Face-to-face interaction — communicating in person
8. Household distractions — interruptions at home
9. Productivity — the amount of useful work completed
10. Concentration — the ability to focus

# 6 Reusable Sentence Structures for Band 6.5

Learn these structures and use them in different topics.

### 1. Introduction

It is often argued that **[topic]** has both advantages and disadvantages.

### 2. Expressing Your Opinion

I believe that the advantages outweigh the disadvantages because **[main reason]**.

### 3. Introducing an Advantage

One major advantage of **[topic]** is that it can **[benefit]**.

### 4. Explaining Your Idea

This is because **[explanation]**.

### 5. Giving a Result

As a result, **[person or group]** can **[positive result]**.

### 6. Giving an Example

For example, **[a simple, relevant example]**.

### 7. Introducing a Disadvantage

However, one significant disadvantage is that **[problem]**.

### 8. Explaining a Problem

This may happen because **[reason]**.

### 9. Giving a Contrast

Although **[positive point]**, **[negative or contrasting point]**.

### 10. Conclusion

In conclusion, although **[topic]** has some drawbacks, I believe that **[your final opinion]**.

# Example: Remote Working

One major advantage of remote working is that it saves time and money. This is because employees do not need to commute to their offices every day. As a result, they can reduce their transportation costs and spend more time with their families. For example, employees who work from home can use the time saved from travelling to complete personal tasks.

However, one significant disadvantage is that remote working can cause social isolation. This is because employees have fewer opportunities to communicate with their colleagues face to face. As a result, some workers may feel lonely and become less motivated.

## My Study Plan

- Learn the 10 vocabulary items for one essay.
- Practise explaining the two advantages and two disadvantages in your own words.
- Write an introduction and one body paragraph first.
- Check your verbs, singular and plural nouns, articles, and sentence structure.
- Move on to a full essay when you can explain your ideas clearly.

**Important:** Vocabulary alone does not guarantee Band 6.5. You also need clear idea development, logical paragraphing, accurate grammar and relevant examples. Use these words naturally rather than forcing them into every sentence.
