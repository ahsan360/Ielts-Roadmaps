# IELTS Writing Task 2

## Question

> **Water scarcity is a serious problem in many countries. What are the causes of this problem, and what solutions can individuals and governments take?**

## Answer

Water shortages are becoming increasingly common in many countries. This essay will discuss the main causes of this problem and suggest some possible solutions that can be taken by individuals and governments.

One of the main causes is the unnecessary waste of water. Many people use more water than they actually need in their daily lives. For example, people may use a large amount of water while bathing, even though a much smaller amount would be sufficient. In addition, some people waste water by washing their vehicles frequently or leaving taps running unnecessarily. As a result, the amount of available water can decrease, making water scarcity a more serious problem over time.

There are several ways to address this issue. First, individuals should be made more aware of the importance of saving water. Governments and other organisations could run public awareness campaigns to teach people how to use water efficiently and recycle it where possible. In addition, governments should introduce stricter laws to control unnecessary water consumption. For example, they could set reasonable limits on water usage and impose fines on people who waste excessive amounts of water. These measures would encourage people to use water more responsibly and could significantly reduce water scarcity.

In conclusion, water scarcity is mainly caused by the unnecessary use and waste of water. However, this problem can be reduced if individuals use water responsibly and governments raise public awareness and introduce effective laws to control water consumption.
