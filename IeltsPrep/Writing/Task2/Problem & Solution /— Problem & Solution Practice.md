# IELTS Writing Task 2 — Problem & Solution Practice

> **Goal:** Band 6.5–7+
>
> **Approach:** Use the same basic structure for every essay and change only the topic-specific ideas and vocabulary.

---

# Master Essay Structure

## Introduction

**Sentence 1 — Paraphrase the question**

> [Problem] is becoming increasingly common in many countries.

**Sentence 2 — Essay overview**

> This essay will discuss the main causes of this problem and suggest some possible solutions.

---

## Body Paragraph 1 — Causes / Problems

**Sentence 1 — Main cause**

> One of the main causes is [CAUSE 1].

**Sentence 2 — Explanation**

> [Explain why/how this causes the problem.]

**Sentence 3 — Example**

> For example, [EXAMPLE].

**Sentence 4 — Second cause**

> In addition, [CAUSE 2].

**Sentence 5 — Result**

> As a result, [RESULT].

---

## Body Paragraph 2 — Solutions

**Sentence 1 — Topic sentence**

> There are several ways to address this issue.

**Sentence 2 — First solution**

> First, [SOLUTION 1].

**Sentence 3 — Explanation/example**

> For example, [EXAMPLE].

**Sentence 4 — Second solution**

> In addition, [SOLUTION 2].

**Sentence 5 — Result**

> This would [RESULT 1] and could [RESULT 2].

---

## Conclusion

**Sentence 1 — Summary**

> In conclusion, [PROBLEM] is mainly caused by [CAUSE 1] and [CAUSE 2].

**Sentence 2 — Final solution**

> However, this problem can be reduced if [SOLUTION 1] and [SOLUTION 2].

---

# Essential Sentence Structures

## 1. Subject + Verb + Object

> Technology affects family relationships.

**Structure:**

`Subject + Verb + Object`

---

## 2. Subject + Be + Adjective

> Obesity is a serious problem.

**Structure:**

`Subject + be + adjective`

---

## 3. Subject + Verb + Adverb

> People increasingly use technology.

**Structure:**

`Subject + Verb + Adverb`

---

## 4. Because Clause

> Because technology is widely available, children spend more time indoors.

**Structure:**

`Because + Subject + Verb, Subject + Verb + Object`

---

## 5. Because of

> Because of modern technology, many people spend less time with their families.

**Structure:**

`Because of + noun, Subject + Verb + Object`

---

## 6. Result

> People spend more time online. As a result, they have less time for their families.

**Structure:**

`Cause. As a result, + consequence.`

---

## 7. Conditional Sentence

> If governments improve public transport, people may use fewer private cars.

**Structure:**

`If + Subject + Verb, Subject + may/can/will + Verb`

---

## 8. Solution

> Governments should introduce stricter laws.

**Structure:**

`Subject + should + Verb + Object`

---

## 9. Passive Voice

> Stricter laws should be introduced.

**Structure:**

`Subject + should be + past participle`

---

## 10. Relative Clause

> People who use social media excessively may feel isolated.

**Structure:**

`Noun + who/which/that + Verb`

---

## 11. Although

> Although technology has many benefits, it can damage family relationships.

**Structure:**

`Although + Subject + Verb, Subject + Verb`

---

## 12. This Would / Could

> This would reduce pollution and could improve public health.

**Structure:**

`This would + Verb + Object + and could + Verb + Object`

# Essay 1 — Art: Fewer People Visiting Galleries

## Question

> In many countries, fewer people are visiting art galleries and exhibitions. What do you think are the main causes of this problem, and what measures can be taken to encourage more people to engage with art?

## Answer

Art galleries and exhibitions are becoming less popular in many countries. Although art has an important role in culture and education, fewer people are interested in visiting these places nowadays. This essay will discuss the main causes of this problem and suggest some possible solutions.

One of the main causes is the rapid growth of digital entertainment. People can now watch films, videos and other forms of entertainment from their phones without leaving their homes. For example, young people may spend several hours using social media or watching online content instead of visiting an art gallery. Another reason is that some galleries may not be attractive or accessible to ordinary people. If exhibitions are expensive or difficult to reach, many people may simply choose other activities. As a result, fewer people develop an interest in traditional forms of art.

There are several ways to address this issue. First, galleries should make exhibitions more interesting and affordable. For example, they could offer free entry to students and organise interactive exhibitions where visitors can participate rather than simply look at paintings. In addition, galleries should use social media to promote their exhibitions. Short videos and online advertisements could attract younger people and encourage them to visit. Governments could also provide financial support to make galleries more accessible. These measures would make art more appealing and could increase the number of visitors.

In conclusion, the decline in gallery visits is mainly caused by digital entertainment and limited access to exhibitions. However, this problem can be reduced if galleries become more affordable, interactive and attractive, particularly to younger people.

# Essay 2 — Art: Traditional Art Disappearing

## Question

> Many traditional art forms are in danger of disappearing due to modern technology and changing tastes. What are the main problems caused by this decline, and what can be done to solve them?

## Answer

Many traditional art forms are becoming less popular because of modern technology and changing tastes. This is a serious concern because traditional art is an important part of a country's culture and history. This essay will discuss the main problems caused by this decline and suggest some possible solutions.

One of the main problems is the loss of cultural heritage. Traditional paintings, music, crafts and other art forms often show the history and identity of a community. If young people stop learning these skills, valuable cultural knowledge may disappear. For example, a traditional craft may no longer exist if there are no young people willing to learn it from older artists. Another problem is that traditional artists may lose their income because fewer people want to buy their work. As a result, they may stop practising their art, which can make the problem even worse.

There are several ways to address this issue. First, governments should provide financial support to traditional artists. For example, they could give grants to artists and organise cultural festivals where traditional art can be displayed. In addition, schools should teach students about traditional art and culture. Young people could learn traditional skills through special classes or workshops. This would increase their interest in cultural activities and could help these skills pass from one generation to another.

In conclusion, the decline of traditional art can lead to cultural loss and financial difficulties for artists. However, governments and schools can help preserve these art forms by providing financial support and educating younger generations about their cultural importance.

# Essay 3 — Communication: Online Communication and Isolation

## Question

> Despite the ease of communicating online, many people feel more isolated than ever before. What are the reasons for this problem, and what can be done to address it?

## Answer

Although online communication has made it easier for people to contact each other, many individuals feel increasingly isolated. This is because digital communication cannot always replace real-life interaction. This essay will discuss the main causes of this problem and suggest some possible solutions.

One of the main causes is the excessive use of social media. People often communicate through messages and comments instead of having face-to-face conversations. For example, a person may have hundreds of online friends but rarely meet anyone in person. Another reason is that online communication can be less meaningful than direct interaction. People cannot always understand another person's emotions through a short message or online comment. As a result, they may feel lonely even though they are constantly connected to the internet.

There are several ways to address this issue. First, individuals should reduce unnecessary screen time and spend more time with their friends and family. For example, people could arrange regular meetings instead of communicating only through social media. In addition, schools and communities should organise more group activities. Sports, cultural events and community programmes can give people opportunities to interact directly. This would improve social relationships and could reduce feelings of loneliness.

In conclusion, excessive social media use and the lack of meaningful face-to-face communication are major causes of isolation. However, this problem can be reduced if people limit their screen time and participate in more real-life social activities.

# Essay 4 — Crime: Youth Crime

## Question

> Young people today are more likely to commit crimes than in the past. Why do you think this is happening, and what measures can be taken to reduce youth crime?

## Answer

Youth crime is a serious problem in many societies. There are several reasons why some young people become involved in criminal activities, including negative peer pressure and a lack of parental supervision. This essay will discuss these causes and suggest some possible solutions.

One of the main causes is peer pressure. Young people often want to be accepted by their friends and may copy their behaviour, even when it is harmful. For example, a teenager may steal something because his friends encourage him to do so. Another important cause is a lack of parental supervision. When parents are too busy to monitor their children's behaviour, young people may spend more time with negative groups. As a result, they may become involved in activities such as stealing, fighting or other crimes.

There are several ways to address this issue. First, parents should spend more time with their children and guide them properly. They should know who their children's friends are and explain the consequences of criminal behaviour. In addition, governments should provide young people with more educational, sporting and training opportunities. For example, community centres could organise sports programmes and vocational courses for teenagers. This would keep young people engaged in useful activities and could reduce their involvement in crime.

In conclusion, peer pressure and poor parental supervision are important causes of youth crime. However, this problem can be reduced if parents provide proper guidance and governments give young people more opportunities to participate in positive activities.

# Essay 5 — Education: Academic Pressure

## Question

> Children are under increasing pressure to perform well in academics. What are the causes, and how can this problem be addressed?

## Answer

Academic pressure among children is becoming increasingly common in many countries. While good academic performance is important, excessive pressure can negatively affect children's well-being and interest in learning. This essay will discuss the main causes of this problem and suggest some possible solutions.

One of the main causes is the high expectations of parents. Many parents believe that excellent academic results are necessary for their children to obtain good jobs in the future. For example, some parents may force their children to study for several hours every day and expect them to achieve the highest marks. Another cause is strong competition among students. Because university places and good jobs can be limited, children may feel that they must perform better than their classmates. As a result, they may experience stress and lose interest in education.

There are several ways to address this issue. First, parents should set realistic expectations and give their children enough time to rest. They should focus on effort and improvement rather than only on examination results. In addition, schools should provide counselling services and create a healthier learning environment. For example, teachers can help students manage their study time and deal with academic stress. This would improve students' well-being and could help them develop a more positive attitude towards learning.

In conclusion, high parental expectations and strong competition are the main causes of academic pressure. However, this problem can be reduced if parents provide balanced support and schools offer students appropriate academic and emotional guidance.

# Essay 6 — Education: Rising University Costs

## Question

> In many countries, the cost of university education is rising significantly. What are the main reasons for this, and what solutions can be implemented to make education more affordable?

## Answer

The cost of university education is increasing significantly in many countries. This can create serious financial difficulties for students and their families. This essay will discuss the main reasons for this problem and suggest some possible solutions.

One of the main causes is the increasing cost of providing high-quality education. Universities need money to pay teachers, maintain buildings and provide modern equipment. For example, universities may need expensive computers and laboratory equipment for students. Another reason is the growing demand for higher education. As more students want university degrees, some institutions may increase their fees to cover their costs. As a result, many students from low-income families may struggle to continue their education.

There are several ways to address this issue. First, governments should provide more financial support to universities and students. For example, they could offer scholarships and low-interest student loans to students from poorer families. In addition, universities should reduce unnecessary expenses and provide affordable online courses. Online education can reduce the cost of buildings and other facilities. This would lower the financial burden on students and could make higher education available to a larger number of people.

In conclusion, rising university costs are mainly caused by the increasing cost of providing education and growing demand for university degrees. However, governments and universities can make education more affordable through financial support, scholarships and better cost management.

# Essay 7 — Environment: Endangered Animals

## Question

> With deforestation, urban development and illegal hunting, many animal species are becoming endangered and some are even facing extinction. Do you think it is important to protect animals? What can be done to deal with this problem?

## Answer

Many animal species are becoming endangered because of human activities such as deforestation, urban development and illegal hunting. I believe that protecting animals is extremely important because they are an essential part of the natural environment. This essay will discuss the importance of protecting animals and suggest some possible solutions.

One important reason to protect animals is that they help maintain the natural balance. Every species has a role in the food chain, so the disappearance of one species can affect other animals and plants. For example, if a particular animal disappears, the population of other species may increase or decrease significantly. Another reason is that future generations should have the opportunity to see and learn about different forms of wildlife. If animals become extinct, they cannot be brought back. As a result, protecting wildlife is important for both the environment and future generations.

There are several ways to address this issue. First, governments should protect forests and create more national parks. These areas can provide safe habitats for endangered animals. In addition, stricter laws should be introduced to prevent illegal hunting. For example, people who hunt endangered animals should receive heavy fines or other legal punishment. Governments should also educate the public about the importance of wildlife. This would reduce harmful human activities and could help protect endangered species.

In conclusion, protecting animals is essential for maintaining environmental balance and preserving wildlife for future generations. Governments should protect natural habitats, enforce strict laws and increase public awareness.

# Essay 8 — Environment: Lack of Individual Action

## Question

> Many people think it is very important to protect the environment but they make no effort to do it themselves. Why do you think this is the case? What actions should individuals take to protect the environment?

## Answer

Many people understand that environmental protection is important, but they do not make much effort to change their own behaviour. This is a serious problem because individual actions can have a significant effect when millions of people behave in the same way. This essay will discuss the main reasons for this behaviour and suggest some actions that individuals can take.

One of the main reasons is that people often choose convenience over environmental protection. For example, they may use plastic bags because they are cheap and easy to carry. Another reason is that some people believe that their individual actions will have little effect on the environment. They may think that governments and large companies are mainly responsible for solving environmental problems. As a result, they continue to waste water, use private cars and produce unnecessary waste.

There are several ways individuals can address this issue. First, people should reduce their use of plastic and unnecessary energy. For example, they can use reusable bags, save electricity and avoid wasting water. In addition, individuals should use public transportation, cycle or walk whenever possible. For example, people can walk when travelling short distances instead of using a car. This would reduce pollution and could help protect natural resources.

In conclusion, convenience and a lack of personal responsibility are major reasons why people fail to protect the environment. However, individuals can make a meaningful contribution by reducing waste, saving energy and choosing environmentally friendly forms of transport.

# Essay 9 — Environment: Climate Change

## Question

> Climate change is one of the biggest threats facing the world today. What are the causes of climate change, and what measures can individuals and governments take to prevent it?

## Answer

Climate change is one of the most serious environmental problems facing the world today. It can affect weather, agriculture, wildlife and human life. This essay will discuss the main causes of climate change and suggest some measures that individuals and governments can take to address it.

One of the main causes is the burning of fossil fuels. Cars, factories and power plants burn coal, oil and gas, which release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere. For example, large numbers of petrol and diesel vehicles produce significant amounts of carbon emissions. Another major cause is deforestation. Trees absorb carbon dioxide, so cutting down large areas of forest can increase the amount of this gas in the atmosphere. As a result, global temperatures can rise and the climate can change.

There are several ways to address this issue. First, governments should invest in renewable energy such as solar and wind power. They should also protect forests and introduce strict laws against illegal deforestation. In addition, individuals should reduce their use of private cars and unnecessary electricity. For example, people can use public transport and walk for short journeys. This would reduce greenhouse gas emissions and could slow the effects of climate change.

In conclusion, climate change is mainly caused by fossil fuel use and deforestation. However, governments and individuals can reduce this problem by using renewable energy, protecting forests and adopting more environmentally friendly lifestyles.

## Answer

Technology has become an essential part of modern family life, but its excessive use can negatively affect family relationships. This problem is mainly caused by excessive screen time and the growing popularity of online entertainment. This essay will discuss these causes and suggest some possible solutions.

# Essay 13 — Food: Food Wastage

## Question

> In many wealthy nations, large amounts of food are wasted every day. Why is this happening, and how can food wastage be minimized?

## Answer

Large amounts of food are wasted every day in many wealthy countries. This is a serious problem because wasted food also means wasted money, energy and natural resources. This essay will discuss the main causes of food waste and suggest some possible solutions.

One of the main causes is that people buy more food than they need. Because food is relatively affordable in wealthy countries, consumers may purchase large quantities without planning their meals carefully. For example, families may buy too many vegetables and other fresh products and throw them away when they expire. Another cause is waste by restaurants and supermarkets. These businesses may throw away food that is not sold quickly or does not look attractive enough for customers. As a result, large amounts of perfectly usable food may end up in rubbish bins.

There are several ways to address this issue. First, individuals should plan their shopping carefully and buy only the amount of food they need. For example, families can make a shopping list before going to the supermarket. In addition, supermarkets and restaurants should donate suitable unused food to charities. Governments could also encourage businesses to reduce food waste through regulations and public campaigns. This would reduce unnecessary waste and could help people who do not have enough food.

In conclusion, excessive purchasing by consumers and waste by businesses are major causes of food wastage. However, this problem can be reduced through careful shopping, better food management, donations and stronger public awareness.

# Essay 14 — Food: Food Advertising and Children

> Food advertisements, especially those aimed at children, have been linked to poor eating habits. What are the effects of food marketing on children's diets, and how should it be regulated?

## Answer

Food advertising has a significant influence on children's eating habits. Many advertisements encourage children to consume sugary drinks, sweets and fast food, which can lead to unhealthy diets. This essay will discuss the effects of food marketing on children and suggest some ways to regulate it.

One of the main effects is that children may develop a strong preference for unhealthy food. Advertisements often make these products look attractive by using bright colours, popular characters and interesting offers. Another effect is that frequent advertising can make unhealthy products seem like a normal part of everyday life. This would help parents make better choices and could reduce children's consumption of unhealthy food.

In conclusion, food advertising can encourage children to develop poor eating habits and consume unhealthy products. However, governments can reduce this influence by restricting advertisements and requiring companies to provide clear nutritional information.

# Essay 15 — Global Issues: Local Businesses

## Question

> With the rise of global brands and international trade, many local businesses are being driven out of the market. What can governments do to protect local businesses from this global competition?

## Answer

The growth of global brands and international trade has created strong competition for local businesses. Although international companies can provide consumers with more choices, small local businesses may struggle to survive. This essay will discuss the main problems caused by global competition and suggest some measures governments can take to support local businesses.

One of the main problems is that large international companies often have more financial resources than small local businesses. They can produce goods in large quantities and sell them at lower prices. For example, a small local shop may find it difficult to compete with a large international supermarket. Another problem is that global brands often have powerful advertising campaigns. They can spend large amounts of money on television, social media and online advertising. As a result, local companies may lose customers and eventually close.

There are several ways governments can address this issue. First, they should provide financial support to small local businesses. For example, governments could offer low-interest loans, tax reductions and training programmes to help local companies develop. In addition, governments could promote local products through national campaigns and local markets. For example, special events could encourage consumers to buy products made by local businesses. This would help small companies attract customers and could protect local employment.

In conclusion, local businesses often struggle because global companies have greater financial and marketing power. However, governments can protect local businesses by providing financial support, reducing their tax burden and promoting locally produced goods.

- [ ] Did I answer every part of the question?
- [ ] Did I write at least 250 words?
- [ ] Did I write a clear introduction?
- [ ] Did I use two main causes/problems?
- [ ] Did I explain each main idea?
- [ ] Did I give relevant examples?
- [ ] Did I provide clear solutions?
- [ ] Did I explain how the solutions work?
- [ ] Did I write a clear conclusion?
- [ ] Did I check subject–verb agreement?
- [ ] Did I check singular and plural nouns?
- [ ] Did I check articles: `a / an / the`?
- [ ] Did I check verb forms?
- [ ] Did I check prepositions?
- [ ] Did I avoid unnecessarily complicated vocabulary?
- [ ] Did I use a mixture of simple and complex sentences?

# Golden Rule

> **Do not memorise 15 essays word-for-word.**

Instead, memorise:

1. The **4-paragraph structure**
2. The **12 sentence structures**
3. The **cause vocabulary**
4. The **solution vocabulary**
5. Two causes and two solutions for each common topic

Then, when you see a new IELTS question, you can build the essay using the same framework.

**Target:**

> Clear ideas + accurate grammar + relevant examples + controlled sentence variety = **Band 6.5–7+**
