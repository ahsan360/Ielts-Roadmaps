# Essay 27 — Entertainment

### Question
Children spend a lot of time watching television and playing video games. Why is this happening? Is this a positive or negative development?

## 1. Simple Ideas

### Why is this happening?
1. **Easy access:** Most children have televisions, smartphones and gaming devices at home.
2. **Enjoyment:** Video games contain colourful graphics, exciting stories and rewards.
3. **Busy parents:** Some parents allow their children to use screens to keep them occupied.

### Is it positive or negative?

**Positive effects**
- Educational programmes teach children about science, history and different cultures.
- Some video games improve concentration and problem-solving skills.

**Negative effects**
- Excessive screen time can lead to physical inactivity and health problems.
- Children may have less time for studying, reading and outdoor activities.
- They may spend less time communicating with family members.

**My opinion:** This is mainly a negative development because excessive screen time can harm children's health and development.

## 2. Ten Vocabulary Words and Phrases

| Vocabulary | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| 1. Screen time | Time spent using electronic screens |
| 2. Easily accessible | Easy to obtain or use |
| 3. Engaging | Interesting and enjoyable |
| 4. Educational programmes | Shows that help people learn |
| 5. Problem-solving skills | Ability to solve difficulties |
| 6. Excessive use | Using something too much |
| 7. Physically inactive | Not doing enough physical activity |
| 8. Health problems | Issues affecting health |
| 9. Outdoor activities | Activities performed outside |
| 10. Keep children occupied | Keep children busy |

## 3. Memory Formula

**Access + Enjoyment + Busy parents → Learning benefits + Health risks**
