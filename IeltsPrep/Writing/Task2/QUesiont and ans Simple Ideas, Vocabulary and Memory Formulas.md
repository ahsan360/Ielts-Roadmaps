# Essay 27 — Entertainment

### Question
Children spend a lot of time watching television and playing video games. Why is this happening? Is this a positive or negative development?

## 1. Simple Ideas

### Why is this happening?
1. **Easy access:** Most children have televisions, smartphones and gaming devices at home.
2. **Enjoyment:** Video games contain colourful graphics, exciting stories and rewards.
3. **Busy parents:** Some parents allow their children to use screens to keep them occupied.

### Is it positive or negative?

**Positive effects**
- Educational programmes teach children about science, history and different cultures.
- Some video games improve concentration and problem-solving skills.

**Negative effects**
- Excessive screen time can lead to physical inactivity and health problems.
- Children may have less time for studying, reading and outdoor activities.
- They may spend less time communicating with family members.

**My opinion:** This is mainly a negative development because excessive screen time can harm children's health and development.

## 2. Ten Vocabulary Words and Phrases

| Vocabulary | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| 1. Screen time | Time spent using electronic screens |
| 2. Easily accessible | Easy to obtain or use |
| 3. Engaging | Interesting and enjoyable |
| 4. Educational programmes | Shows that help people learn |
| 5. Problem-solving skills | Ability to solve difficulties |
| 6. Excessive use | Using something too much |
| 7. Physically inactive | Not doing enough physical activity |
| 8. Health problems | Issues affecting health |
| 9. Outdoor activities | Activities performed outside |
| 10. Keep children occupied | Keep children busy |

## 3. Memory Formula

**Access + Enjoyment + Busy parents → Learning benefits + Health risks**

# Essay 40 — Family: Later Marriage

### Question
In many countries, people are getting married at a later age than in the past. Why is this happening? What effects does this have on society?

## 1. Simple Ideas

### Why do people marry later?
1. **Education:** Young people spend more years studying at university.
2. **Career:** They want to establish a stable career before starting a family.
3. **Money:** They want to save money and become financially independent.
4. **Personal readiness:** They want to be emotionally prepared for marriage.

### What effects does this have on society?

**Positive effects**
- People may become more emotionally mature and financially stable.
- They may make more thoughtful decisions when choosing a partner.
- They may be better prepared to raise children.

**Negative effects**
- People may have fewer children, contributing to an ageing population.
- Some people may experience loneliness if they remain unmarried for a long time.

**My opinion:** Later marriage can improve personal maturity and financial stability, but it may also contribute to lower birth rates.

## 2. Ten Vocabulary Words and Phrases

| Vocabulary | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| 1. Get married later | Marry at an older age |
| 2. Career development | Progress in one's career |
| 3. Financial stability | Having a secure financial situation |
| 4. Financially independent | Able to support oneself |
| 5. Emotionally mature | Able to manage emotions responsibly |
| 6. Choose a partner | Decide whom to marry |
| 7. Raise children | Look after children as they grow |
| 8. Birth rate | Number of births in a population |
| 9. Ageing population | A population with more older people |
| 10. Experience loneliness | Feel alone or isolated |

## 3. Memory Formula

**Education + Career + Money → Maturity + Fewer births**

# Essay 43 — Family: Living Alone

### Question
Some people prefer to live alone in modern cities. Why do you think this is the case? What impact does this have on society?

## 1. Simple Ideas

### Why do people live alone?
1. **Financial independence:** Stable jobs allow people to pay their own expenses.
2. **Freedom:** They can make their own decisions without consulting others.
3. **Privacy:** They can enjoy their personal space.
4. **Urbanisation:** People move to cities for education and employment opportunities.

### What impact does this have on society?

**Positive effects**
- People learn to manage their finances, cooking and cleaning independently.
- They have more freedom to pursue their careers and personal interests.

**Negative effects**
- Living alone can lead to loneliness, particularly when social interaction is limited.
- Family relationships may become weaker.
- More separate households can increase housing and living costs.

**My opinion:** Living alone promotes independence, but it can also increase loneliness and weaken family connections.

# Essay 46 — Food and Farming: Increased Food Production

### Question
Food production has increased dramatically due to modern technology. What are the reasons for this increase? What problems can it cause?

## 1. Simple Ideas

### Why has food production increased?
1. **Modern machinery:** Tractors and harvesters allow farmers to work faster.
2. **Improved seeds:** Better crop varieties produce higher yields.
3. **Fertilisers and pesticides:** Fertilisers support plant growth, while pesticides protect crops from pests.
4. **Modern technology:** Irrigation systems and digital tools help farmers reduce crop losses.

### What problems can it cause?
1. **Soil pollution:** Excessive chemical use can damage soil quality.
2. **Water pollution:** Chemicals can enter rivers and groundwater.
3. **Wildlife damage:** Pesticides can harm insects, birds and other animals.
4. **Health risks:** Incorrect use of agricultural chemicals may contaminate food.

**My opinion:** Modern technology is beneficial, but it should be used responsibly to protect the environment and human health.

## 2. Ten Vocabulary Words and Phrases

| Vocabulary | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| 1. Food production | The process of producing food |
| 2. Agricultural machinery | Machines used in farming |
| 3. Improved seeds | Seeds that produce better crops |
| 4. Crop yield | Amount of crops produced |
| 5. Irrigation systems | Systems that supply water to crops |
| 6. Chemical fertilisers | Chemicals that help plants grow |
| 7. Pesticides | Substances used to control pests |
| 8. Soil quality | The condition of the soil |
| 9. Water pollution | Contamination of water |
| 10. Sustainable farming | Farming that protects resources for the future |

## 3. Memory Formula

**Machinery + Seeds + Chemicals → More food + Pollution**

# Essay 48 — Food and Farming: Vegetarian and Vegan Diets

### Question
Many people choose to follow a vegetarian or vegan diet. Why do people choose this lifestyle? What are the benefits for personal health and the environment?

## 1. Simple Ideas

### Why do people choose these diets?
1. **Animal welfare:** They do not want animals to suffer or be killed for food.
2. **Health:** They want to eat more nutritious plant-based foods.
3. **Environmental concerns:** They want to reduce the environmental impact of food production.

### What are the health benefits?
- Fruits, vegetables and whole grains provide fibre, vitamins and minerals.
- A balanced plant-based diet can help people maintain a healthy weight.
- Eating more vegetables and whole grains may benefit heart health.
- Vegan diets must be planned carefully to provide sufficient vitamin B12, iron and protein.

### What are the environmental benefits?
- Producing some plant-based foods requires fewer natural resources than producing meat.
- Reducing meat consumption can lower certain environmental impacts.
- Greater reliance on plant foods may reduce pressure on land and water resources.

**My opinion:** A well-planned plant-based diet can benefit both personal health and the environment.

## 2. Ten Vocabulary Words and Phrases

| Vocabulary | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| 1. Vegetarian diet | A diet that excludes meat and fish |
| 2. Vegan diet | A diet that excludes animal-derived foods |
| 3. Animal welfare | The well-being of animals |
| 4. Plant-based foods | Foods mainly derived from plants |
| 5. Balanced diet | A diet containing necessary nutrients |
| 6. Nutritional benefits | Positive effects on health from nutrients |
| 7. Maintain a healthy weight | Keep body weight within a healthy range |
| 8. Heart health | The condition of the heart |
| 9. Natural resources | Water, land and other resources from nature |
| 10. Environmental impact | The effect of an activity on the environment |

## 3. Memory Formula

**Animal welfare + Health + Environment → Nutritional benefits + Resource conservation**

# Four-Day Memorisation Plan

## Day 1: Understand the Ideas
- Read all five essay questions.
- Memorise the three or four main ideas for each question.
- Explain each idea aloud in your own words.

## Day 2: Learn the Vocabulary
- Learn five vocabulary items from each essay.
- Make one simple sentence using each item.
- Review the meanings before learning the remaining five.

## Day 3: Practise Paragraphs
- Write one introduction for each essay.
- Write one body paragraph without looking at your notes.
- Check grammar, sentence structure and idea development.

## Day 4: Practise Full Essays
- Write one complete essay in 40 minutes.
- Aim for approximately 250–290 words.
- Check whether you have answered every part of the question.
- Correct grammatical errors and remove irrelevant ideas.

# Useful Sentence Structures for Band 6.5–7

### 1. Giving a reason
One major reason why **[topic]** is becoming increasingly common is that **[reason]**.

*Example:* One major reason why children spend more time playing video games is that these activities are easily accessible.

### 2. Explaining a result
As a result, **[consequence]**.

*Example:* As a result, children may have less time for outdoor activities.

### 3. Presenting a positive effect
One significant benefit of **[topic]** is that **[benefit]**.

*Example:* One significant benefit of living alone is that people learn to manage their finances independently.

### 4. Presenting a negative effect
However, excessive **[activity]** can lead to **[problem]**.

*Example:* However, excessive screen time can lead to physical inactivity.

### 5. Giving a balanced opinion
Although **[positive point]**, I believe that **[your main opinion]** because **[reason]**.

*Example:* Although video games can improve problem-solving skills, I believe that excessive screen time is harmful because it reduces physical activity.

### 6. Writing a conclusion
In conclusion, **[main reason or summary]**. Although **[opposing point]**, I believe that **[your opinion]**.

# Final Advice

Do not memorise all five essays word for word. Instead, remember the main ideas, learn the vocabulary and practise explaining each point in your own words.

For a Band 6.5–7 response, prioritise:

1. **Task Response:** Answer every part of the question.
2. **Coherence and Cohesion:** Organise ideas into clear paragraphs.
3. **Lexical Resource:** Use appropriate vocabulary accurately.
4. **Grammatical Range and Accuracy:** Combine simple and complex sentences while maintaining grammatical accuracy.

**The key principle:** Clear ideas + relevant explanations + accurate grammar + appropriate vocabulary = a stronger IELTS Writing Task 2 response.
