# IELTS Writing Task 2 — Topic-Based Revision Guide[cite: 1]

## How to use this guide[cite: 1]

Do not memorise entire essays. Memorise adaptable **ideas, examples, and results**. In the exam, build a body paragraph using:[cite: 1]

**Main idea → Explanation → Example → Result**[cite: 1]

**Example**[cite: 1]
- **Idea:** Online learning saves time.[cite: 1]
- **Explanation:** Students do not need to travel to educational institutions.[cite: 1]
- **Example:** A student in a rural area can attend an online course from home.[cite: 1]
- **Result:** Education becomes more accessible and convenient.[cite: 1]

# 12. Quick Essay-Type Strategy[cite: 1]

Use the same topic ideas for different question types, but answer every part of the question.[cite: 1]

## A. Discussion + Opinion[cite: 1]

**Structure**[cite: 1]
1. Introduction: Paraphrase the question and state your opinion.[cite: 1]
2. Body paragraph 1: Explain the first viewpoint.[cite: 1]
3. Body paragraph 2: Explain the second viewpoint and your preferred position.[cite: 1]
4. Conclusion: Summarise both views and reaffirm your opinion.[cite: 1]

**Useful phrases**[cite: 1]
- On the one hand, ...[cite: 1]
- On the other hand, ...[cite: 1]
- Although both arguments have merit, I believe that ...[cite: 1]
- In conclusion, while ..., I would argue that ...[cite: 1]

## B. Agree or Disagree[cite: 1]

**Structure**[cite: 1]
1. Introduction: Paraphrase the statement and give a clear position.[cite: 1]
2. Body paragraph 1: Explain your first reason.[cite: 1]
3. Body paragraph 2: Explain your second reason.[cite: 1]
4. Conclusion: Restate your position.[cite: 1]

**Useful phrases**[cite: 1]
- I largely agree with this view because ...[cite: 1]
- One compelling reason is that ...[cite: 1]
- Furthermore, ...[cite: 1]
- For these reasons, I believe that ...[cite: 1]

## C. Causes and Solutions[cite: 1]

**Structure**[cite: 1]
1. Introduction: Introduce the problem.[cite: 1]
2. Body paragraph 1: Explain the main causes.[cite: 1]
3. Body paragraph 2: Propose practical solutions.[cite: 1]
4. Conclusion: Summarise the causes and measures.[cite: 1]

**Useful phrases**[cite: 1]
- One major contributing factor is ...[cite: 1]
- This problem is partly attributable to ...[cite: 1]
- To address this issue, governments should ...[cite: 1]
- These measures could help to ...[cite: 1]

## D. Advantages and Disadvantages[cite: 1]

**Structure**[cite: 1]
1. Introduction: Paraphrase the development.[cite: 1]
2. Body paragraph 1: Explain the advantages.[cite: 1]
3. Body paragraph 2: Explain the disadvantages.[cite: 1]
4. Conclusion: Give a balanced judgement if the question asks whether one side outweighs the other.[cite: 1]

**Useful phrases**[cite: 1]
- One significant advantage is that ...[cite: 1]
- Another benefit is ...[cite: 1]
- Nevertheless, this development also has drawbacks.[cite: 1]
- On balance, I believe the advantages outweigh the disadvantages because ...[cite: 1]

## E. Two-Part / Mixed Questions[cite: 1]

**Structure**[cite: 1]
1. Introduction: Briefly introduce the issue and, if appropriate, answer both questions.[cite: 1]
2. Body paragraph 1: Answer the first question.[cite: 1]
3. Body paragraph 2: Answer the second question.[cite: 1]
4. Conclusion: Summarise both answers.[cite: 1]

**Useful phrases**[cite: 1]
- There are several reasons why this occurs.[cite: 1]
- One important explanation is that ...[cite: 1]
- Regarding its effects, ...[cite: 1]
- Overall, this trend can be attributed to ..., and its main consequence is ...[cite: 1]

# Final Revision Checklist[cite: 1]

Before the exam, practise recalling two strong ideas for each topic rather than memorising every row.[cite: 1]

- [ ] State a clear position when the question asks for an opinion.[cite: 1]
- [ ] Answer every part of the question.[cite: 1]
- [ ] Explain each idea instead of merely listing it.[cite: 1]
- [ ] Include a relevant, realistic example.[cite: 1]
- [ ] Show the consequence of the example or argument.[cite: 1]
- [ ] Use linking words naturally rather than excessively.[cite: 1]
- [ ] Check articles, subject–verb agreement, verb tense, and sentence structure.[cite: 1]
- [ ] Aim for at least 250 words in Writing Task 2.[cite: 1]

**Remember:** Clear ideas, logical development, relevant examples, and accurate grammar are more valuable than unnecessarily complicated vocabulary.[cite: 1]
