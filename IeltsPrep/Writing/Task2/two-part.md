# IELTS Writing Task 2 — Band 6.5–7 Sample Essays

## Essay 27 — Entertainment

### Question

Children spend a lot of time watching television and playing video games. Why is this happening? Is this a positive or negative development?

### Sample Answer

Nowadays, many children spend a significant amount of their free time watching television and playing video games. This trend is becoming increasingly common because digital entertainment is easily available and attractive to young people. In my opinion, although these activities can have some benefits, spending too much time on them is mainly a negative development.

There are several reasons why children are increasingly interested in television and video games. First, modern technology has made entertainment much more accessible. Most families have televisions, smartphones, computers or gaming devices at home, so children can use them whenever they want. Second, video games are designed to be highly engaging. They often contain colourful graphics, interesting stories and rewards, which encourage children to continue playing. In addition, some parents are busy with work and household responsibilities. As a result, they may allow their children to watch television or play games because these activities keep them occupied.

There are some positive aspects to this development. Certain television programmes can be educational and help children learn about science, history and different cultures. Similarly, some video games can improve problem-solving skills, concentration and hand-eye coordination. Furthermore, playing games with friends can help children develop communication and teamwork skills.

However, excessive use of electronic entertainment can have serious disadvantages. Children who spend many hours in front of screens may become physically inactive, which can contribute to obesity and other health problems. They may also have less time for studying, reading, playing outdoors and communicating with family members. In some cases, excessive gaming can become addictive and negatively affect academic performance.

In conclusion, children spend more time watching television and playing video games mainly because these forms of entertainment are easily accessible and highly engaging. Although they can provide educational and social benefits, I believe that excessive screen time is largely a negative development.

## Essay 40 — Family

### Question

In many countries, people are getting married at a later age than in the past. Why is this happening? What effects does this have on society?

### Sample Answer

In many parts of the world, people are choosing to get married later than previous generations. This change is mainly caused by longer periods of education, career ambitions and changing attitudes towards marriage. Although later marriage can have some positive effects, it may also create certain social problems.

One important reason for this trend is the increasing importance of education and career development. Many young people now spend several years at university before entering the workforce. After completing their education, they often want to establish a stable career before starting a family. In addition, the cost of living has increased considerably in many countries. Therefore, young adults may want to save enough money to buy a house, secure a good job and become financially independent before getting married.

Another reason is that attitudes towards marriage have changed. In the past, marriage was often considered an essential part of adult life, and people were expected to marry at a relatively young age. Today, however, many individuals believe that marriage should happen when they are emotionally and financially prepared. People also have greater freedom to live independently and develop their personal interests before settling down.

This development can have several positive effects on society. People who marry later may be more financially stable and emotionally mature. They may also make more thoughtful decisions about choosing a partner. As a result, some couples may have stronger relationships and be better prepared to raise children.

Nevertheless, later marriage can also have negative consequences. If people delay starting families for too long, they may have fewer children, which can contribute to an ageing population and a smaller workforce in the future. Furthermore, some older people may experience loneliness if they remain unmarried for a long period.

In conclusion, people are marrying later mainly because they want to pursue education, establish careers and achieve financial stability. While this trend can lead to greater personal and financial maturity, it may also contribute to lower birth rates and an ageing society.

## Essay 43 — Family

### Question

Some people prefer to live alone in modern cities. Why do you think this is the case? What impact does this have on society?

### Sample Answer

In many modern cities, an increasing number of people prefer to live alone rather than with their families or other people. This is mainly because people have become more independent and have greater financial freedom. Although living alone can provide several personal benefits, it can also have some negative effects on society.

There are several reasons why people choose to live alone. First, modern employment opportunities have made it easier for individuals to become financially independent. Many young adults have stable jobs and can afford to rent or buy their own homes. Therefore, they do not necessarily need to live with their parents. Second, people today often value privacy and personal freedom. Living alone allows them to decide how to spend their time without having to consider the preferences of other family members.

Another important factor is urbanisation. People often move from their hometowns to large cities to study or find better employment opportunities. Since their families may remain in other areas, these individuals may choose to live alone. In addition, the traditional idea of a large family household is becoming less common in many societies.

Living alone can have positive effects. It can help people become more responsible because they have to manage their finances, cooking, cleaning and other household tasks independently. It can also give individuals more freedom to focus on their careers, education and personal interests.

However, there are also negative consequences. People who live alone may experience loneliness, especially when they have limited social interaction. If this lifestyle becomes increasingly common, family relationships may become weaker. Moreover, a society with many single-person households may have higher housing and living costs because more homes are required for the same number of people.

In conclusion, people increasingly choose to live alone because of financial independence, personal freedom and changes in modern lifestyles. While this can encourage independence and personal development, it may also increase loneliness and weaken traditional family connections.

## Essay 46 — Food and Farming

### Question

Food production has increased dramatically due to modern technology. What are the reasons for this increase? What problems can it cause?

### Sample Answer

Modern technology has significantly increased the amount of food that can be produced around the world. This is mainly because farmers now have access to advanced machinery, improved farming methods and better scientific knowledge. Although higher food production can help meet the needs of a growing population, it can also create environmental and health-related problems.

One major reason for the increase in food production is the use of modern agricultural machinery. Tractors, harvesters and irrigation systems allow farmers to cultivate larger areas of land in less time. As a result, farmers can produce more food with less manual labour. Another important factor is the development of improved seeds. Scientists have created crop varieties that can grow faster and produce higher yields. In addition, modern fertilisers and pesticides help crops grow effectively and protect them from insects and diseases.

Technology has also improved the way farmers manage their land. For example, modern irrigation systems can provide crops with the right amount of water, while digital tools can help farmers monitor weather conditions and soil quality. These developments allow farmers to make better decisions and reduce crop losses.

However, this increase in food production can cause several problems. The excessive use of chemical fertilisers and pesticides can damage soil and pollute rivers and groundwater. It may also harm insects, birds and other wildlife. Furthermore, intensive farming can reduce soil quality over time if land is continuously used without proper management. Another concern is that some farmers may depend heavily on chemical products to maintain high levels of production.

There can also be health concerns. If agricultural chemicals are used incorrectly, harmful substances may remain on food and potentially affect consumers. Therefore, strict regulations and proper farming practices are necessary.

In conclusion, modern technology has increased food production through machinery, improved seeds, irrigation and scientific farming techniques. However, excessive use of chemicals and intensive farming can damage the environment and create potential health risks. Therefore, technology should be used responsibly and sustainably.

## Essay 48 — Food and Farming

### Question

Many people choose to follow a vegetarian or vegan diet. Why do people choose this lifestyle? What are the benefits for personal health and the environment?

### Sample Answer

In recent years, an increasing number of people have chosen to follow vegetarian or vegan diets. People make this decision for various reasons, including health concerns, ethical beliefs and environmental awareness. In my opinion, this lifestyle can provide several important benefits for both individuals and the planet when the diet is properly planned.

One major reason why people become vegetarian or vegan is concern about animal welfare. Some individuals believe that animals should not be killed or used for food, and therefore they avoid meat and other animal products. Another reason is health. Many people believe that eating more vegetables, fruits, beans, nuts and whole grains can help them maintain a healthy lifestyle. In addition, some people adopt this diet because they are concerned about the possible health risks associated with consuming large amounts of processed or red meat.

There are several potential health benefits of a vegetarian or vegan diet. A well-balanced plant-based diet can provide plenty of fibre, vitamins, minerals and antioxidants. It may also help people maintain a healthy weight because many plant-based foods are relatively low in calories. Furthermore, eating more vegetables and whole grains can support good heart health. However, people following a strict vegan diet need to plan their meals carefully because they may need nutrients such as vitamin B12, iron and protein from fortified foods or suitable alternatives.

A plant-based lifestyle can also benefit the environment. Producing meat generally requires considerable amounts of land and water, and livestock farming contributes to greenhouse gas emissions. Therefore, reducing meat consumption can potentially lower pressure on natural resources and reduce environmental damage. In addition, growing plant-based foods can require fewer resources in many cases.

In conclusion, people choose vegetarian or vegan diets mainly because of concerns about animal welfare, personal health and the environment. A carefully planned plant-based diet can improve certain aspects of health while also reducing pressure on natural resources. Therefore, this lifestyle can be beneficial when followed in a balanced and informed way.
