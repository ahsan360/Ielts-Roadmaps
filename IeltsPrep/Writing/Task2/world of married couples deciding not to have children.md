# IELTS Writing Task 2 — Advantages and Disadvantages

## Question

**There is an increasing trend around the world of married couples deciding not to have children. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages for couples who decide to do this.**

---

## Introduction

An increasing number of married couples around the world are choosing to remain childless. Although this decision can provide couples with greater career opportunities and more personal freedom, it may also make it more difficult for them to maintain certain social relationships and receive family support in old age.

---

## Body Paragraph 1 — Advantages

One major advantage of remaining childless is that couples can devote more time and energy to their careers. Raising a child is a demanding responsibility that requires considerable time and attention, whereas child-free couples generally have fewer family obligations. As a result, they may be able to work longer hours, pursue professional development and concentrate more fully on their career goals.

Another advantage is that they have more leisure time. For example, couples without children can travel, socialise with friends or pursue hobbies more easily because they do not have to organise their lives around childcare.

---

## Body Paragraph 2 — Disadvantages

However, remaining childless also has some disadvantages. One potential drawback is that couples may find it difficult to maintain close relationships with friends who have children. Parents often spend much of their free time with other families whose lifestyles are similar to theirs, which may gradually reduce opportunities for social interaction with childless couples.

Another disadvantage is the possibility of having limited family support in old age. Adult children often provide emotional and practical assistance to their ageing parents, whereas childless couples may need to rely more heavily on friends, relatives or professional carers as they become older.

---

## Conclusion

In conclusion, remaining childless can allow couples to devote more time to their careers and enjoy greater personal freedom. Nevertheless, it may also create difficulties in maintaining some social relationships and can leave couples with less family support in later life.
