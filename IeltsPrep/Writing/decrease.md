## Useful Sentence Patterns

### Verb Pattern

- decreased steadily
- declined gradually
- fell sharply
- dropped dramatically
- plunged suddenly
- plummeted rapidly
- dipped slightly
- contracted considerably

---

### Noun Pattern

- There was a decrease in...
- There was a sharp decline in...
- The graph illustrates a substantial reduction in...
- The chart shows a dramatic fall in...

## Useful Phrases

- decreased from...to...
- fell to...
- dropped by...
- declined steadily
- reached its lowest point at...
- hit a low of...
- bottomed out at...

---

## Examples

- Sales fell from 500 to 200.
- The unemployment rate declined steadily.
- Prices plummeted dramatically.
- Production reached its lowest point in 2018.
