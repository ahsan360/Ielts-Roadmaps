## Useful Sentence Patterns

### Verb Pattern

- increased significantly
- rose steadily
- climbed gradually
- grew rapidly
- surged dramatically
- soared sharply
- jumped considerably
- rocketed suddenly

---

### Noun Pattern

- There was an increase in...
- There was a sharp rise in...
- The graph shows significant growth in...
- The chart illustrates a dramatic surge in...

## Examples

- Sales increased from 20 to 80.
- The figure rose steadily.
- Revenue surged dramatically.
- Production peaked at 900 units.
