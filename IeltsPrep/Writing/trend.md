# IELTS Academic Writing Task 1
# Trend Vocabulary

## Upward Trend

- show an upward trend
- exhibit an upward trend
- follow an upward trend
- experience an upward trend
- witness an upward trend

### Examples

- Sales showed an upward trend.
- The population exhibited an upward trend.

---

## Downward Trend

- show a downward trend
- exhibit a downward trend
- follow a downward trend
- experience a downward trend
- witness a downward trend

### Examples

- The unemployment rate showed a downward trend.
- Oil prices experienced a downward trend.

---

## Stable Trend

- remain stable
- remain constant
- remain unchanged
- stay the same
- level off
- plateau
- flatten out
- stabilise

### Examples

- Sales remained stable.
- The figure levelled off after 2018.

---

## Fluctuating Trend

- fluctuate
- fluctuate considerably
- fluctuate dramatically
- fluctuate slightly
- vary
- oscillate

### Nouns

- fluctuation
- variation
- volatility

### Examples

- The number fluctuated throughout the period.
- Prices experienced considerable fluctuations.
